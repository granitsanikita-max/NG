# Deep Research playbook — change log (scratch)

**Doc:** Notion → Product Launch Playbook → "Step 1 — Deep Market Research"
**Changed:** 2026-10-18 · by Daily Tasks station (at Nikita's direction)
**Why:** customer-research lists were coming back surface-level (1–2 items) and
desires were never laddered to their root — so every downstream step was built
on shallow data.

## What changed (3 surgical edits, nothing else touched)

1. **Global "Count it, don't guess" rule** — added: every customer list
   (pains · desires · needs · objections · failed solutions · triggers ·
   misconceptions · beliefs she holds · emotional states) must list EVERY
   distinct item in the data, each with its count, ranked — never a top 1–2.
   Only 1–2 items = under-collected. (§9B belief-chain ≤6 exception left intact.)

2. **§10 (Everything about the ICP)** — the "pulled from §2C" bullet now reads
   "list every distinct one, then rank" (all of them, not a top handful).

3. **New §10A — Desire Ladder (surface → core)** — inserted between §10 and
   §10B. Ladder EVERY surface desire ("why does she want that?" ×3–5) to a core
   human driver, extract every distinct core desire, THEN rank the cores by
   rolled-up count. Output = full map + ranked core-desire list. Repoints the §9
   One Belief at the #1 core (not the top surface desire). Mirrors §13, which
   already ladders the problem to its root.

## Ownership note
This is the Deep Research station's methodology. Daily Tasks made the edit on
direct request. If the Deep Research station wants to refine wording, it owns it.
