# Ad Copy Chief Checklist (Nikita's rules) — run on EVERY ad before showing it

## Clarity & voice
1. Direct ads only: "you" voice in brand voice. No first-person headlines.
2. PROBLEM FIRST: image headline AND primary line 1 name her problem in her own words (body part + moment). Never open on a scene, a fact, a twist or a cute call-out ("New moms, read this…").
3. Say "your baby" on first mention. Never open with "her/him/she/he" with nothing it refers to.
4. Clarity beats cleverness. A tired mom gets every line at a glance. No ad-writer phrases ("bent on your pillow", "one hour straight", "that's proof" without saying of what).
5. Unique mechanism: one plain line, crystal clear, repeated once.
6. Don't copy research wording verbatim.

## Targeting / ICP
7. Write to one mom. Specific new-mom details (feed times, bassinet, latch, contact nap). No line a 65+ woman would nod along to (e.g. "your age").
8. Match awareness + sophistication (stage 4 into 5).
9. Desire, not features. Hit 4+ LF8s.

## Every line earns its place
10. Cut fluff, duplicates, bullets that repeat the paragraph, off-message benefits (arm cutout only in D3).
11. Cutting must keep effectiveness: problem, mechanism, proof, objection, lift + drop, identity line.
12. Chief only what makes it stronger.
12b. SHORTER PASS on EVERY ad, every time: can each line say the same thing in fewer words? Cut repeats (two "that's why" doing one job, product facts said twice). Report the cuts and why effectiveness holds.
12c. Pedro: lead with her desire and pain, not education. Facts only as short proof after she feels it; never in the image subhead.

## Persuasion pieces that must be present
13. Authority/proof only where "who says so?" comes up. Numbers need a source ("2014 study on head angle"). No invented numbers. Placeholder expert = test only.
14. Objection answered before the CTA. Offer: 60 nights, full refund, keep the pillow; 5 year warranty if it goes flat; free shipping.
15. Lift then Drop; the drop calls back to this ad's own scene.
16. Clear CTA: Tap Shop Now.

## Compliance
17. No dashes. No medical words. No "neck pain" hook (use stiff / tight). US spelling.
18. D4: no posture/hump/body-change claims. D5: no medicine named, no results promised.
19. Safe sleep: baby always on her own surface; pillow only under mom's head.

## Format
Image text (headline + subhead) · Primary text · Headline field · Description.
