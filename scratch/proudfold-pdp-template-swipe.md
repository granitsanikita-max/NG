## Do NOT copy from these pages (brand + Meta compliance)
- Fake/rolling countdowns, "almost sold out", "15 people viewing" (Goda, Earthly Derm) — our brand promise is "no games".
- Clinician/doctor endorsement blocks (Sculptique, Goda) — no fake authority.
- Raw-skin / face before-afters — dignified bodies only, comfort captions.
- Any % result stats until we have our own data.
- Pre-selected subscription — ours is opt-in.
- Cure/treat/antifungal wording anywhere on the page.
