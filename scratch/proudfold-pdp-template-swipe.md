### Sections to borrow
**From Polar (put right after the hero):**
- "Made for women who…" checklist of quote chips in HER words ("I'm constantly lifting my fold to dry it", "Powder turns to paste by lunch", "I've stopped wearing dresses in summer") → closes with "If that sounds like you, ProudFold was made for you."
- "Until now, it was [X] or living with it": two red-X lists. **Diaper cream/powder** (made for babies, turns to paste, comes back) vs. **living with it** (raw by the end of a shift, the smell, hiding at the pool). Ends with one switcher quote.
- "Now there's an easier way" + 3 numbered steps (lift, smooth on a thin layer, get dressed).
- "Will it feel greasy?" fear section answered with short quote cards (our real fears: greasy, stains, stings).
- Comparison vs. diaper cream with a cost-per-day row.
- 13-question FAQ (more objections answered = fewer bounces from a burned buyer).
- Buy box with a free-gift progress bar ("0 of 2 unlocked") + "Is this a subscription? No, you pay once" line (fits our opt-in sub).

**From Stasis:**
- "A note from our founder: I built this because…": dark founder card with a pull quote. This is our real-founder moat (brain 05). Only use it with a real person and a real story.
- Ingredient explorer: one card per ingredient with the **real MSDS dose** + why it's there. Swap our existing ingredients component into this layout.
- "How it's made" numbered honesty list: no pixie dust, real amounts, what it is AND isn't, ships from the US. Turns the brand's honesty into a section.
- Repeated thin CTA strips between sections, each with a new one-liner.

**From Serene Herbs:**
- "Who in your home needs this?" tappable list → "Which one sounds like you?": the big-boob crew (under-breast) · the apron-belly fold · thighs that rub on long shifts · caring for mom · after weight loss (GLP-1). Each opens a 2-line "how ProudFold helps" (situation language only).
- Founder video thumbnail + "Started in a kitchen" story block (if a real founder video exists).

## Do NOT copy from these pages (brand + Meta compliance)
- Fake/rolling countdowns, "almost sold out", "15 people viewing" (Polar's "87% sold" bar too) — our brand promise is "no games".
- Clinician/doctor endorsement blocks (Sculptique, Stasis) — no fake authority.
- Raw-skin / face before-afters (incl. Polar's before/after cards) — dignified bodies only, comfort captions.
- Any % result stats until we have our own data.
- Pre-selected subscription — ours is opt-in.
- Cure/treat/antifungal wording anywhere on the page.
