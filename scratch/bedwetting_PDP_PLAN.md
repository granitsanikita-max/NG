### § 19 — Full FAQ (16 Qs, buyer's words, correct-use teaching, one volunteered "no")

1. `**Will he just sleep through it?**` — First 3 nights yes, probably. That's exactly when you walk him up. By night 5, his brain starts noticing. That's the whole method. (Concede then win.)
2. `**Does he wear it at sleepovers?**` — No. Never. The alarm is a home training tool for the 8 weeks BEFORE the trip. At the trip, he goes dry — or he wears discreet pull-ups for that one night.
3. `**Will it wake his brother?**` — Not on vibration mode. Vibration goes to the receiver across his room, not his brother's. If you pick the 2-receiver kit, one goes to your room and the other to his desk, bypassing the shared bedroom entirely.
4. `**Isn't this a punishment? Will it scare him?**` — No. "Should not use systems that penalise." (NICE 1.7.2) Our plan rewards every get-up. It never punishes a wet night. An adult who used one as a kid: "My parents were never angry when the alarm went off. I was excited for my brain to learn."
5. `**He's 11. Too old?**` — Alarms are **recommended first-line for kids 7 and up** (RCH). The older the child, the stronger his buy-in — and buy-in is the #1 predictor of success.
6. `**He's 7. Too young?**` — 7 is the peak age for bedwetting. He's exactly who this is for.
7. `**My doctor said wait.**` — AAP also says alarms are the first-line intervention when a family is ready. "Wait" is a default. Starting is a choice. His date won't wait.
8. `**What if the first alarm failed us?**` — "The most common cause of failure is the child's inability to wake up to the alarm. Parents help." (CPS) Most alarms sell the beep, not the plan. The plan is what trains him. That's what we ship.
9. `**What if it comes back?**` — Up to half relapse once (CPS). You restart the alarm. It works faster the second time.
10. `**Can't we just use the pill for camp?**` — You can. Desmopressin works ONLY on the nights taken (RCH). The alarm plan works for life. Many families do both: pill for the trip, plan for the months before.
11. `**Will I lose sleep?**` — The first week, one wake-up on wet nights. Then it falls. "The alarm goes off later and fewer times per night" is NICE's #1 early sign of response.
12. `**How do I use the chart?**` — Over his bed. He marks a sticker every time he gets up to the alarm. Not for dry nights. He marks for the ACTION. Rewards go to the chart, not to his breakfast.
13. `**Does this treat daytime wetting?**` — No. If he wets in the day too, see your doctor first.
14. `**What about constipation?**` — Check for it first. "If your child is constipated, treat the constipation first, or the alarm may not work." (RCH)
15. `**Subscription?**` — None, ever. One payment. No refills. No auto-anything. (Her #1 "don't do that" from the Trustpilot reviews of the last category.)
16. `**How do I cancel the plan and get a refund?**` — Email hello@[brand].com from your order email. Refund in 3–5 business days. Keep the unit, keep the stickers, keep the chart. Our only ask is you tell us what didn't work.
