## 1. Blockers — the page can't be finished until these exist

1. **Offer** (Offer step): 1 vs 2 receivers, price, tiers, what's in the box (get-up chart + plan card + countdown), guarantee length + written terms that match the ad word for word.
2. **Sample + the 6 filmed tests** (first drops, sweat/skin, clip hold, range through 2 walls, 3-night battery, one-button off). Gallery slides, the demo video and every hardware claim depend on them.
3. **Real photos/video of the unit** — supplier images aren't allowed as hero (F2: no stock or cheap-looking images).
4. **Number sheet** (Universal F3): price, savings, guarantee days, delivery window (CJ 5–11 days + processing), timelines (6–16 wks), "50–75% (AAP)". Ad, advertorial, PDP and cart all pull from one sheet.
5. **Reviews: zero.** Never fabricate. The plan below runs without them, and adds seeded/labelled reviews only once real.

## 2. Section-by-section (doc Blueprint → our version)

1. **Top bar** — the risk reversal in one line (e.g. "[X]-week guarantee · one payment, no subscription · ships in [real window]"). Real numbers only.
2. **Proof line above the title** — we have no rating. Use an **honest authority line instead of a count**: "A first-line treatment in doctors' guidelines (NICE)". Not an endorsement, no badge styling that implies one. Swap in a real un-rounded review count once we have one.
3. **H1 = name + benefit, subhead = mechanism.** Hit ≥4 of the 7 ingredients (pain, promise, specificity, simplicity, credibility, timeframe, curiosity). Direction: dry nights + start before the date + get-up practice. Timeframe must be honest ("start 8 weeks before camp"), never "dry in 2 weeks".
4. **Gallery story stack** (each slide has a burned-in claim, kid shown ~7–10, never a shamed child):
   1) hero + the promise · 2) pain/old way: pull-ups & "just wait" = a patch, not practice · 3) how it works: clip feels first drops → alarm across the room → he gets up → reset → point on the chart · 4) what's in the box (unit + get-up chart + plan card) · 5) the 8-week countdown to camp · 6) vibration mode = won't wake his brother · 7) size in hand (8 g, no cord) · 8) proof: NICE/AAP/RCH lines, quoted and named · 9) guarantee · 10) lifestyle: the sleepover yes. Test-proof slides (sweat, range) are added **only after the sample passes**.
5. **4 bullets** (Nikita formula): 2 life-after (fewer 3 a.m. sheet changes · says yes to the sleepover/camp) + 2 why-it-works (practice waking at the first drops · rewards the get-up, never punishes a wet bed).
6. **Price** — dollar + % saving only if the compare-at is real (Doc 4: no fake compare-at, unlike Unikor). Show **per-night price over 8 weeks** (price ÷ 56) and contrast it with the cost of a night of pull-ups (cost needs sourcing).
7. **Buy box** — "1. Choose your kit" tiles (e.g. 1 kid / 2 kids / + parent receiver, per the offer), each with a descriptor and "who it's for". Decision rule at the selector: "Shares a room? Use vibration mode." · "Pick 2 receivers if you sleep down the hall" (only if the 2-receiver kit exists). One button, price in the button. A delivery-date estimate under the button only if accurate.
8. **Under the button** — the guarantee written as a full sentence covering a **used** alarm (the category's gap), plus one proof line (an AAP quote until we have customers). Trust trio: guarantee · one payment, no subscription · real delivery window + named support email and reply time. List exclusions honestly.
9. **"Why you'll love it" accordion, open by default**: the get-up plan in the box — the part no competitor ships. Plus 3–4 embedded objections high on the page, in this order (Doc 2 forces): it actually wakes him (honest: you help the first nights) → it keeps working (only proven claims) → the first 1–3 weeks are hard → support and refunds are real.
10. **Review strip** — **SKIP at launch** (no fake reviews). Fill it with labelled parent quotes from public forums ("what parents say", source named), or leave it out. Later, cast real reviews by objection: the skeptic, the burned-alarm mom, the 10–12-year-old, the room-sharer, the "dry before camp" win.
11. **Value props** (benefit header, how in the body, matching image): won't shame him (it's not his fault) · works for deep sleepers by practising the wake-up (with the honest caveat) · quiet vibration for shared rooms · his chart, his date (buy-in, the #1 objection among 8–12 parents).
12. **Mechanism** — plant the premise first ("it's partly a waking problem — his body's signal can't wake him yet"), then the Wake-Up Rep, the fire-drill analogy, 3-step use ("Clip. Get up. Reset.") and a "who it's for / not for" line (rule out a medical cause first; not for daytime wetting).
13. **Comparison** — method vs method, no named rivals: pull-ups · waiting · lifting · pills · dry-night charts vs get-up practice. Use hedge verbs, cite NICE/RCH/CPS, and date any numbers.
14. **Specs** — honest spec panel: 8 g clip, wireless, 10 m range (spec, until tested), sound/vibration/both, rechargeable, **one tone, no volume control** stated plainly. Volunteered negative = trust.
15. **Authority at the claim** — AAP 50–75% inside the "does it work" section; NICE get-up reward rule inside the chart section; RCH "2 months before camp" inside the countdown section. Never "doctor-recommended", never a fake doctor.
16. **Results timeline** — "Night 1–7: you help him up · Weeks 1–3: smaller patches, alarm goes off later (NICE early signs) · Weeks 6–16: many kids get dry." Honest line: "This takes weeks, not days. That's why you start 8 weeks before the date." Roadmap = the 8-week plan.
17. **Guarantee block** in full, with claim steps shown. Long enough for the method (8–16 weeks+).
18. **Full reviews** — placeholder until real ones exist; show full distribution and incentive labels once there.
19. **FAQ in her words**: "Will he just sleep through it?" (concede-then-win) · "Does he wear it at sleepovers?" (No — it's home training for the weeks before) · "Will it wake his brother?" · "Is this a punishment / will it scare him?" · "What if it comes back?" (relapse is real, restart right away) · "Can't we just use the pill for camp?" · "He's 11 — too old?" · correct-use teaching (place the receiver across his room, help him up the first nights, check for constipation first) · one volunteered "no" (no volume control / not a cure) · subscription: "none, ever".
20. **Founder line** — **no real founder story yet → don't invent one.** Use a signed brand note in the coach voice, or ask Nikita for a true one.
21. **Offer repeated + closing CTA**, real urgency only (camp-season date math is true urgency: "camp in [month]? start by [date]"). Sticky buy bar on mobile. Email capture after reviews (free 8-week plan PDF — no health data or under-13 data to the pixel).

## 3. Compliance overlay on every PDP line (from Doc 3/4)

- SAY: "a first-line treatment in doctors' guidelines", "successful 50% to 75% of the time (AAP)", "usually 6–16 weeks", "start at least 2 months before camp", "not his fault", "reward the get-up, never punish".
- NEVER: 97%/98%, "2 weeks", cure, permanent, FDA approved/cleared, pediatrician-recommended, "wakes the deepest sleeper", dB figures, "the 1993 protocol", fake doctors/reviews, shamed-child imagery, fake timers/stock, a fake compare-at.
