# RecoverMama congruence audit: advertorial vs PDP vs homepage (2026-11-08)

Checked live: homepage, /products/the-night-shift-pillow (rendered, incl. Kaching), advertorial preview, refund + shipping policy.
Nothing was changed. Ordered by how much it hurts conversion.

## Fix ASAP (money leaks)

1. **Ad-to-PDP price shock.** The advertorial sells "$79.95" and "2 for $129". The PDP lands with the Set preselected and the price under the title shows **$159.90 / $239.90**, while Kaching shows **$129.52** and Add to Cart shows **$129.52**. So she sees 3 different prices in 5 seconds.
   - Fix: the title price must match the selected bundle (or hide it and let Kaching show price).
   - Set the Kaching Set to a fixed **$129.00**. The advertorial says "$129 ($64.50 each)"; $129.52 / 2 = $64.76.
2. **Single bundle badge says "SAVE $39".** $119.95 - $79.95 = **$40**. Change it to SAVE $40.
3. **Upsell names change in the cart.** Kaching sells "Microwavable Neck & Shoulder Heat Wrap" and "Lie Back Neck Stretcher". The Shopify products (homepage cards, cart, checkout) are "Night Shift Warm Wrap" and "Nap-Time Neck Rest". She adds one thing and checks out another name. Rename the Shopify products to match.
4. **The free bonus is a $0 product in Shop All.** /collections/all lists "Neck Reset Guide" (/products/digital-product) at $0.00. It devalues "FREE bonus", and anyone can order it alone (fulfilment cost). Hide it from the Online Store catalog and search, keeping it for Kaching only.
5. **Default color is Dark Gray.** Every advertorial visual (hero, product shot, offer box, Dana's pick) is the **White** pillow. Default the PDP to White Mesh so she lands on the pillow she just saw.

## Copy mismatches (trust)

6. **Shipping time.** The homepage FAQ says "within **5 to 10** business days". The PDP, advertorial and shipping policy say **4 to 10**. Change the homepage to 4 to 10.
7. **Rise time.** The advertorial says "give it **a few hours** to rise". The PDP, homepage and shipping policy say **24 to 72 hours**, and the PDP timeline says "a day or so". She'll unroll it, it will still be flat at bedtime, and she'll email for a refund. Change the advertorial to "give it a day or so to fully rise".
8. **Ratings count.** The advertorial fold says "★★★★★ 2,413 Ratings" and the homepage says "Loved by 2,400+". The PDP shows no rating count near the title, only ~9 written reviews. Clicking through from "2,413 ratings" to a page with no count reads like a bait and switch. Add a star + count line under the PDP title.
9. **Offer name.** The advertorial says "Founding **New-Mom** pricing" (x4). The PDP banner says "Founding **Mom** Pricing ... our first 500 pillows". Pick one name. Also the advertorial never says "first 500" while the PDP does; adding "first 500" to the advertorial makes the urgency line true and consistent.
10. **Crib vs bassinet.** The advertorial uses "crib" everywhere (you asked for the simpler word). The homepage feature card still says "curled toward the **bassinet**".
11. **Same first name on two pages.** "Alyssa M." (advertorial review) and "Alyssa P." (PDP review) have different stories. Anyone reading both notices. Rename one.

## Still live from earlier lists

13. Homepage footer "Track My Order" goes to /account (login wall). Point it at a tracking page or the shipping policy.
14. The PDP Size & Fit says 12 cm for every color. Your own size image says White and the other colors are **14 cm**, only Light Gray/Dark Gray/Navy are 12 cm. Make the text match the image.

## Ad-approval risk (affects whether the advertorial can run)

15. **Hero anatomy.** The hero GIF and its labels show a red disc pressing on a nerve ("Squeezed"). With the PDP footer "Not a medical device" and the advertorial disclaimer, that image implies a medical condition (disc bulge or nerve compression) the product claims to fix. Meta reviewers flag landing pages for exactly this. Keep the visual but soften the labels ("Neck bent all night" is fine; "Squeezed" is the risky one), or swap the left panel for the side-view X-ray style (strain glow, no nerve).
16. **The two-finger test (advertorial "Try this now")** is not a recognised clinical test. The real at-home check physical therapists give is the **nose / head-tilt check**: lying on your side, your nose should line up with the middle of your chest and your head shouldn't tilt down toward the mattress. Sources: Limitless PT, Therapeutic Associates PT, Pillowise. Swapping it in makes the box defensible and gives us an easy, clear image (a selfie of a mom on her side with a line from nose to chest).
