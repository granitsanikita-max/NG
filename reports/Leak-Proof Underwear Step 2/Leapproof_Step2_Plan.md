## 1. THE FUNNEL MAP (do this before any page)

**Chosen funnel: Quiz Funnel.** From the Funnel Selection Board, the Quiz Funnel is for *"personalization-heavy / multi-variant products"* and *"regulated or sensitive categories where eligibility is the frame"* — goal: *"kill decision fatigue — diagnose a need, prescribe a bundle."* That is exactly our product. It also solves three problems no other funnel does for us:

1. **Meta compliance (§2B).** We can't say "do you leak?" in the ad. The quiz lets *her* self-select condition + severity *on-site* — the compliance note literally said "move condition/severity qualification to the landing-page quiz where the user self-selects."
2. **Fit/size = the #1 return driver.** The quiz recommends her size + absorbency tier → cuts the category's biggest cost.
3. **Segmentation.** It sorts her into postpartum / perimenopause / athlete → personalized reveal copy + personalized email/retargeting.

**The flow (each page continues the conversation, never restarts the pitch):**

```
Meta ad (angle: "own the moment")
   → Quiz Landing Page   [one job: sell the quiz, get the first tap]
   → 15-question Quiz     [one job: diagnose + do the selling]
   → Gate (late/soft, Skip)   [one job: capture email/SMS as "send your match"]
   → Labor-illusion loader    [one job: make the result feel computed + confirm the deal]
   → Reveal / Offer page   [one job: prescribe ONE bundle, pre-selected, + guarantee]
   → Gift picker → Order bump → Cart drawer
   → Checkout (recap: first ship, cadence, renewal $, guarantee)
   → Post-purchase 1-click upsell   [the uncontested lane]
```

- **PDP still exists** but is the BOF/retargeting + direct-nav surface, not the main acquisition path. The quiz reveal does the PDP's decision work.
- Part C (pre-sell/advertorial) is built *after* Step 3 picks the ad angle. For a quiz funnel the **quiz LP is the pre-sell** — a separate advertorial is only added if a cold angle needs belief-shifting before the quiz.

## 2. PART A — THE GRAND-SLAM OFFER

*Offer ≠ discount. Test offers (stacked benefits), never discount depths. The offer is the biggest lever on the whole store — designed before any copy.*

### 2.1 Offer TYPE (we stack all three, in priority order)

| Type | Role for us | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Bundle** (primary) | The core buy. Same product, multi-pack. | Apparel/multiples = textbook bundle category (True Classic = ~$500M on this). She wants pairs in rotation. |
| **First-Order pop-up** (layer) | Email/SMS capture + first-purchase offer. | Cold first orders. Framed as the quiz reward. |
| **Subscription — "Refresh Rotation"** (LTV layer) | Quarterly auto-ship, replace worn pairs. | Nobody owns subscription in US washable incontinence. This is the white space. |

### 2.2 The bundle ladder (honest anchors — the wedge against the clones' fake −52%)

Retail is set at **brand level** (so it survives the Phase-2 product swap). Margins shown at BOTH test and real COGS.

| Tier | Name (by result, not qty) | Price | Per-pair | Honest anchor | GM @ test COGS | GM @ real COGS ($10.50/u) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-pack | **"The Tryout"** | **$29** | $29.00 | none — this is the *real* single-price anchor every bundle beats | $22.60 (78%) | $18.50 (64%) |
| **3-pack — HERO** | **"The Everyday Set"** | **$69** | $23.00 | 3 × $29 = **$87 → save $18 (21%)** | $58.50 (85%) | $37.50 (54%) |
| 5-pack | **"The Full Rotation"** | **$95** | $19.00 | 5 × $29 = **$145 → save $50 (34%)** | $80.25 (84%) | $42.50 (45%) |
| 7-pack | **"The Total Reset"** | **$119** | $17.00 | 7 × $29 = **$203 → save $84 (41%)** | $98.35 (83%) | $45.50 (38%) |

**Rules applied:**
- **Anchor to the single.** The $29 1-pack is deliberately unattractive — it exists to make every multipack look huge and to catch retargeting/trial. Every "save $X" is arithmetic off a price we *actually charge*, not an invented compare-at. (Everdries shows −52% off a price that was never real; we show a smaller but *defensible* 21–41%. Honesty is the brand wedge against their 2.5★ "misleading marketing" reviews.)
- **Auto-select the middle / the prescribed one.** In the quiz the reveal *pre-selects the tier the quiz prescribed* (usually the 3-pack for light/moderate, 5-pack for heavy or "every day"). On the PDP, the 3-pack is pre-selected and badged "Most popular."
- **Name by result, not quantity** ("Everyday Set," not "3-pack").
- **Price positioning:** sits *above* the clone/Everdries $8–$12/pair dropship floor (so we read as a real brand) and *below* the period-brand $20–$47/pair ceiling.
- **Bundles aren't locked to 1/3/5** — the 7-pack "Total Reset" is added for heavy/every-day users and is justified by the laundry-cycle logic in the quiz ("enough to never do an emergency wash").

### 2.3 Free gift (cheap, high-perceived-value, on-brand)

- **Gift:** a branded **discreet mesh wash + travel pouch** (doubles as a machine-wash bag and a "carry-a-spare-in-your-bag" pouch). Landed COGS ~$1.00–$1.50. Present as **"$14 value — FREE."**
- **Threshold: auto-adds at $59+** → the **3-pack hero and up qualify; the 1-pack does not.** This pulls single-pair shoppers up to the hero to "unlock the free wash bag."
- **Mechanic:** EG Auto Add to Cart Free Gift app + Slide Cart, so it visibly drops in ("🎁 Free wash bag added!").
- **Step-up (optional, copies Knix's consumable-GWP):** at $120+ add a **free overnight-absorbency pair** ("$29 value") to push 5/7-pack baskets.
- **Why a gift not a discount:** "free" beats "% off" psychologically, and a physical gift that improves the *experience* (not a tote) drives usage → retention.

### 2.4 Order bump + post-purchase one-click (the uncontested lane)

- **Order bump (in the Slide Cart, pre-checkout):** *"Add 1 Overnight / Heavy-Day pair — $19"* (vs $29 standalone). Same hero SKU, highest-absorbency variant. ~$19 of near-pure incremental margin, zero new CAC.
- **Post-purchase 1-click (right after checkout, no re-entering payment):** *"Add a second 3-pack at 25% off — one tap: $52 ($17.33/pair)."* **No incontinence competitor runs post-purchase one-click** — only Knix (a period brand) does. 10–20% take-rate on a relevant refill is normal → straight AOV lift with no ad cost. App: AfterSell / ReConvert.

### 2.5 Subscription — "Refresh Rotation" (the LTV white space)

- **Offer: subscribe & save 15%, 90-day cadence** (quarterly auto-ship of a 3-pack).
- **Framing:** underwear doesn't deplete like pads, so position as *rotation refresh / wear-and-tear replacement* ("replace your leak underwear every ~6 months for reliable protection"), not consumable replenishment.
- **Front-load:** bigger first-box value (free pouch + first-order code applies), small recurring. First box also carries the gift.
- **Make the sub the obvious deal on the reveal:** one-time pays more, no free pouch, no 15%. Mechanic: Recharge or Shopify Subscriptions.
- This + the guarantee is the combination **nobody owns in US washable incontinence.**

### 2.6 Guarantee — the hero of the entire offer

> **"60-Night Leak-Test Guarantee — wear them, wash them, put them through real leaks for 60 nights. If they don't hold, full refund and keep the pairs. No returns to ship back."**

- Kills Everdries' two biggest Trustpilot liabilities at once — "leak protection fails" **and** "buyer pays return shipping / no prepaid label / can't return once worn."
- Keep-the-pairs + no-return-shipping is *more generous* than Knix's 30-day and matches attn:grace/Modibodi's 60-day — and is cheap to honor (worn underwear has ~zero salvage value, so "keep it" costs the same refund minus the reverse-logistics headache).
- Add a **confidence stat once we have real data** ("fewer than X% ask for a refund"). **Do not fabricate the number** — seed it after real orders.
- **Lead every ad and the PDP + the loader with it.**

### 2.7 First-order pop-up (capture + first purchase)

- **Offer: 15% off first order** (email + SMS) — mid of the 10–20% competitor band; protects margin while matching the category.
- **Framed as a question first** (segments before it discounts): *"What are you looking to feel confident doing again?"* → Workouts / Everyday / Laughing & sneezing / Nights → capture → route to a matching welcome flow. (This popup = the quiz on-ramp; its answer passes into quiz Q1.)
- **One big number, never conflicting offers:** the site + popup combine into one advertised number. A 3-pack $69 − 15% = **$58.65 + free pouch + free shipping** is a strong, defensible first-order offer stacked on an *honest* anchor.
- **$ vs %:** our AOV is < $100, so **% off wins** ("15% off" > "$5 off"). Test store-credit framing later.
- **No fake countdowns / no "store closing."** The clones' perpetual timers are the trust liability we exploit. If urgency is needed, a *real* 72-hour first-order window tied to the code.

### 2.8 Always-on components (checklist)

- [x] **Price anchor** — the $29 single is the honest "should cost" every bundle beats.
- [x] **Reason for the discount** — "launch / first-order" + bundle = volume; never gimmicky.
- [x] **Guarantee** — 60-Night Leak-Test, keep the pairs (+ confidence stat later).
- [x] **Protect margin** — retail jacked to brand level; "discount" lands at the price we always intended.
- [x] **Target AOV = ~$85–$95** (hero bundle + free-gift threshold pull + order bump + ~15% post-purchase take). Build the whole store to hit it.

### 2.9 The 2–3 offer VARIANTS to test (offers, not discount depths)

| Variant | What's different | Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Gift + Guarantee (control)** | Bundle ladder + free wash pouch @ $59 + 60-night guarantee + 15% popup | Honest anchor + risk-reversal beats the clones' fake urgency |
| **B — BOGO framing** | "Buy 2, Get 1 Free" = the 3-pack reframed ("free" > "% off"), same economics | "Free always beats % off" (Live Fresh finding) lifts CVR |
| **C — Subscription-forward** | Reveal pre-selects "Refresh Rotation" sub (15% off + free pouch) as default vs one-time | Higher 90-day LTV even if first-order CVR is flat |

**Measure BOTH first-order CVR AND 90-day LTV** for each (an offer can win order #1 and crater repeats). Run via Intelligems (Everdries' exact A/B tool).

### 2.10 AOV / break-even math (why the stack is non-optional)

Assume a realistic category **Meta CAC ~$35** (crowded lane, rising fatigue; conservative). At **real COGS $10.50/u**:

| Order shape | Revenue | COGS | GM before CAC | Net after $35 CAC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 pair only | $29 | $10.50 | $18.50 | **−$16.50 (loses money)** |
| 3-pack hero | $69 | $31.50 | $37.50 | **+$2.50** |
| 3-pack + free pouch + $19 bump | $88 | $42.00 | $46.00 | **+$11.00** |
| 5-pack + bump | $114 | $63.00 | $51.00 | **+$16.00** |
| 3-pack + bump + post-purchase 2nd 3-pack @25% | $140 | $73.50 | $66.50 | **+$31.50** |

**Break-even ROAS at real COGS ≈ 1.9–2.0 on the hero bundle; the stack pushes contribution to $11–$31/order.** During the Phase-1 test (test COGS ~$3.50/u) every line is far more profitable — the test is structurally de-risked. **The rule:** 3-pack is the only hero in ads; free pouch + free shipping gated at $59 so the 1-pack is always the worse deal; post-purchase one-click + subscription carry LTV.

## 3. PART B — THE STORE / PAGES

### 3.1 THE 15-QUESTION QUIZ (this is where most of the selling happens)

**Name:** *"The 60-Second Leak-Test"* (named for the output, in brand voice — not "take a quiz").
**Entry CTA (never "take a quiz"):** *"Find Your Match + Price in 60 Seconds."*
**Intro screen:** time cost ("~60 seconds") · value preview ("You'll get: your absorbency match · your size · your price") · 3 trust logos/press · headline matched to the ad angle.
**Structure:** 3 named chapters, honest progress counter, one question per screen, single-select auto-advances, "why we ask" on every sensitive field, proof card every 2–3 screens.

**CHAPTER 1 — "YOUR MOMENT" (warm-up; easy, personal)**

- **Q1 — identity (easiest first tap):** *"What's the moment you want to stop thinking about?"*
  → The sneeze that catches me off guard · Laughing so hard it backfires · Mid-workout — jumping, running, lifting · Everyday — I never fully relax *(sets segment; passes in from the popup answer if taken)*
- **Q2 — onset → segment/belief ("why we ask: the cause shapes the fabric + absorbency we match you to"):** *"When did this start?"*
  → After having my kid(s) *(postpartum)* · The last couple of years, kind of out of nowhere *(perimenopause)* · Since I started training seriously *(athlete)* · Honestly, it's been a while
- **Q3 — confession (plants the problem):** *"Be honest — what are you using right now?"*
  → A pad or liner, most days · Period underwear, "just in case" · Nothing — I just cross my legs and hope · I avoid the trigger (pee first, skip the trampoline)

> **Proof card (normalize):** *"1 in 3 women who've had a baby leak when they sneeze, laugh or run. You're not broken — and it's fixable."* (cite source once seeded)

**CHAPTER 2 — "WHAT'S REALLY HAPPENING" (diagnosis → absorbency tier)**

- **Q4 — severity → TIER ("why we ask: this sets your absorbency so you're never over- or under-protected"):** *"How much, on a normal day?"*
  → A few drops, enough to feel it *(Light)* · A real amount on a big sneeze or jump *(Moderate)* · Enough that a pad doesn't feel like enough *(Heavy)*  → **routes absorbency tier**
- **Q5 — triggers (multi-select; Next disabled until picked):** *"What sets it off? (pick all that apply)"*
  → Sneezing/coughing · Laughing · Jumping / running / HIIT · Lifting heavy · Cold weather · On the way to the bathroom
- **Q6 — mechanism as data (plants the unique mechanism):** *"Did you know most 'leak-proof' underwear is actually built for period blood, not urine?"*
  → No — wait, there's a difference? · Yes, and that's my problem — it doesn't hold a fast gush · I kind of suspected it

> **Proof card (seed the mechanism):** *"Urine comes fast and thin. Period blood comes slow and thick. We engineer the gusset for a burst — not a trickle."*

- **Q7 — objection surfacing:** *"What's stopped you from fixing this already?"*
  → I don't want to feel like I'm wearing a diaper · I'm worried it's bulky or shows under leggings · I've tried stuff that leaked anyway · I didn't know real options existed
- **Q8 — emotional stake (confession):** *"Has it ever made you skip something?"*
  → Skipped a class / the trampoline with my kids · Changed what I wear (always dark, always a backup) · Held back a laugh-till-you-cry moment · Not yet — and I want to keep it that way

> **Proof card (outcome, strongest — right before the gate):** *"Most women testing Leapproof said they stopped thinking about leaks within 2 weeks."* (seed with real review data before launch)

**CHAPTER 3 — "YOUR FIT & LIFE" (fit → size, cut, bundle)**

- **Q9 — value → BUNDLE SIZE (late, no-shame price-sensitivity):** *"For something you wear this often, what matters most?"*
  → I want the one that just works · Best value for the money · Start small and prove it first  → **routes 1 / 3 / 5-pack lean**
- **Q10 — size ("why we ask: fit is the #1 reason leak underwear fails — we match it exactly"; decision rule: "between sizes? size up for coverage"):** *"What's your usual underwear size?"* → XS–3XL
- **Q11 — cut (planted trait):** *"Which feels like you?"* → Bikini · Hi-waist (tummy-smoothing) · Boyshort · Lightest-day cut
- **Q12 — use-case → bundle composition:** *"Where will you wear these most?"* → Every day, all day · Workouts & active days · Nights / heavy days · A mix
- **Q13 — rotation → qty justification:** *"How often do you want to do laundry for these?"* → I want enough to barely think about it *(→ 5/7-pack)* · A normal weekly wash is fine *(→ 3-pack)* · Just testing *(→ 1/3-pack)*
- **Q14 — vibe (low-stakes fun):** *"Pick your vibe."* → Everyday black · Nude-for-anything · A little color
- **Q15 — names the pain (last question before reveal):** *"If one thing changed, what would it be?"*
  → Move, sneeze and laugh without the second-guess · Ditch the pads for good · Just feel like myself again → CTA **"See My Match"**

**CONFIRM → GATE → LOADER → REVEAL:**

- **Confirmation (yes loop):** *"Here's what you told us — look right?"* (plays back severity, size, use-case) → YES / edit.
- **Gate — Late & soft, with Skip (maximizes completion while still capturing most emails), framed as results delivery:** *"Where should we send your match + your 60-Night Leak-Test details?"* [email + SMS] · Skip link. Ask name first ("so we can save your match"). **Email 1 delivers exactly that.** (Eligibility-style framing available if a sensitive ad angle needs it.)
- **Loader (labor-illusion, 4–8s):** *"Matching your absorbency across 3 tiers… checking your size… applying your 15% + free wash bag… confirming your 60-Night Leak-Test."* — confirms the deal **before any price**, shows the guarantee + PFAS-free test badge.
- **Reveal / Offer:**
  - **Identity label** by segment: *"The Comeback"* (postpartum) · *"The Reset"* (perimenopause) · *"The Unbothered Athlete"* (athlete).
  - **"Built for you because"** checklist of her answers + a "why this works" paragraph citing them (severity → tier, size, use-case).
  - **ONE hero bundle, pre-selected** (the tier the quiz prescribed), "RECOMMENDED" badge.
  - **Price per-pair, original anchored, 15% applied, renewal in $** if subscription ("renews at $X every 90 days").
  - **Down-sell below the hero** (the 1-pack "Tryout" for price objectors).
  - **Cross-sell** = the overnight pair, justified by her "nights/heavy" answer.
  - **Reviews beside the button:** quiz-accuracy reviews first, then outcome reviews.
  - **60-Night Leak-Test guarantee + human fallback** ("not sure of your size? Message us").
  - Price the hero just under the $59 free-ship line where it makes sense, so the free-pouch meter nudges one more pair.
- **Pre-checkout:** free-gift confirm → order-bump page (overnight pair, clear Skip) → checkout recap (first ship, cadence, renewal $, guarantee near Pay).

### 3.2 PDP SPEC (BOF / retargeting / direct — 9-point order per Step 2, enriched with the PDP reference)

1. **Outcome headline above the fold** ("Move, sneeze, laugh — own the moment") + hero image/video + star rating. (Above-the-fold answers: what is it, what's in it for me, why trust it. 5-second test.)
2. **Benefit bullets** (feature → benefit, never a naked feature): "4-layer urine-rated gusset → holds a full sneeze, not just a drip."
3. **Unique mechanism** (hidden cause → named fix): built for urine (fast/thin/high-volume), not period blood.
4. **Proof** — reviews w/ names + photos, UGC, 3rd-party absorbency test, PFAS-free lab badge (careful with before/after on Meta).
5. **Comparison chart** — vs pads (bulk, waste, cost), vs period underwear (wrong gusset), vs diapers (dignity).
6. **How it works** (the layer stack, only as much as needs explaining).
7. **FAQ / objections** (from Step 1's objection list: "will it hold?", "is it bulky?", "like a diaper?", washing/smell, sizing).
8. **Authority / as-seen-in + the 60-Night guarantee** as its own section.
9. **Sticky Add-to-Cart**, trust notes under the button (returns/guarantee/free shipping), real urgency only.

**Buy box:** subscription option present, one-time "defined by what it lacks" (no free pouch, no 15%), per-pair price shown, renewal/cancel stated under the button. Mobile-first; message-match the ad + quiz (never restart the pitch).

### 3.3 The rest of the pages (needed for ad approval + trust)

- **About / brand story** — the "own the moment, not the life-stage" origin; why we built for urine not blood; PFAS-free stance.
- **Reviews** page (Loox/Okendo).
- **Contact** (real support, Gorgias or email).
- **Shipping / Returns / Refund** — spell out the 60-Night keep-the-pairs terms.
- **Terms / Privacy** — required for Meta approval.

### 3.4 Cart + upsells (per the Cart/Checkout reference)

- **Add-to-Cart opens a slide drawer, not a page.** Upsells live *inside* the drawer, never blocking it.
- **Free-shipping / free-gift progress meter** at the top ("You're $X from a free wash bag + free shipping").
- **Order bump in the drawer** (overnight pair $19). Every bump passes the same-problem test.
- **Numbers match the quiz/PDP**, discounts auto-apply, one savings total above the subtotal.
- **Renewal stated in $ before Pay.** Reassure at both buttons (Checkout + Pay).
- **Checkout = designed, not default** (Shopify checkout branding + recap block).
- **Post-purchase thank-you:** kill anxiety (what happens next, ship date), then the one-click upsell.

## 4. PART B2 — TRACKING & ANALYTICS (the part most people get wrong)

- **Meta Pixel + Conversions API (CAPI / server-side).** Confirm ViewContent / AddToCart / InitiateCheckout / Purchase + quiz events (QuizStart, QuizComplete, Lead at the gate) fire.
- **Email/SMS popup asks a question first** ("what do you want to feel confident doing again?") → capture → route to matching welcome flow. **The popup's actual offer = the 15% First-Order offer framed as one number** — never a default 10%.
- **GA4 + a profit dashboard** (Triple Whale-style) to read AOV + 90-day LTV per offer variant.
- **Fire a REAL test purchase** and confirm it lands in Meta + analytics on **mobile** BEFORE spending a dollar. *If a test order doesn't show up in Meta + analytics, we are NOT done.*

## 6. ECONOMICS / THE MOAT

- **Target AOV: $85–$95.** **Break-even ROAS ≈ 1.9–2.0** (real COGS) on the hero; stack pushes to $11–$31 contribution/order; Phase-1 test margins are far fatter.
- **Target CAC: ≤ $35** to start; the funnel is built so we can out-bid competitors because our AOV + CVR are higher — we win on funnel math, not on having the "best" product.
- **The moat (nobody owns this):** honest anchor + real keep-the-pairs guarantee + subscription + post-purchase one-click, in US washable incontinence, under a credible named brand. Everdries/clones have the mechanics but 2.5★ trust + fake anchors; period brands have trust but no incontinence frame/subscription; Jude has sub+guarantee but underwear is a UK bolt-on.
