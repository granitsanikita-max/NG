## 🟨 §2B · Compliance — SAY / DON'T SAY (read before writing any copy)

**Product's ACTUAL category:** absorbent apparel; if marketed for incontinence it is an **FDA Class I, 510(k)-EXEMPT protective incontinence garment (21 CFR 876.5920, product code EYQ)** — freely advertisable; **the risk is the COPY language, not the product class.** Keep framing as apparel/absorbency/comfort to stay out of device/drug territory. `[P-FDA]`

| ✅ SAFE TO SAY | ❌ NEVER SAY |
|---|---|
| "Stay dry and confident" / "worry-free" / "all-day confidence" (outcome framing — passes Meta "you" test) `[P-Meta]` | "Do you suffer from incontinence?" / "Struggling with bladder leaks?" / "Tired of embarrassing leaks?" (Meta **Personal Attributes** auto-reject — asserts the viewer's medical condition) `[P-Meta]` |
| "Leak-resistant underwear for women who want extra protection" (product + self-selecting group) | "100% leak-proof" / "zero leaks" / "never leaks" (absolute — unsubstantiable; **NAD killed "zero leaks"** in this category) `[P-NAD]` |
| "Absorbs up to X ml (lab-tested, [method])" — ONLY with the test on file `[P-FTC]` | "Medical-grade" / "therapeutic" / "clinically proven to treat incontinence" (unsubstantiated; may trigger FDA device status) `[P-FDA]` |
| "Helps manage light bladder leaks" (symptom-management — Meta allows) `[P-Meta]` | "Prevents UTIs" / "cures/eliminates incontinence" (disease claim — FDA + FTC + Meta cure-ban) `[P-FTC]` |
| "PFAS-free, verified by third-party testing" — ONLY with total-fluorine + targeted PFAS report on file. (CA AB 1817 + NY, effective Jan 1 2025: ship with **no intentionally-added PFAS, or total organic fluorine BELOW 100 ppm — dropping to 50 ppm in 2027**.) `[P-PFAS]` | "Non-toxic" / "chemical-free" (bare, unqualified) — **Thinx ~$4–5M-settlement exposure** `[P-PFAS]` |
| Real, consenting, disclosed customer/PT testimonials | "Recommended by gynecologists" / any fake or non-consenting doctor endorsement (Endorsement Guides + Fake Reviews Rule + §43(a)/right-of-publicity) — **the signature scam tactic; avoid entirely** `[P-FTC][P-Fake]` |
| "Common but not normal — and manageable" (cites Mayo/Cleveland) | Shame/appearance hooks ("embarrassing leaks", "smell") — Meta **appearance-inferiority** rule `[P-Meta]` |
| Move condition/severity qualification to the **landing-page quiz** (she self-selects) | Fake countdown timers, "only 3 left" that never changes, fake "news article" advertorials — FTC dark-patterns/native-ad deception + the **2024 Fake Reviews Rule** `[P-Fake]` |

**Why your last ads probably got rejected (pre-empt):** second-person condition language ("your bladder leaks"), absolute "100% leakproof", or shame hooks. **How to reach her WITHOUT naming the condition:** lead with the *moment* (the sneeze/jump/trampoline) + confidence/outcome, and let her self-identify ("for women who want to jump without thinking about it"); keep all condition education on the landing page (Meta reviews LPs too — same rules apply). `[P-Meta]`

(UK/EU note, if expanding: ASA/MHRA treat "relieves/treats incontinence" as a **medicinal claim** needing a licensed device — keep to absorbency/apparel claims; substantiate every stat/testimonial. `[P-ASA]`)

## ⭐ TOP 5 TAKEAWAYS
1. **"Do you suffer from incontinence?" and "100% leak-proof" are instant Meta Personal-Attributes / NAD rejections** → lead ads with the MOMENT (sneeze/jump/trampoline) + confidence framing, and push condition/severity qualification to the landing-page quiz where she self-selects.
2. **Confirmed white space: NOBODY in the incontinence DR lane pairs real trust (named founder + published absorbency test + real reviews) with a money-back wear-test guarantee + a replenishment subscription** → that exact combination is the offer and the moat.
3. **Everdries/clones run NO subscription and a fake-anchor bundle with no real guarantee; Knix's Rebuy+AIOD upsell drives a $109 AOV** → out-economics them with subscribe-&-save + an honest guarantee + a real upsell engine, not a bigger fake discount.
4. **Price ladder: the product costs <$2.60/unit at AliExpress and sells DTC at $8.66–$47/unit** → price $12–$18/unit in bundles (above the clone floor, below the period-brand ceiling); re-run Pre-0 at real COGS.
5. **Locked positioning: "the blunt, athlete-to-athlete brand FOR proven, no-shame protection and AGAINST an industry that sells women pads instead of the truth"** → it passes the Uncopyable Test (incumbents would indict themselves or cannibalize their clinical/period equity); every surface must execute this one line.

## 4B · Brand Positioning System (Liquid Death model) — LOCKED

- **The Enemy:** the **disposable-industrial complex and the lie it sells** — an industry (and a medical culture) that tells women leaking is "just normal after kids / at your age" and hands them a pad to buy forever instead of a real fix. Found inside the category's own 1★ reviews ("fell for the marketing", 2.5★ Everdries, "still need a pad") `[C101]` and the one VERIFIED on-record line — **Fortune: the category's "growth isn't due so much to population aging as it is marketing"** `[H3] CONFIRMED`. (The spicier Bloomberg/Kimberly-Clark-exec quotes were cut in §20 as unverified — the enemy stands on the Fortune line + the 1★ reviews + the disposable lock-in, no fabricated quote needed.)
- **What we stand FOR:** **the truth that leaking is common but NOT normal, not shameful, and not a life sentence — and that you deserve gear that actually holds, proven, not whispered.** We publish the test. We show the 1★ reviews. We'd rather lose a sale than lie. `[C134][C89]`
- **The Attitude:** blunt, dry-funny, athlete-to-athlete, matter-of-fact about pee. The opposite of the whole category's hushed/clinical/pastel register. "If you wee yourself, you just rock on — but you shouldn't have to." `[C126]`
- **The Uncopyable Test:** if Everdries/the clones adopted "we publish third-party proof and show our bad reviews" tomorrow, it would **indict them** (their 2.5★ and fake testimonials are the whole problem). If Jude/Thinx chased the active-30s–40s athlete frame, they'd **cannibalize** their clinical-50+ / menstruation equity and confuse their channels. ✅ Real moat.
- **Whole-store thread:** the position lives on every surface — **Name** (athletic/engineering, not "-dri/-sure/continence"); **About-page manifesto** (common-but-not-normal + France story + founder's active origin); **product page** (published ml test, PFAS cert, real reviews incl. misses); **offer/guarantee** (wear-test refund); **which reviews we show** (the misses too); **voice** (athlete-to-athlete).
- **Weakness-as-weapon:** small/new/broke = the underdog vs. the Everdries ad-flood and the diaper giants. "We can't outspend them — so we out-honest them. We're the brand that shows you the lab test and the bad reviews the big guys hide." David vs. the disposable Goliaths.
- **Guardrails:** (1) we must ACTUALLY be it — publish real tests, real reviews, a real guarantee (a hypocritical "honest" brand detonates worse than none); (2) attack the category PATTERN, never name-and-shame a specific competitor (defamation risk).

**🔒 LOCKED one-liner:**
> **"We are the blunt, athlete-to-athlete brand that stands FOR proven, no-shame protection and AGAINST an industry that sells women pads instead of the truth — leak-proof underwear for active women who refuse to pee themselves quietly and refuse to be called old."**

Everything downstream (§13B story, §14 voice, offer, store) executes this line.

## The Funnel Map

**Chosen funnel: the Discovery Stack — Advertorial (pre-sell) → Offer Page.** Why: she's problem/solution-aware (§5), the market is Stage 4–5 + ~⅓ burned (§8), and single-hero products default to the Discovery Stack. She needs the mechanism + proof BEFORE the offer, and cold traffic must not hit a bare PDP (§7). `[Funnel board]`

| Stage | What it does for THIS product |
|---|---|
| **Ad** | Stops the scroll on a relatable MOMENT (the double-under / trampoline / treadmill / sneeze) + a credible mechanism tease ("built for urine, not blood — and we lab-tested it"). Job: earn the click, NOT the sale. Compliant framing (no "you suffer from…"). |
| **Pre-sell page (Advertorial)** | Gives the **mechanism (UMP→UMS: urine is fast/thin, period products fail, our sealed urine-tuned core holds it) + proof (published ml absorbency test, PFAS cert, pour-demo, real reviews) + the "common but not normal / France" story.** Walks the §9B belief chain. Continues the conversation; doesn't repeat the ad. |
| **Product page** | Sells THIS product + the offer: ml-rated absorbency tiers, fit quiz (true-US sizing), real reviews incl. misses, the **wear-test money-back guarantee** as a headline, bundle ladder (3-pack hero). Offer built in Step 2 from §15 inputs. |
| **Cart** | Raises order value: bundle up-tier (add a pair / add overnight), free-shipping threshold, subscribe-&-save (replenish every 2–3 months). |
| **After purchase** | Kills regret + drives repeat: post-purchase **fit follow-up** (reduces returns, seeds exchanges), **replenishment reminder at ~60–90 days**, new-color cross-sell, review/UGC request. This is where the LTV (and the economics) are made. |

(No pre-sell page would be a mistake here — she's mid-funnel-awareness and burned; the advertorial is mandatory.)
