## 2026-09-05
- [ad_creative] TikTok slideshow / hook structure — slide1 pain point (90% of outcome), product mid, buy link end, slides 5-6 = proof; caption front-loads one searchable keyword; extract shared pattern across 3+ winners then repeat with variation — src: https://x.com/ceonadz/status/2095490126948544600
- [scaling_testing] Meta 2026 = one CBO scaling campaign + broad targeting + pile of winning creatives; stop over-segmenting into many ABO ad sets — src: https://x.com/nicktheriot_
- [store_cro] Tier-based visual mix-and-match bundles (buy 1/2/3, per-unit savings) lift AOV + CVR vs single-unit page — src: https://x.com/adityasingh_58
- [scaling_testing] Don't kill ads on 3 days of noisy early data; set a min-spend kill rule (~1-2x product price/ad) before judging — src: https://x.com/ruben_vdzr
- [ad_creative] AI UGC reads fake when actor+product+message aren't aligned; fix alignment, not just render quality — src: https://x.com/kamil_sattar
- [scaling_testing] "Dayscaling" = scale budget on winners intraday, not once/day (method gated) — src: https://x.com/edjhlim
- [ad_creative] ~60% of a $100M brand's winning ads came from outside the in-house creative team (real creators/UGC) — favor creator volume over polished in-house — src: https://x.com/dkrd19
- [ad_creative] Reuse proven winning ads to scale spend into BFCM rather than testing fresh creative right before peak — src: https://x.com/LafranceDa75675
