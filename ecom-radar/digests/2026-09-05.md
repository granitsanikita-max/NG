1. **Steal the TikTok slideshow structure for your hooks** — 11/12
   - What it is: @ceonadz's full slideshow formula — slide 1 = pain point,
     product in the middle, buy link at the end, slides 5–6 show it working /
     the result. "First slide is 90% of the outcome." Caption front-loads ONE
     searchable keyword.
   - Why it's worth it for you: it's a hook-writing framework, not just a
     TikTok trick. The "pain-point-first, proof-in-the-middle" beat maps
     straight onto your paid UGC ad scripts and your product-page above-fold.
   - Do this: pick your current product, search the niche on TikTok, pull 3+
     winners, extract the shared pattern (not one example — the pattern), then
     write 3 hook variations using pain-point slide 1. Test them as your next
     ad batch.
   - Source: https://x.com/ceonadz/status/2095490126948544600 (@ceonadz)

2. **Collapse your Meta account to one CBO scaling campaign** — 10/12
   - What it is: Nick Theriot: "FB Ads in 2026 is just one scaling campaign,
     broad targeting, CBO, a pile of winning creatives. Everything else is
     noise."
   - Why it's worth it for you: you're one person — over-segmented ABO
     structures eat your time and split your data. Consolidation lets Meta's
     algo do the work while you focus on creative volume (your actual edge).
   - Do this: audit your current campaign structure. If you're running many
     tiny ad sets, consolidate winners into one broad CBO and feed it creative
     instead of managing audiences.
   - Source: https://x.com/nicktheriot_ (@nicktheriot_)

3. **Add a tier-based visual bundle to your product page** — 10/12
   - What it is: @adityasingh_58 — visual, tier-based mix-and-match bundles
     ("buy 1 / buy 2 save X / buy 3 save more") lift AOV and conversion vs a
     single-unit page.
   - Why it's worth it for you: cheapest lever you have. Same traffic, same ad
     spend, higher order value — directly widens your margin on every winning
     product.
   - Do this: on your current store, add a 3-tier quantity bundle with a clear
     visual + per-unit savings on the buy box. Measure AOV before/after.
   - Source: https://x.com/adityasingh_58 (@adityasingh_58)

4. **Stop killing ads at day 3** — 9/12
   - What it is: @ruben_vdzr — "Killing a good ad too early has cost me more
     than any bad product ever did." He'd run something 3 days, see
     inconsistent early data, and kill it before it stabilized.
   - Why it's worth it for you: early ad data is noisy. Cutting on 3 days of
     wobble throws away creatives that would've worked — expensive when good
     creative is the hard part.
   - Do this: set a written kill rule (e.g. min spend = 1–2x product price per
     ad before any decision) and hold to it instead of reacting to day-1/2.
   - Source: https://x.com/ruben_vdzr (@ruben_vdzr)
