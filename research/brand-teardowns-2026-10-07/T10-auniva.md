# Brand Teardown: Auniva (formerly Alevia) — "Pomegranate 89"

**Date:** 2026-10-07
**Domain:** auniva.co (US, Austin TX) · Product page: https://auniva.co/products/pomegranate-89
**Category:** Herbal supplement (ingestible) · Health > Nutrition & Wellness > Herbal Supplements
**TL;DR:** Single-ingredient organic pomegranate-extract capsule sold as a **natural erectile-dysfunction + heart-health fix for men 50+**. Part of a large, aggressive portfolio supplement operation (formerly "Alevia," rebranded to "Auniva" ~Oct 2026) running thousands of advertorial/persona-page Meta ads. Very high compliance risk.

---

## 1. PRODUCT
- **What it is:** "Organic Pomegranate Extract Capsules." Ingestible capsule, **700mg organic pomegranate extract per serving**, 2 capsules/morning, 30 servings/bottle. Single ingredient, "no blends or fillers." Made in USA, third-party tested, non-GMO. (Evidence: product page + FAQ.)
- **Key ingredient/benefit:** Punicalagins (pomegranate polyphenols) pitched as "the only antioxidants small enough to reach the 1mm vessels" that cause ED, then "clear" larger heart vessels. Benefit stack: performance, heart health, energy, "zero side effects."
- **Topical vs ingestible:** **Ingestible** (oral capsule / supplement facts panel shown). Definitely not a serum/oil.
- **Price/variants:** Buy 1 **$29.99** (vs $59.99, "50% off") · Buy 2 Get 1 Free **$59.99** (vs $179.97) · Buy 3 Get 2 Free **$89.99** (vs $299.95). Published store price point is $39.99/bottle (WH store data); AOV ≈ **$70.95** (WH). Effective per-bottle on bundles ~$18–30.
- **Subscription:** Yes — "Save 25% with Automatic Refills, ship every month, cancel anytime." (Primary monetization + source of complaints — see §5.)
- **Guarantee:** **90-Day "Empty Bottle" money-back**, no need to return bottles.

## 2. POSITIONING
- **Core promise (verbatim hero):** *"Clears the smallest vessels in your body, the ones giving you ED, so performance comes back naturally and your heart stays protected from what's coming next."*
- **Big idea (verbatim):** *"ED Isn't a Sex Problem. It's a Heart Warning."* / *"Your penis is the canary. Your heart is the coal mine. Men with ED have up to 50 times higher risk of cardiac events."*
- **Enemy:** (a) **PDE5 prescription pills** — "the little blue pill," the 4-hour window, flushing, headaches, cost, "forces blood through clogged pipes while damage keeps building"; (b) **other natural ED supplements** (L-arginine, L-citrulline, beetroot, maca, horny goat weed, shilajit, test-boosters) framed as "work for a week then quit"; (c) doctors who "say it's just age."
- **Mechanism:** Punicalagins reach 1mm penile vessels → clear damage → smallest vessels recover first → larger heart vessels follow. Staged "90-Day Vessel Restoration" timeline (Wk1-2 energy, Wk3-4 performance, Wk5-8 heart, Wk9-12 protection).
- **Avatar:** **Men ~50-75**, married, with ED they're embarrassed about and quiet cardiovascular anxiety (cholesterol, BP, family heart-attack history). Testimonials are men 54-73.
- **Tone:** Alarmist health-warning + reassuring natural-root-cause + intimate first-person "Reddit rant" native voice.

## 5. WEAKNESSES
- **Compliance (the big one):** Classic illegal supplement drug-claim profile. A dietary supplement is making **disease treatment/prevention claims** — treats ED, "clears vessels," prevents cardiac events ("50x higher risk"), lowers cholesterol ("numbers came down 30 points, doctor cancelled my statin"), direct comparison to Viagra/Cialis. This is FTC/FDA-actionable (unapproved drug + deceptive claims). Testimonials claim life-saving heart outcomes.
- **Fake trust signals:** Reused reviewer stock photos, persona advertiser pages, fake "clinic"/"support group" pages, "surveyed daily users" stats with no source. Easily disprovable.
- **Reviews / reputation:** Trustpilot (legacy alevia.com, 4.5/1,199) is riddled with complaint clusters WH flagged: **"Unauthorized subscription enrollment and unexpected charges" (13), "Unwanted subscription enrollment and difficult cancellation" (9), "Unwanted shipments despite cancellation" (5), "Unclear/deceptive subscription terms" (5), "Return shipping costs unfairly charged," "product ineffective."** The rebrand Alevia→Auniva ("a name we can trademark and protect") is partly reputation laundering / resetting the review trail.
- **Platform risk:** 9,935 ads + fake personas + health claims = high Meta ban/zuck'd risk; they mitigate with page sprawl and no-EU targeting, but ad-account mortality is baked in.
- **Saturation:** The "ancient fruit punicalagins for ED/heart" + "forum confession" angle is now run at enormous volume by this operator — the specific angle is saturating within their own footprint.

## 6. HOW WE BEAT THEM
**(a) Creative:** Keep the proven *mechanism story* (small-vessel/heart-warning is a genuinely strong, novel hook) but go **legitimate + differentiated**: real on-camera practitioner or real customer VSL/UGC, verifiable third-party COA, named clinical references on punicalagins — compliant "supports healthy circulation" framing with strong curiosity hooks. Our edge = trust where they're hollow. Also attack *their* weakness directly in soft-angle creative: "the pomegranate brand that won't trap you in a subscription."
**(b) Funnel:** Win on honesty as a conversion lever — **transparent, one-click-cancel, no forced subscription** (make "no auto-refill trap" a headline benefit), real verified reviews (Loox/Okendo), visible COA/lab results, real founder + real support. Keep their bundle economics (Buy 2 Get 1) but lead with a cleaner 60-day guarantee you actually honor. This converts the large pool of their burned/refund-seeking customers.
**(c) Market/avatar:** Same core avatar (men 50-70, heart-anxious) but **stay in compliant lane** (circulation/energy/vitality "structure-function" claims, not ED-cure) to survive on-platform long-term — durability beats their burn-and-churn. Optional flank: the *spouse* angle (women buying for partners) and a cardiovascular-wellness general-health positioning that isn't one disclaimer away from an FTC letter.

## 7. MARGIN (INFERENCE)
- **Unit cost:** Pomegranate-extract capsules (60ct, single ingredient) landed from contract manufacturer ≈ **$3–6/bottle** (China/US blend).
- **Price realized:** AOV **$70.95** (WH) ≈ ~2 bottles/order at bundle pricing.
- **Landed + fulfillment per order:** COGS ~$8–12 (2 bottles) + pick/pack/ship ~$6–8 + processing ~3% (~$2) ≈ **$16–22/order**.
- **Contribution/order (pre-ad):** ≈ **$49–55 (~70-75% margin).**
- **Break-even ROAS:** ≈ **1.35–1.45** on paper; realistically **~1.6–1.9** once refunds/chargebacks (high, given subscription complaints) and churn are factored. Subscription refills are where the model actually prints — first order often near break-even, profit on refill #2+.

## 8. VERDICT
- **BUILD / TEST / AVOID → TEST (the angle), AVOID (the execution).**
- **Biggest reason:** The *mechanism/positioning* ("ED is an early heart warning → punicalagins restore the smallest vessels first") is a genuinely strong, under-used hook with proven scale (1,900+ ED ads, 140k+ visits/mo at peak). Worth testing a **compliant** version. But copy the operator's *execution* (disease claims, fake personas/clinics, forced subscriptions) and you inherit FTC/FDA + Meta ban exposure and the same refund/chargeback reputation spiral. Build the clean version or don't build it.
- **Compliance risk: HIGH.** As currently run it is a textbook unapproved-drug-claim supplement funnel with deceptive trust signals and subscription-trap complaints. Any direct clone is high-risk; a structure-function-compliant circulation/vitality product is the only safe path.
