# Brand Teardown — JCKED "Liquid Pryme"

**Domain:** jcked.com · **Product URL:** https://jcked.com/products/liquid-pryme
**Teardown date:** 2026-10-07 · Store HQ: Tampa, Florida US · Shopify (Shrine Pro theme) · Store created 2025-06-13
**Sources:** on-site scrape, WinningHunter store + Meta ad data, Trustpilot, web search. INFERENCE = marked.

---

## 1. PRODUCT
- **What it is:** A **liquid natural testosterone-booster shot** for men. Flavored (Fruit Punch), zero-stimulant. Dose: 1.5 Tbsp (22.5 mL / ½ cap) once daily. Positioned as a "5-Stage Testosterone System," not a single-ingredient T booster.
- **Ingredients (19 actives, 5 stages):**
  1. **Boost Test:** D-Aspartic Acid, Tongkat Ali, D3, K2, B6, Magnesium, Zinc, Boron
  2. **Block Estrogen:** Calcium D-Glucarate, DIM, Luteolin
  3. **Build Muscle:** L-Leucine, Betaine (TMG), Calcium HMB
  4. **Fuel Drive:** Mucuna Pruriens, Fenugreek, Coleus Forskohlii
  5. **Fast Recovery:** Ashwagandha, Shilajit
- **Claims (verbatim hero):** "Boost Testosterone, Drive & Lean Muscle" / "Raise free & total testosterone" / "Build lean muscle & strength" / "Restore drive, mood & energy." Full FDA disclaimer present ("not intended to diagnose, treat, cure...").
- **Price/variants:** og:price listed $59.95; PDP shows **Buy 1 $49.95** (compare $80), **Buy 2 $99.90** (compare $160, "most popular"), **Buy 3 $149.85** (compare $240). ~$49.95/bottle ≈ 1 month supply.
- **Free-gift ladder (drives AOV):** Buy 1 = 12-Week Shred Challenge ($29.95 value); Buy 2 adds 7× L-Carnitine sachets; Buy 3 adds shaker + microfiber towel ("up to $90 in free gifts"). AOV reported **$66.94**.
- **Subscription:** NOT on Pryme itself — hidden inside the "free gifts." Fine print: *"your L-Carnitine ships and bills every 4 weeks, and your 12-week challenge is free for 4 weeks, then $29.95/month."* So the "free" gifts convert to recurring billing. "Save 20% + Free Shipping on refills, cancel anytime."
- **Guarantee:** 365-Day Money-Back Guarantee ("full refund even if bottles are empty"). + $4.95 shipping-protection upsell in cart.

## 2. POSITIONING
- **Promise:** Reclaim the testosterone, drive, strength and body of your 20s–30s — naturally, no needles/TRT.
- **Enemy:** Age-related low-T + synthetic alternatives ("No shots. No chemicals," "not a hormone or steroid; no PCT required"). Also positions against underdosed pill/powder competitors.
- **Mechanism:** "5-Stage Testosterone System" + "Liquid Fast-Absorb" (absorbs faster than pills) + "Dedicated Estrogen Control" (the differentiator most T products "ignore"). Pseudo-scientific staged story.
- **Avatar:** **Men 40+** (the bundled 12-Week Shred Challenge is explicitly "built for men 40 plus," coaches "Coach Jeff"). Gym-goers feeling flat/low-drive. Explicitly "not for women."
- **Tone:** Masculine, locker-room, reassurance-heavy ("no jitters, no crash, feel like myself again"). Testimonials emphasize drive/libido + gym PRs.
- **Aesthetic:** Black/white high-contrast, 3D bottle renders, stat cards, timeline/stages infographics, comparison table.
- **Hero copy verbatim:** *"Liquid Pryme — Boost Testosterone, Drive & Lean Muscle."* Rotating bar: *"OVER 55,000+ HAPPY CUSTOMERS · 5-STAGE TESTOSTERONE SYSTEM · LIQUID FAST-ABSORB · DEDICATED ESTROGEN CONTROL."*

## 3. OFFER & FUNNEL
- **Landing type:** Conversion-optimized Shopify PDP (Shrine Pro) with heavy CRO stack. Also runs a **listicle advertorial** (`/pages/5-reasons-why-list1`) for cold traffic.

- **Urgency/scarcity:** Countdown timer ("BIG DEAL DAYS UP TO 55% OFF + FREE GIFTS", HRS:MIN:SEC), 10-min cart reservation timer, "You're saving $105.00."

## 5. WEAKNESSES
- **Review-trust gap (biggest):** On-site claims **"4.7★ / 55,000+ reviews"** but external **Trustpilot = 2.8★ from 33 reviews** (WH pulled an even lower 2.5/5 sample). The 55k number is almost certainly aggregated across all products/not independently verified. INFERENCE: the "55,000 happy customers" and before/after photos are the soft underbelly a competitor or regulator can attack.
- **Documented Trustpilot complaint themes (WH gap analysis):** "Drop shipping from China despite USA branding" (×2), "Subscription sign-up without clear consent" (×3), "Difficulty canceling subscriptions," "Unwanted automatic reshipment," "Delayed delivery," "Incorrect order fulfillment." **0% reply rate to negative reviews.**
- **Deceptive subscription:** "Free gifts" silently enroll buyers into recurring billing ($29.95/mo + L-Carnitine every 4 wks) — FTC "negative option" / click-to-cancel exposure in the US.
- **Compliance / Meta policy risk (HIGH):**
  - **Fake doctor pages** ("Dr. Margaret Sinclair MD," "Dr. Andrew Caldwell PhD") = fabricated medical authority → Meta personal-health & misrepresentation violations, FTC endorsement-guide violations.
  - **Testosterone/"drive & libido" claims** trip Meta's health/sexual-performance ad policies and personal-attribute ("feeling off lately?") rules — prone to disables.
  - Supplement structure/function claims pushed near disease territory.
- **Banned-ingredient / formulation risk (INFERENCE MED):** 19 actives in one 22.5mL liquid shot means most are almost certainly **sub-clinical/underdosed** (label-decoration, no Supplement Facts panel shown). DIM + Tongkat + Ashwagandha liquid stability is questionable. No COA/third-party testing shown. Fenugreek/Tongkat flagged in some sports-testing contexts.
- **"USA" branding vs China drop-ship** = provenance claim risk.
- **Saturation:** Men's T/gym supplement is extremely crowded (Tongkat/Fadogia/shilajit everywhere). Differentiation rests on format (liquid) + estrogen-control story, both copyable.

## 6. HOW WE BEAT THEM
**(a) Creative:** They lean on fake-doctor pages and generic "feeling off lately" hooks — legally fragile and getting formulaic. Win with **clean, compliant, genuinely-UGC founder/athlete testimonials** and a real named expert (actual licensed advisor) → lower account-ban risk + higher trust. Use **specific, demonstrable mechanisms** ("liquid absorbs faster" is their one ownable claim — beat it with a visual sublingual/absorption demo). Open TikTok paid + Spark Ads (they've ignored it entirely — wide-open channel at lower CPMs).

**(b) Funnel:** Match their CRO stack (bundle ladder, gift ladder, AfterSell) but **make the subscription transparent** — explicit opt-in checkbox, 1-click cancel. Turn their #1 complaint (hidden subscription, no cancel) into our headline trust promise. Keep the 365-day guarantee but back it with **real third-party reviews (Judge.me/Okendo) and a published COA** — directly undercutting their 2.8 Trustpilot.

**(c) Market/avatar:** They own "men 40+ gym." Flank: (i) go younger 28–38 "early edge / prevention" angle, or (ii) go deeper on a single hero mechanism (e.g., sleep+T, or libido-first for relationships) rather than a vague 5-stage everything-shot. Also capture their **Mexico/Spanish-speaking 4–6% spillover** with a dedicated ES funnel they're not optimizing.

## 8. VERDICT
**TEST (with a compliant angle) — lean BUILD on the category, AVOID copying their playbook.**
- **Biggest reason:** The *category* is validated and scaling hard ($1M–$1.8M/mo, 14× traffic growth, strong AOV + subscription LTV), and the liquid-T format + estrogen-control story clearly converts. But JCKED's growth is propped up by **fake doctor pages, undisclosed subscriptions, and a 2.8 Trustpilot** — a brittle foundation. A cleaner, compliant operator can take share on trust and open TikTok.
- **Compliance risk: HIGH.** Fake medical-authority pages + testosterone/libido health claims + negative-option subscription + "USA vs China" provenance = stacked Meta-disable and FTC exposure. Only enter if you can run it *clean* (real endorsers, transparent subscription, COA, honest reviews). Do not replicate the fake-doctor network.
