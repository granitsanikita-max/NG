**Formulas** (as on the page):
- CM $ = Price − (landed per unit × units) − (3% × Price) − other per-order costs. E.g. $119 − $46.00 − $3.57 − $9.50 = **$59.93**.
- CM % = CM $ ÷ Price → $59.93 ÷ $119 = 50.4%.
- Break-even ROAS = Price ÷ CM $ → $119 ÷ $59.93 = 1.99. Break-even CPA = CM $.
- Target CPA at n% net = CM $ − (n × Price). 20%: $59.93 − $23.80 = $36.13. 30%: $59.93 − $35.70 = $24.23.
- Target ROAS = Price ÷ Target CPA → $119 ÷ $36.13 = 3.29; $119 ÷ $24.23 = 4.91.
- Scale line = the ~30% net CPA (at or under it → scale). Kill line = the ~20% net CPA (consistently above it → fix or kill).
- CM% is above 30% in every variant, so no target is impossible. The single pair's 30%-net CPA ($1.13) is impractical: it stays an anchor only.
