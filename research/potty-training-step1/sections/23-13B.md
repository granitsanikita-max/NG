**13B · Discovery Story** — the origin story of the mechanism/product: the struggle before it, how it was "discovered," the turning point. This is the spine of the advertorial. Even for a sourced product, build it from **real, verifiable history** — the true history of the product, its ingredients / materials / design, or the mechanism (who first found or used it, when, how, what happened to it). Every fact gets a source (§20 B fact-checks it); never invent a founder, an expert, or an event. Then pull real emotional beats from customer stories in the research. Collect the §18B history candidates before writing this section (the features / triggers / history agent gathers them) and name the one you build from — don't run a separate history search.
