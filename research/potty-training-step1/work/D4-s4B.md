# §4B · Brand Positioning System — LOCKED (2026-10-06, end of §11)

## The locked line
> **"We are the straight-talking brand that stands FOR putting the truth on the label — exactly how much it holds, in your kid's size — and AGAINST a potty-training industry built for toddlers and sold on promises it can't keep — the training underwear for big kids who are still getting there, and the parents who are done being judged for it."**

## The Enemy (locked)
**The enemy is the toddler-ized, overpromising potty-training industry.** It's built in its own 1★ reviews:
- **"Leakproof" with no number.**
  - Objections: "won't hold pee / leaks" 47 · "holds only one small accident" 14 · "ads overpromise" 5.
  - "It doesn't hold more that 10-20ml" [C:N205] (VERBATIM 1★).
- **Speed promises** and the blame that follows when they fail.
  - 3-day / Oh Crap is a failed solution 23 times.
  - "convincing readers that if you try and fail, it's because you weren't paying close enough attention" [C:S149] (SNIPPET).
- **Fake people and hidden tricks.**
  - scam / hidden subscription 16; honest-checkout need 13.
  - 7 persona pages at the leader [M47-A].
- **The toddler box:** sized and shaped for 18 months–4 years [M1], while big kids are left with diapers or nothing.
  - size / big-kid products pain 18; bigger-sizes need 24.

**Changed from draft:** "speed promise" and "toddler box" were kept and sharpened. "Shame" moved from enemy to **what we stand for** (no-shame). The enemy is the *industry pattern*, never a person or a named brand.

## What we stand FOR (locked)
1. **The truth on the label:** ml per size, poured on camera, re-tested per batch. If it won't hold something, we say so.
2. **Every kid gets there on their own road:** no timelines, no shame, no taking sides in method or therapy wars.
3. **Big kids deserve real underwear.**

**The identity she adopts:** "I'm the parent who doesn't fall for the hype, and doesn't let anyone shame my kid."

## The Attitude (locked)
**Blunt, dry, warm to kids.** Short sentences and numbers.
- **Never:** a sob story, fear in the hook, or "you / your child + condition" (§2B).
- **Sample voice:**
  - "Holds 210 ml. Not a drop more. We checked." (numbers illustrative until tested)
  - "Some kids are dry at 3. Some at 8. Both are fine."

## The Uncopyable Test (locked: PASS)
- **UpAiry:** printing ml exposes "3x more liquid"; "no timelines" contradicts "WEEKS not MONTHS" and "Week 2+ Fully Trained"; "real people only" indicts its 7 persona pages. → **It indicts them.**
- **BrightKidCo:** contradicts "Potty Trained in 7 Days" and "30 Days Potty Trained Promise". → **It indicts them.**
- **MooMoo / BIG ELEPHANT:** they could print a number. But a marketplace listing can't hold a creed or a community, and BIG ELEPHANT's honesty already reads as a disclaimer ("Limited Absorbency"). → **The bundle is uncopyable for them; the number alone is not.** So we never rely on the number alone.

## Whole-store thread (locked)
- **Name:** neutral, big-kid-coded, non-medical (no autism / incontinence / potty in the domain or handles).
- **About:** the manifesto, "Built for the kids the potty-training industry forgot. Labelled with the truth."
- **PDP:** pour test per size, spec table, size chart to 12, the "won't hold" box, "Talk to your pediatrician too."
- **Offer:**
  - worn-product guarantee + free size swaps
  - no subscription, nothing pre-ticked
  - per-pair price shown
- **Reviews:** ages 5+ first; critical reviews shown with our reply.
- **Voice:** the attitude above, on every surface, including order emails.

## Weakness-as-weapon (locked)
- "No fake moms. Just us, a measuring jug and sizes nobody else stocks."
- "Our first 40 families, by name."
- Small batches are why we can make a size 10.

## Guardrails
1. **BE it:** the real pour-test data is on file before any ml claim (FTC substantiation, §2B). The sizes exist (OEM run) before the "to size 12" claim.
2. **Attack the pattern, never a named competitor.**
