      Which size should I get?

      We recommend sizing down if you are in between sizes to ensure the correct fit.

      Our sizes typically run:

      • Small: 1-2 years (11-24 lbs | 5-11 kg)
      • Medium: 2-3 years (24-33 lbs | 11-15kg)
      • Large: 3-4 years (33-42 lbs | 15-19kg)

      Can I wash them?

      Yes! You can simply toss them in the washing machine and then dry them either in a dryer or by line drying.

      For best results, use the delicate setting for both washing and drying.

      water_drop

      Are they 100% leak proof?

      Our training panties are thoughtfully designed to support faster and more effective potty training.

      Crafted with advanced materials to maximize absorbency and comfort, they help contain accidents while encouraging children to recognize the need for the toilet.

      For optimal performance, selecting the correct size and considering individual child characteristics is important.

      While we have invested significant effort into making our panties as leak-resistant as possible, maintaining their proven effectiveness in potty training means that 100% leak-proof performance cannot be guaranteed in every case.
