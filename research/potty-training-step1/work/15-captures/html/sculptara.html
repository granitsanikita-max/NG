        What age are these for?

        Designed for toddlers who are ready to start potty training, typically 18 months to 3 years. Every kid is different — the sign they're ready isn't age, it's awareness. 

        If your child shows any signs of knowing when they're going (hiding in a corner, pulling at their diaper, telling you after) they're ready for these.

        What size should I get?

        Check the size chart before ordering. Measure your child's waist and match it to the guide. They come with roughly 5cm of stretch built in so there's some flexibility. If your child is between sizes, go with the larger one for comfort.

        How many pairs do I need?

        Most parents start with a pack of 6-10 to get through the first few weeks with enough rotation for accidents and washing. You'll go through more in the first week as your child is learning and fewer as they start getting it right.

        Can I wash and reuse them?

        Yes. They're fully machine washable and designed to last through hundreds of washes without losing absorption or shape. That's part of the savings — you're not throwing money in the bin every day like you do with disposables.

        What if they don't work for us?

        They come with a 60-day money-back guarantee. If your child doesn't make progress, send them back. But most kids respond within the first few weeks.
