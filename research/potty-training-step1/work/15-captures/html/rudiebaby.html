      Big Kid Undies (With a Safety Net Built In) 🩲

       

      The final step before real undies. Soft bamboo underwear that catches small accidents while helping kids feel ready for the big kid world.

       

      Why They Work

      • Feel-wet design: lets kids recognise accidents and build toilet confidence
      • Pull-up style: easy for little ones to dress themselves
      • Reusable & eco-friendly: saves money and reduces waste compared to disposables
      • Comfy & safe: 95% bamboo on skin, PFAS-free, OEKO-TEX certified


      What Makes Them Different

      These aren't nappies or training pants. They're the bridge to proper underwear, designed for kids who are nearly there but still have the occasional miss. Light absorbency catches small accidents without the bulk.

        QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

        Everything parents ask us

        Still unsure? Our Aussie team is happy to help.

        What stage of toilet training are these for? +

        Training Underwear is designed for stage three, when children are mostly dry and ready to wear underwear but may still have the occasional small accident.

        How much do they absorb? +

        The slim four-layer gusset is designed for small accidents and leaks. It is not intended to contain a full bladder release or replace a nappy.

        Which size should I choose? +

        Choose your child’s usual clothing size: 2/3, 3/4 or 4/6. The size guide beside the selector includes waist and leg measurements if your child is between sizes.

        Can I mix Whales and Unicorns in a bundle? +

        Yes. Choose a print separately for every pair in the 5-pair or 10-pair pack. You can mix both prints or repeat a favourite.

        How many pairs do I need? +

        One pair is a simple way to try the fit. Five pairs gives you a useful starter rotation, while ten pairs gives more room for daycare, outings and wash days.

        Can they be used overnight? +

        No. Training Underwear is for light daytime protection during the final toilet-training stage. Use an appropriate night nappy or dedicated bedwetting product overnight.

        How do I wash them? +

        Change after an accident, then machine wash cold. Tumble dry on low or line dry, following the care label.

        When will my order ship? +

        Orders placed before 1pm AWST on a dispatch day usually ship the same day. Free shipping applies to Australian orders over $60.
