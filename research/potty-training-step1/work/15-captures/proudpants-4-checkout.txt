Your Questions, Answered

Everything parents ask us before trying ProudPants.

How is ProudPants different from a regular pull-up?
+

Pull-ups are designed to feel dry no matter what, which is exactly the problem. The six layers of cotton gauze against your child's skin let them feel wetness right away, while a waterproof barrier layer holds back small accidents long enough to reach the potty. That immediate feedback is what actually teaches the connection between the feeling and the bathroom.

Are they waterproof? How long do they hold?
+

No, and that is by design. ProudPants are not diapers. The waterproof barrier layer holds back a small accident and buys a little time to reach the potty, but the panel is not built to hold a full bladder, and the wetness reaches your child immediately either way. That wet feeling is the whole point: it teaches your child to connect the urge with the potty. If they stayed dry like a pull-up, there would be nothing to learn from.

Will this work for my child? They're older, strong-willed, or just not interested.
+

ProudPants is designed to meet kids where they are, whatever their pace. Because the fabric gives real, honest feedback instead of masking it, many parents of older or more reluctant trainees find it helps rebuild the connection that traditional pull-ups removed. Every child's timeline is different - there's no deadline here, just a better tool for the process.

What about nighttime or naps?
+

ProudPants are daytime training pants, not overnight diapers. The waterproof barrier layer contains small daytime accidents, but the panel is not built to hold a full bladder for hours, so we don't recommend them for sleep. For naps and nights, use a pull-up or a mattress protector. Their job is to let your child feel accidents and learn, not to keep them dry all night.

How do I wash them?
+

ProudPants are fully machine washable and reusable - just toss them in with a regular cold wash and let them air dry or tumble dry low. No special detergents or hand-washing required.

What pack sizes are available?
+

ProudPants comes in packs of 5, 10, and 15, and you can also buy single pairs. Pick the option that matches how much you train. The 10-pack is the most popular for full-time use.

What if it doesn't work for us?
+

You're covered by our 60-day money-back guarantee - if you don't see real progress, send it back for a full refund.
