Frequently Asked Questions

How Are These Different Than Pull-Ups?

Pull-ups are designed to absorb wetness quickly. KidConfident is designed to keep the feeling noticeable while helping contain small accidents — giving kids the feedback they need to start connecting the dots.

What If My Child Doesn't Care About Being Wet?

That’s actually pretty common at first. The goal isn’t for them to hate being wet — it’s for them to notice it.

 

KidConfident keeps the sensation more noticeable than a pull-up, so even if they don’t react right away, they’re getting clearer feedback each time. 

 

Over repeated accidents, that can help them start connecting “I’m wet” → “I peed” → “I should use the potty.”

Can They Be Used Overnight?

KidConfident training pants are designed primarily for daytime potty training, since overnight accidents are usually larger and happen while your child is asleep.

 

For bedtime, you can add our Leakproof Undie Covers over their KidConfident training pants for extra overnight protection and fewer sheet changes.

How Do I Know If My Child Is Ready To Potty Train?

There isn’t one perfect age or “readiness date.” Potty training starts with body awareness — noticing the signals their body is already sending.

 

That’s why we recommend focusing less on waiting for the perfect moment and more on giving them clear feedback. Staying in highly absorbent pull-ups for too long can make wetness easier to ignore, while KidConfident helps keep that feedback noticeable so they can start connecting feeling → accident → potty.

Why Does Potty Training Seem To Take Longer For Kids Today?

There’s probably no single reason, but one big thing has changed: disposables have gotten much better at making accidents disappear.

 

That’s great for preventing messes — but it can also make wetness less noticeable. And when kids get less feedback from an accident, there’s less opportunity to build the body awareness potty training depends on.

 

KidConfident is designed to bring that feedback back: feel it → notice it → connect the dots.
