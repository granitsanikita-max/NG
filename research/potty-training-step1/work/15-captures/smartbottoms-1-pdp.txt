The Daytime Trainer features 2 super absorbent layers of bamboo fabric in the wet zone and a waterproof layer to help prevent puddles,  the perfect combination for children that are nearly self-sufficient in using the toilet but may still have the occasional accident. The stretchy waist and legs allow your child to easily pull the trainer up & down on their own, giving them confidence and independence in this new stage of their life. The natural fiber interior allows your child to instantly feel when they have had an accident which will help them better understand when they need to use the toilet. Many of our customers will continue to use the Daytime Trainers, even once accident free, due to how trim and comfortable they are for their child.



While our trainers do have a waterproof layer to help prevent puddles, they are not intended to replace a diaper. The Daytime Trainer will hold a small accident but if a toddler completely eliminates their bladder, or has more than one accident, it will likely leak. If your child is not showing signs of readiness continuing to use a diaper is recommended. If they are having frequent accidents or are new to potty training, we recommend starting out with our Nighttime Trainer, they will offer twice the absorbency to help catch bigger accidents.

For our step-by-step guide to potty training, click here.


Do you feel like your child isn't quite ready to potty train yet? We have a solution for you!  Our Pull-On Diaper is designed similarly to the trainer but has the absorbency and leak protection you would expect in a diaper.  


 Please Note: Our sizing DOES NOT correlate to disposable diaper sizing. Please check our size chart available on our website before ordering.
 
Material Contents:
Interior:  4 layers of 80% Bamboo Rayon and 20% Polyester (backside of fabric only)
Waistbands, legs, & sides: 94% Polyester and 6 % Spandex
Wet Zone is reinforced with laminated TPU
 
Care Instructions:
This trainer is made from natural fibers and will need to be washed and dried a minimum of three times before first use. It will increase in absorbency up to 8-10 washes.
Remove any solid waste from trainer and rinse with warm water. Wash on hot with the amount of detergent recommended for your machine for a heavily soiled load. Dry on warm. Do not use bleach or fabric softener.
