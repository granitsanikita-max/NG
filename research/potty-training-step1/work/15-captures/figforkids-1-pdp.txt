Potty training is a journey. We're here to make it a little softer.

Our Girls Potty Training Set is designed to make the transition into potty training as gentle as possible. Little ones learning to use the potty need underwear that's gentle, breathable, and comfortable—especially when accidents happen. Our GOTS-certified organic cotton is free from harsh chemicals, synthetic materials, and irritating finishes that can bother sensitive skin during this transition.

With 4 pairs of organic cotton underwear in styles and prints she'll love, plus a handy tote for on-the-go accidents and a rewards chart to celebrate every success, everything she needs is right here. Each pair features a moisture-control gusset (comprised of 2 layers of GOTS certified organic cotton) to help contain little accidents while keeping her comfortable. 

Each pack includes 2 bikinis (one in Leopards and one in Sky), 2 hipsters (one in Flowers and one in Fig), 1 organic cotton tote, and a rewards chart with stickers.
