DESCRIPTION
Lil Learnerz 2.0 – Potty Training Just Got an Upgrade!

Say hello to the Lil Learnerz 2.0! Our next generation of potty training pants designed to help your toddler make the big leap from diapers to big kid undies with confidence! These thoughtfully designed training pants offer a perfect balance of independence, comfort, and protection as your little one tackles this important milestone.

AWJ INTERRIOR

Now featuring a Athletic Wicking Jersey (AWJ) interior, Lil Learnerz 2.0 keeps things cool, breathable, and silky-smooth. This fabric is made with enhanced airflow while helping your little learner feel comfortable and confident all day long.

POCKET STYLE TRAINER

Lil Learnerz 2.0 now features a STUFFABLE POCKET, which allows you increase the absorbency of the trainer if your little one needs a little added help for naps, night time or in the beginning stages of potty training. Otherwise, the Lil Learners are pre-built with a dual-layer microfiber core, for absorbing accidents, and a waterproof outer layer to keep clothing and car seats protected.

SNAP SIDE RELEASE

Designed for easy ups and downs, Lil Learnerz 2.0 look and feel just like real underwear with more generous sizing and side-snapping release panels in ALL SIZES - for quick changes. Perfect for early learners who still need a little extra help.

MORE GENEROUS SIZING

Available in sizes XS to XXL (15 lbs - 75+ lbs), Lil Learnerz 2.0 are the perfect potty training partner for every stage of your child’s journey.
