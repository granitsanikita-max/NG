+

BrightKidCo was created for kids who are ready to learn what their body is telling them—without the overwhelming feeling of soaked clothing.

FAQs
We already put underwear under the pull-up. Isn't that the same?
+

Close — but the pull-up on top still wicks the moisture away and adds bulk, so the signal stays weak and it still feels like a diaper. BrightKidCo keeps the gentle signal on the skin and protects clothes in one thin layer — no diaper feel, no double change.

My toddler never tells me when they need to go. Will this help?
+

That's usually the whole problem — not stubbornness. Telling you comes after noticing, and noticing needs feedback. The learning happens between body and brain, no words required: feel it, notice it, connect it. Most parents describe the first win without a single sentence from the child: "He paused." "She looked down."

My kid hates wet clothes. Won't this make it worse?
+

The signal is small and short — a brief "uh-oh", not cold soaked fabric clinging to the skin. The core absorbs right after. It's plain underwear that creates the soaked, miserable feeling most kids react to — exactly what the 3 layers prevent.

What makes this different from a pull-up?
+

A pull-up keeps your child so dry the brain never gets the feedback it needs to learn. BrightKidCo lets that one gentle signal through — while still catching the accident.

What about poop accidents?
+

Solid accidents: shake into the toilet, rinse, machine wash. Messier ones: rinse first, then wash as normal. Stays soft after hundreds of washes.

How long until we see progress?
+

Every child is different — and that's okay. In our customer survey of 5,679 parents, 92% reported noticing clearer body awareness within the first 2 weeks. Some kids need longer. The first signs are small: a pause, a look down, a hand on the pants.

What does the guarantee cover?
+

Try it at your child's pace. If potty training doesn't feel calmer or clearly moving forward, email us within 100 days for a full refund. No pressure. No judgment.

Which size does my child need?
+

Three sizes: S, M and L — size chart in the product photos above. Wrong fit? We exchange sizes for free.
