 Due to the nature of our product, Peejamas  does not accept returns for washed or worn  jammies. We are happy to offer exchanges and refunds for unused Peejamas within 30 days from the date your order ships from our warehouse. Clearance and final sale items are  not   eligible  for returns or exchanges. Please remember to try on Peejamas before pre-washing or removing any tags in order to be eligible for an exchange. This includes sewn in tags and paper tags and packaging. If a return does not include tags or packaging it may incur additional restocking fees. If your product arrives damaged or defective through no fault of your own, Peejamas will happily provide you with a pre-paid return label to exchange your product. Shipping costs are not refundable. For help with exchanges or returns, please email us at contact@peejamas.com.
