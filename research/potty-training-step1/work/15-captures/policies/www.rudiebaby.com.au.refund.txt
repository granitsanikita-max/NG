Here's what we do offer: 60-Day Risk-Free Trial (First-Time Customers) Never tried Rudie Baby before? We want you to feel confident giving us a go. If your first order doesn't work out, here's how the trial works: You can test one item from your first order If it's not right for your family, contact us within 60 days of receiving your order and we'll refund that one item (excluding shipping) Ordered more than one item? We can offer store credit for up to three additional items This trial applies to first-time customers only Sizing Please take a moment to check our sizing guide before placing your order. Damaged or Faulty Items If something arrives damaged or isn't working as it should, we want to sort it out quickly. Contact us within 7 days of receiving your order and include photos of the issue.
