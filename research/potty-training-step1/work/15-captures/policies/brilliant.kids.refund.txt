This policy explains how returns, refunds and our 75-Day Potty Progress Guarantee work. There are two separate ways to get your money back, and they have different rules. Please read the one that applies to you. 1. The 75-Day Potty Progress Guarantee (for used product) This is the guarantee advertised on our homepage and product pages. It exists precisely because potty training requires actually using the product. If you have used Brilliant Kids Potty Training Underwear with your child and you have not seen potty training progress, you may request a full refund of the purchase price within 75 days of delivery — even if the underwear has been worn, washed or used, and even if the tags and original packaging are gone. To claim the guarantee: Email us at support@brilliant.kids within 75 days of the delivery date. Include your order number and a short description of how the product was used (for example, how many weeks and how often). You do not need to ship the product back to us. For hygiene reasons we ask you to keep it or dispose of it. Once your claim is approved, we refund the full purchase price you paid for the underwear to your original payment method within 10 business days . Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless required by law. The guarantee is limited to one claim per customer or household , and applies to Brilliant Kids Potty Training Underwear purchased directly from brilliant.kids. 2. Standard Returns (unused product — changed your mind) If you simply changed your mind and have not used the product, you have 75 days after receiving your item to request a return. To be eligible for a standard return, your item must be in the same condition you received it, unworn and unused, with tags, and in its original packaging. You will also need your receipt or proof of purchase. To start a return, contact us at support@brilliant.kids . If your return is accepted, we will send you return instructions and the approved return address. Items sent back to us without first requesting a return will not be accepted. Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the item arrived damaged, defective or incorrect. Authorized returns are sent to: ROKTAL & Co.
