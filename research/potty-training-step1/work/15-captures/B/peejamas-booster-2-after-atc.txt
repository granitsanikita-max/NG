Overnight Booster Inserts (3-Pack)

Extra Absorbency for a Worry-Free Night

Perfect for heavier wetters or kids just starting potty training, our Peejamas Booster Inserts provide an extra layer of protection to help prevent nighttime leaks and accidents. Designed to fit seamlessly inside Peejamas or Training Pants, these inserts boost absorbency without adding bulk—so your child stays comfortable all night.

Why You’ll Love Them:

✔ Enhanced Absorbency – Each insert absorbs up to 4 ounces of urine, reducing nighttime messes.
✔ Slim & Comfortable – A thin, low-profile design ensures comfort without feeling bulky.
✔ Washable & Reusable – Easy to clean and designed to last, making them an eco-friendly alternative to disposables.
✔ Adjustable Protection – Start with one booster, and if needed, layer a second for extra security.
✔ Compatible with Peejamas & Trainers – Available in multiple sizes to fit different Peejamas products.

How It Works:

Simply place the insert inside your child's Peejamas or Training Pants before bedtime. When paired with Nighttime Jammies (which hold ~10 oz of urine), these inserts can increase absorbency by 40%!

📢 Includes 3 booster inserts per pack for easy rotation and washing.

Product Details:
Material: 4 total layers—Soft Bamboo Terry (260 GSM) & Microfiber Fleece (280 GSM) for quick absorption.
Sizing Options:
2T - 4T (Training Pants & Peejamas)
5T - 6/7 (Peejamas)
8/9 - 10/11 (Peejamas)
Care Instructions: Pre-wash at least 3 times before first use to maximize absorbency.
