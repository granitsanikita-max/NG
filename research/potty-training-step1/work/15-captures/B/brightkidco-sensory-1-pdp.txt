How It Works
+

BrightKidCo was created for children who need a gentle, consistent way to recognize when they're wet—without the overwhelming feeling of soaked clothing.

Unlike pull-ups that quickly wick moisture away, our Body Signal Learning Layer™ helps preserve the body's natural wetness cue while the absorbent core manages small accidents.

This allows many children to begin connecting the feeling of being wet with using the potty, while still helping reduce messes around the house.

Every child learns at their own pace, but many families notice clearer body awareness within the first few weeks of consistent daytime use.

FAQs
We already do underwear under the pull-up. Isn't that the same?
+
Close — but the pull-up on top still wicks moisture away and adds bulk, so the signal stays weak and it still feels like a diaper. BrightKidCo keeps the gentle signal on the skin and protects clothes in one thin layer — no diaper feel, no double change.
My child is non-verbal. Does this still work?
+
Yes — the learning happens between body and brain, no words needed: feel it, notice it, connect it. Parents describe the first wins without a single word: "He paused." "She looked down."
My child melts down over wet clothes. Won't this make it worse?
+
The signal is small and predictable — a brief "uh-oh", not cold soaked fabric on the skin. The core absorbs right after. For many sensory-sensitive kids it's the soaked, clinging feel of regular underwear that triggers meltdowns — exactly what the 3 layers prevent.
What makes this different from a pull-up?
+
A pull-up keeps your child so dry the brain never gets the feedback it needs to learn. BrightKidCo lets that one gentle signal through — while still catching the accident.
What about poop accidents?
+
Solid accidents: shake into the toilet, rinse, machine wash. Messier ones: rinse first, then wash as normal. Stays soft after hundreds of washes.
How long until we see progress?
+
Every child is different — and that's okay. Many families notice clearer body awareness within 2–4 weeks. Some kids need longer. The first signs are small: a pause, a look down, a hand on the pants.
What does the guarantee cover?
+
Try it at your child's pace. If potty learning doesn't feel calmer or clearly moving forward, email us within 100 days for a full refund. No pressure. No judgment.
Which size does my child need?
+
Three sizes: S, M and L — size chart in the product photos above. Wrong fit? We exchange sizes for free.
