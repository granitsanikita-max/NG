Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents need to know about natural saffron support for kids

What's the right dosage for my child?
Ages 4-6: Start with 1 gummy daily with breakfast
Ages 7-12: 1-2 gummies daily with breakfast
Best results: Take consistently at the same time each day

Start with the lower dose for the first week to assess tolerance, then increase if needed.
Are there any side effects?
Saffron is remarkably well-tolerated. Unlike ADHD medications, it won't suppress appetite, disrupt sleep, or stunt growth. Occasionally, children may experience mild stomach upset if taken on an empty stomach - this is why we recommend taking with breakfast. If any concerns arise, simply reduce the dose or discontinue use.
Is this safe to give my child daily?
Yes, absolutely. Our saffron gummies are made with clinically-studied dosages specifically safe for children ages 4-12. Saffron has been used safely for centuries, and our formula contains the same amounts shown effective in pediatric research studies. Each gummy is third-party tested for purity and contains no artificial stimulants or harsh chemicals.
Will this replace my child's ADHD medication?
Our saffron gummies are a natural supplement, not a medication replacement. However, clinical studies show saffron can be as effective as prescription ADHD medications for focus and mood regulation. Work with your healthcare provider to determine if natural support could reduce or replace pharmaceutical interventions.
Can my child take this with their current ADHD medication?
Always consult your pediatrician before combining any supplement with prescription medications. Many families successfully use our saffron gummies alongside reduced medication doses with their doctor's supervision. Saffron works differently than stimulant medications and may complement existing treatments.
Do these contain St. John's Wort?
No — zero St. John's Wort. A lot of the other big-name kids' calm supplements still use it, and St. John's Wort is well-documented to interfere with how common medications work.

We stripped it out completely during our V2 reformulation, so there's nothing in Saphire known to clash with your child's meds. As always, check with your child's doctor first.
What if my picky eater won't take them?
95% of kids love the natural fruit taste! Our gummies are sugar-free but naturally sweet, so most children think they're getting a treat. If your child is extremely picky, you can start with half a gummy or crush it into their favorite smoothie or yogurt. We also offer a 60-day money-back guarantee if taste becomes an issue.
What if it doesn't work for my child?
Every child is unique. While 80%+ of families see significant improvements, we offer a full 60-day money-back guarantee because we want you to have enough time to see real results. If you don't notice positive changes after 6-8 weeks of consistent use, simply return the unused portion for a complete refund.
Is this just a "phase" solution or long-term support?
Think of saffron like a daily vitamin for emotional and cognitive health. Many families use it long-term as ongoing support for their child's natural brain development and emotional regulation. As children grow and develop better coping skills, some families gradually reduce usage with their healthcare provider's guidance.
How do I know if it's working?
Look for these positive changes:
• Calmer morning routines with less resistance
• Teachers reporting better classroom behavior
• Improved ability to sit through homework
• Fewer explosive emotional outbursts
• Better sleep and easier bedtimes
• More cooperation and less defiance

Keep a simple daily log to track improvements over the first month.
How should I store the gummies?
Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keep the bottle tightly sealed and out of reach of children (they taste so good, kids might want more than their daily dose!). No refrigeration needed, making them perfect for travel or school trips.
How long before I see improvements?
Most parents notice initial changes within 1-2 weeks - calmer mornings, fewer outbursts, better cooperation. Significant improvements in focus and classroom behavior typically develop over 4-6 weeks of consistent daily use. Remember, you're supporting your child's natural brain chemistry, which takes time to rebalance.
Can I give this to multiple children?
Absolutely! Many families find our saffron gummies helpful for siblings, especially in households where ADHD or emotional regulation challenges run in the family. Each child should follow the age-appropriate dosing guidelines. Consider our family bundle options for better value.
Can my teenager take these too?
Yes! While our gummies are formulated for ages 4-12, teenagers can safely take 2 gummies daily. However, teens might prefer our adult capsule format for a more mature delivery method. Saffron continues to be beneficial for focus, mood regulation, and stress management through adolescence.
Why saffron instead of other natural options?
Saffron is the only natural ingredient with clinical studies showing it's as effective as ADHD medication. Unlike other supplements that work on single pathways, saffron supports mood, focus, emotional regulation, and sleep simultaneously. It's been safely used in children for generations and has the strongest research backing.
What makes your saffron different from others?
We use premium Spanish saffron extract in clinically-studied dosages specifically formulated for children. Our gummies are sugar-free, third-party tested, and made in GMP-certified facilities. Most importantly, we focus exclusively on kids' needs - not adult formulations scaled down.
My child's teacher wants to know more. What can I tell them?
Share that saffron is a natural spice with clinical research supporting its use for attention and emotional regulation in children. It's non-stimulant, won't affect their appetite or sleep, and has been shown in studies to be as effective as traditional ADHD medications for improving classroom behavior and focus.
