    Refund policy

    Return Policies for underwear are tricky, aren’t they?

    At this time, WunderUndies is unable to accept returns. As much as we would love to be able to, nobody wants to worry if their underwear has been previously worn by somebody else.

    If you’re at all worried about choosing the right size for your kiddo, please consult the size chart, and take advantage of our Find My Size Trial Pack! You get 2 consecutive sizes in the same print and design for just $10 with free shipping! Also, don’t hesitate to reach out if you have questions.

    All unopened underwear can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Buyer must pay shipping.

    If you have a quality issue, or something else you need assistance with in regard to returns, please use the Contact Form to reach out. I will do my best to make sure you’re happy.
