Magnesium Glycinate

Calm & nervous system

Magnesium is one of the most common deficiencies in children with focus and behavioral challenges. The glycinate form absorbs gently and supports a relaxed nervous system helping reduce the hyperarousal, irritability, and restlessness that make everyday moments so hard.

Choline + Inositol

Focus & cognitive signaling

Choline and Inositol work together to support neurotransmitter production and cell membrane signaling, directly supporting a child's ability to focus, retain information, and process thoughts clearly.

L-Theanine

Calm and focus

+ Read more ›

L-Theanine promotes alpha brain wave activity, the same relaxed-but-alert state associated with focused attention. It takes the edge off without dulling. Kids stay calm, present, and still themselves. No sedation. No personality suppression. Just steadier.

Zinc

Dopamine & impulse control

+ Read more ›

Zinc plays a direct role in dopamine regulation — the neurotransmitter most associated with motivation, attention, and impulse control. Research consistently shows children with focus challenges tend to have lower zinc levels than their peers.

Gut-Brain Blend

Probiotics for the brain-gut axis

Over 90% of serotonin is produced in the gut. Our multi-strain probiotic blend, paired with prebiotic fiber, supports a healthy gut environment — which directly influences your child's mood, stress response, and cognitive function from the inside out.
