Training undies your toddler won't want to take off.

Stage 3 is the bit between training pants and regular undies. Mostly dry, the odd accident still happens, your toddler's ready to feel like a big kid.

Built to be worn, not tolerated.

Real undies. Real safety net.

95% bamboo viscose, so soft they won't want to take them off. The slim 4-layer leakproof gusset is hidden inside the lining, not bulked up between the legs.

Outside, they look like regular undies. Inside, enough to catch one accident before the toilet.

Big-Kid Feel

A slim shape helps children practise wearing underwear without the bulk of a disposable pull-up.

Bamboo Soft

The stretchy 95% bamboo shell is made for comfortable all-day wear at home, daycare and outings.

Simple Sizing

Three familiar clothing sizes cover ages two to six, with waist and leg measurements in the size guide.

Wash and Reuse

Keep clean pairs in rotation and wash them again after each small accident.

Two Fun Prints

Whales and Unicorns make getting dressed feel more exciting during the final toilet-training stage.
