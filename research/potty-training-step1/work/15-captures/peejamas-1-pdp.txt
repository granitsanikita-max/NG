The perfect transitional big kid undies, these trainers will absorb and contain small accidents (1 oz.) to transition your child away from diapers, reinforce potty training best practices, and send them on their way to potty training success!

Though less absorbent than our nighttime Peejamas, our daytime training pants use the same materials and design as Peejamas, but they are significantly less super-absorbent fabric.

Daytime trainers are 100% cotton.

Benefits of Peejamas' Training Pants:

Comfort and Fit:
Peejamas Daytime Trainers are 100% cotton and designed with comfort in mind, providing a soft, safe, and cozy fit for your child to wear throughout the day. 
Absorbency with Awareness:
These trainers come with a level of absorbency to handle small accidents, ensuring a degree of protection while still allowing your child to feel wetness, an essential aspect of the learning experience.
Design and Appeal for Motivation:
Peejamas offers a range of fun and colorful designs that can capture your child's interest and make the potty training journey more enjoyable. Appealing designs can serve as positive reinforcement, motivating your child to participate in the process.
Ease of Use:
Peejamas Trainers feature an easy pull-up design, promoting independence for your child as they navigate the potty training process.
