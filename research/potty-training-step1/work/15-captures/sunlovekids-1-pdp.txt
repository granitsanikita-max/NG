Secure and Worry-Free Purchase

Make potty training easy with our 100% pure cotton training pants! Designed for babies aged 6 months to 3 years, these reusable diaper pants feature a multi-layer gauze core with a built-in waterproof crotch to catch minor leaks. Highly breathable, sweat-absorbent, and ultra-soft, they keep your toddler comfortable while helping them learn bladder control. Perfect for all-season wear, they are the ideal transition from diapers to regular underwear.

 

Key Features

100% Pure Cotton Gauze: Ultra-soft, breathable, and skin-friendly fabric that absorbs sweat and protects delicate baby skin.

Waterproof & Leak-Proof: Integrated waterproof lining in the crotch area effectively prevents messy leaks.

Smart Potty Training: Helps toddlers sense wetness to accelerate toilet training and bodily awareness.

All-Season Comfort: Lightweight, medium-thin design offers perfect comfort for year-round wear.

Snug & Elastic Fit: Gentle elastic waistband ensures a secure, comfortable fit without restricting movement.

 

Specifications

Material: 100% Pure Cotton (Gauze)

Age Range: 3 Months to 3 Years Old

Thickness: Medium-Thin (All Seasons)

Softness: Highly Soft

Elasticity: Slight Elasticity

Breathability: Excellent
