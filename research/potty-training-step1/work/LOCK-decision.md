### 2 · Criterion #2: true brand positioning / culture
- B carries a creed people can join: no shame, the truth on the label, big kids deserve real underwear.
- It passes the Uncopyable Test (it indicts UpAiry and BrightKidCo; §4B).
- D only has an *angle* (honesty) inside UpAiry's market.

### 3 · Margins
- B supports **$11–14/pair**. Price anchors: BrightKidCo sensory $15/pair; Super Undies $34.99/pair; DTC bundle zone $6.80–8.60 [D4-s15].
- PROVISIONAL at OEM landed ~$4.60/pair (INFERENCE):
  - 10 pairs at $119 → CM ≈ $59.93 (50%), break-even ROAS ≈ 2.0.
  - 6 pairs at $84 → CM ≈ $46.88 (56%).
- D at UpAiry-level prices is thin: 5 for $35 → CM 36%, break-even ROAS 2.78 [D1-Pre0-rerun].
- **Caveat:** B needs MOQ cash, a CPC lab test and US 3PL stock.

1. **Enemy:** "size ceiling" → **toddler-ized, overpromising industry**.
2. **Scope:** narrowed to **daytime, nearly-there** kids. Full-containment and night are declared out of scope.
3. **Mechanism:** generic "measured capacity" → **size-matched *Big-Kid Hold***. Feel-wet is demoted to a construction fact.
4. **Emotion:** deadline-fear → **Fear / Apathy → Courage**.
5. **Night lane:** from "hold, weak demand" to **TAKEN** (supplements).
6. **Channel:** from "risk: untargetable" to **reachable via story pre-sell** (supplement evidence), with TikTok creators first.
7. **Ops:** from "risk" to **precondition** (OEM, CPC, US 3PL).
8. **§4 contrast:** big-kid context made mandatory; Courage tone; per-size number.
9. **§4B:** "shame" moved from enemy to stance; "no therapy wars" added; line reworded.
