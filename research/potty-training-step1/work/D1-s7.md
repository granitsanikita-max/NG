# §7 · Funnel level

**Answer: TOP of funnel (cold, problem-aware).**
- Launch ads reach the §3 ICP cold. She is problem-aware: 67% of segment snippets, with only 8% product-aware (§5).
- On Meta we can't target her by condition. Health detailed targeting was removed on 2022-01-19 [M47-D §3], and "you / your child + condition" copy is banned (§2B).
- So we buy broad US-parent reach and let the **creative self-select** her.

**What that means for the ad's job:**
- **Stop the right parent and earn the click.** That means a recognisable big-kid moment:
  - spare pants in the backpack
  - a size-10 label next to a size-5T pull-up
  - a pour test with a number
- **Don't** sell bundles in the ad. One idea per ad: the moment, then "there's gear built for this".

**What that means for the first page:**
- **Pre-sell first, not straight to the PDP:**
  - a real creator's first-person video page
  - or a short founder letter / advertorial
- **Its job:** reframe (bigger kid → bigger accident → toddler gear fails), show the pour test, then pass to the proof-PDP.
- **Exception:** retargeting and TikTok Shop traffic (which buys on-platform) can go straight to the product. TikTok Shop shoppers are already in a buying context. PeekabooCo sold 624 units in 30 days with 3 creators [M22].

**Evidence that pre-sell works at this stage in this audience:**
- The school-age wetting supplement brands now scaling on US Meta run long first-person story ads to advertorials:
  - Saphire → trysaphire.com/pages/bed-wetting-5rw, 684 active ads on one page
  - Bloomwise → 420 active
  - (DATA [W4][W5])
- That's the same parent at the same stage. It shows that cold traffic → story pre-sell → offer is the working shape.
- **We copy the structure, not their personas or claims.**

## So What → do this
- Build two first pages before launch:
  1. **A creator-story pre-sell** (real parent of a 6–8-year-old, disclosed).
  2. **A founder "why we made size 10" letter with the pour test.**
- A/B them against straight-to-PDP. Final pick goes in the Funnel Map.
