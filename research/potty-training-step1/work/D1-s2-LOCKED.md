## Decision (locked)
**MARKET: "Big kids still learning."**
- **Who:** US parents of children aged about **5–9** who are past the toddler stage but still have **daytime accidents**.
- **Their painful problem:**
  - Every product on the shelf is built, sized and sold for a toddler.
  - Pull-ups top out and look like diapers.
  - Toddler trainers flood on a big-kid accident.
  - Plain underwear means a soaked outfit and a call from school.
- **Core proof community:** autistic, ADHD, sensory and speech-delayed families. They are reached through creators, communities and SEO, and **never named in ad copy**.
- **Scope:** daytime, nearly-there kids. Full-incontinence and night needs are out of scope at launch (they are disposables territory). We say so openly.

**Wedge (how we attack):**
- **Mechanism gap:** the ***Big-Kid Hold*** (working name). Capacity is matched to each size, pour-tested on camera, and printed on the pack. Nobody in US paid social states a ml figure (0 of 908 ads).
- **Format gap:** real-creator first-person video and a pour-test demo. Lead on TikTok and TikTok Shop (0 US TikTok ads for "training underwear"), plus Meta broad with story pre-sell.
- **Avatar gap:** school-age dignity. Sizes 4–12, big-kid designs, sensory-gentle build stated as construction facts only.

**Runner-up (one line):** "the honest-proof trainer" for mainstream 2–4-year-olds, TikTok-first.
- It stays a **later size-down extension**, not the market.
- It fails the gate as a market, because it's UpAiry's red ocean.

**Killed (unchanged):**
- Working-parent / daycare hand-off: UpAiry runs it in the US.
- Night bedwetting: now **also** TAKEN by supplement brands on Meta (Saphire 684 + 226 + 149 active ads; Bloomwise 420 active) [W3–W5], and by Goodnites on shelf.

1. **Gap in the market (#1): YES.** See the gate row above. It's the only candidate that passes.
2. **True brand positioning / culture (#2): YES.** Locked line in work/D4-s4B.md.

1. **The number:** no sample has been pour-tested. If a real-underwear-looking size 8 can't hold a meaningful volume, the mechanism shrinks to "buys time to reach the bathroom". The market holds, but proof is weaker.
2. **Sourcing:** OEM + CPC + US 3PL are required. There's no dropship launch.
3. **Meta health classification:** keep the domain and handles neutral; check Events Manager on day 1. Super Undies is already categorised "Health - Other" [W2].
4. **Price anchor:** MooMoo 9Y at ~$3–4/pair. We win only with a visible ml difference.
5. **Re-entry:** BrightKidCo could return with bigger sizes. Re-check the Ad Library monthly.
