# The Funnel Map — "Big kids still learning"

*Doc 4. Writer B, 2026-10-06. Funnel picked from the Notion **🧭 Funnel Selection Board** (https://app.notion.com/p/3e0d53123cd681bda4d6fbc54fd7b296, opened via the Notion connector this run, last edited 2026-09-30) [B48]. Inputs: awareness §5, belief chain §9B (work/D3-s9-REWRITE.md), mechanism §13, story §13B, offer §15 FINAL, triggers §18, compliance §2B.*

## Pick: the **Discovery Stack — Advertorial (creator-story pre-sell) → Offer Page (proof PDP)**

**Why (board rules + our data):**
- **Her awareness:** in the locked cut **55 of 79 (70%) are problem-aware**, 22 (28%) solution-aware, **2 (3%) product-aware** (work/2C-tally.md). She knows the accidents; she doesn't know a big-kid product exists ("training underwear for older kids" searches return non-absorbent sensory brands and night disposables, §5 [W12]).
- **Board:** an Advertorial is for traffic that must "shift beliefs + build trust via a third-party story", and "works best problem-unaware → aware". She has to swap **three beliefs before she'll look at a price** (§9B 1–3: "I'm not failing", "pull-ups are the wrong tool", "trainers fail because they hold a toddler's pee").
- **The product is a single hero** (one pant, sizes 4–12) → the board's default for a single hero is the Discovery Stack.
- **Proof the stack reaches this parent:** Saphire and Bloomwise reach the same parent at scale with first-person story advertorials → offer pages (§1 LOCKED [U26][W5]). We copy the *format* with real, disclosed creators — never their personas (§2B).

**Runner-up (one line):** the **Listicle** ("5 things nobody tells you about big-kid accidents at school") as a *second* pre-sell to test, because she carries many objections at once (leaks 47, scam 16, waste 15, sizing 13, price 10). **Not picked:** Quiz funnel (one product; a size-finder lives on the PDP instead) · Offer Page direct (only 3% product-aware — §5 says don't run product-first ads) · Home Page (board: never cold traffic).

**Each page continues the conversation — it never repeats the last one:**
the ad shows the **moment** → the pre-sell explains **why** (the cause + the mechanism) → the PDP shows **the number and the offer** → the cart **completes the school-day plan** → after purchase **keeps her brave and the kid in the right size**.

## Stage 1 · Ad — *does:* stops her at the school moment and promises a straight answer
- **Job:** name the **situation** (never the viewer's child or a condition) and open a loop the pre-sell closes. No price, no product claim.
- **Hooks (from §18 triggers 1, 3, 4, 5, 8):** "Week 3 of first grade. Week 3 of spare pants." · "What came home in the backpack today." · "The text from school." · "Pull-ups were the school's idea." · "Not back to pull-ups. Sideways."
- **Format:** real creator, first person about her own 6–9-year-old, disclosed ("paid partnership" / "gifted") (§2B [M54]); the 10-second **sippy-cup demo** (a kid's water bottle poured into a toddler trainer — spills) as the visual (§13 metaphor). Dressed kid only, school context, never underwear-only shots.
- **Emotion:** Fear acknowledged in one line → Courage (§6, §16). Not fear as the hook (§4B).
- **Channels:** TikTok creators first (0 US TikTok "training underwear" ads [W6]); Meta broad, US parents 25–54, creative self-selects (no health targeting) (§2B).
- **Hand-off:** "Here's why it keeps happening (it's not him) →" to the pre-sell.

## Stage 2 · Pre-sell page — *does:* moves Beliefs 1 → 3 and introduces the *Big-Kid Hold* with proof
- **Format:** creator-story advertorial (a named, real parent of a 6–9-year-old, first person, disclosed) or a founder letter. Editorial layout, phone-first. Neutral domain, "Talk to your pediatrician too" in the body (§2B).
- **Mechanism used:** the ***Big-Kid Hold*** (§13). **UMP:** "the toddler-sized hold" — big-kid training pants are toddler pants in a bigger waistband; the fabric got bigger, the capacity didn't. **UMS:** Size it → Pour it → Print it.
- **Proof used (in this order):**
  1. **She's not alone:** "Up to 10 percent of 5-year-olds" have wetting accidents (Boston Children's [B1]); 7–10% of 5–13-year-olds [M64].
  2. **The cause is the gear:** expected bladder capacity 30 × (age + 1) ml for ages 4–12 [B4] (age 7 ≈ 240 ml) vs honest toddler trainers rated "1 pee (60–90 ml)" [P49]; a 9-10Y pack titled "**Toddler** Potty Training Pants" [B28].
  3. **Her own words:** "It doesn't hold more that 10-20ml" [C:N205]; "because of his size and the amount of liquid output, these leaked very easily onto his clothes" [C:N226].
  4. **The history beat (§13B):** 1989 "big kid" = a diaper with an underwear story; 1999 the answer to bigger kids was a **size 6 diaper** [P1][P9]; 2026 the shelf still has no number. *(Fair-to-Brazelton line stays in.)*
  5. **The demo:** the sippy-cup pour, then our size-8 pour with the number on screen — **only after samples are pour-tested** (§2B protocol). Until then: run the toddler-trainer half only.
  6. **The honest limit (power move, §11):** "These aren't diapers. If your kid needs a full bladder held, or nights, this isn't the product."
- **What it does NOT repeat:** the ad's scene. It starts at "why".
- **CTA:** "See what each size holds →" (to the PDP, size pre-selected from a 2-question size picker if used).

## Stage 3 · Product page (Offer Page) — *does:* sells THIS product + the School-Day Kit (Beliefs 4 → 6)
- **Above the fold:** per-size pour video + the number printed for the selected size · "Packed. Handled. Nobody knows." (§16) · the tier selector with the kit pre-selected.
- **Offer inputs it sells (§15 FINAL → full offer built in Step 2):**
  - 1 pair $19 (anchor) · 6 "School Week" $84 · **10-pair "School-Day Kit" $119, pre-selected** (10 pairs + wet bag + discreet backpack spare pouch + teacher note card) · 15 "Full Term" $159.
  - **The Number Guarantee:** 100 days; if a pair doesn't hold the number printed for its size, or it doesn't work for your kid — full refund, keep the pants; free size swaps for 100 days. Policy text = headline.
  - Free shipping from 6 pairs · US stock, 2–5 days · no subscription · nothing pre-ticked · no timer.
  - First-order pop-up: "$15 off your first kit" vs "$15 store credit" (AOV > $100 rule).
- **Proof blocks:** spec table by layer (never "100% cotton") · size chart to 12 by weight + waist + thigh · "would you know?" under-trousers photo · "what it won't hold" box · reviews from parents of **5+ first**, critical ones shown with replies · CPSIA / CPC badge · checkout screenshot ("this is everything you'll pay").
- **Objection order (§11 ranks):** leaks 47 → scam 16 → waste 15 → one small accident 14 → sizing 13 → price 10 → diaper feel 5.
- **What it does NOT repeat:** the cause story. One line links back ("Why toddler trainers flood →").

## Stage 4 · Cart — *does:* raises order value by finishing the school-day plan
- **Default:** the $119 kit is already in (pre-selected on the PDP); the cart shows **"$11.90 a pair · free shipping · no subscription · nothing pre-ticked."**
- **Add-ons (all unticked, one tap each):**
  - **"+3 pairs in the next size up — $33"** (they grow; answers sizing 13 / bigger sizes 24).
  - **"Extra backpack spare pouch + wet bag — $14"** (a second bag for the second backpack / after-school club).
  - From the 6-pair tier: a one-line "Upgrade to the School-Day Kit for $35 more (+4 pairs, + the kit)".
- **Free-shipping bar:** starts at 6 pairs (table stakes, §15).
- **Never:** pre-ticked shipping protection, a countdown, a gift that hides a subscription (UpAiry / Bloomwise patterns, §15).

## Stage 5 · After purchase — *does:* kills regret, upsells the next size, gets repeat buys
- **Thank-you page one-click upsell** (post-purchase app class: AfterSell / ReConvert, as used by Saphire / Kid Confident [B37][O8]): "**Add 5 pairs in the next size at kit price ($59)**" — one click, no re-entry. One offer only.
- **Kill regret (unboxing):** a printed card in the box: the number for each size in the order, "These aren't diapers — here's what they'll handle", the guarantee in one sentence, and the teacher note card ready to hand over.
- **Email / SMS flow:**
  1. Day 0: "What's in the kit and how to pack it" + **"Frequent accidents after 5? Talk to your pediatrician too."** (§2B required line).
  2. Day 2: "Pour-test them yourself" (30-second home test video — makes the number hers).
  3. Day 5: first-week check-in → free size swap link (100 days).
  4. Day 14: review ask (age of kid + school grade; no photos of kids in underwear).
  5. Day 45: "Second backpack? After-school club?" extra pouch / pairs.
  6. **Size-up reminder (opt-in, no subscription):** at signup she enters size + month; we email when the next size is likely needed (INFERENCE timing ~10–12 months; confirm with returns data).
- **Repeat / referral:** "Another parent in the class asking?" — a give-$15 / get-$15 link, no kid data collected.
- **Measure (reference rule):** first-order CVR **and** 90-day LTV; post-purchase survey Q1 "How did you feel sending them on day 1?" (Fear → Courage tracking, §16).
