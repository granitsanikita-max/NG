## The level, stated
- **Category: Level 4 → 5.**
  - Mechanism-saturated: the same feel-wet mechanism at 6+ brands.
  - Identity hooks are already among the longest runners.
  - 14% of all snippets are burned, rising to 25 of 27 for the UpAiry failed-solution rows (tally #11).
- **Segment (big kids 5–9): effectively product Level 1–2, method Level 5.**
  - No daytime big-kid trainer is advertised on Meta or TikTok in the US: 0 ads for "accidents at school" underwear; 0 TikTok ads for "training underwear" [W5][W6].
  - But she has stopped believing *methods and promises*.

**So the match is:**
1. **Sell identification first:** "for big kids still getting there", plus the no-shame, no-timeline creed (§4B).
2. **Prove with a new, different mechanism the incumbents structurally can't copy:** a **number** (ml per size, pour-tested on camera) instead of a ™ story.
3. **Never** lead with feel-wet. Mention it once as a construction fact.

## So What → do this
- Hooks = identity + a concrete proof object:
  - the ml number
  - the size-10 label
  - the pour test
- **Ban** these from all copy: speed claims, "™ learning layer" language, "leakproof".
- Re-check this level before the January scale. If a big-kid competitor appears, upgrade from "a number" to "a number per size + an independent lab sheet".
