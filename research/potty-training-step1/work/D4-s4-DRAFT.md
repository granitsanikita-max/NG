# §4 · Contrast — DRAFT (locked at the end of §11 → work/D4-s4.md)

**The contrast in one line:** every competitor looks, sounds and sells like a toddler brand having a flash sale. We look like a **lab report for big kids**: real kid-sized, measured, plain-spoken and calm.

**Evidence base:**
- §1 ad log: 40 ads from the 3 direct competitors (work/ad-log.csv).
- §1 TAKEN map.
- §15 offer walk.
- The PDP screenshots of UpAiry, Kid Confident and BrightKidCo: work/15-captures/*-1-pdp.png, viewed 2026-10-06 [O1][O6][O9].

**Execution only:** identity (enemy, stance, attitude) is in §4B.

---

## 1 · Visual style
**What they all do:**
- Toddler imagery and toddler prints:
  - BrightKidCo's PDP hero is **four toddlers standing in nothing but the training underwear**.
  - UpAiry's hero is a toddler in the pants.
  - Kid Confident shows the same age.
- Cartoon animal prints.
- Bright green "sale" palettes.
- A "Week 1 → Week 2 → Week 3" progress graphic.
- (VERBATIM, screenshots [O1][O6][O9].)
- In words: "toddler" is in **10 of 40** logged ad hooks (tally #19). UpAiry's PDP says "Specifically engineered for children aged 18 months to 4 years" [M1].

**Our opposite:**
- **No child is ever shown in underwear only.** It's also a Meta risk (§2B).
- Use flat-lays, the pants folded in a hand, a measuring jug and a scale.
- Show **big-kid** context: a backpack, a school hallway, sneakers, a size-10 label on a ruler.
- Prints are plain or kid-chosen "big-kid" designs (stripes, solids, space), not baby animals.
- Palette: off-white, ink black and one signal colour. It should look like a product spec sheet, not a sale.

## 2 · Tone
**What they all do:**
- First-person confessions from personas:
  - UpAiry runs 7 pages of fake moms (≈1,684 ads) [M47-A].
  - Kid Confident has "Mom Hacks with Kara".
  - BrightKidCo has "Tina Dell".
- The emotion is fear, guilt or deadline: "My daughter's daycare is kicking her out in 30 days."
- 18 of 40 hooks open with "I / my" (tally #19).

**Our opposite:**
- **Real, named people only.** That means the founder, real parents and real teachers, with a paid / gifted disclosure.
- Calm and matter-of-fact, a little dry: "Some kids are still getting there at 7. Here's exactly what these hold."
- Never a sob story.

## 3 · Claim type
**What they all do:**
- **Feel-the-wet mechanisms with ™ names:** Feel & Learn™, Body-Signal Learning Layer™, TrueFeel™ (§2 [M47-A]).
- **Speed claims:**
  - "Potty trained in WEEKS not MONTHS" [M1]
  - "Potty Trained in 7 Days" / "1-2 Weeks" (BrightKidCo, ad log)
  - "Week 2+ Fully Trained" [M2]
- Mechanism and upgraded-mechanism leads = **14 of 40** logged ads (tally #17).
- **0 of 908** unique ads state ml or oz [M23].

**Our opposite:**
- **A number, not a promise:** "Size 8: holds ~X ml in our pour test (video)."
- No timeline, ever.
- Feel-wet is stated once, as a construction fact, never as the lead.

## 4 · Offer shape
**What they all do:**
- "Buy X Get Y Free" stacks.
- Countdown bars. UpAiry's "HALLOWEEN SALE" timer read 00:00:00 [Audit].
- "Low stock" alerts.
- Pre-ticked $3.50–$5.99 shipping protection.
- Free "gifts" that hide a subscription (19 complaints [M24]).
- Guarantees whose policy text contradicts the headline.
- (All from §15 summary [O1][O6][O9].)

**Our opposite:**
- **A plain per-pair price ladder** ("$X per pair at 6, $Y at 10").
- Nothing pre-ticked.
- No timer.
- **A worn-product guarantee whose policy matches its headline**, plus free size swaps.
- No subscription. An optional "size-up" reorder reminder is sent by email only.

## 5 · Page format
**What they all do:**
- Long persona advertorials, including fake news ("THE DAILY PARENT… Allen Jahiger, Parenting Researcher" [M5]).
- An "Us vs Pull-Ups" checkmark table. It appears on all three PDPs (screenshots).
- Inconsistent review counts (UpAiry shows 5 different counts on one page [Audit]).

**Our opposite:**
- **A proof page:**
  - pour-test video above the fold
  - a spec table (layers, fabric, seams, ml per size)
  - a **size-by-weight-and-waist chart up to 10/12**
  - a short "What it will hold / what it won't" box
  - one real review count
- The pre-sell is a **real creator's video or a founder letter**, never a fake news page.
