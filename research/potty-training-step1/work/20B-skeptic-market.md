# §20 B · Adversarial skeptic — MARKET, WEDGE, COMPETITORS, ECONOMICS, AUTHORITY

*Skeptic brief: refute, don't confirm. All re-checks live on 2026-10-06 (Winning Hunter, Firecrawl, WebSearch / WebFetch, Europe PMC). This file covers the market-side claims. The persuasion / mechanism / history facts are in work/20B-skeptic-facts.md, which another skeptic wrote in parallel.*

## Verdict in one paragraph
The locked market survives, but **"uncontested" was overstated**. A live US paid-social competitor exists that the docs missed: **CARER (carerspk.com)**. It runs kids' washable "incontinence" boxers for ages 4–16 on US Meta, with the copy "hidden **100ml** leak protection… dry **at school**". 27 of these ads are indexed since Nov 2025, and one was still seen on 2026-10-05. Marketplace sellers already print ml too: TIICHOO 30–40 ml, Carer Amazon 50–80 ml. So these claims are **dead**: "nobody states a ml figure", "nobody frames school", "every absorbent seller refuses worn returns" and "first to print a number". The reach proof from Saphire and Bloomwise is weaker than stated: Saphire sells mainly mood gummies, and Bloomwise's school-accident hook is about **constipation and soiling**. What still holds:
- nobody sells a **size-matched, pour-tested** number;
- nobody sells a **training** (not incontinence) identity for 5–9;
- nobody has a brand creed;
- nobody sells a **School-Day Kit**.

The CARER contestant is weak: one flat 100 ml across 10 sizes, an adult-incontinence store, product-code names and 3.9★ from 48 reviews. **Purple Ocean Gate: still PASS, narrowed.** Painkiller PASS. Uncontested PASS only for the bundle, not for any single element. Market PASS (≈1.4–2.0M kids, corrected upward).
