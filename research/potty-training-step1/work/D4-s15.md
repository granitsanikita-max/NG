# 15 · Competitor Offers + Price Ladder

*Doc 4 — Brand, Offer & Funnel. Captured 2026-10-06, US storefronts where available. Sources are numbered [O1]… (list at the end).*

## How this was captured

- **Price data:** Shopify `products.json` / `[handle].js` for every live Shopify store. This covers every variant, tier and compare-at price (raw files are in `work/15-captures/js/`).
- **Cart walk:** one headless Playwright script went product page → add to cart → cart → checkout for each store and **stopped at the payment step**. It entered no data, no payment details and placed no order. Text and screenshots for each step are in `work/15-captures/[store]-[1-pdp|2-after-atc|3-cart|4-checkout].txt/.png`.
  - Where the add-to-cart button would not click, a retry added the first in-stock variant through Shopify's public `/cart/add` link and then walked cart → checkout.
- **Policies:** each store's `/policies/refund-policy`.
- **Post-purchase upsells:** these only appear after payment, so they are **INFERENCE**. They come from Winning Hunter `get_store_details` apps [O5][O8][O11][O14][O18][O21][O40] plus app signatures in each product page's HTML [O74].
- **Pop-up offers:** most Klaviyo/Privy pop-ups load late, and the script dismissed overlays. A pop-up offer is recorded only where its text was visible on the page; elsewhere the block says "not captured".

**Table stakes** (at least half of the walked DTC underwear sellers do these, so we must match them):
- Quantity tiers framed as **"Buy X, Get Y Free"** with a default or pre-selected middle or large tier (UpAiry, Kid Confident, BrightKidCo, RaiseCalm, ProudPants, Sculptara, shopnola, BlossomNest, Rudie).
- **Free digital gifts** (ebook / printables / reward chart) with a claimed $ value, unlocked at the bundle tier.
- **Free shipping at the bundle tier** or above a $45–70 threshold.
- A **30–100-day money-back guarantee** headline.
- A **countdown / "limited stock" / seasonal sale** bar.
- A struck-through compare-at anchor.
- A **"save $80–100+/month vs disposables"** claim.

**Best-in-class:**
- **UpAiry's stack** is proven by its $1.8M–3.4M/30d estimate [O5]: B2G3 → B4G6, a gift unlock ladder, a 75-day guarantee with a prepaid return label and free size swaps, and ships in 1 day.
- On guarantee honesty, **Brilliant Kids' 75-day *used-product* refund ("keep it or dispose of it")** is the cleanest risk reversal [O44].
- On value framing, **ProudPants' per-piece ladder ($7.80 → $5.90 → $5.27/pc)** is clearest [O19].

**Nobody offers** (the open slots, which go to the Offer inputs below):
1. A **stated, tested absorbency number** (ml/oz) on a daytime trainer, with a "holds one full pee or we refund" promise. Only Super Undies and Peejamas quantify absorbency, and both are niche [O34][O36].
2. A **daycare / multi-caregiver kit**: labelled pairs, a wet bag, a caregiver instruction card and a daycare note. BrightKidCo sells wet bags as a $39.99 add-on; Fig includes a tote; **nobody frames a kit for daycare** [O9][O30].
3. A **worn-product guarantee that matches its own policy *and* covers size**:
   - BrightKidCo, shopnola and Sculptara contradict themselves.
   - Rudie and My Carry Potty refuse returns.
   - UpAiry requires "unworn".
4. A **transparent size-up / replacement plan**. No honest subscription exists; UpAiry's subscription is a complaint source [O5].
5. **No pre-ticked fees.** UpAiry, Fvaulity and Kanga auto-add protection, and Kid Confident charges $5.99 shipping on small orders. "Everything included, nothing pre-ticked" is unclaimed.
6. **Sizes beyond 4–5T in the main DTC bundles.** ProudPants stops at 4T; UpAiry runs S–L to 55 lb; Kid Confident sells Big Kid sizes separately.
7. **A method included with the product.** BLF sells only the method and Oh Crap sells only courses. The DTC "ebooks" are giveaways that set no claim; no one bundles a named day-by-day plan as the core of the offer.

- **Problems the offer stack must solve:**

  - "Home Week Kit" (5 pairs).
  - **"Home + Daycare Kit"** (10 pairs + wet bag + caregiver card + daycare note): the gap from Nobody offers #2.

  - "Full Switch" (15 pairs).
  - Use **same-product multi-packs**, not mixed bundles (reference rule).
  - **Auto-select the middle tier = target AOV.**

  - Anchor to the single ($19).

  - A **long, worn-product-friendly results guarantee whose policy text matches the headline**, e.g. 90 days, used pairs refunded, keep them, free size swaps. Add a confidence stat once real ("x% claim").

  - Single **$19** (anchor).
  - 5 pairs **$49**.
