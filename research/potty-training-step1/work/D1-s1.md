- **Avatar:** mom of a 2.5–5-year-old under outside pressure (daycare, mother-in-law, school). Sub-avatars:
  - working mom (Bec, Tina)
  - educator authority (Kate: "worked in early childhood education for 11 years… three different daycares")
  - kindergarten mandate (Kereisa)
  - rash (Kereisa)

  - Ad log, 16 rows: 4 video / 12 image.
  - File A, ~320 ads: 212 video / 80 image / 15 carousel / 13 DCO.
  - Long-copy first-person statics dominate the persona pages; UGC video on the main page.
- **Repeating hooks:** "7 accidents in one day"; "STILL in diapers"; daycare / preschool "kicking her out"; 1957 / Vietnam stats; "F*CK POTTY TRAINING (respectfully)"; "Potty training pants are a SCAM!! … $21.00"; "Please STOP using diapers".

- Or ad → advertorial `/pages/daycare-mandate`. Its job: "Daily Parent" news story on the school mandate → "pediatric OTs recommend".
- Or ad → **PDP**. Its job: close with gift tiers.
- Also `/pages/adv-v5`, `/pages/adv-v2`, `/pages/black-friday` [M23].
- Apps: GemPages, Kaching bundles, UpCart, Klaviyo, Parcel Panel, Triple Whale [M24].

**Offer**

  - Inconsistent proof (8 different customer counts, §Audit).

- **Don't fight UpAiry on its own turf.** Its turf is persona advertorials, feel-wet, daycare and working-parent guilt, toddler late-trainer shame, and the $21 / "6 free pairs" offer.

  - Fit: toddler-only creative.
  - Platform: **zero TikTok ads**.
- BrightKidCo's autism / sensory angle is **proven-but-abandoned**. Revisit it in §2 under compliance-safe framing.
- Log every new hook against work/ad-log.csv before testing, so we never ship clone #20.
