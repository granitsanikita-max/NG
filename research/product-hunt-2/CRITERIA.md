# Product hunt #2 — brand-first filter (2026-10-06)
Owner rule: positioning can NEVER be "the honest/trustworthy/quality one". A product must carry a real culture + strong position, or it's out.

Hard gates (all must pass):
1. Proven demand — ≥1 store ≥$100k/mo (Winning Hunter get_store_details) OR ads scaling ≥30 days with ≥20 active ads.
2. Margin — landed cost ≤25% of selling price; AOV ≥$40 possible (bundles ok).
3. Solves a real problem (painkiller or strong desire), not a gadget.
4. Culture test — name all four:
   a. TRIBE: a specific identity the buyer already has or wants (e.g. runners, hunters, new dads, nurses, gardeners, lifters) — not "moms" or "people with X".
   b. BELIEF/ENEMY: something that tribe believes / is against that current sellers ignore.
   c. PRODUCT CARRIES IT: a design/spec/ritual that embodies the belief (not just copy).
   d. CULTURE ASSETS: rituals, language, content formats, community the brand can own.
5. Competitors are generic / functional / fake-funnel — nobody owns the tribe.
6. Sourceable (AliExpress/1688/CJ or easy private label), no supplements, no medical/disease claims, no heavy certification.
