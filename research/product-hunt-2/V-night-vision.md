# V — Deep validation: budget digital night vision for hog & predator hunters
Date: 2026-10-06. Product tested: handheld digital NV monocular/binocular (IR 850/940 nm, records video) sold at **$149–199**. Tribe tested: night hog hunters + predator (coyote) callers.
Sources: WinningHunter (WH) MCP, Firecrawl, WebSearch. Every claim has a URL. Anything marked **INFERENCE** is my estimate, not a sourced figure.

---

## TL;DR verdict: **KILL** (as specified). Do not spend test money on it before Nikita looks at the pivot note in §6.
- **Demand is real, but it's for a different price point.** The money in this category sits at **$40–95** (Amazon/TTS commodity units) and **$330–400** (handheld thermal). The $149–199 digital tier is squeezed between the two, and nobody there is doing $100k/mo.
- **Margin fails.** A spec good enough to justify $179 lands at about **40% of price** (gate is ≤25%) once 2026 China duties are included.
- **Culture works against the product.** The tribe is real, but its core belief is that cheap night gear is a trap ("when you go cheap on thermal/NV you ultimately spend more"). The genre's signature content (kill footage, weapon-mounted views) also can't run as Meta/TikTok ads.

## 3. PLATFORM / COMPLIANCE RISK — CRITICAL

### Meta (verbatim policy, https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/weapons-ammunitions-explosives )
- Banned: "Products that might be attached or used with a weapon to enhance or customize its form or function… **Optics and flashlights, such as telescopic sight, iron sight, target magnification or focus**."
- Allowed at **18+**: "Items that aren't directly sold as part of a physical weapon… Equipment related to the practice of hunting."
- Depiction: "Weapons in a… hunting context providing the context is non-violent and there is no attempt to buy, sell…" Also banned: "The pure depiction of weapons if there is no discussion or advocacy."
- **So a handheld monocular/binocular can advertise on Meta at 18+.** It fails as soon as it is shown or described as rifle-mounted. Enforcement is noisy in both directions: Meta has approved banned scope ads (https://www.techtransparencyproject.org/articles/gun-ads-flow-meta-platforms ). **INFERENCE:** expect false-positive rejections and account flags in the "hunting" vertical.

### TikTok (verbatim, https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-dangerous-products-or-services?lang=en )
- "We do not allow **ad content and landing pages** to display or promote the use of dangerous weapons… Guns, gun parts, and ammunition."
- "Police or military gear and equipment… Products designed with police or military elements" are not allowed. General-use devices are OK.
- **So on TikTok: no gun anywhere in frame, including the landing page.** Avoid "tactical / military / NODs / PVS" framing, which is Night Operators' whole language.

### Wording and imagery that gets ads rejected (both platforms)
Kills ads: "scope", "sight", "reticle", "crosshair", "rifle mount", "picatinny/weaver", "weapon light", "kill", "smoke", "tactical", "military-grade", "NODs/PVS". Rifles in frame (TikTok). Through-the-scope reticle footage. Dead or bleeding animals (graphic content). Bundles that include a rifle-mount adapter on the LP.
Safe to use: "spotter", "scan the pasture", "count the sounder", "see what's tearing up your field", "landowner", "feeder", "wildlife", handheld or tripod shots only.
**Consequence:** the tribe's #1 content format, night-kill footage (§5), **cannot run as ads**. Organic content only.

### Trade / regulatory
- **ITAR/EAR:** apply to *exports*, not to imports for US sale. Digital consumer NV is at most EAR99/6A993 (AT controls). Thermal with >111k pixels or >60 Hz is 6A003 if re-exported. **Don't ship internationally** (https://www.bis.gov/revisions-license-requirements-license-exception-eligibility-certain-thermal-imaging-cameras-foreign-made-military ; https://open-gov.usebase.io/eccn/6/6A993 ).
- **HTS / duty:** NV monoculars fall under **9005.80.4020/4040 at 8% MFN** (CBP rulings: https://www.tarifflens.ai/rulings/809648 ; https://open-gov.usebase.io/rulings/887583 ). Thermal cameras fall under 8525.89 (https://rulings.cbp.gov/docs/hq/2025/h331315 ). Section 301 China duties remain; IEEPA tariffs ended 2026-02-24 and Section 122 adds 10% (https://www.calcmytariff.com/country/china/consumer-electronics ; https://www.china-briefing.com/news/us-china-tariff-rates-2025 ). **INFERENCE:** total ≈ 8% + 25% (301) + 10% (122, if still in force) = **33–43%**.
- **FCC:** non-WiFi units are digital devices under Part 15B and need an SDoC. **WiFi models are Part 15C intentional radiators and need a TCB-certified FCC ID** (https://www.compliancegate.com/fcc-intentional-radiators/ ). Pick a supplier whose WiFi module already has an FCC ID, or skip WiFi.
- **Lithium:** 18650 inside the unit ships as UN3481, PI 967 Section II (≤100 Wh, ≤30% state of charge by air, battery mark) (https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/Lithium-Battery-Shipping-Guide_508_2026.pdf ). Spare batteries sold alone are UN3480 and much harder to ship, so keep them installed or packed with the equipment.
