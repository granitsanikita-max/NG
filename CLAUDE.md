# NG — Nikita's dropshipping brands

## Active brand: Leapproof (leapproof.com)
Leak-proof underwear for active 30s–40s women who leak on a sneeze/jump/run (postpartum, perimenopause, athletes) — "not a diaper, not a period."

**Before any Leapproof work (ads, copy, store, offer, quiz, emails), read `brand/leapproof/BRAND_BIBLE.md`.**
It has positioning, ICP, objections, mechanism (Leak-Lock), voice + banned words, compliance, offer ladder, and the quiz funnel. Raw research + the 155-snippet customer data bank are in `brand/leapproof/research/`.

Non-negotiables for any copy: no "incontinence/discreet/dignity/protection" in ads, no absolute leak claims, no fake doctors/reviews/urgency, open on the moment (sneeze/jump/run), never shame.

**Offer (locked v5, AUD): read `brand/leapproof/OFFER.md`; NUMBER_SHEET.md wins on any conflict.** No singles, no Big-Day pair. 3 for $69 / 5 for $89 (hero) / 10 for $159 AUD. Gifts: **3 = none · 5 = Wash Bag · 10 = Wash Bag + Core-Care Wash Sheets.** Comeback Plan out until a named PT makes it. CJ launch guarantee: **30-Night Leak-Test** (from delivery, full refund, keep the pairs, one claim per customer) + Fit Promise. Quiz reward = "your free gifts are unlocked" (not $15 off). Upsells per NUMBER_SHEET §6.

**Funnel (plan v2, chiefed): before writing ANY funnel/page/quiz/checkout copy, read `brand/leapproof/funnel/FUNNEL_PLAN.md` and pull every number from `brand/leapproof/funnel/NUMBER_SHEET.md` (single source of truth; never type a number that isn't there).** Flow: ad → landing → quiz "Find Your Leak Match" → product page (reveal, set pre-selected) → checkout → 1-click upgrade → thank-you, built in Funnelish on Shopify. The page rules come from Nikita's "Funnel & Store Page Fundamentals", saved verbatim in `brand/leapproof/fundamentals/` (00 copywriting · 01 universal · 02 PDP · 04 pre-sales · 06 quiz · 07 cart/checkout · 08 swipes + kill check). Chief every page against its checklist there. Open decisions D1–D15 are in FUNNEL_PLAN §0; anything marked PENDING in the number sheet doesn't go live until Nikita OKs it.
Products come from CJ Dropshipping (listed into Shopify via the CJ app so orders auto-route to CJ). On the CJ period panty, copy runs the **CJ column** of the claims matrix (FUNNEL_PLAN §2): no "built for pee, not blood," no attacking period underwear, no pour demo, no Leak-Lock mechanism.
