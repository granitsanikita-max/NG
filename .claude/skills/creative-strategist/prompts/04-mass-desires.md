# Prompt #4 — Identifying the Dominant Mass Desires

Read (these are the attached Customer Avatar / Foundational docs): {{BRAND_DOCS}}, plus `{{RUN_DIR}}/02-discussions.md` and `{{RUN_DIR}}/03-mechanism.md`.

**Situation**
You are analyzing a product to understand the diverse motivations that drive different customer segments to purchase it. A customer avatar document has been provided that contains demographic, psychographic, and behavioral information about the target market. Your goal is to uncover the underlying desires that make this product appealing to fundamentally different groups of people.

**Task**
Identify exactly 5 fundamentally different desires that this product fulfills. Each desire must represent a completely distinct outcome that appeals to a separate segment of the market, not variations or sub-categories of the same core want.

**Objective**
The objective is to reveal the mass desires—outcomes that thousands of people in the market already want—so that marketing, positioning, and messaging can be tailored to resonate with each distinct segment. This analysis will help understand why different types of customers purchase the same product for entirely different reasons.

**Knowledge**
Mass desire definition: What thousands of people in your market already want. It should be specific enough to be meaningful but broad enough to be widely shared.

Critical distinction test: Each desire must seek to GET something DIFFERENT from the product. If two desires could be combined into one broader desire, they are too similar and must be reconsidered. The desires should be so distinct that one person seeking desire A would not necessarily care about desire B.

**Output Requirements**
For each of the 5 desires:
1. "I want [specific outcome]" (segment description: who typically feels this desire most strongly)
2. "I want [specific outcome]" (segment description: …)
3. "I want [specific outcome]" (segment description: …)
4. "I want [specific outcome]" (segment description: …)
5. "I want [specific outcome]" (segment description: …)

The assistant should ensure that:
- Each desire is framed from the customer's first-person perspective as a simple "I want" statement
- Each desire represents a completely different outcome, not a variation of the same theme
- The segment description clearly identifies who feels this desire most intensely
- No two desires overlap or could be merged into a single broader desire
- Each desire reflects a mass market want, not a niche preference
