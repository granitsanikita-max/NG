# Prompt 4 — Five dominant mass desires

Read: brand docs {{BRAND_DOCS}} (foundational + customer avatar), `{{RUN_DIR}}/01-research.md`, `02-discussions.md`, `03-mechanism.md`.

Identify the **5 dominant mass desires** for this product's market.

A mass desire:
- is a want that ALREADY EXISTS and is shared by a large group; the product doesn't create it
- is about what they want to achieve or become (the transformation), not the problem and not a product feature
- defines one emotional creative bucket; the 5 must be clearly distinct from each other (different segments or motivations), giving 5 different entry points into the market

For each desire, output exactly this format:

### Mass desire {{n}}: "I want to …"  (first person, in customer language)
- **Who holds it (segment):** life situation, demographics, typical context
- **Why it runs deep:** the core human fear or want under it (e.g. being seen as inadequate, weak, incompetent; belonging; status; safety; freedom)
- **Evidence:** 2–3 verbatim quotes from 02-discussions.md
- **Pain that blocks it today**
- **Transformation the product enables:** using only the mechanism and facts from the docs
- **Typical awareness level of this segment**

Finish with a one-line ranking of the 5 by estimated market size, and why.
