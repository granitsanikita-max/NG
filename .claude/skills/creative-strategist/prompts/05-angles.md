# Prompt 5 — Five marketing angles for ONE mass desire

Read: brand docs {{BRAND_DOCS}}, `{{RUN_DIR}}/02-discussions.md`, `03-mechanism.md`, `04-desires.md`.
Angles already written for other desires (avoid duplicating their stories): {{PREVIOUS_ANGLE_FILES}}

**Work ONLY on this desire:**
> Mass desire {{DESIRE_NUMBER}}: {{DESIRE_TEXT}}

Create **5 marketing angles** for it. An angle is ONE specific story that activates this desire in a recognisable real-life situation: a moment of realisation where the viewer thinks "that's me". It is not a hook and not a headline. The 5 must explore different contexts, fears and moments of realisation (different people, places, triggers), all landing on the SAME desire.

For each angle output exactly:

### D{{DESIRE_NUMBER}}A{{n}} — "<angle name>"
- **Target audience:** who exactly, in which situation
- **Story arc:** 4–7 sentences: the ordinary routine → the creeping problem → the moment it hits → the realisation
- **Unique problem mechanism:** why it's happening, in this story's terms
- **Unique solution mechanism:** how the product fixes that cause (facts from docs only)
- **Key emotional moment:** the single frame an ad could freeze
- **Headline options (3):** pulled from the story, in customer language (e.g. "My dog wasn't being bad. My body was breaking down.")
- **Awareness level:** unaware / problem / solution / product / most aware
- **Recommended static format:** from the matrix (organic screenshot, benefit callouts, comparison, review, offer+urgency, before/after)
- **Key objection this angle must handle**
- **Visual idea:** what the image shows, in one line

Quality check before returning: each story is specific (named situation, concrete details), believable, and clearly triggers the desire above, not a different one.
