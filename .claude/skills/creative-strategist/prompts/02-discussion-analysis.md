**Situation**
You are analyzing public discussion forum posts from a specific customer demographic to inform high-converting advertising copy. These forum posts contain authentic customer language expressing real problems and pain points. This analysis will directly feed into a copywriting process that applies Breakthrough Advertising (Eugene Schwartz) and RMBC (Rapport, Message, Benefit, Close) method principles to create persuasive advertisements that drive profitable conversions at scale.

**Task**
The assistant should analyze 20 URLs to public discussion forum posts and extract deep insights about the customer's problems, pain points, emotional states, and psychological conditions. The analysis must identify:
- Specific keywords and phrases used verbatim by customers
- Language patterns and semantic structures
- Emotional states (frustration, anxiety, hope, desperation, etc.)
- Psychological states of being (urgency, fear, desire, confusion, etc.)
- Common jargon and terminology specific to this demographic
- Recurring themes and problem patterns
- The customer's perspective and framing of their problems

**Objective**
Provide a comprehensive research foundation that enables the creation of highly persuasive advertising copy by deeply understanding the customer avatar's authentic voice, pain points, and psychological triggers. The output should equip copywriters with the exact language, emotional insights, and psychological understanding needed to craft advertisements that resonate at a profound level and drive conversions.

**Knowledge**
The analysis will be used specifically for:
- Writing copy based on Breakthrough Advertising principles (stages of awareness, sophistication levels, mass desire)
- Applying RMBC method (building rapport through shared understanding, delivering resonant messages, highlighting benefits, and closing effectively)
- Creating advertisements designed for profitable conversions at scale

The assistant should prioritize extracting information that directly supports persuasive copywriting, including the customer's exact words, emotional triggers, and psychological pain points.

**Output Requirements**
1. **Executive Summary**: Overview of the dominant pain points and emotional/psychological themes
2. **Verbatim Language Bank**: 20-30 most commonly used phrases exactly as customers wrote them; 15-20 recurring keywords; 10-15 pieces of demographic-specific jargon
3. **Emotional State Analysis**: primary emotions (with frequency indicators); emotional intensity levels; emotional progression patterns (how feelings evolve in discussions)
4. **Psychological State Analysis**: core psychological drivers (fear, desire, urgency, etc.); underlying beliefs and assumptions; decision-making barriers and hesitations; motivational triggers
5. **Pain Point Hierarchy**: rank problems by frequency and intensity; distinguish surface-level complaints from deeper underlying pains; identify the "pain behind the pain"
6. **Language Pattern Analysis**: common sentence structures; metaphors and analogies; question patterns revealing uncertainty or seeking; comparison frameworks
7. **Copywriting Intelligence**: stage of awareness indicators; market sophistication signals; objections and concerns that must be addressed; desired outcomes and transformation language; social proof and authority signals they respond to
8. **Strategic Recommendations**: key psychological hooks; rapport-building language; benefit framing opportunities; urgency and scarcity angles present in the discussions

Present all findings with specific examples and quotes from the forum posts. End with a source table: # · title · URL.
