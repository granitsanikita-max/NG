## If FUNNEL_STAGE = BOFU (original prompt)
**Situation**
You are developing Bottom of Funnel (BOFU) Facebook advertising campaigns for a product where the audience is already familiar with the brand and close to making a purchase decision. These prospects have moved through awareness and consideration stages and now need the final push to convert. The advertising must speak directly to different psychological motivations that drive purchase decisions.

**Task**
Create 5 distinct Facebook Ad variations, each consisting of Primary Text (the main ad copy above the image) and a Headline (the text below the image). *(Adapted: all 5 serve THIS ad's desire and angle; vary the hook, proof element and objection handled.)*

**Objective**
Generate conversion-focused ad copy that speaks to the diverse psychological motivations within the target audience, maximizing the likelihood that at least one variation will deeply resonate with each prospect segment and drive them to complete their purchase.

**Knowledge**
Mass market desires typically include: fear of loss/missing out, desire for gain/improvement, need for belonging/social acceptance, pursuit of pleasure/comfort, avoidance of pain/effort, status/recognition, security/safety, novelty/curiosity, and identity/self-expression.

For Bottom of Funnel audiences:
- Use direct, action-oriented language that assumes familiarity with the product
- Include specific product benefits rather than general brand messaging
- Incorporate urgency or scarcity elements where appropriate
- Address final objections or hesitations that prevent purchase
- Use social proof, guarantees, or risk-reversal when relevant
- Keep Primary Text between 125-150 characters for optimal mobile display
- Keep Headlines between 25-40 characters for maximum impact
- Avoid redundancy between Primary Text and Headline; they should complement each other
- Ensure each variation feels authentically different in tone, angle, and emotional appeal

When analyzing the foundational documents, identify: core product features and their emotional benefits; brand voice and tone; existing testimonials or proof points; USPs; pricing, guarantees, special offers; target demographics and psychographics. Map real product attributes to the desire; never force artificial connections.

## If FUNNEL_STAGE = TOFU (built to the BOFU tab's structure from course 6.06/6.07: "native long-form copy for native-style image ads")
**Situation**
You are developing Top of Funnel (TOFU) Facebook ads for people who are unaware or only problem aware. They don't know the product, may not know their problem has a cause or a fix, and are scrolling a feed of friends and creators. An ad that looks like an ad gets skipped. Native image ads often carry NO text on the image, so the primary text carries the entire angle.

**Task**
Create 5 Primary Text + Headline variations for THIS ad's desire and angle. All 5 tell the angle's story; each opens on a different moment of it (A: the Key Emotional Moment · B: the ordinary routine before it · C: a verbatim forum line · D: the realisation · E: a blunt confession).

**Knowledge / rules**
- Read like a real post from the angle's Target Audience (first person, or a wife/friend if the angle is told that way), in verbatim forum language from 02-discussions.md. Short lines, white space, plain words, no hype, no emoji, no exclamation marks unless the brief allows them.
- **Line 1 is the hook and does the image's job:** it must make the problem-aware reader stop ("Everyone keeps telling me I look great. I keep checking the middle of my shirt.").
- Body follows the angle exactly: situation → the moment it hit → the realisation (Unique Problem Mechanism: why nothing he tried fixed it, in his words) → the discovery (Unique Solution Mechanism; product named late, never in the first third) → the desire achieved, described as a scene not a claim → soft CTA.
- **Native long-form:** Primary Text 900–2,000 characters. If the ad goes to an advertorial or listicle, the CTA points to the story ("I wrote up what actually changed. Link below."), not to a sale. No prices in the first two-thirds.
- Headline (under the image) 25–40 characters: curiosity or identity, not an offer ("Why my shirts still told on me").
- Only brief-approved claims. Sample-gated claims stay out until approved. No body-change promises if the brief bans them.
- Score each variation 1–5 on: stops a problem-aware scroller · sounds like a real person · one desire only. Rewrite any variation scoring below 4 before returning.

## If FUNNEL_STAGE = MOFU (built to the BOFU tab's structure from course 6.06/6.07)
**Situation**
You are developing Middle of Funnel (MOFU) Facebook ads for people who are solution or product aware. They know products like this exist, have probably tried one, and are skeptical ("they all roll up", "doesn't work for me").

**Task**
Create 5 Primary Text + Headline variations for THIS ad's desire and angle. Each leads with a different objection or comparison.

**Knowledge / rules**
- Lead with the knowledge gap: why what they tried failed (Unique Problem Mechanism) → why this works differently (Unique Solution Mechanism) → the angle's key objection handled → proof or guarantee from the brief → CTA
- Comparison framing allowed (us vs "miracle shirts" / old solutions); never name a competitor unless the brief allows it
- Primary Text 250–500 characters; Headline 25–40 characters (mechanism or benefit)
- Customer language from 02-discussions.md; only brief-approved claims, offers and proof
