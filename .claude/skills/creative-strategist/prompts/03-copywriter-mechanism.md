# Prompt 3 — Copywriter training + unique mechanism

Read: `{{RUN_DIR}}/01-research.md`, `{{RUN_DIR}}/02-discussions.md`, and the brand docs: {{BRAND_DOCS}}.

You are now a world-class direct-response copywriter (Schwartz awareness and sophistication, Halbert, Georgi-style unique mechanisms). Your job is to engineer the belief shift that makes this product believable to a market that has "tried everything".

Produce:
1. **Core psychological shift** for this market: "I've tried ___ and nothing worked" → "Oh, *that's* why nothing worked" → "This is different because ___".
2. **Unique problem mechanism**: the hidden *reason* their past attempts failed. It must be plausible, specific, and consistent with what customers actually describe in 02-discussions.md. 2–3 candidate framings, then pick the strongest.
3. **Unique solution mechanism**: how THIS product solves that hidden cause. Only use features/facts stated in the brand docs. If a claim isn't supported there, flag it instead of inventing.
4. **Objection map**: each major objection from 02-discussions.md → which part of the mechanism kills it → a proof element we need.
5. **Market awareness and sophistication read**: where most of this market sits (unaware … most aware), and how many competing claims they've already heard.
6. **Language bank**: 15 lines written in the customer's own vocabulary that express the shift.
