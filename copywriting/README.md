# Winning Copy Study

Goal: learn copy from the masters well enough to write ads, product pages and emails yourself — then get them graded.

## Study plan (do it in this order)

1. **Read `03-fundamentals.md` once.** Don't memorize it — it's the map.
2. **Hand-copy one ad a day** from `02-full-texts.md`, pen and paper. Halbert made his students do this; it puts the rhythm in your hands. Order:
   1. Caples — "They Laughed When I Sat Down at the Piano" (story hook)
   2. Sackheim — "Do You Make These Mistakes in English?" (self-diagnosis hook)
   3. Hopkins — Puffed Wheat "A Million a Month" (mechanism + social proof)
   4. Hopkins — Pepsodent "Your Teeth Tonight" (invisible enemy + free trial)
3. **Then open the scans** linked in `01-swipe-file.md` for the copyrighted ones and hand-copy those too: Halbert's Coat of Arms letter, Halbert's Desperate Housewife diet ad, Sugarman's BluBlocker, Schwartz's Burn Disease.
4. **After each one, answer in 3 lines:** What's the hook? What's the proof? What objection did they kill and how?
5. **Write your own.** Take your current product, pick one technique from the ad you copied that day, and write a hook + 150-word body using it.
6. **Paste it to Claude** with: product, price, target buyer, platform (Meta / TikTok / product page / email). It gets scored against the scorecard in `03-fundamentals.md`.

## Books worth owning (in priority order)

1. **Joseph Sugarman — *The Adweek Copywriting Handbook*.** He sold physical gadgets through ads. Closest thing to dropshipping that exists in the classics.
2. **Eugene Schwartz — *Breakthrough Advertising*.** Awareness levels + market sophistication. Hardest read, biggest payoff.
3. **Gary Halbert — *The Boron Letters*.** Free online: https://www.thegaryhalbertletter.com/Boron/TChapter1.htm
4. **Claude Hopkins — *Scientific Advertising* (1923).** Free: https://archive.org/details/scientific-advertising
5. **John Caples — *Tested Advertising Methods*.** Headlines.
