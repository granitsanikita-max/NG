# Supplier Shortlist — Urine-Rated Incontinence Underwear (US women's leak-proof brand)

> Compiled 2026-10-10. Builds on `product_and_sourcing.md`. Target customer: active women 30s–40s with **stress incontinence** (fast, sometimes high-volume urine leaks — NOT menstrual). Product spec we grade against: URINE-rated, ml-stated (moderate 100–150 ml core, plus light ~50 ml and a heavy/overnight 200 ml+ option); high-waist brief + boyshort; wicking top + multi-layer core + heat-bonded TPU/PUL film + **FLATLOCK sealed leg/side edges**; cotton or bamboo main body; OEKO-TEX Std 100 + willingness to supply a 3rd-party finished-garment PFAS (total-fluorine) report + absorbency report; low MOQ private-label (ideally 50–300 pcs).
>
> **Honesty header — the single biggest finding:** The "incontinence underwear" category on sourcing platforms is ~80% **period panties with the word "incontinence" keyword-stuffed into the title**. Blood ≠ urine: urine is faster and higher-volume, so a 4-layer period gusset rated "55 ml heavy flow" is NOT a true urine product even when the listing says "incontinence." I have flagged every option below as **TRUE INCONTINENCE** (urine-engineered, ml for urine) vs **PERIOD-CROSSOVER** (period core relabeled). Grade accordingly.

## Scoring key
- ✅ meets spec · ⚠️ partial / needs verification · ❌ fails / not stated
- **"No ml rating"** is flagged explicitly wherever a seller gives tampons/cups/"heavy flow" instead of ml.

**Benchmark read-through for our spec:**
1. **Daytime moderate = 50–100 ml is the real market norm** (Knix SuperPlus 84 ml, Confitex 74 ml, Speax 100 ml). Our "moderate 100–150 ml" target already **beats** the mainstream daytime tier — good, but don't claim 150 ml unless the lab backs it.
2. **200 ml+ only exists via a booster insert** (Modibodi 250, Aisle 236). To offer a heavy/overnight SKU, copy the **snap-in booster** architecture (Lynmiss already has a "floating reusable pad" — same idea) rather than one thick gusset.
3. **Fabric gap = our wedge:** Knix/Saalt/Speax are **nylon/poly-dominant**. Our **cotton/bamboo main body** (Lynmiss, Aisle, Modibodi do this) is a genuine differentiator for the "natural, non-synthetic" angle — lead with it.
4. **Side-seam seal is the make-or-break** — Knix patented "360 LeakSeal," Everdries fails exactly here. Our flatlock-sealed edge spec must be sampled and wash-tested (20–50 cycles) before any claim.
5. **PFAS credibility ladder:** Confitex (independent lab) > Aisle (certified) > Modibodi/Saalt (OEKO-TEX) > Knix/Speax (supplier attestation). To out-trust them, publish a **finished-garment total-fluorine report** — none of the mass brands do this openly.

### What to ask every supplier in the first email (the gating questions)
1. **Urine ml rating per style**, and by **which test method** (Hohenstein FEMTECHMAS-6513 / Bureau Veritas / ISO 15621) — not tampons/cups, not "heavy flow."
2. **Finished-garment PFAS report** (total fluorine + restricted-PFAS list) on a *production sample*, from a CNAS/A2LA-accredited lab — plus OEKO-TEX Std 100 **certificate number** (to verify on oeko-tex.com). State up front you will also commission your own batch PFAS test.
3. **Gusset/leg-edge construction:** confirm **flatlock (4-needle/6-thread)** at crotch & leg openings, and **heat-bonded** (not iron-pressed) TPU/PUL film + micron thickness; ask for video of the heat-bonding line.
4. **Fabric content** of main body (want cotton or bamboo-dominant, not nylon-dominant) + GSM.
5. **Sample cost & lead time**, OEM MOQ at 50 / 300 / 500, per-unit at each tier, **custom logo waistband + tagless label + retail packaging** cost/MOQ.
6. **200 ml+ heavy/overnight option** via snap-in booster pad (not a bulky single gusset).
7. **US warehouse / DDP** availability and current landed cost incl. post-2025 duties (de-minimis gone).
8. Wash-durability: cycles-to-delamination claim + whether dryer-safe.

### Known gaps / honesty notes
- **No supplier publishes a finished-garment PFAS report on the website** — every PFAS claim above is marketing until you get the lab PDF on a production sample. Confitex is the only one with *independent* verification already in hand.
- **ml ratings are self-reported** and often conflate blood vs urine; several "incontinence" listings are period panties. Treat all ml as "verify on your own pour-test (50/100/150 ml saline)."
- Could not open live Alibaba supplier chat to get **sealed OEM quotes/sample fees** — those require direct contact (emails/WhatsApp provided above).
- **Xiamen Yishangyi** from prior notes was not reconfirmed as a women's-incontinence low-MOQ fit; the "newyifa" Fujian name that surfaced is a 50k+ MOQ disposable maker — do not pursue for a startup washable line.
