## TOP PICKS — clear all 3 bars

### 1. Silent vibrating alarm watch (for people who sleep through everything) ⭐ my #1
- **Pain (high/emotional):** chronic oversleeping — missing work, exams, flights; heavy sleepers; shift workers; people who can't use a loud alarm without waking a partner; deaf/HoH. Real anxiety ("I've slept through every alarm I own").
- **Validated — hard:** HiStrips (histrips.com) "Onyx Sleep Watch." **1,702 ad variations** in market, 20+ countries (US/GB/AU/CA/NZ-led). Store doing **$3.6M–$6.5M/mo**, traffic **55k→958k visits/mo in 9 months (17×)**, AOV ~$60, pro stack (Loox, Klaviyo, Intelligems, Triple Whale, Kaching bundles). Trustpilot 4.0, 98.75% reply rate.
- **Margin:** vibrating wristband ~$8–12 landed → sells inside a $40–60 offer. Strong.
- **Link:** histrips.com/products/onyx-sleep-watch
- **Watch-out:** #1 complaint cluster = "vibration too weak to wake heavy sleepers" — the product's whole promise. If I run this, I source a genuinely strong-motor unit and lead the guarantee on wake-strength. Also watch the subscription/shipping-protection complaints (don't copy the sneaky bits).

### 2. Lower-back / sciatica compression (ISG) belt
- **Pain (high, evergreen):** lower-back pain + sciatica = the #1 cause of disability worldwide; brutal, daily, emotional ("every step is a wince").
- **Validated:** Beschwerdelos "Orthogürtel" $39.99, **168 ad variations**, running **Sep 2025→Oct 2026 (13 mo)**, US/CH/AT/DE. Parent store Orthotal doing **$500k–$1M/mo**. Evergreen category — always some winner running it.
- **Margin:** neoprene/compression belt ~$5–8 → $40. Fat.
- **Link:** orthotal.com/products/orthogurtel-3
- **Watch-out:** EU/German-dominant (US only ~14% of that store's traffic) — English market is more open. Medical-claim discipline + returns.

### 3. Orthopedic cervical / neck-support pillow
- **Pain (high, double):** chronic neck + shoulder pain AND wrecked sleep — two pains in one. Huge, evergreen.
- **Validated:** Benessere Serenium $59.95 (145 ads, IT), Soya Paris $119.90 (**583 ads, 2024→2026, CH/BE/FR/LU**), Blackroll (**1,618 ads**). Multiple winners at once = durable demand.
- **Margin:** memory-foam pillow ~$8–15 → $60–120 (high AOV). Strong, but check shipping bulk/weight.
- **Watch-out:** heavier to ship than a flat item; size/firmness returns.
