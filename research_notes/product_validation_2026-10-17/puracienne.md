## VALIDATION FRAMEWORK

### 1) GAP FIRST — is anyone underserved?
The **core human "general adult parasite/detox"** angle is fully served and overcrowded (women ~35-65, health-anxious, US/UK/AU; identical Okinawa-grandma / fake-doctor advertorials). Genuine gaps:
- **PET parasite / gut cleanse (strongest gap).** Only Green Paw Wellness ($24-42k/mo) + Furbos (8 ads, launched today) seen — tiny vs 7,653 human ads. High emotional willingness-to-pay, repeat/subscription-friendly, and dogs/cats *actually* get worms (claim is defensible, lower compliance risk than human disease claims).
- **Compliant "bloating / gut-reset" reframe for an underserved demo** — the whole niche is feminine-coded; a **men's "gut reset / energy / bloat"** framing (or a specific diet community, e.g. sushi/raw-fish eaters, which the copy already hints at) is unoccupied — *if* claims drop the word "parasite".
- Not gaps: kids/family (compliance suicide), premium clinical (Microbe Formulas/CellCore own it).

### 2) IF NO GAP — beat incumbents on a small budget? (evidence per lever)
- **Ad volume:** ❌ impossible. Incumbents run 4,171 / 2,805 / 1,786 ads. A low-budget brand cannot out-spend; must win on *angle*, not reach.
- **Positioning:** ❌ the doctor-persona advertorial is done by ~15+ identical operators; no edge cloning it. A *differentiated* identity (pet, or clean compliant brand) is the only edge.
- **Funnel:** ⚠️ incumbents = advertorial → bundle → subscription. Matchable, but table-stakes, not an advantage.
- **Back-end economics:** ⚠️ this is where the war is won — Resilia's **$65 AOV comes from subscription + bundles**, not the front-end product. A newcomer with only a single $28 sale and no subscription will be out-bid on every impression.

### 3) MARGIN
- **Landed COGS (est):** herbal tincture dropper bottle $1.50-4 ea white-label; 2-bottle "kill+purge" set **$4-9 landed** (+$2-6 ship depending on 3PL vs China dropship).
- **Sell:** $28-40 single; bundles $60-130.
- **Gross margin:** **~78-88%** single, higher on bundles. Healthy, standard supplement economics.
- **BUT:** niche CPAs are high (**~$25-45 est**) from saturation + compliance-driven ad-account churn. Net contribution on a lone $28 sale is thin/negative; **profit depends entirely on bundle AOV + subscription LTV.**

### 4) COMPLIANCE — **HIGH RISK**
- Meta prohibits: implying personal health attributes ("do you have parasites?"), unverified supplement/disease claims, miracle-cure/detox claims, and impersonating medical professionals. This funnel does **all of it** (fake parasitologist, blames IBS/fatigue/brain-fog on parasites).
- Evidence it's already greyhat: burner **"Dr." pages created days before launch**, domain rotation (puracienne + sovarello), cloned templates → operators expect frequent **ad rejections + account bans**, requiring cloaking, spare BMs, and constant creative/page churn.
- **TikTok Shop:** supplement + disease/detox claims → rejected; parasite framing won't pass. Organic TikTok possible only with heavily softened claims.
- Net: sustained spend requires an agency-ad-account / cloaking infrastructure that a low-budget solo operator usually lacks.

### 5) VERDICT — **SKIP the clone; ENTER-WITH-ANGLE only via a differentiated sub-niche**
A me-too "parasite drops + fake-doctor advertorial" is a **SKIP** for a new low-budget brand: saturated, dominated by $100M-scale and 1,000+-ad operators, high CPAs, brutal Meta/TikTok compliance, and unwinnable without a subscription back-end and ban-resistant ad infrastructure.
The only defensible entry is **ENTER-WITH-ANGLE**: go **pet gut/parasite cleanse** (far less saturated, defensible claims, lower ban risk, natural subscription/repeat) **or** a **compliant human "gut-reset/bloating" brand for an underserved demo (men / specific diet community)** that drops the "parasite" disease framing. Either way, win on angle + brand + subscription back-end — never on ad volume.
