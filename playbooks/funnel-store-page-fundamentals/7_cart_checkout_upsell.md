**One line:** review, reassure, convert. Nikita's CRO notes name the two biggest mistakes here: "forcing onto a separate cart page" and "extra steps add friction/confusion."

## Fundamentals

Ordered by impact. Always on.

### 1. Add to Cart opens a drawer, not a page
- **Rule:** The Add to Cart tap slides a drawer over the page the buyer is on. Never redirect to a separate cart page. A single-offer funnel can skip the cart and go straight to checkout.
- **Why it works:** Every page load is a chance to leave, and a drawer keeps the product they just chose in view behind it. Nikita names "forcing onto a separate cart page" as the biggest cart mistake.
- **Weak:** Ryze, the only one of 16 to do it, redirects to a full /cart page. **Strong:** Grüns' drawer slides in already celebrating: "Congrats! You've unlocked FREE SHIPPING!"
- **Evidence:** 14 of 16 stores open a drawer (Rebuy on Hiya, Magic Spoon and Olipop, Upcart on Grüns, custom builds elsewhere). Miracle's lander doubles as its PDP, and its "PROCEED TO CHECKOUT" button skips the cart entirely.

### 2. Upsells live inside the drawer, never in front of it
- **Rule:** Nothing pops up between the Add to Cart tap and the drawer. Put the upgrade and the cross-sells inside the drawer and the checkout, where the buyer can ignore them and still move forward.
- **Why it works:** A modal at peak intent forces a decision before the buyer has seen their cart. An offer inside the drawer is a choice they can skip without breaking stride.
- **Weak:** An "Add a second one for $19?" modal that must be closed before the cart appears *(illustrative)*. **Strong:** Hiya's drawer shows "Customers Also Bought" under the line item, four items each with a one-tap "+ Add".
- **Evidence:** 0 of 16 stores run a blocking upsell interstitial. 13 of 14 drawers carry cross-sells. Nikita's "Pre-purchase upsell pop-up" is honored as the drawer's upgrade card (Zone 1, and the Blueprint table where the sources disagree).

### 3. The cart's numbers match the PDP, and discounts apply themselves
- **Rule:** Every compare-at price, saving, gift value, discount and review count in the drawer and checkout is identical to the PDP (→ Universal F3). Auto-apply every advertised discount and show the code or tag in the drawer.
- **Why it works:** The cart is the first place the buyer sees the real total. A number that moved, or a discount that vanished, reads as bait and switch at the exact moment they decide to pay.
- **Weak:** True Classic's PDP says "$34.99 $28, 20% OFF with FALLSALE \[Apply Code\]", and the cart still shows $34.99. **Strong:** MUD\\WTR shows "🏷 FALL43 (Save 43%)" already applied on the drawer line, so the PDP price is the cart price.
- **Evidence:** 8 stores auto-apply and say so ("50% Off Auto-Applied Today!" Hiya, "DISCOUNT AUTOMATICALLY APPLIED" Grüns). Grüns' PDP compare-at is $66.65 while its checkout's regular price is $49.99. HexClad's gift is "$179 Value" on the PDP and $139 struck in the cart. Mellow's funnel shows 2,400, 34,672 and 50,868 as its review count across advertorial, cart and PDP. Fixes in the Zone 2 warning.

### 4. The cart is a review: show what they chose and let them fix it there
- **Rule:** Each line names the variant, the quantity and the delivery cadence. Bundle contents are listed. Frequency, size or count can be changed inside the drawer.
- **Why it works:** Review is the first of Nikita's three cart jobs. A buyer who spots a mistake and can't fix it in place has to hit the back button, and the back button is an exit.
- **Weak:** A line reading "Starter Kit x1 $34" with no cadence, so the buyer can't tell whether they just subscribed *(illustrative)*. **Strong:** "First Order Discount / Delivered every month", with the number of kids editable right in the cart (Hiya).
- **Evidence:** AG1 heads the drawer "SUBSCRIPTION PURCHASES / Every 90 days". Magic Spoon puts a frequency dropdown on the line. Dr. Squatch shows "Show bundle items: Gameday Fresh x1…". Lume lists all four picks of its builder bundle on one line.

### 5. Reassure at both buttons: Checkout and Pay
- **Rule:** Put the named guarantee, the review count and the cancel terms in the drawer footer under the Checkout button, and again beside the pay button at checkout (→ Universal 3.3 for the wording).
- **Why it works:** Fear peaks twice, at the Checkout tap and at the Pay tap. A guarantee that disappears after the PDP feels like it stayed on the PDP.
- **Weak:** A drawer that ends at the subtotal, then a stock checkout with no guarantee anywhere on it *(illustrative)*. **Strong:** "4.8 stars, 100k+ Reviews | Pause or cancel anytime | 30-day money-back guarantee" under the drawer's "Secure Checkout 🔒" button (Grüns).
- **Evidence:** 12 of 16 restate the guarantee in the drawer or checkout. Miracle puts "TRY IT RISK FREE! - 100 DAY MONEY BACK GUARANTEE!" next to the pay button. Ollie's checkout near the pay button: "Success! 70% off your first box + FREE gift", "30 day money back guarantee", "Shipping is always free". Olipop's drawer: "45 Day Money-Back Guarantee".

### 6. The renewal is stated in dollars before they pay
- **Rule:** For any subscription, the checkout shows the recurring amount, the cadence and how to cancel, in plain words (→ Universal 3.3 for the hero version).
- **Why it works:** Hard-to-cancel and unexpected recurring charges are the most common complaint across subscription brands in the Trustpilot gap analysis (Hiya, MUD\\WTR, Dr. Squatch). A dollar figure at checkout is a refund and chargeback shield.
- **Weak:** Ollie's checkout itemizes the first box but never shows the renewal as a dollar figure, only "This order has a recurring charge" with auto-renew legal text. **Strong:** "Recurring subtotal $30.00 every month", then "cancel at any time, no questions asked - simply email… or text us" (Hiya checkout).
- **Evidence:** Magic Spoon "$31.20 every 30 days". Grüns "Recurring subtotal $49.99 every 4 weeks". MUD\\WTR's authorization spells out "must cancel with at least three (3) days' notice". Ryze puts "$36.00 Refill Ships in 30 Days" under its PDP button, so checkout holds no surprise.

### 7. One savings total, directly above the subtotal
- **Rule:** Add up every discount and gift value into one savings line, and put it directly above the subtotal in the drawer and the checkout.
- **Why it works:** The last look at the money decides the click. One big saving beside the price makes the total feel earned, and buyers never add up line items themselves.
- **Weak:** A subtotal alone, with the discount hidden inside each line price *(illustrative)*. **Strong:** "Products: $671.00 / Your savings: $272.00 / Subtotal: $399.00" (HexClad drawer).
- **Evidence:** Magic Spoon "SAVINGS -$30.80" in green above "SUBTOTAL (2 ITEMS) $31.20". Caraway "Before Savings $675" above "Subtotal $445". Ollie "TOTAL SAVINGS $67.96". Counting gift values into the saving only works when those values match the PDP (F3).

### 8. The checkout is a page you design, not a default
- **Rule:** Add a trust header (review count, guarantee, shipping) and a sidebar with the guarantee, one stat and 2 to 3 short testimonials. Put express pay first.
- **Why it works:** Most brands leave the checkout blank, so the proof that sold the product vanishes right before payment. The leaders carry it to the last screen.
- **Weak:** Our Place's checkout is stock Shopify: express pay, a long marketing opt-in and no trust content. **Strong:** A header reading "⭐150K+ 5 Star Reviews" and "FREE Shipping | 30-Day Guarantee | Secure Checkout", with trust blocks below: "Excellent 150,000+ Reviews / Over 1M Shipped Products / Free shipping within the U.S. / 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee… we'll make it right!" (Ryze).
- **Evidence:** Custom headers on 4 checkouts (Ryze, MUD\\WTR, HexClad, Hiya), sidebar proof on 5 (Hiya, Grüns, Dr. Squatch, True Classic, Ryze). Grüns' sidebar: "Less than 1% of customers use our Money-Back Guarantee / 4.8 stars from 100,000+ reviews / 1 Million+ Members and more than 2 Billion Gummies sold / Clinically and 3rd Party Tested". Express pay sits at the top of every Shopify checkout in the set.

### 9. Every paid add-on is opt-in, and every exit is easy to see
- **Rule:** Never pre-tick package protection, warranties, memberships or bumps. Show each one unticked, with its price and what it covers. Keep "no thanks" links readable.
- **Why it works:** A charge the buyer didn't choose becomes a "why was I charged" email, a refund or a chargeback. A new store has no order history to absorb those, and payment providers watch dispute rates.
- **Weak:** "🔒 PROTECTED CHECKOUT" as the main button, "Package Protection & Sustainability $2.24, Included in Protected Checkout" in the drawer, and the escape as a small underlined link: "Checkout without package protection" (True Classic). **Strong:** "☐ Add package protection for $2.24. Covers loss, theft and damage in transit." *(illustrative)*
- **Evidence:** Opt-out protection on True Classic and Caraway ("Checkout + Caraway Package Protection $8.50"). Magic Spoon's Redo toggle is on by default: "By leaving the toggle selected…". The full judgement call is in Zone 7.

### 10. Urgency in the cart is true, or it's gone
- **Rule:** No reservation timers, no resetting countdowns, no rolling daily deadlines (→ Universal 3.4). Use urgency you can prove: a real delivery date, real stock, a first-order-only offer.
- **Why it works:** The buyer is already in. A fake clock at checkout reads as pressure exactly when they're looking for a reason to trust you. Nikita: fake urgency "looks desperate, kills credibility."
- **Weak:** "This popular product is in high demand ⭐ We will reserve your order for 09:52 minutes." (Hiya checkout). **Strong:** "Expected delivery 10/08 - 10/13" in green inside the drawer (True Classic).
- **Evidence:** Reservation timers on Hiya, Caraway and Miracle, and a resetting countdown on Ryze. Full list in Zone 7, "Seen, don't copy."

### 11. Every upsell passes the same-problem test
- **Rule:** Offer only what (1) solves the same problem better, faster or easier, or (2) solves a new problem the first purchase creates (Nikita). The upsell may cost more than the main product.
- **Why it works:** An upsell that continues the buyer's goal feels like help. One that changes the subject feels like a pitch, and it makes them doubt the first purchase too.
- **Weak:** Ridge's checkout offers a "Complete Charging Kit: Wall Charger Set" to someone buying a wallet. **Strong:** A buyer adds a cast-iron skillet, and the offer is the seasoning kit that stops it rusting, the new problem the skillet creates *(illustrative)*.
- **Evidence:** Nikita's Chad funnel notes: "solve the same problem the main product solves, but better/faster/easier" or "solve a NEW problem created after the first was solved (e.g. weight-loss buyer still has a bad diet → upsell fixes the diet)." And: "Upsells can be MORE expensive than the main product." MUD\\WTR's drawer upgrade sells the same product at a lower daily cost ("$0.89/day" against the cart's "$1.13/day").

### 12. After payment, kill the anxiety, then sell
- **Rule:** The first thing after payment confirms the order and answers "what happens now." Only then show one offer (Nikita).
- **Why it works:** Doubt peaks right after the card is charged. A buyer who feels safe says yes to a second offer. A buyer who feels ambushed asks for a refund on the first.
- **Weak:** A thank-you page that opens with third-party offers before the order number *(illustrative)*. **Strong:** "You're all set. Order #\[X\] ships \[day\]. Here's exactly what happens next." Then one offer for the problem they just bought for *(illustrative)*.
- **Evidence:** Nikita: "make them feel good about the purchase, kill their anxiety, THEN work toward selling the next product." AfterSell is installed on 8 of the 16 stores walked. Rokt's third-party thank-you offers on Dr. Squatch show up in Trustpilot complaints about unexpected charges. Not observed live (Zone 10).

## Techniques

Pick per goal. Lines marked → Universal are owned by the Universal page. Only the cart and checkout placement is given here.

### Zone 1: Add-to-cart behaviour

- **Slide-out drawer.** The default for every store with a cart (F1).
	- Swipe: Hiya's Rebuy drawer slides in with "50% OFF SITEWIDE + FREE Kids Daily Greens with $45+!" across the top (Hiya)
	- **Good for →** every Shopify store, any catalog size.
	- **When to use →** always, unless the funnel sells one offer from one page.
- **Straight to checkout.** The page's button skips the cart.
	- Swipe: "PROCEED TO CHECKOUT", with "Ships by Thu, Oct 8th / Limited Inventory at this price remaining / FREE Shipping & Returns / 100 DAY money-back guarantee" under it (Miracle)
	- **Good for →** single-offer funnels where the lander is the PDP.
	- **When to use →** when every choice (size, quantity, add-on) is already made on the page. The bump then moves to the checkout (Zone 9). Drop "Limited Inventory" unless it is true.
- **Pre-purchase upsell as a drawer card.** Nikita's "Pre-purchase upsell pop-up," built as the first card inside the drawer instead of a modal in front of it.
	- Swipe: "Save $65, 90 Serving Supply, Shipped every 3 months, $0.89/day $145 $80 \[Upgrade\]" on a black card under the line item (MUD\\WTR)
	- **Good for →** stores with a natural bigger pack or kit.
	- **When to use →** always, over a blocking modal. If you want to try a modal, test it against the card with a testing tool (→ Universal 3.8).
- **Cart-state test before traffic.** Make sure the cart survives a reload, a deep link and a slow tap.
	- Swipe (the failure): Hiya's cart lived in Rebuy's script, so /cart and /checkout showed an empty cart while the drawer held items. Lume's drawer Checkout button would not navigate in the test browser.
	- **Good for →** any store running a drawer app or a headless front end.
	- **When to use →** before launch and after every theme or app update. Test on a real phone.

**Seen, don't copy: the forced cart page.** Ryze sends the buyer to /cart. It is the move Nikita calls the biggest cart mistake. Use the drawer.

### Zone 2: Drawer anatomy

Build the drawer top to bottom in this order. The gift sits above the main line, so the buyer reads the reward before the cost.

1. **Offer band.** The live offer restated in one line.
2. **Meter** (Zone 3).
3. **Gift lines** at $0.00 against a struck value (Zone 4).
4. **The order:** variant, quantity and cadence, editable (F4).
5. **One upgrade card:** a bigger pack or the subscription (Zone 5).
6. **2 to 4 cross-sells** under a header that gives a reason (Zone 6).
7. **Savings total, then subtotal** (F7).
8. **Full-width checkout button,** with express pay beside it if you test it.
9. **Trust footer:** guarantee, review count, cancel terms (F5).

- **Offer band at the top.** One line that confirms the deal they came for is live.
	- Swipe: "Limited Time Discount Auto-Applied" (Grüns) / "YOU'RE SAVING $30 WITH BULK DISCOUNT" (AG1)
	- **Good for →** any store running an offer.
	- **When to use →** always, worded exactly like the ad and the PDP.
- **Cadence on the line item.** The line says how often it ships and what the next one costs.
	- Swipe: "$40 refill ships every 4 weeks w/sub" (MUD\\WTR) / "Ships Every 30 Days $27.00 / $45" (Ryze) / a "SAVING W/ MONTHLY" tag on the line (Grüns)
	- **Good for →** subscriptions.
	- **When to use →** on every subscription line.
- **Checkout button named for safety.** Full width, the strongest color in the drawer, with a lock.
	- Swipe: "Secure Checkout 🔒" (Grüns) / "🔒 Secure Checkout" (Bloom) / "CONTINUE TO CHECKOUT" (Our Place)
	- **Good for →** every drawer.
	- **When to use →** always.
- **Express pay in the drawer.** A Shop Pay button beside Checkout skips the form for returning Shop users.
	- Swipe: a Shop Pay button beside Checkout (MUD\\WTR, the only one of 16 to put it in the drawer)
	- **Good for →** mobile-heavy traffic.
	- **When to use →** as a test. It can pull taps away from the drawer's own offers.
- **Collapsed promo field.** Hide the code box behind a link so an open field doesn't send buyers off to hunt for codes.
	- Swipe: "Have a promo code?" collapsed (AG1) / "Add Promo Code +" (Olipop)
	- **Good for →** stores whose discount is auto-applied.
	- **When to use →** always, paired with an auto-applied offer (F3).
- **Clean drawer.** No meter, no cross-sells. Just the order, the gifts and the saving.
	- Swipe: "SUBSCRIPTION PURCHASES / Every 90 days", "FREE GIFTS: AG1 Pro Welcome Kit, Yeti Shaker, Free", "YOU'RE SAVING $30 WITH BULK DISCOUNT", then Checkout (AG1)
	- **Good for →** premium-restraint brands and single-hero-product stores (→ Universal 1.13).
	- **When to use →** when the PDP already sold the bigger plan and an extra offer would cheapen it.
- **Gift note.** Let the buyer mark the order as a gift in the drawer.
	- Swipe: "Add gift note" (Our Place) / a "Make this a gift" checkbox (Caraway)
	- **Good for →** giftable products, Q4.
	- **When to use →** whenever a real share of orders are gifts.
- **Points earned on this order.** → Universal 3.1 (instant reward). In the drawer: "Earn 150 Lume Points, Log In or Sign Up" (Lume). At checkout: "Earn and redeem Bloom Nutrition rewards ⚡ powered by tyb" (Bloom).
- **Cause picker that costs the buyer nothing.** → Universal 3.8 (cause micro-trust). In the drawer and again at checkout: "Choose your impact: At no extra cost, select a nonprofit and 1% of your purchase will be donated" (Olipop).

**Warning: anchor mismatches at the cart.** These surface at the most trust-critical moment in the funnel.

### Zone 3: Progress meters

→ Universal 3.1 owns the spend-threshold meter. In the drawer:

- **Endowed-progress start.** The meter opens with the first milestone already done, so the next one feels like finishing something.
	- Swipe: "✓ Free Shipping" already filled, then "$30.00 away from your free gift" (Hiya) / "You're $30 Away From a Free Mini Deo!" with "Unlocked: Free Shipping" (Lume)
	- **Good for →** stores whose typical order already clears the shipping line.
	- **When to use →** set milestone one at or below the typical order and milestone two about one item above it.
- **Two-milestone meter.** Shipping first, then a gift or a percent off.
	- Swipe: "Spend $430 more to unlock 20% off!" with "$90 Free Shipping ✓" and "$875 20% Off" nodes (Caraway) / "Free Shipping" and "Full-Size Colostrum" nodes (Bloom) / "Free Shipping $15 / Free Mini $35 / Free Body Wash $55" (Native)
	- **Good for →** catalogs with add-ons priced to close the gap.
	- **When to use →** when you have a cheap, wanted gift for milestone two.
- **"Or subscribe" alternate path.** The meter offers subscribing as a second way to finish it.
	- Swipe: "Spend $14.01 or subscribe to unlock FREE shipping" (Olipop)
	- **Good for →** stores where free shipping is a subscriber perk.
	- **When to use →** whenever one-time buyers pay shipping. The line stays literally true.
- **Meter carried into checkout.** The gap follows the buyer to the payment page.
	- Swipe: "YOU'RE $16.01 AWAY FROM FREE SHIPPING!" (Bloom checkout)
	- **Good for →** low-order-value consumables.
	- **When to use →** when a cheap add-on in checkout can close the gap.
- **Dollar gap, not a percent.** Write the exact amount left.
	- Swipe: "Add $89.01 more to get Free Shipping" (True Classic) / "Add $34.00 to Unlock Free Shipping" (Dr. Squatch)
	- **Good for →** every meter.
	- **When to use →** always. Put a cross-sell priced near the gap right under it.
- **Threshold at the hero price.** Set free shipping at the price of the main product, so every hero buyer opens the drawer to a win.
	- Swipe: "Free shipping $135+ and free returns to 94104-4901" on a $135 pan (the ZIP filled from the visitor's location), then "Congrats! You get free standard shipping" in the drawer (Our Place)
	- **Good for →** single-hero stores with cheap accessories.
	- **When to use →** when the hero price can carry the shipping cost. Otherwise set the threshold just above your median order (seen: $25 to $135, median about $55).
- **No meter when shipping is free.** Skip the bar and say it in the footer.
	- Swipe: "Free Shipping, Free Returns, Lifetime Warranty" (Ridge drawer footer)
	- **Good for →** higher-ticket stores.
	- **When to use →** when free shipping is unconditional.

### Zone 4: Gifts in the cart

→ Universal 3.1 owns gift stacking, gift values and choose-your-gift. In the drawer:

- **Gift as the first line.** The free item sits above the product at $0.00 against a struck value.
	- Swipe: "PSL Bag, Free gift, $25 FREE" as the top line (MUD\\WTR) / "Free Bowl & Spoon Set - Subscriber Gift $0.00 $23.00" (Magic Spoon) / "You got this FREE, HYBRID DEEP SAUTÉ PAN $0.00 $139.00" (HexClad)
	- **Good for →** any store with a gift with purchase.
	- **When to use →** always. The reward is read before the cost.
- **Auto-add the gift.** The gift lands in the cart by itself once the condition is met.
	- Swipe: Magic Spoon's subscriber gift line is added automatically. Bloom runs the "EG Auto Add to Cart Free Gift" app for its threshold gift.
	- **Good for →** every gift.
	- **When to use →** by default. A gift the buyer must tick ("BONUS ☐ GET 3x FREE TOWELS", Miracle) is a gift some buyers will miss.
- **Gift banner for kits.** One line names every free item.
	- Swipe: "FREE GIFTS: AG1 Pro Welcome Kit, Yeti Shaker, Free" (AG1)
	- **Good for →** welcome kits and multi-gift offers.
	- **When to use →** when there are two or more gifts.
- **Threshold gift as the meter's goal.** The gift is milestone two, announced in the top bar first.
	- Swipe: "FREE Full-Size Colostrum on $85+ ($30 Value)" in the top bar, then the "Full-Size Colostrum" node in the drawer meter (Bloom) / "GET FREE Kids Daily Greens with orders $45+!" (Hiya)
	- **Good for →** raising order value without a discount.
	- **When to use →** set the threshold about one item above the median order.
- **Gift value inside the saving.** Count the gift into the struck total and the savings line.
	- Swipe: "$289.00 → $267.00" at checkout, with the $22 gift counted into "Total Savings $22.00" (AG1) / "$85 $34" as the drawer subtotal (MUD\\WTR)
	- **Good for →** making a subscription feel generous.
	- **When to use →** only when the gift's value is real and identical on the PDP (Zone 2 warning).
- **Pick-your-gift step.** → Universal 3.1. In the cart flow: a builder step before the drawer, with the prompt changing as they finish ("Choose 2 deodorants!" then "Choose a free item!", Lume). Choosing makes the gift theirs, which raises the cost of leaving.

### Zone 5: Upgrade, trade-up and subscription in the cart

- **Trade-up card that replaces the line.** "Upgrade" or "Swap" exchanges the item for a bigger pack instead of adding a second item. Show the new per-day cost against the current one, as MUD\\WTR's card does (Zone 1).
	- Swipe: "Mushroom Coffee Starter Kit (60 servings) $49.50 $80, 38% OFF LIMITED TIME OFFER, \[UPGRADE\]" (Ryze cart page)
	- **Good for →** consumables with 60 or 90-day packs, sets with a bigger tier.
	- **When to use →** one card, directly under the line item.
- **Swap again in checkout.** A second, sweeter chance at the trade-up, above express pay.
	- Swipe: "Upgrade today for FREE GIFTS and $50 in Savings! RYZE Ritual Set $57.60 $115.00 \[Swap\]" (Ryze checkout)
	- **Good for →** stores that offered the trade-up in the cart.
	- **When to use →** as the one checkout offer (Zone 7), when the bigger tier adds gifts the cart card didn't show.
- **Upgrade to subscription in the cart.** For stores that default to one-time on the PDP, a button on the line converts it.
	- Swipe: "Upgrade to Subscription & Save 15%" as a full-width button on the line (Olipop) / "Subscribe and save 15% →" with a "Why subscribe? ▾" accordion (Bloom) / "☐ Upgrade to Subscription SAVE 14.24%, Free shipping on all U.S. subscription orders!" (Lume)
	- **Good for →** consumables that default to one-time.
	- **When to use →** 3 of the 4 one-time-default consumables in the set do it. Use "Upgrade" or "Save," not a bare "Subscribe" (→ ② PDP, Zone 5: status word).
- **Paid membership card.** A low-cost membership offered as a button, with its benefit in dollars and the annual charge stated.
	- Swipe: "Extra 10% OFF when you become an Insider, Just $1/mo - Billed at $12/yr \[Add Membership Now\] See all benefits ▼" (True Classic)
	- **Good for →** apparel and consumables with frequent repeat buyers.
	- **When to use →** only as an opt-in button with the full charge stated, the way True Classic shows it (F9).

### Zone 6: Cross-sells

- **2 to 4 one-tap adds, below the line.** Carousel or two-up tiles, a "+ Add" button, a struck compare price.
	- Swipe: "Picked just for you" carousel with "+ Add" (Bloom) / "Frequently Bought Together" with "ADD FOR $139" (HexClad)
	- **Good for →** every catalog with natural companions.
	- **When to use →** always below the main line, never above it. Past 4 the drawer turns into a store (Ridge shows 8 more under "YOU MAY ALSO LIKE").
- **A reason in the header.** The section title says why these belong with this order.
	- Swipe: "Complete Your Fit" (True Classic) / "Keep the Good Smells Rollin'" (Dr. Squatch) / "COMPLETE YOUR ROUTINE" (Lume) / "Complete your kitchen:" (Caraway) / "MORE FOR YOUR BOWL" (Magic Spoon)
	- **Good for →** routines and systems.
	- **When to use →** always. "You may also like" gives no reason.
- **Bundle price for adding.** The add-on costs less because it joins this order. Match its color or variant to what's in the cart.
	- Swipe: "Ridge KeyCase Gunmetal (color-matched) SAVE 60% $75 $30 \[ADD & SAVE\]" under "BUNDLE & SAVE" (Ridge) / "Pairs Well With: Ceramic Nonstick Always Pan 12.5" 17% off when bundling cart items $109.95 $159" (Our Place)
	- **Good for →** higher-ticket stores.
	- **When to use →** when the discount is real and only applies in the bundle.
- **Second-user cross-sell with a data reason.** Sell the same goal to someone in the buyer's household, backed by your own numbers.
	- Swipe: "Grüns for Kids – Stay on Track Together. Kids are 90% more likely to stick with Grüns when you do too.\* … \*Based on actual customer data" (Grüns)
	- **Good for →** family and household products.
	- **When to use →** only with a real number from your own data, footnoted.
- **Restock what they picked.**
	- Swipe: "Stock up on your favorite flavors" (Olipop)
	- **Good for →** consumables with flavors or scents.
	- **When to use →** when the buyer already knows they like it.
- **Scarcity and newness prefixes, true only.** A one-word tag in front of the cross-sell name.
	- Swipe: "Last few boxes! Cookies & Cream Treats", "Try! Apple Cinnamon Granola", "Viral! Parrot Bowl and Spoon Set $15 $23" (Magic Spoon) / "Going Fast: Non-Toxic Best Seller" (Caraway)
	- **Good for →** catalogs with real drops and real low stock.
	- **When to use →** "Last few" only when stock is actually low (→ Universal 3.4). "Try!" and "New" are always safe.

### Zone 7: Checkout page

What you can add to the checkout depends on your Shopify plan and apps (AfterSell-type extensions handle offers). Check before you design.

- **Trust header strip.** Review count, guarantee and shipping across the top of the checkout (F8).
	- Swipe: "★★★★★ Rated 4.9/5 (100,000+ Reviews)" over "FREE SHIPPING / 30 DAY MONEY BACK GUARANTEE / LIFETIME WARRANTY" (HexClad) / avatars, "1 Million+ Happy Customers", then "Secure Checkout / Fast Shipping / USA Mushrooms" (MUD\\WTR) / "Free Shipping with Every Order / Delivered Fresh Monthly / Zero Commitment" (Hiya icon row)
	- **Good for →** every checkout.
	- **When to use →** always. Only claims true for every option in the cart. "Free Shipping with Every Order" works for Hiya because every order is a subscription.
- **Sidebar proof stack.** The guarantee, one stat and 2 to 3 short testimonials beside the order summary. Proof policy → Universal 3.2.
	- Swipe: testimonial cards from a "Mother of 1" and a "Father of 2" (Hiya) / "Trusted by our customers" with 3 short verified-buyer quotes (True Classic) / "200K+ 5☆ reviews, 10M+ squatchers" (Dr. Squatch)
	- **Good for →** every checkout.
	- **When to use →** always. Cast the quotes to the last fears: arrival, cancelling, "did it work."
- **Guarantee in its own words beside the summary.**
	- Swipe: "Our Guarantee: If you don't love AG1, we'll refund your order anytime within the first 90 days." (AG1 sidebar) / "30-Day Sudisfaction Guarantee… we will make sure to find you a scent you love!" (Dr. Squatch)
	- **Good for →** every checkout.
	- **When to use →** always, with the same length and name as the PDP.
- **Express pay first, one page if you can.** Express buttons above the form. Fewer steps beat more (Nikita: "extra steps add friction/confusion").
	- Swipe: Shop, PayPal and G Pay (Hiya, one-page checkout) / Shop, PayPal, Amazon Pay and G Pay (HexClad, 3-step)
	- **Good for →** every checkout.
	- **When to use →** always on top.
- **What happens next, as a timeline.** First shipment, then the cadence, then the cancel terms, in the order summary.
	- Swipe: "Your pup's plan starts here. Based on your pup's profile, we'll deliver fresh meals every 4 weeks." then "Ships in 3-5 business days" for the Starter Box and "Adjust, pause, or cancel anytime. Ships every 4 weeks." for the subscription (Ollie)
	- **Good for →** subscriptions and anything that ships later.
	- **When to use →** always for subscriptions. Add the renewal dollar figure Ollie leaves out (F6).
- **SMS opt-in with a concrete reason.** Unticked, with what they get for ticking it.
	- Swipe: "Sign up for texts and get a FREE BRICC on your next order" (Dr. Squatch) / "Get 10% Off Your Next Order" (Miracle) / "Want First Dibs on Limited-Edition Flavors? Sign Up for Texts" (Grüns) / "Text me when my AG1 ships and about exclusive health and science content." (AG1)
	- **Good for →** every store running SMS.
	- **When to use →** always. Weak versions in the same set: "Get the latest discounts and offers sent directly to your phone ✨" (True Classic) and the field copy "Click this! Free products, swag, and insider tips." (Grüns). Capture rules → Universal 3.6.
- **BNPL, HSA/FSA and group discounts at checkout.** → Universal 3.1. At checkout: Cash App Pay, Afterpay and Klarna (True Classic), Sezzle (Bloom), Klarna (Our Place), "Now Eligible for HSA/FSA Reimbursement through Truemed" (MUD\\WTR), "Discount available for Military & First Responders" (Ridge). Only where you actually qualify.
- **One in-checkout offer, matched to the order.** A small add-on or a swap, placed above express pay.
	- Swipe: "Try Treats! Double Choco $9.75" and "Try Protein Granola! $9.75" (Magic Spoon) / Energy Sticks $10.49 and Creatine $19.99 (Bloom)
	- **Good for →** consumables with cheap companions.
	- **When to use →** one offer, priced well below the order. Seen on 5 of 15 checkouts. It must pass the same-problem test (F11).
- **Faster shipping as a paid choice.** Free shipping stays, a faster paid option sits beside it.
	- Swipe: "FREE shipping: Est 4-8 business days" or "Premium Plus: Get it faster! Est 3-5 business days $2.99" (Miracle)
	- **Good for →** stores with slow free shipping.
	- **When to use →** keep free as the default option.
- **A way back that keeps the cart.**
	- Swipe: the checkout nav reads "Edit Order" (AG1)
	- **Good for →** subscriptions and bundles.
	- **When to use →** always. A buyer who wants to change a flavor shouldn't have to rebuild the cart.

**Judgement call: package protection and pre-checked opt-ins.** Widely used by big brands. Recorded as observed:

<tr><td>What</td><td>Who</td><td>How it's set</td></tr>
<tr><td>Package protection</td><td>True Classic, Caraway</td><td>Opt-out. Folded into the checkout button ("🔒 PROTECTED CHECKOUT", "Checkout + Caraway Package Protection $8.50"), with the escape as an underlined link: "Checkout without package protection"</td></tr>

What to do on a new store: make every paid add-on opt-in. Unticked, priced, one line on what it covers. A pre-ticked paid add-on is a chargeback and complaint risk for a store with no order history, and dispute rates are what payment providers watch. For email, leave the box unticked and give a reason, the way Dr. Squatch does for SMS. Pre-ticked consent isn't valid consent in the EU and UK, and it fills the list with people who never asked. Everyone who reaches checkout gives an email anyway, so abandoned carts still get recovered.

**Seen, don't copy: fake urgency at checkout.** Nikita's rule is true urgency only (→ Universal 3.4). None of these go on your store:
- Reservation timers: "Items in your cart are in high demand. We've reserved your cart for 14:54" (Caraway), "DON'T MISS OUT! Your cart is reserved for: 14:50" (Miracle), and Hiya's 10-minute version (F10).
- An evergreen countdown: Ryze's sale timer starts at about 09:50 and shows 09:50 again to every new visitor.
- A rolling deadline: "⏰ Order by Oct. 6 for 55% Off!" on the day of the visit (Grüns).
- A countdown on the popup itself: "0:52" (Bloom).

Use instead: a real delivery date ("Expected delivery 10/08 - 10/13," True Classic), real stock, a first-order-only offer.

### Zone 8: Order page (long-form funnels)

In the Chad funnel (→ Universal 4.3) the sales page hands off to an order page, then the OTO and the downsell. Nikita's rule for it: "as little friction as possible, as clear as possible." On a store, the checkout is the order page. On a funnel builder, you build it.

- **Recap of everything they get, above the form.** Every item, every gift and the total saving, in the sales page's exact words.
	- Swipe: Ollie lists the gift ("🎉 Free gift with purchase" FREE), the Welcome Kit (FREE), the subscription (FREE), the box contents ("4 Fresh Pork, 4 Fresh Beef, 3 Fresh Turkey") and "TOTAL SAVINGS $67.96" (Ollie)
	- **Good for →** long-form and quiz funnels.
	- **When to use →** always. Same offer name, price and gift values as the sales page.
- **Contact fields first.** Email and phone at the top, so a buyer who stops at payment is still a lead.
	- Swipe: "add capture on the SP and order page" (Nikita) / the checkout email field "Used for your order confirmation and cart reminders" (Ollie)
	- **Good for →** expensive cold traffic.
	- **When to use →** every order page. → Universal 3.6.
- **No exits.** No nav, no "continue shopping," no links out except policies.
	- Swipe: "ORDER PAGE: as little friction as possible, as clear as possible." (Nikita)
	- **Good for →** order pages.
	- **When to use →** always.
- **Guarantee beside the pay button.** → F5. On the order page the guarantee sits directly beside the button, not below the fold.
- **Local currency.** → Universal 3.8. Nikita: an "app that auto-detects currency + language." On the order page, the price shown is the price charged, in the buyer's currency.
- **A funnel-builder checkout.** Some long-form funnels run their own checkout instead of the store's.
	- Swipe: Miracle's funnel checkout ("Powered by RTC") carries a warranty bump, a paid shipping upgrade, an SMS box and the guarantee on one page (Miracle)
	- **Good for →** long-form funnels built on a funnel tool.
	- **When to use →** when your funnel tool owns the order page. On Shopify, AfterSell-type apps add the bump and the post-purchase offer instead (Zone 9).

### Zone 9: Order bump, OTO, upsell and downsell

The rules are F11 (same-problem test, may cost more) and F12 (calm first). What passes the test, across niches:

<table header-row="true">
<tr><td>Main product</td><td>Same problem, better, faster or easier</td><td>New problem the first purchase creates</td></tr>
<tr><td>Weight-loss program (Nikita's example)</td><td>A faster or fuller version of the program</td><td>The diet: "weight-loss buyer still has a bad diet → upsell fixes the diet"</td></tr>
<tr><td>30-day supply of a daily product *(illustrative)*</td><td>The 90-day supply at a lower per-day price</td><td>A travel format, because they now take it everywhere</td></tr>
<tr><td>Cast-iron skillet *(illustrative)*</td><td>A second size</td><td>The seasoning kit that stops rust</td></tr>
<tr><td>Teeth-whitening kit *(illustrative)*</td><td>An LED booster that works faster</td><td>A gel for the sensitivity whitening can cause</td></tr>
<tr><td>Running shoes *(illustrative)*</td><td>A second pair to rotate</td><td>Blister-proof socks for the longer runs they now do</td></tr>
</table>

- **Order bump.** One unticked line before payment, at the same discount, priced well under the main order.
	- Swipe: "REDEEM: Yes, Add 2-Year Wear & Tear Warranty For Only $19.99" (Miracle checkout) / "Would Biscuit like a treat? Choose up to 3", each health chew at 70% off with "+ Add to your plan" and a Skip button (Ollie)
	- **Good for →** funnels and quiz flows.
	- **When to use →** one bump on the order page, unticked (F9). In a quiz funnel, placement → ⑥ Quiz Funnel, Zone 8.
- **OTO page after payment.** One offer, one click, no card re-entry. Written as a short VSL or TSL (Nikita).
	- Swipe: "THE OTO PAGE: 1) VSL (video sales letter), 2) TSL (text sales letter)." (Nikita's notes)
	- **Good for →** any store with a natural second product.
	- **When to use →** right after payment. AfterSell-type apps run it on Shopify (installed on 8 of 16 stores walked). Copy craft for the VSL or TSL → Copywriting doc.
- **OTO that opens with the confirmation.** Line one confirms the order. Line two opens the offer. This is Nikita's "kill their anxiety, THEN" on one screen.
	- Swipe: "Your order is confirmed and ships \[day\]. Before it goes out: most people who \[did X\] run into \[new problem\] next." *(illustrative)*
	- **Good for →** every OTO.
	- **When to use →** always.
- **Bigger, pricier OTO.** Don't cap the upsell below the main price. The card is already in, so the post-purchase page is where the bigger offer goes. The cart and checkout carry the small adds.
	- Swipe: "Upsells can be MORE expensive than the main product." (Nikita)
	- **Good for →** high-margin second products and longer plans.
	- **When to use →** when the offer solves a bigger version of the same problem.
- **Downsell.** A cheaper yes for whoever says no: a smaller size, fewer units, a half portion or a payment plan.
	- Swipe: "Half Fresh Plan. From ~~$30.87~~ $9.26/week. Not ready to go all in? Try a half-serving of fresh instead!" (Ollie)
	- **Good for →** expensive OTOs and subscriptions.
	- **When to use →** once, straight after a declined OTO. Ollie also shows it on the reveal, before any objection (→ ⑥ Quiz Funnel, Zone 8).
- **A visible "no thanks."** → Universal 3.6 (decline copy). On the OTO: the decline link is readable and goes straight to the thank-you page. A hidden decline turns into an accidental purchase and a refund request.
- **One OTO, one downsell to start.** Nikita's Chad funnel runs one of each. Add a second OTO only after the first pair makes money without raising refunds.
	- Swipe: "AD → ADVERTORIAL → SALES PAGE → ORDER PAGE → ONE-TIME OFFERS (upsell) → DOWNSELL." (Nikita)
	- **Good for →** every funnel's first build.
	- **When to use →** until the numbers say the buyer wants more.

### Zone 10: Post-purchase and thank-you page

**Not observed live.** The browser walk stopped at the first checkout screen, so no store's thank-you page or post-purchase offer was seen. This zone is built from Nikita's notes and the app evidence: AfterSell (checkout and post-purchase offers) on 8 of 16 stores walked, Rokt detected on Hiya, MUD\\WTR, Grüns and True Classic and listed on Dr. Squatch, and Fairing post-purchase surveys on Dr. Squatch. Treat the order below as a build plan to test.

- **Confirmation first.** Order number, what ships when, how to change or cancel, how to reach a person.
	- Swipe: "You're all set. \[Product\] ships \[day\]. Need to change anything? Reply to your confirmation email and a real person will help." *(illustrative)*
	- **Good for →** every store.
	- **When to use →** at the top of the thank-you page, before any offer (F12).
- **Reassure the decision.** One line on what they'll notice and when (→ Universal F14).
	- Swipe: "Most people notice \[first sign\] by \[day X\]. Here's how to get there faster." *(illustrative)*
	- **Good for →** anything that works over time.
	- **When to use →** right under the confirmation.
- **Then one offer of your own.** A one-click post-purchase offer that passes the same-problem test (F11, Zone 9).
	- Swipe: "Add \[companion\] to this order for \[price\]. Ships in the same box." *(illustrative)*
	- **Good for →** any store with a natural second product.
	- **When to use →** after the confirmation, never before it.
- **Post-purchase survey.** One question on how they found you.
	- Swipe: Fairing's "how'd you hear about us" survey on the thank-you page (Dr. Squatch)
	- **Good for →** every store running paid ads. It patches attribution the pixel misses.
	- **When to use →** after the offer, as the last thing on the page.
- **Third-party thank-you offers: use with care.** Rokt fills the thank-you page with other brands' offers.
	- Swipe (the risk): on Dr. Squatch, and flagged on True Classic too, Trustpilot complaints describe third-party post-purchase offers causing unexpected charges.
	- **Good for →** big brands squeezing margin from huge order volume.
	- **When to use →** not on a new store. The complaints land on your brand, not on the third party.
- **Teach correct use in email 1.** → Universal 3.3 (FAQ that teaches correct use). The first email after purchase prevents the bad review before the first use.
- **Next-order hook.** → Universal 3.1 (second-purchase discount). On the thank-you page and in email 1, name the next-order reward ("You've Got $50 Off Your Second Purchase\*," Caraway, or Dr. Squatch's "FREE BRICC on your next order" for texts).

## Blueprint

### Before you build
1. **Number sheet rows for the cart:** compare-at price, discount, gift values, meter thresholds, renewal price and cadence, guarantee length, review count (→ Universal F3).
2. **Threshold:** just above the median order, or at the hero price.
3. **Gift:** what it is, its value (the same everywhere) and how it's added (auto).
4. **Offers:** one upgrade card, 2 to 4 cross-sells, one checkout offer, one bump (funnels), one OTO, one downsell. Every one passes the same-problem test.
5. **Apps (→ Universal 3.8):** a drawer (Rebuy or Upcart), checkout and post-purchase offers (AfterSell), subscriptions (Recharge, Ordergroove or Skio), a testing tool (Intelligems ran on 9 of 16), a post-purchase survey (Fairing). Protection apps (Redo, Corso, Extend) only as opt-in.

### Drawer, top to bottom
1. Offer band, worded like the ad.
2. Meter: first milestone done, dollar gap to the next, "or subscribe" if shipping is a subscriber perk.
3. Gift lines at $0.00 against the PDP's struck value.
4. The order: variant, quantity, cadence, editable.
5. One upgrade card showing the per-day drop, or the subscription upgrade.
6. 2 to 4 cross-sells under a header with a reason.
7. Savings total, then subtotal.
8. "Secure Checkout" button, full width.
9. Footer: guarantee, review count, cancel terms.

### Checkout, top to bottom
1. Trust header: review count, guarantee, shipping.
2. One offer (a swap or a small add), above express pay.
3. Express pay.
4. Contact: email, then an unticked SMS box with a reason.
5. Order summary: gift lines, savings total, renewal in dollars, what happens next.
6. Sidebar: guarantee, one stat, 2 to 3 short testimonials.
7. Paid add-ons unticked. Guarantee beside the pay button. No timer.

### Long-form order page
1. Recap of everything they get, in the sales page's words.
2. Contact fields first.
3. One unticked bump.
4. Payment, with the guarantee beside the button.
5. No nav, no exits, local currency.

### After payment
1. OTO page: confirmation line, then one VSL or TSL offer, one click.
2. Downsell, once, to whoever declined.
3. Thank-you page: confirmation, what happens next, how to reach a person, one proof line, the survey.
4. Email 1: correct use, then the next-order hook.

### Test pass before traffic
1. Walk the whole path on a real phone: ad, page, drawer, checkout.
2. Reload the page with items in the cart and open /cart and /checkout directly.
3. Check every number against the sheet, at every step.
4. Place a real order, take the OTO, decline the downsell, then refund it.

## Chiefing checklist

**Add to cart and the drawer**
- [ ] Add to Cart opens a drawer on the same page. No separate cart page (F1)
- [ ] Nothing pops up between the tap and the drawer. Upsells sit inside it (F2)
- [ ] The cart survives a reload and a deep link, and Checkout works on a real phone (Zone 1)
- [ ] Drawer order: offer band, meter, gifts, order, one upgrade, 2 to 4 cross-sells, savings, button, trust footer (Zone 2)
- [ ] Each line shows variant, quantity and cadence, and can be edited in the drawer (F4)
- [ ] Every price, saving, gift value and count matches the PDP. The discount is auto-applied and visible (F3, Zone 2 warning)
- [ ] One savings total sits directly above the subtotal (F7)
- [ ] Guarantee, review count and cancel terms in the drawer footer (F5)

**Meter and gifts**
- [ ] Meter starts partly filled and states the gap in dollars. The next milestone is about one item away (Zone 3)
- [ ] Where shipping is a subscriber perk, the meter offers "or subscribe," worded truthfully (Zone 3)
- [ ] Gift auto-added as the first line, at $0.00 against the same value as the PDP (Zone 4)

**Upgrades and cross-sells**
- [ ] One trade-up or subscription upgrade card, showing the per-day drop (Zone 5)
- [ ] 2 to 4 cross-sells under a header that gives a reason (Zone 6)
- [ ] Scarcity tags only on items that really are low (Zone 6)
- [ ] Every upsell, bump and OTO passes the same-problem test (F11)

**Checkout**
- [ ] Trust header and sidebar proof added (F8, Zone 7)
- [ ] Express pay on top. As few steps as the platform allows (Zone 7)
- [ ] Renewal shown in dollars, with cadence and how to cancel (F6)
- [ ] Guarantee beside the pay button (F5)
- [ ] At most one in-checkout offer, matched to the order (Zone 7)
- [ ] Protection, warranties, memberships and bumps unticked. Email and SMS boxes unticked, with a reason (F9)
- [ ] No reservation timer, resetting countdown or rolling deadline (F10)
- [ ] BNPL, HSA/FSA and group discounts shown only where real (Zone 7)

**Order page and offers (long-form)**
- [ ] Order page recaps everything they get, puts contact fields first and has no exits (Zone 8)
- [ ] Price shown and charged in the buyer's currency (Zone 8)
- [ ] One unticked bump (Zone 9)
- [ ] OTO opens with the confirmation, then one VSL or TSL offer (F12, Zone 9)
- [ ] Downsell shown once to whoever declines. The decline link is easy to see (Zone 9)

**After payment**
- [ ] Thank-you page confirms the order, ship date, how to change or cancel and how to reach a person, before any offer (F12, Zone 10)
- [ ] Post-purchase survey asks how they found you (Zone 10)
- [ ] No third-party offers on the thank-you page (Zone 10)
- [ ] Email 1 teaches correct use and names the next-order reward (Zone 10)
- [ ] Test order placed, upsold, declined and refunded on a phone, every number checked against the sheet (Blueprint)
