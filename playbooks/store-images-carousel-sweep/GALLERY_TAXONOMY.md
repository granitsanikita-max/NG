# Hero-carousel / gallery image FORMAT taxonomy (classify every image into these)

Classify each GALLERY SLIDE and each notable INLINE image on the PDP. Use these exact type names.

1. Clean product shot (product on plain/solid/studio background, no text)
2. Product-in-hand / scale shot (a hand or body holds it, shows real size)
3. Lifestyle / in-use (person or scene using it, aspirational, little/no text)
4. Infographic value-prop (product or scene + a short claim/benefit/stat as on-image text)
5. Before / after OR old-way vs new-way (side-by-side transformation or contrast)
6. Comparison vs competitors (us-vs-them checkmark grid or table as an image)
7. Ingredients / what's-inside / materials (breakdown of components, formula, fabric)
8. How-it-works / mechanism (diagram, cutaway, labelled how the thing works)
9. How-to-use / steps (numbered usage steps, routine)
10. Testimonial card (a written review/quote rendered on an image) — RECORD how many reviews on it (1/2/3+)
11. UGC / social-proof wall (grid of real customer photos or video thumbnails)
12. Press / as-seen-in / awards (publication logos or an award badge)
13. Guarantee / risk-reversal (money-back, trial, warranty rendered as an image/seal)
14. Size / fit / dosage guide (sizing chart, how much to take, fit-by-type)
15. Bundle / what's-included (the contents laid out, kit shot)
16. Founder / brand story / mission (a person or origin message)
17. Packaging / unboxing (the box, the pack, what arrives)
18. Certification / lab / testing (third-party tested, cert logos, lab numbers)
19. Expert / authority (a named expert/doctor/creator with credential)
20. Results timeline (day/week/month ladder of what to expect)

For each image also capture: any verbatim on-image text (short), and whether it is a photo, illustration/graphic, or video thumbnail.
