## STANDING RULE — broad front door, specific ads
Every PDP line must land for the loose-skin guy, the dad-bod/beer-belly guy, AND the guy who just wants to look sharper in a shirt. The loose-skin "lost the weight" specificity lives in the ADS + at most ONE inclusive nod on the page — NEVER the gate everyone must pass through.

## Project analysis (Copywriting ③)
- **Surface:** PDP — but PDP-FIRST funnel → it must absorb the advertorial beats (hook → mechanism → proof → guarantee), not just list specs.
- **Awareness:** Solution-aware, burned (tried a compression shirt, it failed). → Prove OUR way beats what he tried, then kill hesitation.
- **Sophistication:** Stage 4→5 (tired claim market). → Upgraded mechanism wrapped in identity; every proof see-it-yourself. Plain/bigger claim = dead on arrival.

## NORTH STAR (the one belief he must hold to buy)
"This is the one undershirt actually built to hold my chest and belly flat under my shirt — so I look sharp and solid in anything I put on — and trying it costs me nothing."

## THE 6 NECESSARY BELIEFS (argument chain, in order)
1. **It's how I look dressed — a fit problem, not a me problem.** The chest/belly showing in a shirt is just my shape under the fabric; a bigger shirt / sucking it in / "one more month" doesn't change how the shirt sits. So clothes alone won't hold it — the fix is the layer underneath. [UNIVERSAL de-shame]
2. **The compression shirts I tried failed for a reason** — they squeeze one short band and ride up by lunch — not because the idea doesn't work. [why-they-failed; keeps the category alive]
3. **This one works differently: it doesn't squeeze you smaller, it holds a soft chest and belly flat and in place — cut long so it stays below the belt.** [UNIQUE MECHANISM — the whole pitch hangs here]
4. **It'll be invisible, comfortable, and in my size (to 5XL), on a body like mine.** [demo / white-shirt / fit proof — SAMPLE-GATED]
5. **This brand is legit and buying is risk-free** — keep-the-tank guarantee, free size-down swap, real people. [trust / risk reversal]
6. **It's worth it and I should get it now** — the Kit value, founding price, be finished before summer/the event. [offer + true urgency]

> Targeted layer (not the gate): the "extra skin can't shrink after a big weight loss" angle lives in the loose-skin AD + one inclusion line ("whether it's loose skin after a big loss or just a soft spot that won't budge — same fix").

## LOCKED MECHANISM (Meta-safe)
"Hold-It-Flat." Instead of squeezing you smaller, Unhidden holds a soft chest and belly flat and in place under your shirt, cut long so the hold stays below the belt. Analogy (wedge layer): the deflated balloon.

## COMPLIANCE RAIL (every line — matches the PDP doc's own law)
Appearance-only, WHILE WORN, 1st/3rd person. NEVER weight-loss / "tightens skin" / timeframes / "do you have…?" / medical / fake proof. No review counts/stars until real; no fake timers; sample-gated claims (long-cut-stays-down, invisible-under-white, real compression, fiber %) stay OUT until the sample is tested. Every number identical across ad/lander/cart.

## FUNDAMENTALS (always on, every section)
argument>hype (North Star→one conclusion) · 3–6 word vacuum opener · 5th–8th grade human voice (stop-slop) · benefit>name · specific/visceral (number · named mechanism · felt image) · objections raised+resolved (at the price + before the CTA) · honesty (raise the flaw, flip it) · facts>opinions · features+benefits+outcomes all present · spell out the money (never make them do math) · biggest fear-reducer under the button · message-match ad→top of page.

## TECHNIQUES for this stage (4→5, solution-aware)
unique mechanism · method-vs-method ("why the ones you tried failed") · that's-why evidence · let-them-reach-the-conclusion · identity / desire-to-belong · category reset · flaw-to-feature · story-stack gallery · sell-the-cure (present pain: your shirt doesn't fit right TODAY).
