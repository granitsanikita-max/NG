====================================================
PART 2 — THE FUNNEL (architecture)
====================================================

DECISION: PDP-FIRST. Ad → one strong long-form product page. NO advertorial/listicle at Launch 1.
Why: (1) North Alpine, the $6–10M/mo leader, sends 25/33 ads straight to PDP — proven here. (2) Our edge is VISUAL (demo/styling video) — it shows, doesn't tell; a PDP carries that; an advertorial bets the launch on narrative copy, the hardest thing to get right. (3) The belief-shift lives IN the PDP (mechanism + "why the ones you tried failed" + demo) — one great page, not two. (4) The playbook says build the PDP now, the pre-sell later once the ad angle is picked. (5) Fewer pages = less for a solo op to get wrong.
MAP: Meta ad → PDP (root = homepage = offer page) → cart → checkout → thank-you (instant playbook delivery) → email flow (tracking · how-to · review ask · size-swap). Support pages: About · Reviews · Contact · Shipping/Returns/Refund · Terms/Privacy.
LATER: once we have a winning ad angle + a converting PDP, test a LISTICLE ("5 reasons…") as the pre-sell challenger (lower copy risk than a narrative advertorial). Narrative advertorial = last, not first.

PART 3 — THE PDP SECTION MAP (conversion-first, premium look)
====================================================

PRINCIPLES (the fix for "too much education / looked unprofessional")
- Conversion carousel, premium execution — argue the buyer in, but clean/consistent, real-body visuals, NOT an arrow-spammed claims board.
- Every section kills an objection or sells the dream. No education for its own sake.
- Show, don't lecture: mechanism = ONE graphic, not a science wall.
- Proof (reviews/press/founder/before-after) stays DOWN-page, never a carousel slide (social proof enters the carousel only as a small badge).
- Mobile-first, 5-sec above-the-fold test, message-match the ad, one consistent design system (the biggest "premium vs dropship" lever).

PART 4 — COMPETITOR IMAGERY: WHAT TO COPY / AVOID + HOW TO LOOK PREMIUM
====================================================
(From a slide-by-slide teardown of 9 competitor PDPs. The market = 3 clusters: agency dropship template (North Alpine / SlimFlex / Aethon — shared files, AI models, fake scarcity), polished dropship (Shaperluv, Gaudenza, QoreLogiq, Aida), and real brands (InShape, Esteem).)

CAROUSEL MODEL TO COPY: Aida runs a DESIGNED slide-by-slide carousel where every slide = one objection (hero -> before/after -> vs-ordinary -> mechanism -> results -> sound-familiar -> use-cases -> how-to-wear -> colours). Best architecture in the market. WE COPY THE STRUCTURE BUT WITH REAL IMAGERY (not AI renders). Our 9-slide plan above already follows this.

STRONGEST IMAGE FORMATS (use these): 1 designed slide-per-objection carousel · 2 before/after on-body, shirt-on, front+side+back · 3 us-vs-them comparison grid · 4 mechanism graphic (hold-it-flat / zones) · 5 "sound familiar?" persona cards (named man + age + his worry) · 6 photo/VIDEO testimonials with a face + specific story · 7 "see it in YOUR size" / goal-based fit quiz · 8 lifestyle use-case grid (work/night-out/weekend) · 9 how-to-wear + how-to-put-on.

OUR VISUAL OPENINGS (nobody does these well = where we win):
- A real HOW-TO-PUT-IT-ON clip (step in, pull up, won't roll) — only Esteem attempts it.
- HONEST BIG-BODY imagery (real 300lb+ / 6'3" / loose-skin bodies) — everyone sizes to 5XL but shows lean AI models. Our actual buyer is unrepresented.
- LOOSE-SKIN-after-weight-loss as a visual story — the #1 real review theme, led by nobody's imagery.
- ROLL-UP-IN-MOTION proof (sit/bend/reach) — everyone claims it, nobody shows it.
- WHITE-SHIRT SHOW-THROUGH test in daylight — big fear, zero proof shown.

HOW TO LOOK PREMIUM (not dropship) — concrete:
- ONE consistent real photo shoot (same model/light/background), on REAL target-size bodies incl. loose-skin. Mixed AI + stock + mismatched crops = the dropship tell.
- Real VIDEO (demo, how-to-wear, customer video reviews — InShape's video reviews are the most convincing format seen).
- Real reviews with TRUE counts + real faces + specific stories. NEVER inflate the count or use AI-summarized single-name imported reviews (Gaudenza/Aida do this and it reads fake).
- A real fit tool (calculator/quiz), not a generic size-chart JPG.
- DROP the fake-scarcity theater — no "only 8 left", no reset countdowns, no "95,250 sold this month". Premium pages (InShape, Esteem) don't do it; it reads cheap.
- Authority only if real (a real founder face or real endorsement), never invented "as seen in" badges.
- One cohesive type/graphic system across all designed slides.
