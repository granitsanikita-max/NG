# 4B · Brand Positioning System

<b>Draft → lock status:</b> like §4, no separate §4B draft existed before §11; this is the first written version, locked at the end of §11 (Doc 3, <code>docs/LOCK.md</code>) after the 1★ reviews and objections were mined, then corrected by V2 in three places (marked). The market did <b>not</b> change.

## The Enemy

<b>The "wait-and-hope" way this category sells</b> — products that hide the wet bed or promise a miracle by Friday, then go quiet when it doesn't work. Found in the category's own 1★ reviews and repeated complaint patterns:

- <b>No delivery / no reply (trust) — 50 bank rows</b> (after V3 removed off-topic gummy reviews): "I never received the unit" \[S25\] \[SNIPPET\]; "contacted them on 6 occasions to return and had absolutely no response" \[S26\] \[VERBATIM\]. Unikor Trustpilot 1.9/5 on 13 \[S26\] \[VERBATIM\].
- <b>Guarantees not honoured:</b> "The 60 day money back guarantee is not real." (Saphire 1★) \[S27\] \[VERBATIM\].
- <b>Devices sold as miracles that fail:</b> doesn't work 44 rows, broke 36, didn't wake him 48 \[S28\] \[DATA\].
- <b>Speed/stat claims with no source</b> (§4 #3) and the status quo "just wait" (the "will grow out of it" belief, 72 rows).
- <b>Guardrail kept:</b> we attack the <i>pattern</i>, never name a competitor in ads or on the store.

## What we stand FOR

"It's not his fault. It takes weeks, not days. The work is the get-up (until he wakes or holds on by himself) — so start in time, before the date." <i>(V2 correction: "the work is the wake-up" → "the work is the get-up, until he wakes or holds on by himself", because kids also get dry by holding on — RCH.)</i> We say "6–16 weeks" even though "2 weeks" sells more. Sources: NICE CG111, RCH, CPS (§2B table).

## The Attitude

<b>The straight-talking coach</b> — calm, plain-spoken, a bit dry, on the kid's side. The incumbents are tearful-confessional (Unikor's "Mom, why can't I stop?", Saphire's persona moms) or hype-urgent (countdowns, "97%", "Sell-Out Risk: High"). Nobody is the honest coach with a plan and a calendar.

## The Uncopyable Test

"If Unikor adopted this tomorrow, would it indict them?" <b>Yes, on attitude + honesty:</b>

- its ads say "within 2 weeks" / "97%"; its PDP runs a permanent compare-at and its listicle a countdown;
- its 60-day ad guarantee contradicts its 30-day "unused" policy;
- its lander says "It isn't the waking up that does it", "You don't reset anything" and charts dry nights \[S29\] \[VERBATIM\];
- the parent alarm and armband are paid add-ons.
- Saphire can't adopt "a first-line treatment in doctors' guidelines" at all — it sells gummies.

<b>Honest limit (V2 correction):</b> the <i>phrases</i> are copyable, and "practice / get up" is <b>already in Unikor's ads</b> — 14 of 59 Unikor copies (45 rows, live 3–16 Nov 2026) say things like "it's a practice problem… Feel it, get up, go." and "He gets up and finishes in the bathroom. Night after night, his brain learns to wake up on its own." \[S30\] \[DATA + VERBATIM\]. Off Meta, Wet-Stop already gives "Silver stars… when your child responds to the alarm and there is a small wet spot" (and "Gold stars… when your child is dry all night" — a mixed chart) \[S31\] \[VERBATIM\]; free behaviour charts also exist as advice/printables ([stopbedwetting.com](http://stopbedwetting.com); MooseBaby NZ — see §15, V4). So "The Wake-Up Rep" survives as our <b>method</b>, not as an untaken mechanism. What is still ours: a <b>reward-only chart that rewards the get-up and the reset (no penalty), inside the start-by-date plan</b> — 0 of 183 copies <i>reward</i> the get-up. → The moat is attitude + honesty + execution, not a phrase.

## Whole-store thread (every surface)

- <b>Name:</b> plain, coach/plan-flavoured — not "Night-Guard" hype.
- <b>About page:</b> a manifesto against "wait and hope".
- <b>Product page proof:</b> the 8-week plan, the honest timeline, tested specs only (one tone, wipe-clean — said plainly).
- <b>Offer / guarantee:</b> terms that match the ad word for word (designed in the offer step).
- <b>Reviews shown:</b> real, dated, including "week 1 was hard".
- <b>Voice:</b> calm coach, grade 5–6 reading level, no shame words ("bedwetter" label, "diaper", "buzzer") — Doc 2 §14.

## Weakness-as-weapon

We're new and small with <b>zero reviews</b> — so we don't fake 1,735. We show the plan, the tests we filmed and a guarantee we'll actually honour. David vs the ad-heavy Goliath with a 1.9 Trustpilot. (Must be true: no fake reviews, no persona doctors.)

## Guardrails (both checked)

1. <b>Be what we claim:</b> the plan/chart must actually ship in the box; the guarantee must match the policy; every hardware claim waits for a tested sample. Open risks carried from the lock: <b>no sample tested</b> (all hardware claims UNVERIFIED); <b>one receiver only</b> (first-nights set-up unresolved — across his room he gets up, but mom may not hear it); <b>one tone, no volume control</b>.
2. <b>Attack the pattern, never a named competitor.</b>
