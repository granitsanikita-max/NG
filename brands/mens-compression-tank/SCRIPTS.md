## ANGLE MAP (revised 2026-11-04 — Nikita: 5 groups, one direct angle each)
> Traffic is **solution-aware / product-aware**, so copy is **direct and concise**: name the problem → show the product → 2–3 benefits → offer. No stories, no wife/daughter/wedding scenes.
> Source: Notion Deep Research Run 2 (Docs 1–2). Every group wants "look flat in a shirt" (363) and every group's hesitation is size (261) / not strong enough (156) / scam (137), so benefits lean on **real compression, stays down, sizes that fit**.

| **G1** | Dad bod / belly | Flattens the beer belly and love handles under any shirt | gut, beer belly, love handles, dad bod, "stop sucking it in", "tuck it in" |

- **Mixed (G1+G2):** beer belly + man boobs in one ad, the market's main combo. Use when the swipe source does both.
- **G4 note:** our SKU (CJYD323488814NM) runs **S–5XL**, so G4 is live. Run 2's "stops at 3XL" was about the old SKU. Real fit on big men is still unproven until the sample is measured.
- **Batch rule:** each swipe keeps the group its source ad targets; across the batch, cover all five.
- **Voice:** man boobs, moobs, gut, beer belly, love handles, dad bod, loose skin, "rides up", "nobody can tell". 6th-grade. Never "shapewear", "slimming", "gyno" in ads.

## Swipe 1 — "DADBOOBS KILLA" infographic static (Bond International) · **G1+G2 mixed** (busy dads 40+, beer belly + man boobs)
**Source:** https://app.winninghunter.com/ad/542288135029675?platform=facebook · Meta 542288135029675 · page "Bond International" · US · 1:1 image · ran 2024-08-21 → 2024-10-13 (54 days) · lander bond-collection.com, $24.95 (vs $49.95), Buy 2 Get 1 Free.
**Evidence: weak** (54 days, tiny page). We're testing the format: the #1 static layout in the category.

### Their ad (verbatim)
- **Image headline:** DADBOOBS KILLA · **subline:** Goodbye to man boobs and love handles
- **Callouts:** burn chest fat · back support · burn belly fat · boost confidence
- **Primary text:** "Busy dads and men over 40, say goodbye to stubborn pounds! Introducing the Ionic Tank Top - your ultimate workout companion. Boost your metabolism, burn fat faster, and wave goodbye to the beer belly and man boobs. Get fit, feel great, and make every workout count! 💪🔥 #FitnessForDads #BurnFatFast"
- **Headline:** FROM DADBOB TO FIT FATHER · **Description:** BUY 2 GET 1 FREE · **CTA:** Shop now

### Why it works
- **Headline = his slang + a kill word.** "DADBOOBS" is what guys call it, so it reads funny, not clinical. Two colours, readable in under a second.
- **Subline names the two things he wants gone:** man boobs, love handles.
- **Product is the hero:** one tank, four callouts pointing at body zones. Understood in 2 seconds without the caption.
- **Caption is 4 direct beats:** call out the audience → name the product → 3 benefits + the pain words → feel-good close. No story. Built for product-aware traffic.
- **Offer sits in the description:** Buy 2 Get 1 Free, the same as our Launch 1 offer.

**What changed and why:** only the fat-burn lines ("burn chest fat / burn belly fat / boost metabolism / burn fat faster") became their look-flatter versions on the same body zones. The brain says the tank burns no fat, and "the ad lied" is our buyer's top complaint. "Back support" became "nobody can tell": posture isn't proven on our SKU, and hiding it is what every group wants (141 mentions). Everything else is kept or swapped one-for-one.

### Image brief (for Ad Creation)
- **Format:** 1:1 square, 1080×1080 static.
- **Background:** soft sky-blue gradient, lightest in the centre, deeper blue at the edges. No texture.
- **Top:** "DADBOOBS KILLA" in an ultra-bold heavy sans, caps, spanning ~90% of the width. "DADBOOBS" in black with a thick yellow outline; "KILLA" in black with a bright-blue outline.
- **Under it:** "Goodbye to man boobs and love handles" in bold black sans, sentence case, one line.
- **Centre (~55% of frame):** OUR black tank (SKU CJYD323488814NM, style 7) as a ghost-mannequin / invisible-body shot, front-on with a slight 3/4 turn. A real photo of our product with its real panel pattern; don't add fake compression lines.
- **4 callouts:** white rounded pills, bold black lowercase text, each with a thin white line ending in a white dot on the tank:
  - left, chest height → "flattens chest"
  - right, upper side seam → "nobody can tell"
  - left, belly height → "flattens belly"
  - right, lower side → "boost confidence"
- **No:** people, shirtless bodies, price on the image, other brands' logos.
