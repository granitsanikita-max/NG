- **Headline (under image):** FROM DADBOB TO FIT FATHER
- **Description:** BUY 2 GET 1 FREE · **CTA:** Shop now

### Why it works
1. **Headline = slang name for his problem + a verb that kills it.** "DADBOOBS" is what guys call it among themselves, so it's funny instead of clinical. He laughs, he stops. Two colours make it readable in under a second.
2. **The subline says the payoff in plain words:** "goodbye to man boobs and love handles".
3. **Product as the hero.** One ghost-mannequin tank, centred, nothing else. Callouts point at the exact body zones, so you get it in 2 seconds without reading the caption.
4. **Callouts = 2 body problems + 2 feelings** (chest, belly / back support, confidence).
5. **Caption = audience call-out → "say goodbye to" → "Introducing X — your ultimate ___" → 3 benefit verbs → the pain words again → feel-good close → emoji + hashtags.** Classic, scannable.
6. **Offer sits in the description slot** (B2G1), and our Launch 1 offer is the same.

### Where we change it (and why)
- **Fat-burn claims are cut** ("burn chest fat / burn belly fat / boost metabolism / burn fat faster"). The brain's Doc 3 §13 says flatly that a compression garment has "no weight loss benefit" and doesn't shrink anything. The "miracle shirt" that promises fat burn is the exact thing our buyers call a scam: "not strong enough / just a tight shirt" 73, "scam / ad lied" 36. So each fat-burn callout becomes the **appearance version on the same body zone**: same format, a claim our guy believes.
- **"Back support" → "stays down when you sit".** Posture isn't proven on our SKU (Doc 3 §13), and riding up is the #1 product complaint (30 tags). ⚠️ This line is gated on the sample sit test (OFFER §13). Until it passes, use the fallback callout: "nobody can tell".
- **Audience call-out aimed at our guy:** "men who lost the weight" + "men over 40", the W1 launch avatar plus the broad #1 sub-avatar (Doc 2 §3A).
- **Added our guy's own words:** loose skin, sizing up, hoodies, "real size", "finished".

### Image brief (for Ad Creation)
- **Format:** 1:1 square, 1080×1080. Static.
- **Background:** soft sky-blue gradient, lighter in the centre, darker blue at the edges. Clean, no texture.
- **Top third, headline:** "MOOBS KILLA" (or "LOOSE SKIN KILLA") in a heavy, ultra-bold condensed sans in caps. First word black fill with a thick yellow outline; "KILLA" black fill with a bright blue outline. Spans almost the full width.
- **Under the headline:** the subline in bold black sans, sentence case, one line, about 40% of the headline's size.
- **Centre:** OUR tank (SKU CJYD323488814NM, style 7, **black**) shown as a ghost mannequin / invisible-body shot, front-on, slight 3/4 turn, filling the middle ~55% of the frame. Use a real product photo of our SKU, not a generic tank. Show its real panel pattern as it is; don't add fake compression lines.
- **Callouts:** 4 white rounded-rectangle pills with black bold lowercase text. Each has a thin white line ending in a white dot on the garment:
  - top-left pill → dot on the left chest: "flattens chest"
  - top-right pill → dot on the right side/back seam: "stays down when you sit" / "nobody can tell"
  - bottom-left pill → dot on the lower belly: "holds belly flat"
  - bottom-right pill → dot on the lower right side: "wear your real size"
- **No:** people, shirtless bodies, before/after, logos of other brands, price on the image.
- **Optional test variant:** a small "BUY 2 GET 1 FREE" yellow badge in the bottom-right corner.
