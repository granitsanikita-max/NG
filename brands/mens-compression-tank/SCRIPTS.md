## ANGLE MAP (revised 2026-11-04 — Nikita: 5 groups, one direct angle each)
> Traffic is **solution-aware / product-aware**, so copy is **direct and concise**: name the problem → show the product → 2–3 benefits → offer. No stories, no wife/daughter/wedding scenes.
> Source: Notion Deep Research Run 2 (Docs 1–2). Every group wants "look flat in a shirt" (363) and every group's hesitation is size (261) / not strong enough (156) / scam (137), so benefits lean on **real compression, stays down, sizes that fit**.

| Tag | Group | Direct angle | His words to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | Dad bod / belly | Flattens the beer belly and love handles under any shirt | gut, beer belly, love handles, dad bod, "stop sucking it in", "tuck it in" |
| **G2** | Chest / man boobs | Flattens man boobs under any shirt, nobody can tell | man boobs, moobs, "nipples poke through", "nobody can tell" |
| **G3** | Lost the weight, still shows | Holds loose skin and leftover moobs flat under your shirt | loose skin, extra skin, "down 80 lbs", "wear your real size" |
| **G4** | Big & tall | Built for big guys, long enough to stay down, sizes to 5XL | big guys, "rides up", "long enough to tuck in", 2XL–5XL |
| **G5** | Men 40+ / 50+ | Look sharp in your shirts again, no gut, no moobs | over 40/50, "look sharp", polo, dress shirt |

- **Mixed (G1+G2):** beer belly + man boobs in one ad, the market's main combo. Use when the swipe source does both.
- **G4 note:** our SKU (CJYD323488814NM) runs **S–5XL**, so G4 is live. Run 2's "stops at 3XL" was about the old SKU. Real fit on big men is still unproven until the sample is measured.
- **Batch rule:** each swipe keeps the group its source ad targets; across the batch, cover all five.
- **Voice:** man boobs, moobs, gut, beer belly, love handles, dad bod, loose skin, "rides up", "nobody can tell". 6th-grade. Never "shapewear", "slimming", "gyno" in ads.
