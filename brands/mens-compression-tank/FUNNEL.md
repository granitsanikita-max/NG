# FUNNEL — Unhidden (men's compression tank)
> Owned by: Offer, store & funnel design. Reads: brain/, PRODUCT.md, RESEARCH.md
> Set 2026-11-04 from Deep Research Run 2 (`brain/04-brand-offer-funnel.md` → The Funnel Map), on Nikita's order "Just do everything that Run 2 says." Offer = `OFFER.md` → Offer Launch 2.

- **Funnel type:** **Discovery Stack — Ad → Advertorial (third-person editorial) → Offer page → Cart → After purchase.** Why: the G3 man is problem → solution-aware and burned by the category; he needs the mechanism and proof before a price. Never cold traffic to the home page.
- **Build first:** one advertorial + one offer page for G3. Later doors reuse the offer page and swap only the pre-sell.
- **Rule:** each page continues the conversation — never repeats what the last one said.

## Page map
| Stage | Job |
|---|---|
| **Ad** | Stop Mark (G3) with *his* moment, earn one click — never the sale. Hook = the milestone, first person, no drug/surgery words: *"I'm down 85 lb. My shirt still told on me."* / *"The scale isn't the finish line. Your shirt is."* Format: shirt-on proof — same man, same shirt, with/without, sitting and standing (UGC video); carousel "4 shirts, one undershirt" (0 carousels in 445 competitor ads). |
| **Advertorial (pre-sell)** | Give the mechanism + proof, in this order: his moment (the milestone that didn't fix the mirror) → **UMP:** extra skin can't shrink, and squeezing doesn't make soft tissue go away — it *moves* it (out under the hem, up into a roll) → why the tanks he tried failed (short, one tight band, ride up — his quotes) → **the forgotten history** (the "John Bull Belly", 1819; 1920s belts for men "beginning to grow a belly"; gone with looser 1940s tailoring) → **the villain** (the FTC-fined "burn fat while you wear it" crowd — so say plainly: it won't burn fat, tighten skin or fix gyno) → **UMS:** *"Squeeze moves it. Hold keeps it."* → **proof:** with/without carousel, sit/bend/arms-up film, white-shirt test (sample-dependent), surgeons' public lines on what compression can and can't do, men's words → payoff *"Look like the man you are now."* → CTA to offer page. (Not InShape's "You did the work…" wording.) |
| **Offer page** | Shirt-on hero (no flat-lay) · honest scope line (chest + belly; looks flatter while worn) · measured size chart in inches + 3-question fit finder (chest, waist, height) · torso length per size · colour logic (nude under white, black under dark) · bundle ladder, free units, middle auto-selected: **$39.95 / "2 + 1 FREE" $79.90 / "3 + 2 FREE" $119.85** · named by result (the under-the-shirt kit) · policy-true 60-day guarantee ("If your shirt doesn't sit flatter, you don't pay for it" — worn OK, free size swap) · size-down swap for men still losing · "step in, pull up" GIF · honest 5–12 business-day delivery window · reviews per group once they exist (never curated-only). |
| **Cart** | One add-on only: next colour at a member price ("Add a nude one for white shirts.") · free-shipping threshold just above the single (~$50) · shipping protection opt-in only, never tied to returns. |
| **After purchase** | Thank-you page fit check (swap the size now, free) · post-purchase one-click extra colour (AfterSell-style) · day-3 email: how to put it on + wear it under a white shirt · day-14 email: shirt-on photo ask (UGC per group) · size-down swap reminder as he keeps losing · review request after first wear, all reviews shown. |

## Later doors (same brand, same mechanism, own pre-sell)

3. Offer page copy: hero, scope line, fit finder questions, colour logic, tier names (by result), guarantee wording, size-down swap, delivery line, FAQ.
4. Cart add-on line, thank-you fit-check copy, day-3 / day-14 / size-down-swap emails, insert card ("how to put it on").
5. Quiz pop-up copy (4 paths).
