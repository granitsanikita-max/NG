## Why it was stopped (the math)
- Break-even CPA ≈ **A$86** (brain B/E ROAS ~1.31 × ~A$114 AOV).
- At CPM A$77–100 and 1.6% CTR, CPC = A$4.80–6.25. At ~2.5% click→purchase, that's a CPA of **A$190–250**.
- Break-even needs a CPC of ~A$2.15. That means a **3.6–4.7% link CTR** at current CPMs, or a **~A$34 CPM** at 1.6% CTR. That's a ~3x gap, not a tweak.
- Market context (Winning Hunter Brand Tracker, 2026-11-05):
  - Active ads: InShape 1,710 · Shaperluv 1,206 · North Alpine 909 · Evolv 198 · Selure 183.
  - Evolv runs the **same CJ tank** (V-pattern).
  - Selure/Shaperluv headline **$10 per tank**; our price was $39.95 single / $26.63 on B2G1.

## Verified facts (useful for future brands)
- **Ad review:** only the 3 F1 Pixar-style 3D story videos were rejected (Nov 3, issue code 2490468; hook "fit the suit in 17 days (without losing a pound)"). All AI-UGC videos and all AI/swiped statics were approved.
- **Competitor video length:** median 44.5s across 84 competitor videos running 90+ days, so length was not the issue.
- **Our F2 videos had a demo**, but the "after" payoff landed at ~20s, by which point only ~7% of viewers remained. Competitor long-runners show the change in one continuous shot within ~7s.
- **Audience Network** clicks inflate video CTR (21–37% CTR, accidental taps).

## Lessons for the next product
1. **Run the CPM × CTR × CVR break-even math before launch.** If break-even needs 4%+ link CTR at expected CPMs, don't launch.
2. **Don't pick a SKU that's identical to a heavy spender's product**, or that's priced above the market's cheapest anchor.
3. **Give each creative ~1,000+ impressions before calling it dead.** Fewer ads per ad set, so each gets a read.
4. **Avoid time-bound body-change hooks** ("in 17 days", "without losing a pound") on a new account.
