## 5. THE THREE PLAYBOOKS — full specs (ready for the creative/store-build station to produce)
Delivered DIGITALLY + INSTANTLY at checkout (download + email) so he starts today while the tank ships. Branded, plain-English, 5th–8th grade (Doc 2 §14 voice). Produced from the keystone video shoot (§ below) + Canva. ~$0 COGS.

**① The Styling Playbook** — *kills E2 "how will it look / how to style it" (our #1 open lane — 0 competitors do this) + E6 show-through.*
- How to wear it under: a tee · a polo · an open button-up · a buttoned dress shirt (photo/clip each).
- The colour rule: black under dark shirts, white under light.
- The real-men styling recipes from the research: "open loose button-up over it," "white shirt + jacket top-button-only drapes and conceals" (stream2, r/bigmenfashionadvice).
- The finished-look before/after (shirt on).

**② The 60-Second Put-On Method** — *kills E1 "it's a fight to get on" (count 40).*
- The step-into technique (step in from the bottom, pull up — never over the head).
- "Tight day one, by day three you forget it's on" (stream2).
- A 20-sec clip + a 1-line insert card.

**③ The Keep-It-Flat Care Card** — *kills L6 "the compression dies after a wash" (durability 20).*
- Cold water, gentle, **hang dry — heat kills the elastic** (stream2).
- "Do this and it keeps its hold for months."

**KEYSTONE ASSET — "The Finish-Line Video" (one shoot → all three playbooks + the PDP hero + ad creative):** a real guy steps into the tank → same tee with/without → sits/bends (hem holds) → white dress shirt over it (no show-through) → styles it under tee/polo/open-shirt → big-frame cut if a real 300lb+ subject exists → closes on the finished look. **Production priority #1; gated on the sample.** One phone, one real body, natural light — doable solo.
