### Verdict
**Better on paper for size range, published spec, weight and price. Riskier on the two things the research calls make-or-break: real compression and invisible under a shirt.**
- It is a gym/training compression tank, not a purpose-built shaper.
- Its visible geometric pattern sits right on the market's #1 complaint.
- It drops the nude colour.

So it is **not yet proven better**. It's a different kind of product.

**What decides it:** run the Notion sample test (Doc 3 §11 / §17) on this SKU before launch:
1. Same plain tee, with vs without the tank. Is there visible flattening front and side?
2. The sit / bend / arms-up test. Does the hem stay at or below the belt?
3. The white-shirt test. Do the pattern or lines show through?
4. Wear it 8+ hours for heat and comfort.
5. 5 washes. Does it hold its shape?

Also confirm with CJ:
- the real fiber % (90/10 poly-spandex vs acetate "milk silk")
- the size-chart units (cm, and jin vs kg)
- whether the geometric panels are **knitted-in zones or printed**

If it fails 1 or 3, the research says don't launch on it ("if the sample feels like a T-shirt, don't sell it" — Doc 3 §17). Ideal: sample both SKUs side by side (2XL, black + white) and keep the one that passes.
