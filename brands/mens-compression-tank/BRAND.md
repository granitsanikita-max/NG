## Brand line (LOCKED in Run 2)
*"We are the blunt, dry-funny brand that stands FOR 'a shirt can't change your body — it can change how you look in your shirt' and AGAINST the miracle shirt — the compression undershirt for men who want to look like the man they are now."*

- **Enemy:** the miracle shirt — promises a body change a garment can't deliver, then hides behind the fine print ("Accelerates Weight Loss", "Ionic", contradicted guarantees).
- **Mechanism:** *"Squeeze moves it. Hold keeps it."* — an even hold from chest to past the belt line, hem below the soft part, stays down.
- **Attitude:** blunt, dry-funny, man-to-man. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no body-shaming question.
- **Moat:** the line + the mechanism + the size-down swap + the attitude on every surface + speed (InShape and Alphacore already say "won't tighten skin", so the line alone isn't the moat).
- **Look:** the shirt is the hero — same man, same shirt, with and without, real light, sitting / bending / arms up. No tank flat-lays above the fold. If our ad could be mistaken for a "BYE DAD BOD 🔥" ad with the logo covered, it fails.
- **About-page manifesto:** "We make one thing: the undershirt that makes your shirt fit. It won't change your body. We'll never say it will."
- **Guardrails:** we must BE it — no inches, no weight loss, no "ionic", no curated-only reviews, a policy that matches the promise. Attack the pattern ("miracle shirts"), never name a competitor. Never sell "trust us, we're small/honest" — let the product proof carry it (Nikita's rule).
