  - **They all do:** product-on-a-bed + clinical ergonomics graphics — spine-alignment diagrams, the "**4-inch gap between your shoulder and mattress**" illustration (Groove), butterfly/5-zone cutaways (Derila), a generic adult in a tidy bedroom. \[S5\] VERBATIM

## **2 · Tone**

  - **They all do:** authoritative, clinical, **outsider-expert** voice — *"Fix Your Sleep Issues With This Pillow"* (Derila), *"Say goodbye to snoring overnight"* (Restora), *"Mystery Neck Pain Has An Answer"* (Groove). Talks *at* the buyer. \[S6\] VERBATIM
  - **Our opposite:** **first-person mom-to-mom** voice from inside the tribe — "nursing neck is for real, yeah, me too" — the familiar voice of §3, never the clinic.

## **3 · Claim type**

  - **They all do:** outcome / quasi-medical claims — anti-snoring, "neck/airway **alignment**," "neck pain relief," "fall asleep faster." The category leans on a fix-your-condition promise (Derila even carries a **"not a medical product"** FDA disclaimer because it skates that line). \[S7\] VERBATIM
  - **Our opposite:** **comfort / recovery-feel framed around her night and her baby**, promising the one thing she buys on (§10A core) — a *lasting, passive* end to the morning stiffness, **not** a temporary dull — *"wake up not already hurting for the 6am feed."* Zero medical/condition claims and **no "your neck pain" personal-attribute hook** (brand rule + §2B guardrails).

## **4 · Offer shape**

  - **They all do:** steep **discount + anchor + urgency** race-to-the-bottom — Derila "up to **70% off**" / "2 for $136.98," Restora "**50% OFF**, Sale Ends Tonight," Groove "**£20 off**." Price is the lever; several even charge **paid shipping** (Derila 6.67–13.33). \[S8\] VERBATIM
  - **Our opposite:** a **mom-specific nighttime-recovery value stack** with **free shipping** and a **long, confidence-first money-back guarantee** — the exact risk-reversal the §11 objections demand (she's burned — nursing-pillow doubt 7, "Boppy was a waste of money," and the return-runaround 1★). Win on *fit-for-her + risk removed*, not on being the cheapest slab. (Exact price + stack **deferred to Step 2** — don't invent one.)

  - **They all do:** **borrowed-authority advertorial** — fake-expert persona pages (Restora's *"Laura J. Mitchell, PhD"*), listicle/"method" articles (Derila's *"military sleep method"*, Groove's *"Side Sleepers — Read This"*). A pretend journalist, not a real user. \[S9\] VERBATIM
  - **Our opposite:** a **real new-mom's first-person story / diary** PDP and advertorial (the nursing-hunch "here's what finally let me wake up not wrecked" account) — native UGC, a real mom, not a manufactured PhD persona.
