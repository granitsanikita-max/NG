## REFERENCE AD BREAKDOWN #1 — CALLIXE "TheraPillow" (green-screen VSL · 107s · 218 days live)
Template for **Ads 1 & 2.** Analyzed from the actual MP4 (frames saved in scratchpad/refs/callixe/).

**Format grammar:** PiP talking-head (presenter keyed into lower-left ~30%) floating over full-screen b-roll, cut to FULL-FRAME presenter at direct-address beats. ~5s per cut. Bold 2-line captions, white text on black box, lower third, word-group timed. Mix of (a) real-person b-roll, (b) 3D medical animations, (c) product beauty + in-use shots, (d) a graphic end card.

**Clip-by-clip (0–107s):**
- **0–10s HOOK + mechanism tease:** PiP PT over a soft neck close-up, then a 3D skull with the orange suboccipital muscles lit up. "Your neck pain doesn't start in your neck…"
- **10–30s PROBLEM:** full-frame PT direct address, intercut with pain-trigger b-roll (man hunched at a laptop; person clutching the nape; a wince).
- **30–40s AGITATE:** PiP PT over a 3D skull ("nothing you've tried has touched these muscles") + someone in pain being massaged.
- **40–50s SOLUTION INTRO:** full-frame PT "a team of physical therapists designed this pillow" → cut to PRODUCT beauty shot on a seamless light-blue backdrop.
- **50–80s MECHANISM — ⚠️ ALL DEVICE CLAIMS:** 3D animations + in-bed product use: "cradles that spot → heats up (glowing blood-flow vessels) → the vibration kicks in (vibration node) → gently pulls your neck into traction → your spine decompresses (vertebrae animation)."
- **80–95s PAYOFF:** a woman relaxing on the pillow; the PT holds the pillow in-hand; price tease ("less than a chiro visit… and that chiropractor isn't coming home with you").
- **95–107s OFFER/CLOSE:** night-sleep b-roll (green charge light visible), a handshake + "90 NIGHT RISK-FREE" graphic, a UGC-style woman holding the pillow to camera, "40% off while it's available."

**CALLIXE's casting:** one male PT ~50s, grey beard, charcoal scrub V-neck; generic adult pain actors (men + older women); a consistent 3D-medical-animation style; multiple women in-bed demoing.

### COPY vs CHANGE (the rules for our green-screen ads)
**COPY:** the PiP-over-b-roll structure; the ~5s cut rhythm; the caption style; the arc (hook → problem → agitate → product reveal → mechanism → payoff → risk-free close); the suboccipital-muscle teaching visual (Ad 2 uses the same anatomy); the "chiropractor isn't coming home with you" + risk-free end card.
**CHANGE:**
- Presenter → our **female postpartum PT**, not a male generic PT.
- Every pain b-roll → the **new mom** (3am nursing, hunched over the baby, holding her neck after a feed) — never generic desk-workers.
- **MECHANISM SECTION — do NOT reuse CALLIXE's device visuals.** No heat / blood-flow, no vibration node, no traction, no spine-decompression animation. Ours is PASSIVE: the **dip cradling the neck / chin staying level**, the **slow-rebound press-and-rebound**, the **side arm-cutout**. (Ad 2's script already does this in words; the b-roll must match.)
- Product → the Night Shift (passive contour pillow), never a powered gadget. No "90 NIGHT" device framing — use our own 60-nights-risk-free line.
