## Compliance guards applied to these specific ads
- Green-screen Ad 2 adapts CALLIXE's suboccipital-muscle VSL **but CALLIXE's real product heats / vibrates / pulls traction — we do NOT.** Night Shift is passive: dip → level chin → muscles rest; foam holds; cutout for the arm. No "heats up / vibration / traction / decompress / spine / realign."
- No fabricated named doctor; the expert is a credible-but-unnamed postpartum PT persona, never "Dr. [Name] endorses this." (See open question 1 — this is the riskiest element per the research.)
- Comfort/relief-feel only ("wake up less stiff," "let your neck rest"). No "your neck pain" hook. No "72% of breastfeeding mothers." Price not stated (deferred).
