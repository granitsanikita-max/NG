The 60 Night Sleep On It Promise

Sleep on your Night Shift Pillow for 60 nights. If your mornings don't feel better, we'll refund your order in full. You don't have to send anything back. Keep the pillow.

How to get a refund

  1. Email hello@recovermama.com within 60 nights of your order being delivered.
  2. Include your order number and the name on the order.
  3. That's it. No forms, no return label, no "are you sure?" emails.

We process refunds within 3 business days of your email. The money goes back to your original payment method. Depending on your bank, it can take 5 to 10 business days to show up.

Damaged or wrong item

If your order arrives damaged, faulty or isn't what you ordered, email us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or refund you, whichever you prefer, at no cost to you.

5 year warranty

If your Night Shift Pillow goes flat or stops holding its shape within 5 years of delivery, with normal use, email us with your order number and a photo and we'll send you a new one.

Cancelling an order

Want to cancel? Email us as soon as you can. If your order hasn't left our fulfilment partner yet, we'll cancel it and refund you in full. If it has already shipped, you're still covered by the 60 Night Sleep On It Promise.
