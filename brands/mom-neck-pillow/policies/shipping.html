Shipping

Free tracked shipping on every order.

Where we ship

We currently ship to the United States. If you're outside the US, email hello@recovermama.com before you order and we'll let you know if we can get it to you.

How long it takes

  • Processing: your order leaves our fulfilment partner within 1 to 3 business days.
  • Delivery: most orders arrive within 7 to 14 business days after that.

We ship from our fulfilment partner's warehouse overseas, which is why it takes a little longer than a big box store. Every order is tracked.

Tracking

As soon as your order ships, we email you a tracking link. Tracking can take a few days to start updating after the first scan. That's normal.

How it arrives

Your Night Shift Pillow comes vacuum rolled so it ships safely. Unroll it, take it out of the plastic and give it 24 to 72 hours to fully rise. You can sleep on it the first night.

Lost or delayed orders

If your tracking hasn't moved in 30 days, or your order hasn't arrived within 30 business days of shipping, email us with your order number. We'll send a replacement or refund you in full.

Wrong address

Please check your shipping address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us straight away. We can fix it if your order hasn't shipped yet.

Questions

Email hello@recovermama.com. We reply within 1 business day.
