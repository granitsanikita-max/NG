# PDP IMAGE MAP (2026-11-07) · supersedes the 8-slide gallery list in FUNNEL.md S2
Sources: Notion "Reference: Store Images (Hero Carousel Formats)" + Carousel Image Library artifact (184 brands) + FUNNEL.md objection map + COPY.md v2.

## Rules we're building to
- **Conversion / education carousel** (new brand, cold traffic): one claim per slide, text on almost every slide, 10 slides.
- **Slide 1 = clean product** (Law 1). Not the lifestyle shot the old FUNNEL plan had.
- **No testimonial slide, no fake proof, no certs, no expert.** Winners keep reviews down page; we have no real reviews or experts anyway. Authority = the real published study (Hansraj 2014). Real verbatim mom words only (research quotes, labelled honestly).
- **No sample is coming**, so stills are AI generated from the real CJ product photos as reference. The product must match exactly: butterfly shape, centre dip, two side wings, arm cutouts, zip cover, Light Gray default. Motion = REAL supplier footage wherever possible (ask CJ / Yiwu Renfan / 1688 Nantong factory for product videos).
- **Format:** 1:1 square, 2048 px, phone first. Headline max 6 words, big enough to read on a phone. Palette: lilac gradient backgrounds, plum `#2E2340` text, Rosehip `#B8476A` accent. Motion slides = short muted looping **MP4** product media (not GIF: GIFs are huge and slow on mobile).
- All on-image copy follows COPY.md rules: no dashes, no "firm", no medical words.
