## 0 · Verdict on the GemPages template

**Keep it as the visual shell.** Soft rounded cards and gradient backgrounds fit the 3am palette (Plum / Milk / Rosehip / Skin to Skin / Lilac / Lamplight) and it builds fast.

**But it's short, and 4 of its 10 blocks run on proof we don't have:**

| Template block | Problem | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Bar chart "Scientifically Validated Results" | We have no study of our pillow. Inventing one is fake. | **Repurpose** into the real, sourced head-load chart (Hansraj 2014): looking down loads the neck 27 / 40 / 49 / 60 lb. Education, not product proof. |
| Stats bento ("50,000+", "Dermatologist Approved", "92%") | Zero customers, no expert. All would be invented. Brain: "never fabricate." | **Repurpose** into a feature bento (arm cutout / two heights / keeps its shape / washable cover) around one 3am photo. Real numbers drop in after launch. |
| Review cards with before/after photos | No reviews yet. Before/after on a body = Meta health-ad risk + fake. | Keep the card design. Fill with **real seeded mom reviews** (see §5), tagged by situation, no before/after. |
| "As seen in" press logos | Never featured anywhere. Fake press = the fastest way to look like a scam. | **Delete.** Replace with a trust strip (90 nights · free US shipping · 5-yr warranty · secure checkout). |

**Missing sections the Notion blueprint says a PDP needs (add them in GemPages):**
1. "Why your pillow fails you at night" — problem + short mechanism (retargeting traffic lands here without reading the advertorial).
2. Us vs them comparison (old pillow vs Night Shift).
3. Guarantee block, explained in full.
4. Full FAQ (the template's 4-tab accordion is not enough for 13 objections).
5. Mission block (honest — "built for", not "by moms", unless that's true).
6. Final offer repeat + CTA, sticky mobile buy bar, email capture.

Result: 10 template blocks → **15 sections**. That's normal for a cold-skeptical buyer (Notion F14: long is fine if every section kills a live objection).

### S2 · Hero (gallery + buy box)  ← the most important section on the page
- **Template block:** hero (gallery left, buy column right).
- **Job:** before any scroll, answer: what do I get, is it for me, why this one, can I trust it. Must continue the advertorial's world (dark 3am, the mom, "Night Shift") — the Unhidden lesson: a first screen that breaks the story kills the click.
- **Proof line (above title):** no reviews yet → **do not show stars.** Use: `The 90-Night Sleep-On-It Promise · Free US shipping`. Swap to `★ 4.x · [exact count] mom reviews` the day real reviews exist (un-rounded count).
- **H1:** `The Night Shift Pillow`
- **Benefit line under H1:** `Your neck works the day shift. Let it rest at night.`
- **Subhead (mechanism in one line):** `A contour memory-foam pillow that cradles your neck and keeps its shape all night — with an arm cutout for sleeping curled toward the baby.`
- **4 bullets (Notion formula: 2 life-after, 2 why-it-works):**
  - ✓ Wake up less stiff for the 6am feed.
  - ✓ Sleep on your side toward the baby — no dead arm.
  - ✓ Slow-rebound foam holds its shape till morning, not flat by 2am.
  - ✓ Two heights: flip it to fit back or side sleeping.
- **Buy box (numbered, tiles, no dropdowns):**
  - **1. Choose your colour** — tiles with descriptors: Light Gray *(calm, goes with any bedding — most chosen)* · Dark Gray *(hides night-feed spills)* · Navy *(deep and cosy)*. Default Light Gray.
  - **2. Choose your kit** (Kaching):
    - **1 Pillow — $79** ~~$119~~ · Save $40 (34%) · *about 88¢ a night over the 90-night promise*
    - **2 Pillows — $129** ~~$238~~ · Save $109 (46%) · tag **"One for him / one for the nursing chair"** · pick both colours
  - **Free with every order (thumbnails + struck values):** The 3am Neck Reset audio ~~$39~~ · Nursing-Posture Guide ~~$29~~ · New-Mom Wind-Down audio ~~$19~~ → `$87 of bonuses, free`.
  - **Button:** `Get My Night Shift Pillow — $79` (price inside the button).
  - **Delivery line:** real CJ times. CJ quotes 5–11 days ex-China → show `Arrives in about 7–12 business days · tracked`. Never a fake 2-day date. (Verify on the first test order.)
- **Under the button (biggest fear reducer, as a sentence — Notion F7):** `Sleep on it for 90 nights. If your mornings don't feel better, email us — full refund, we pay the return label, and you keep the bonuses.` + one proof line (once real: `Loved by [n] new moms`; until then: `Free US shipping · 5-year warranty · secure checkout`).
- **Gallery (must sell with no other copy — Notion F2). 8 slides, words burned on each:**
  1. Hero: mom asleep at night on the pillow, lamp glow. `Let your neck clock out.`
  2. The pain: mom hunched feeding at 3am, phone glow. `Feed. Hold. Rock. Look down. All day.`
  3. Why old pillows fail: flat pillow vs neck gap. `Most pillows are flat by 2am.`
  4. The shape: butterfly contour callouts. `Cradles your neck, not just your head.`
  5. Side-sleeping toward the baby: arm cutout callout. `Somewhere for your arm to go.`
  6. Two heights: back vs side diagram. `Flip it to fit you.`
  7. Press-and-rebound demo (GIF/video). `Keeps its shape all night.`
  8. Washable cover + promise: `Zip-off washable cover · 90-night promise · free shipping.`
  ⚠ Slides 4–7 need the **sample** to confirm the real zones, two heights and rebound. Shoot them from the sample, not CJ renders.
- **Kills:** #3 is it for me, #4 fit, #6 hassle, #7 goes flat, #2 money (per-night math), #9 returns, #5 legit.

### S3 · "Why you'll love it" accordion + image
- **Template block:** accordion (4 tabs) + image.
- **Image:** mom side-sleeping toward the bassinet, arm through the cutout.
- **Tab 1 — OPEN by default — "Why moms love it":** the 3-line case: works while you sleep (nothing to keep up), built for side-sleeping toward the baby, keeps its shape all night.
- **Tab 2 — "Size & fit":** 23.6 × 15.7 × 4.7 in (60×40×12 cm — confirm on sample). Rule: `Back sleeper → lower side. Side sleeper → higher side.` Answers "too thick / pushed my head forward" (5'3" 1★).
- **Tab 3 — "Materials & care":** slow-rebound memory foam + zip-off machine-washable cover. Honest line: `New memory foam has a faint smell for the first day or two — air it out and it's gone.` (kills #7 chemical smell before she finds it).
- **Tab 4 — "Shipping & returns":** real delivery window, tracked, the 90-night promise, free return label.
- **Kills:** #4 fit, #7 smell, #12 size, #9 returns.

### S4 · "Why your pillow fails you at night" (NEW — problem + short mechanism)
- **Job:** for traffic that skipped the advertorial. Takes the blame off her, installs The Night Shift idea in 3 beats. Doesn't re-teach the full advertorial.
- **Headline:** `It's not your posture. It's the hours your neck never gets back.`
- **3 beats (icons):** (1) All day your head tips down over the baby. (2) Stretches, massage and chiro only touch it for minutes. (3) At night a flat pillow lets your neck flop — so sleep never becomes rest.
- **Then:** `The Night Shift Pillow holds your neck in a comfortable, supported position the whole time you sleep — the one window nothing else uses.`
- **Kills:** #1 will it work / "temporary dull", Belief 2 & 3 from the brain's chain.

### S5 · Head-load chart (repurposed "scientifically validated" bar chart)
- **Template block:** bar chart.
- **Headline:** `What looking down at your baby does to your neck`
- **Bars:** Head upright ~10–12 lb · tilted 15° = 27 lb · 30° = 40 lb · 45° = 49 lb · 60° = 60 lb.
- **Footnote:** `Hansraj KK, Surgical Technology International, 2014. Forces on the neck at different head angles. Education only — not a study of this pillow.`
- **Close line:** `You hold that angle for hours a day. Your neck deserves the night off.`
- **Why it's allowed:** a real published study about the problem, clearly labelled as not being about our product. Never imply the pillow was tested.
- **Kills:** Belief 1 ("it's real, not just mom life"), Belief 2.

### S7 · What to expect (timeline)
- **Template block:** timeline.
- **Headline:** `What your first nights look like`

### S9 · Guarantee block (NEW)
- **Headline:** `The 90-Night Sleep-On-It Promise`
- **Body:** `Sleep on it for 90 nights. If your mornings don't feel better, send us one email. No forms, no "are you sure?" emails. Full refund, we pay the return label, and you keep the bonuses. And if it ever flattens in the next 5 years, we replace it.`
- **Why so explicit:** she's been burned (20 of 401) and the category's 1★ reviews complain about return runarounds. This is the brain's #1 risk reversal.
- ⚠ Rename from the brain's "Wake-Up-Lighter Promise" — "lighter" is vague and flagged until the sample. Must be honoured exactly as written.
- **Kills:** #2, #5, #9.

### S10 · Reviews (template review cards, no before/after)
- **Template block:** review cards (3 across) → expand to a wall as reviews arrive.
- **Headline (when real):** `[n] new moms are finally sleeping on it` — never "Real people, real results".
- **Cast each featured card to kill one objection** (Notion Zone 13): the skeptic ("tried every pillow"), the side-sleeper ("my arm finally has somewhere to go"), the firmness one ("weird for 2 nights, now I can't go back"), the practical one ("threw the cover in the wash after a spit-up"), the partner ("bought the 2-pack, my husband stole one").
- **Each card:** first name + last initial, situation tag (`Breastfeeding · 4 mo` / `Side sleeper` / `C-section`), photo of her bed/pillow (not her body), colour bought.
- **Faces must match the ICP:** moms 25–40.
- **Until reviews exist:** see §5. Do not launch with invented reviews on this brand — brain rule + Meta scrutiny on a fresh account.
- **Kills:** #3, #1, POWER-MOVE, #5.

### S11 · "You're not alone" strip (honest social proof of the PROBLEM)
- **Job:** before we have product reviews, use real public mom words about the pain — proof she isn't imagining it.
- **Format:** 3–4 short anonymised quotes, attributed to the community, not to our product: `"Me at 3am after nursing my baby." — a mom on r/beyondthebump` · `"No one tells you how bad the neck and shoulder cramps are." ` · `"Am I just going to have this neck hump forever?!"`
- **Rule:** clearly the voice of moms in general, never presented as customer reviews. Short quotes only.
- **Kills:** Belief 1 (real and common), "no one warns you" (7 of 401).

### S12 · Mission (NEW)
- **Headline:** `For the mom who gives everything — and is allowed one thing back.`
- **Body:** the brand's position in 3 lines: the generic slab, the "it's just mom life" brush-off, the fixes that only dull it — and a pillow built for her nights instead.
- ⚠ **Only say "by moms / we're moms" if it's literally true.** The brain bans an invented founder. Default copy speaks *for* her, not *as* her.
- **Kills:** Belief 5 + 6 ("made for me", "I'm allowed this").

### S13 · Complete your 3am kit (template product carousel)
- **Template block:** product carousel.
- **Tiles:** Spare Night Shift Pillowcase $19 · Night Shift Warm Wrap $39 · Nap-Time Neck Rest $34.
- **Placement:** after the main argument (Notion Zone 16). Same items return as cart bump (case) and post-purchase (neck rest / 2nd pillow) — keep prices identical everywhere.
- ⚠ Neck Rest stays off the PDP until its images/description are fully cleaned of "traction / spine correction / pain" (Meta crawls the domain).

### S14 · Full FAQ (NEW — expands the template accordion)
Headers in her words. First answer short and confident.
1. **"It feels firmer than my pillow — is that normal?"** Yes, for a night or two. That firmness is what stops your neck flopping. Give it two nights. *(POWER-MOVE)*
2. **"I've tried other pillows. Why would this one be different?"** Most go flat by morning and have one height and no side support. This one keeps its shape, has two heights and an arm cutout — and you have 90 nights to see. *(#1)*
3. **"Will it fit me? I'm petite / tall / a side sleeper."** Two heights: lower side for back sleeping, higher side for side sleeping. Flip it. *(#4)*
4. **"Can I use it while I'm breastfeeding? I can't take much right now."** Yes — it's just a pillow. Nothing to take, nothing on the baby. *(#10)*
5. **"Is it okay for side-lying or room-sharing?"** It's for your own sleep. Always follow safe-sleep guidance for your baby — keep the baby on their own flat, clear sleep surface. *(#11 — safety framing, never a co-sleeping claim)*
6. **"Does it smell?"** New memory foam has a faint smell for a day or two. Unzip, air it out, and it's gone. *(#7)*
7. **"Will it go flat?"** Slow-rebound foam springs back. If it ever flattens within 5 years, we replace it. *(#7 — ⚠ confirm on sample)*
8. **"How do I wash it?"** The cover zips off and goes in the washing machine. The foam itself doesn't — spot-clean only. *(be precise — Notion F9 warns about exactly this mismatch)*
9. **"How long does shipping take?"** About 7–12 business days, tracked. *(real number)*
10. **"What if I don't like it?"** 90 nights. One email, full refund, we pay the return label, keep the bonuses. *(#9)*
11. **"Is it big enough? Will it look okay on my bed?"** 23.6 × 15.7 × 4.7 in — about a standard pillow's footprint. *(#12)*
12. **"Is this a medical device?"** No. It's a comfort pillow, not a treatment. If you have pain that worries you, talk to your doctor or a physio. *(the volunteered "no" — Notion Zone 14; also protects compliance)*
13. **"Is it a good gift for a new mom?"** Yes — the 2-pack covers her and her partner. *(gift sub-angle)*
