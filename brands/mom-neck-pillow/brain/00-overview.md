# 00 — Overview (read first)

**Step 1 — Deep Market Research for the mom-neck pillow.** The contour / butterfly-shaped
slow-rebound **memory-foam cervical neck pillow** (side-sleeper arm cutout, height-zoned),
sourced on CJ Dropshipping / AliExpress (many SKUs, exact SKU not yet chosen).
Market: **USA · English · Facebook/Meta only.** Dated 2026-11-06.

## The call (ICP fixed by Nikita)
The **new / postpartum mom** (≈25–40, baby 0–18 mo) with **"mom neck"** — own her
**nighttime recovery** with the contour pillow.

## Wedge — CONDITIONAL PASS (§20B honest flag)
The mom-neck PAIN is validated and large — 233 of 401 ICP snippets name neck pain from
nursing; ~50–82% of breastfeeding moms report neck/upper-body pain. The mom-neck-PILLOW
slot is open at the *brand* level: Neurogena runs the mom-neck angle on a **roller** at
$79.95 (not a pillow), and **Dosaze** (a scaled ~$1.2–2.0M/mo contour-pillow brand) is
**already testing** the mom/nursing-neck pillow *angle* in US creative ("Moms Need a
Better Pillow," last seen 2026-11-05) — but as ~2 of ~1,320 ads on a generic brand with
no mom PDP. **The opening = a committed mom-FIRST brand / PDP / night-recovery identity,
and speed matters because a scaled incumbent is already poking at it.** (§1 originally
missed Dosaze because it searched the literal string "mom neck"; Dosaze says "nursing
neck." The §20 verify pass caught and corrected this — the wedge is contested, not clean
white space.)

## Mechanism — "The Night Shift"
Her old pillow wastes the one window (sleep) her neck could rest. Lead on WHY the pillows
she tried failed HER (flat by morning / wrong height / back-only / no side support). Say
"let your neck rest" — **never treat / cure / repair.** Only holds if a physical sample
proves the height, the zones, and the side-sleeper support.

## Brand-line direction
"The tender-but-defiant brand that stands FOR a new mom's body being worth recovering
too… the night their neck actually recovers." The moat is **behavioral** (§20B-corrected) —
commit the whole brand / PDP / reviews, not one clever ad.

## Hard rules baked into the research
Meta ads only · US first · front-end profit · **no medical claims** (comfort / support /
relief-feel language only — a pillow is a comfort textile, it does not treat/cure/prevent) ·
no Meta personal-attribute hooks · never use trust/reviews/founder as the edge ·
Amazon = info only · no copying a competitor brand 1:1.

## Blockers before launch
Chosen SKU + true landed cost not yet picked · reviews / mom-UGC seeding (the single OPEN
item, §11) · a physical sample to prove length / height-zones / breathing.
