## Verified competitor facts behind the lock (WinningHunter, US Meta, pulled 2026-11-08/09)
- Category leader is **Mellow Sleep** (CloudAlign $49.99): ~11,054 active ads, ~3M visits/mo, runs ads from a real chiropractor's page and real publisher pages (Scary Mommy, Bustle, Us Mag, NY Post). Advertorial /pages/adv-cap-game-changer: 146 ads, 322 days.
- **Dosaze**: 380 active ads; its #1 ad is bedroom UGC "My chiropractor told me to switch pillows"; it scales by re-shooting the same script with new creators.
- **Every mom-neck long-form story ad tested (Lanarie, Naprelle, Frilume, Natica) was dropped within 9 days.** No competitor runs a mom-specific advertorial, mom UGC video, or the night angle.
- Neurogena dropped the mom-neck roller; Restora's page pivoted to supplements.

## Hard rules for Campaign 1 creative
- Format 1 needs a **real credentialed person** (postpartum PT / chiropractor / lactation consultant). No invented doctors.
- Ad hook echoes the advertorial headline; story-style ads use Learn more.
- Comfort words only (rest, hold, wake up less stiff) — never treat / fix / cure / repair; no "your neck pain" personal-attribute hooks.
