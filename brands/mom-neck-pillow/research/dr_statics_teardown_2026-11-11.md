# DR static teardown · 33 proven competitor statics (Dosaze, Mellow, Groove) · 2026-11-11
Source: Nikita's swipe doc (Google Doc 1ffuoJToKyV1iZQeaytT-5OEzskCBfL0q_mQqgIlerME) + primary text pulled from WinningHunter (dr_statics_primary_text_2026-11-11.md).

## IMAGE
1. 3 zones: headline top 25–35% · product hero middle 40–50% (¾ angle, contour visible) · proof/trust strip bottom. Text never covers the product.
2. Longest runners are the simplest (#1 273d = 2 lines + product; #3 162d = headline + 1 line + product on bed). One idea per ad.
3. Headline 3–9 words: outcome ("Wake up without…") or problem + twist ("Neck pain? CANCELED."); second beat lands it ("Finally." / "THIS IS DIFFERENT").
4. Heavy sans, navy/black on light; serif for premium/editorial. One flat bold background colour per ad.
5. People in only 4/33 (all asleep, calm). Product does the talking; mechanism shown visually (flat vs contour, red vs green neck).
6. Mellow re-aims one pillow at one niche per ad with one word (CPAP, MIGRAINE, side sleepers, runners) → our play = "new moms".
7. Risk reversal as a headline (#16, #27). Ours (keep it) is stronger.
8. GAP: 0/33 show a mom or baby. Groove #23 literally targets "side sleepers in your 60s" → generic neck copy pulls older audiences.

## PRIMARY TEXT
1. Three lengths, all run long: short 60–130 chars (Mellow; image does the work) · medium 300–500 (Dosaze core) · long 700–1,300 (Dosaze stack, Groove story). Longest runner (#1) = 93 chars.
2. Dosaze master template (#3, #9, #10, #27, #30, #31):
   HOOK (blame shift / counter-intuitive) → MECHANISM (flat pillow, neck bends 8 hours, that's why you wake stiff) → SOLUTION (product holds neck in line) → ✨ benefit bullets incl. offer → "Powered by:" ✅ trust → proof line → offer + risk reversal → "👉 Tap Shop Now".
3. Hook types: blame shift ("You're blaming your mattress. Your age… It's the pillow." #9; "Plot twist: it might not be your age" #25) · counter-intuitive ("you don't need more sleep. You need a better pillow." #10) · objection mirror ("Compare it to the other tab you have open" #16) · curiosity/story ("The funniest reviews always end the same way" #11) · audience callout ("WAKE-UP CALL for side sleepers in your 60s!" #23; runners #28) · old-style question ("Tired of waking up…?" #4, #18).
4. Every ad sells the SAME mechanism: the 8 hours. "No amount of stretching undoes eight hours of it." = our UMP. We make it mom-specific: the hours after the last feed.
5. Split of fields: image = the message; headline field under the image = often the offer; description = guarantee/shipping.
6. Formatting: 1–2 sentence paragraphs, white space, emojis as signposts (😩 pain, ✨/✅ bullets, 🎁/🔥 offer, 👉 CTA).
7. Copy is a template: Dosaze reuses identical primary text across different images (#4=#8, #10=#12, #26=#32). They test images, not copy.
8. Their proof stack (stars, reviews, units sold, press, %) and claims ("heal", "inflamed", "damage", "92% less pain", "designed with chiropractors") are NOT available to us. Replace with mechanism specificity + her words + the 60-night keep-it guarantee.
