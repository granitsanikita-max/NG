# Advertorial rules from Nikita (2026-11-08) — apply to every line

Framework: PASTOR (Problem, Agitate, Story, Transition, Testimonial, Offer, Response). Angle A: "The 12-Month Window" + The Night Shift mechanism. Copy is as long as it needs to be, no fluff: every line has a job.

1. Above the fold: nothing that doesn't do a job. Author/byline goes BELOW the fold, under the first image. No read time, no takeaways box up top.
2. Highlighting: sparse. Only in headline/subheadline to make one thing pop. Not everywhere (the market doesn't do it).
3. Implied effort: as low as possible. Minimise time, effort, sacrifice she thinks it takes. Watch words that imply work ("find out", "learn how", "follow", "steps", "routine", "method with N steps"). Prefer "discover", "see", passive wins ("while you sleep", "nothing to do but lie down").
4. Speak to her exactly: her words, her moment (3am feed, the locked neck, the bassinet), never generic.
5. Copywriting fundamentals = make or break (more rules to come from Nikita).

## ② Fundamentals vs Techniques (Nikita, pasted)
- PRIME DIRECTIVE: every element's only job is to get them to the next word. Copy is a chain; each line earns the next.
- Fundamentals = non-negotiable, active in every line (section ④ to come). Nail first.
- Techniques = toolbox, chosen by goal + awareness + sophistication (section ③). Cold advertorial technique ≠ warm PDP technique.
- Copy CHANNELS desire, never creates it (BA). Desire comes from research (brain/02, /03): aim it at this product.
- No formula is the idea. Formulas/swipes only for wording (③E). Copying a headline from another market = "echo ad" (BA).
- ORDER OF WORK: research inputs (③A) → surface (③A) → awareness + sophistication (③B–C) → headline (③E) → make every fundamental (④) active → layer techniques (⑤) → chief it (⑥).
- Tags: (BA) = Breakthrough Advertising (Schwartz); (house rule) = ours.

### Applied to this advertorial (my read)
- Desire to channel (brain §3/§16): wake up not already hurting; permission to recover without taking from the baby; certainty it won't be permanent. NOT created, aimed.
- Awareness (brain §5): 58% problem-aware, 33% solution-aware, 9% product-aware → cold advertorial starts at the problem, product late.

## ③ Analyse the goal (filled for THIS advertorial, 2026-11-08)
A. Surface = Pre-Sales Advertorial. Reader cold/skeptical, guard up. Lean on: story · curiosity & contrast · belief change · native editorial voice. Must EARN the right to sell (product late).
- ONE desire: wake up NOT already hurting, and know it won't stay this way (brain: #1 desire "lasting relief / a real fix" 30/401 → core = control/certainty, fear it's permanent).
- ONE performance: holds her neck supported all night without going flat, so the hours she's already asleep do the work. Specs (slow-rebound foam, 2 heights, arm cutout) = proof only, never hook.
- Primary image (build on, don't fight): "a pillow is the thing you sleep on every night" + "contour pillows feel strange at first / pillows go flat". Build on: yes it feels new for 2 nights; most pillows DO go flat, that's the point.
- Personality trait (leads headline): EFFORTLESS — works while you sleep, nothing to do. Supports: made-for-moms, gentle, honest.
- Life-Force 8 hit (5): freedom from pain · comfortable living · care & protection of loved ones (lift the baby without wincing) · survival/longer life (not the hump at 40) · social approval (not the cranky, broken mom). Learned boosters: convenience, efficiency, being informed, economy (vs chiro), curiosity.
B. Awareness: Problem Aware (58%) with a Solution-aware tail (33%) burned by pillows. Headline starts with the problem (+ hidden fear / limit removed / "without" the thing she dreads). Copy must agitate → reveal solution + mechanism → product as inevitable fix.
C. Sophistication: general pillow market Stage 4 (shoulder gap, dual height, arm tunnel, too flat/too tall all copied). Plays: (1) NEW mechanism "The Night Shift" (the night undoes the day) — new, legitimate, believable; (2) Splinter move: own new moms, a sub-group the big players ignore, with its own mechanism + proof; (3) Identification to bring her in. Effectively a reset toward Stage 1 for "pillow for new moms".

## ③E Headline rules (Nikita, pasted) — summary
Job = get the next line read; first 5–10 words decide it. Order: entry point → claim type → sharpen wording → 7-ingredient check (4–5+ across H1+sub). Believable at first glance; strongest claim ≠ strongest headline; never lead with the physical product; ONE desire/performance/trait. Stage 3+ = make it fresh (contradict how they think it works, mechanism+claim in one line, paradox, state the difference). Pull-in forms: warn, question, call out who it's for, customer quote, named case. Headline that contradicts its photo forces the read. Rotate angle across campaign, keep the idea.
