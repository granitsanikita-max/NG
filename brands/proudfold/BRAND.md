## Compliance guardrail (NON-NEGOTIABLE downstream)
Lead with cosmetic **skin-protectant / barrier / soothe / cool / comfort** claims only. **NEVER** "kills yeast/bacteria," "antifungal," "cures/treats/heals intertrigo/candida/HS," no "dries to a powder," no raw-skin before/after, no "do YOU have…?" personal-attribute targeting. Reach by situation (heat, sweat, long shifts), not diagnosis. Undecylenic acid is 0.01% (≪ the 10% antifungal threshold) → the antifungal claim is both illegal and false. These rules keep a young ad account alive. Full SAY/DON'T table: `brain/04-brand-offer-funnel.md` §2B.
