## 1. COMPETITOR OFFER TEARDOWN — how each one actually makes money (live, 2026-10-20)

### Healora — the control ($7–12.5M/mo, FB/IG only)
- **Ladder (ad funnel /pages/v1_ss):** autoship **$24.95 / $34.95 / $44.95** (1/2/3 tubes) · one-time **$29.95 / $39.95 / $49.95** · compare-at **$39.99 / $79.90 / $99.90**. **2-pack = "Most Popular"** hero. ## 2. THE STRATEGIC CALLS (what the teardown + framework decide)

### Offer TYPE = Honest Front-Loaded Subscription (core) + result-named Bundle (AOV) + First-Order pop-up (cold capture)
ProudFold is a **consumable, reorder-LTV** product → the Grand-Slam framework points consumables/skincare straight at a **Front-Loaded Subscription** (subscriber month-6 LTV ≈ $226 vs $138 one-time; only ~10% of one-time buyers ever subscribe later, so win it on order #1). Bundles are the one-time AOV layer; the pop-up is the cold-first-order layer.

### The ONE big decision — the subscription (flag for Nikita)
The category's #1 complaint is the **sub-trap**. So running a subscription is walking into the exact minefield our brand is built against — AND a sub on a health-adjacent product raises chargeback/dispute risk that can kill a young Meta ad account (why Clairon runs none).
**Resolution (recommended): run the subscription, but make it the honest OPPOSITE — and turn the category's poison into our proof:**
- **Opt-IN, never pre-selected.** One-time is the default radio; subscribe is the clearly-better-value choice she actively picks. (This alone kills the dispute risk + is the honest contrast.)
- **One-click cancel, no auto-enroll, no retention script**, skip/pause/cancel from the account or one email. **Say it loudly** — "the only fold-care subscription you can actually cancel" — and show the cancel button in the creative/PDP.
- **Be profitable on order #1 via the bundle** (like Healora's front end) so subscription LTV is *upside, not survival.* A sub dispute then can't sink us.
**Fallback (lowest account risk):** launch one-time-only (Clairon model) + an honest "reorder reminder" email flow, add the opt-in sub once trust/accounts are seasoned. Costs us the LTV engine up front. → **This is the A/B test below (Variant A vs B). Nikita's call on which leads.**

### The guarantee is the hero of the offer (she's a burned buyer — risk reversal does the heavy lifting)
**The 365-Day Keep-It Comfort Guarantee** — *"Use the whole tube. If your folds don't feel calmer, cooler and more comfortable, email us for a full refund and keep the tube — no returning it, no forms, no 'unopened' fine print, no questions. One real person reads every email."* Stated **identically everywhere** (beats Healora's 365/90 split), **honors opened/used product** (beats Orgatics' unredeemable unopened-only), longest + boldest in the niche. Add a real refund-rate confidence stat ("only X% ask") once we have data — never fabricated.

**Product = 1 tube ≈ 30-day supply. Bundles named by RESULT, not quantity. Subscribe = opt-in, front-loaded.**

| 1 tube — **"The Starter"** | **$29.95** | $24.95 (first order 25% off) | $39.95 | $24.95 | Best for one fold |

- **Subscription discount = front-loaded: 25% off first order, 15% off every refill** (protects margin; beats the comfort-lane flat 15% on order #1). Recurring every 30/45/60 days (her choice).
- **Anchor to the single** ($29.95) so the Duo/Supply look like steals. **Free shipping at $40+** (nudges single → Duo; standard, honest). Single ships for a small flat fee OR add the mini to clear $40.
- **Reason for the "discount" (honest, not a fake clearance):** a real **launch / founding-customer price** — "founding price while we're new; it goes up as we grow." True, and the opposite of Healora's fake "$87k overstock."
- **Target AOV ≈ $50** (the Duo). Build the whole store to land there.

### First-Order pop-up (cold capture; layers on top)
- **Ask a question first** ("Where does your skin rub most — under-breast, belly, thighs?") → feels custom → routes to a matching welcome flow. (Also doubles as zero-party data for retargeting.)
- **Offer = NOT "10% off."** AOV < $100 so %-off generally wins, but the framework's 8-way test says **store-credit framing beats everything.** Test **"$15 ProudFold credit toward your first order"** vs an **odd-number % (e.g. 23% off your first tube)**. Stack site + pop-up into ONE number.
- Do **not** use a free guide as the pop-up bait (low intent) — the guides are *gifts inside the offer*, not the opt-in hook.

### Always-on components (framework)
Price anchor (single anchors the bundles; honest compare-at only) · honest reason-for-price (founding-customer launch price) · the 365-day keep-it guarantee w/ confidence stat added later · protect margin (list price set so the "discount" lands at the price we always meant to charge) · target AOV = the Duo (~$50) · **measure BOTH first-order conversion AND 90-day LTV.**

## 5. THE 2 OFFER VARIANTS TO TEST (framework: test offers, not discount depths)
- **Variant A — "The Honest Subscription"** (LTV play): opt-in front-loaded sub (25%/15%) + full gift stack + keep-it, Duo default, "the only fold-care subscription you can actually cancel." Highest LTV; needs disciplined opt-in to limit dispute risk.
- **Variant B — "The No-Trap Bundle"** (lowest account risk): NO sub; result-named bundles (Duo/Supply, "buy-2-get-1" framing for the Supply) + full gift stack + keep-it + honest reorder-reminder email. Attacks the trap hardest; safest for a young ad account; weaker LTV.
- The **pop-up (store-credit vs odd-%)** and the **guarantee** ride on both. Run A vs B to decide the subscription question with real data; track first-order CVR **and** 90-day LTV on each.

## 6. OPEN ITEMS (Nikita decides / confirm before build)
1. **Subscription vs no-subscription at launch** (Variant A vs B) — the one big call. My rec: A, but opt-in + profitable-on-order-1 so the sub is upside not survival.
