## 2. THE STRATEGIC CALLS (what the teardown + framework decide)

### Offer TYPE = Honest Front-Loaded Subscription (core) + result-named Bundle (AOV) + First-Order pop-up (cold capture)
ProudFold is a **consumable, reorder-LTV** product → the Grand-Slam framework points consumables/skincare straight at a **Front-Loaded Subscription** (subscriber month-6 LTV ≈ $226 vs $138 one-time; only ~10% of one-time buyers ever subscribe later, so win it on order #1). Bundles are the one-time AOV layer; the pop-up is the cold-first-order layer.

### The ONE big decision — the subscription (flag for Nikita)
The category's #1 complaint is the **sub-trap**. So running a subscription is walking into the exact minefield our brand is built against — AND a sub on a health-adjacent product raises chargeback/dispute risk that can kill a young Meta ad account (why Clairon runs none).
**Resolution (recommended): run the subscription, but make it the honest OPPOSITE — and turn the category's poison into our proof:**
- **Opt-IN, never pre-selected.** One-time is the default radio; subscribe is the clearly-better-value choice she actively picks. (This alone kills the dispute risk + is the honest contrast.)
- **One-click cancel, no auto-enroll, no retention script**, skip/pause/cancel from the account or one email. **Say it loudly** — "the only fold-care subscription you can actually cancel" — and show the cancel button in the creative/PDP.
- **Be profitable on order #1 via the bundle** (like Healora's front end) so subscription LTV is *upside, not survival.* A sub dispute then can't sink us.
**Fallback (lowest account risk):** launch one-time-only (Clairon model) + an honest "reorder reminder" email flow, add the opt-in sub once trust/accounts are seasoned. Costs us the LTV engine up front. → **This is the A/B test below (Variant A vs B). Nikita's call on which leads.**

### The guarantee is the hero of the offer (she's a burned buyer — risk reversal does the heavy lifting)
**The 60-Day Keep-It Comfort Guarantee** (LOCKED — 90 is the push-harder ceiling; a full year was rejected as too long for a new brand) — *"Use the whole tube. If your folds don't feel calmer, cooler and more comfortable, email us for a full refund and keep the tube — no returning it, no forms, no 'unopened' fine print, no questions. One real person reads every email."* The edge is NOT the length — it's the 4 things no rival does: stated **identically everywhere** (beats Healora's 365/90 split), **honors opened/used product** (beats Orgatics' unredeemable unopened-only), **keep-the-tube no return**, **a real person answers**. 60 days matches Lume (the best comfort-lane brand) = honest + category-normal, short liability tail, abuse-resistant. Add a real refund-rate confidence stat ("only X% ask") once we have data — never fabricated.

## 5. THE 2 OFFER VARIANTS TO TEST (framework: test offers, not discount depths)
- **Variant A — "The Honest Subscription"** (LTV play): opt-in front-loaded sub (25%/15%) + full gift stack + keep-it, Duo default, "the only fold-care subscription you can actually cancel." Highest LTV; needs disciplined opt-in to limit dispute risk.
- **Variant B — "The No-Trap Bundle"** (lowest account risk): NO sub; result-named bundles (Duo/Supply, "buy-2-get-1" framing for the Supply) + full gift stack + keep-it + honest reorder-reminder email. Attacks the trap hardest; safest for a young ad account; weaker LTV.
- The **pop-up (store-credit vs odd-%)** and the **guarantee** ride on both. Run A vs B to decide the subscription question with real data; track first-order CVR **and** 90-day LTV on each.

## 5B. FUNNEL (launch) — Discovery Stack (Nikita's call 2026-10-20)
**Ad → third-person EDITORIAL advertorial (journalistic / review style — NOT a sales script, NOT a founder pitch) → focused product/offer page → cart/upsells.**
- Matches the Funnel Selection Board **Discovery Stack** (default for a single-hero product) AND the Funnel Fundamentals "Pick the page" route for **cold + skeptical + tried-everything → story advertorial → PDP.** Dawn is solution-aware, burned, Stage 4→5 → she needs the editorial trust step *before* landing on anything that looks like a store (her scam alarm).
- **The advertorial is a THIRD-PERSON editorial** — reads like an article written *about* the problem (e.g. "Why nothing in the pharmacy aisle fixes fold rash — and what finally does"), honest mechanism + real sources + a real customer's story told editorially. It is NOT a first-person founder pitch and NOT a sales script (that instantly reads as an ad = defeats the purpose).
- **Brand-integrity line (the ONLY constraint on the third-person voice):** honest third person — **no *fabricated* person.** No fake "Dr. Rebecca Mills / Barbara Collins" expert byline, no invented nurse/derm, no made-up quotes or reviews. That fabrication is Healora's move; ours stays real.
- **The real founder voice lives elsewhere** — the **About page** + a trust block on the product page (+ optionally a founder/UGC *ad*). **Never in the advertorial.**
- **NOT a single long click-through page** (that model is for ad → straight to one page with NO advertorial). Because we run a separate advertorial, the product page must **continue the conversation, never restart it** (Fundamentals secret #1). The advertorial carries the editorial case + "friction not your fault" reframe + second-skin mechanism + proof; the **product page then goes straight to the offer + buy box + objection-cleanup + reviews + guarantee** — it does NOT re-tell the whole case. Two focused pages beat two mega-pages.
- **Guardrail:** the product page is still a full CONVERSION page for a skeptic (offer stack + proof placed at each claim + FAQ/objections + comparison + 60-day guarantee) — focused, **not a thin hero+bullets PDP.**
- Compliance stays in the barrier/comfort lane on every page (Meta reviews the lander too).
- **Page architecture (lean build — Nikita's call):** the ROOT domain (proudfold.com = "homepage") **IS** the product/offer page — ONE build serves both (standard for a single-product store; saves a page). Cold paid traffic goes **ad → advertorial → this page**; the root page also catches direct / brand / retargeting / organic (so we're NOT sending cold paid to a homepage — paid lands on the advertorial). Build it as a **focused, COMPLETE DR offer page** (outcome hero → offer stack → benefits → brief mechanism → proof → comparison → FAQ → 60-day guarantee → sticky ATC) — NOT a second long editorial that re-tells the advertorial. **Two main builds:** advertorial (separate, editorial) + root product/offer page. Small required pages (Meta approval + trust): About, reviews, contact, shipping/returns/refund, terms/privacy.
- **Build order (Step 2 doc):** Part A offer ✅ → **Part B = the focused product/offer page NOW** → **Part C = the advertorial AFTER the Step-3 ad angle** (it must match the winning ad).
