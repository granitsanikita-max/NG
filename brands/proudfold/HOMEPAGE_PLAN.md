# ProudFold — THE OFFER PAGE (homepage / click-through / PDP) — PLAN v3 (CONVERSION-FIRST)
**Station:** Copywriting · **Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Replaces:** the raw GemShoes template
**Mode:** copywriting-skills test build. Reviews are written here (craft demo); at real launch the same
slots/voices get seeded-real reviews. No fabricated counts/press anywhere.

> **What changed from v2 (the conversion-first pass Nikita asked for):** the page was belief-complete but
> info-clustered — 3–4 education beats stacked before the desire/proof/offer accelerants, and two
> mechanism sections overlapping. v3 fixes that WITHOUT losing belief-building (which IS the sale for a
> burned "nothing-works" buyer):
> - **Merged** mechanism + how-it-works → one "the fix" section · ingredients + IS/ISN'T → one trust
>   section · made-for-you + where-to-use + how-to-use → one identity+clarity section. (19 → 16 sections.)
> - **Every section now carries a CONVERSION JOB** (kill an objection / change a belief / create desire /
>   reverse risk / prompt action). If a section only *informs*, it was cut or merged.
> - **Interleaved** a CTA band or an embedded review after every belief/education beat — she's never more
>   than a screen from a reason to act or a proof point.
> - **Pulled desire + proof higher** (future-pace + reviews moved up, not hoarded at the back).
> - Education is kept **short + scannable** (header + 2–3 lines + one visual) so a ready buyer skims past
>   while a skeptic digs in. The **buy box is at the top**, so the hot buyer converts immediately; the body
>   exists to convert the skeptic.

## 0 · STRATEGY (unchanged core)
**Dawn:** 45–62, plus-size (16–24)/big-busted (DD+), burned by borrowed baby/foot/Rx fixes, dismissed by
doctors, scammed by sub-traps. Wound = exhaustion + being unseen. Driver = **dignity.** A skeptic whose
wallet's already been bitten.

**Belief chain to install:** B1 *not your fault (friction)* → B2 *everything you tried was borrowed* → B3
*a barrier that stops the rub breaks the cycle* → B4 *this brand is honest, no trap* → B5 *made on purpose,
for me.* **Identity flip:** afterthought → seen (the name flips shame → pride).

**Conversion thesis:** the template IS our enemy (fake Dr. Bavlsik, fake badges, "54K reviews / 9,595 left").
Dawn's been burned by exactly that. We win by doing the loud honest opposite — **for a burned buyer, honesty
out-converts hype**, and a faceless funnel can't copy it.

**Voice = the BRAND** (not Linda — she's the advertorial). Blunt, warm, zero-shame, "you" by situation.
**Compliance:** barrier/soothe/cool/comfort only; never cure/antifungal/powder/"do you have…"; no fabrication.

**Authority = what counts to HER (not doctors/brands/badges — she distrusts all three):** peer (women who
lived it — the reviews) + transparency (the brand refuses to lie). That's it.
