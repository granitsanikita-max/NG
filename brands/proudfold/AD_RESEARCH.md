> **TOP-OF-FUNNEL ONLY.** Full re-sweep 2026-10-23 (replaces the earlier all-funnel doc): every competitor cluster on Meta via WinningHunter, every page, image + video, ~25 video transcripts, each creative classified TOF vs BOF. >
> **TOF = cold, first-touch.** The creative's only job is to stop the scroll of a stranger who's never heard of the brand and pull them into the funnel. Signals: opens on a problem/pain, a story/confession, mechanism-education, a misdiagnosis reframe, a founder/expert origin, a UGC demo, or a curiosity hook — and runs to an advertorial/listicle bridge or a cold product page. **Not** a deal, countdown, "back in stock," or cart ad.

> **👁️ SEE ANY AD:** every link is a Meta Ad Library link — click to view the live image or watch the video. Format: `https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=<AD_ID>`. §7 is a scroll-and-click visual board (video + image).

## 1. THE TOF VERDICT — video vs image, who wins cold with what
Top-of-funnel in this niche splits by brand type:

- **The direct medical-cream / oral brands (Healora, Clairon, Orgatics, DermaSoothX, Lupina) run TOF on STATIC advertorials.** Their cold engine is the **advertorial "article" image → editorial bridge page**, plus long-form **confession/story** and **mechanism/misdiagnosis** statics. Healora's single biggest-spend creative in the whole cluster is a TOF advertorial static (**$7.4k / 674k views in 46 days**). Orgatics and DermaSoothX run **zero TOF video** — 100% image advertorials. Their long-running *videos* are almost all BOF (discount/urgency retargeting). → On the exact 45–62 FB buyer, **the proven cold unit is the advertorial/confession static.**
- **The video-native brands (Veloma, Ionix) and the comfort lane (Megababe/Lume/Fresh/Thigh Society) run TOF on UGC VIDEO.** Expert/mechanism VSLs, misdiagnosis reframes, 2-woman confession skits, glide/barrier demos, doctor-origin loose-skin stories. Longest-running cold videos in the sweep: Ionix "here's why" 238d, Veloma men 199d, Veloma 2-woman skit 140d.

**What this means for us (lean budget, new account, Discovery-Stack funnel):** run **both**, but they do different jobs. The **advertorial/confession static → bridge page** is the cheapest proven cold unit and fits our funnel exactly. The **UGC video** (glide/barrier demo + confession) is the higher-ceiling scroll-stopper and is where the comfort lane wins. Nearly every medical-brand TOF creative routes to an **advertorial/listicle bridge** (`/adv/…`, `/pages/listicle…`), not straight to a PDP — that matches our ad → advertorial → PDP plan.

## 6. TOF LANDING ARCHITECTURE (matters for our funnel)
Almost every medical-brand TOF creative routes to an **advertorial/listicle bridge**, not straight to the store — exactly the ad → advertorial → PDP Discovery Stack we're building (RESEARCH.md §5B):
- Healora → `offer.tryhealora.com/…/adv/<angle>` (e.g. `/adv/7-reasons-your-skin-fold-rash-keeps-coming-back`, `/adv/nurse-authority`, `/adv-doctors-dismissal`), `shop.healora.com/pages/pdp-ww`
- Clairon → `/pages/funnel-wca|wau|wus|wuk|men`, `/pages/5-reasons-men`, `/pages/diab-listicle-01` (suffixes = geo/audience splits of one funnel)
- Veloma → `/pages/boob-itch-advertorial`, `/pages/listicle_1`, `/pages/breasts-if-the-itch-underback`
- Orgatics → `/pages/intertrigo-3` · DermaSoothX → `/7signsofsensitizedskin` · Lupina → `/pages/intertrigo-adv2`
Comfort/video brands (Megababe/Fresh/Ionix) more often run **cold to a product page**. Takeaway: the proven cold path for the *story/mechanism* angles is a bridge page; the *demo/UGC* angles can go cold-to-PDP.
