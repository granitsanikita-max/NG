## 1 · STRATEGIC FRAME (why these angles, not the niche's winners)

**The niche's #1 proven cold hook is OFF-LIMITS for us.** The longest-running competitor videos (Veloma 199d,
Clairon, Lupina) all run the **"it's not chafing — it's a fungal infection / it's yeast"** misdiagnosis
reframe. ProudFold is locked to the **comfort/barrier lane: NO antifungal / cure / yeast / "kills" claims**
(COPY §8, MSDS, FTC). So we **cannot** copy the medical reframe.

**Our lane = the COMFORT sub-lane** the doc flags as the video winners: **Megababe / Lume / Fresh / Body
Glide / Thigh Society** — UGC **glide/barrier demos, confessions, problem-solution.** That's where comfort
brands win cold, it's fully compliant, and it fits our ICP's trust map.

**Three things every ad must respect (from COPY §1–§5):**
- **Trust map:** peers > influencers > brands. → **UGC / real-woman voice**, never brand/founder voice.
- **The one job (dominant): CHANGE STATE** — *"I've tried everything / maybe it's just me / I'm gross"* →
  *"it was never me, it's **friction**, and the honest barrier made for the fold finally worked."*
- **Belief chain to install:** B1 not-my-fault-it's-friction · B2 everything-failed-wrong-tool · B3
  a-barrier-breaks-the-cycle · B4 I-can-trust-this-one · B5 worth-trying-once-more.

**Compliance red-lines baked into every hook:** comfort/barrier/anti-chafe only — "stops the rub, keeps the
fold dry, soothes, cools, stays put." Never cure/heal/treat/antifungal/yeast. Describe friction discomfort
("raw from rubbing," "stings by noon"), **not** disease. Keep it **dignified** — skip the competitors'
"musty odor / smell" disgust beats (undignified + reads as infection).

## 3 · THE PLAN — 3 FORMATS × 3 VARIANTS = 9 VIDEO ADS

Test matrix = **format (how it persuades) × angle (the emotional entry).** All UGC, phone-shot, vertical
9:16, re-cast for a **45–62 big-busted / plus-size woman.** Each ~20–40s. Full scripts + visual hooks come
next (SCRIPTS.md); this fixes the concept, hook direction, belief, and destination.

### FORMAT A — UGC GLIDE / BARRIER DEMO  *(show it work — rational + scroll-stop; ref V1 Megababe)*
Real-seeming woman, phone cam, swipes ProudFold on a fold, shows it glide on and stay. States the physics
plainly. Most on-lens, lowest ban-risk. **→ Destination: advertorial (Wave 1); candidate for cold-to-PDP split in Wave 2.**
- **A1 — "The workaround I'm done with."** Hook: *"This summer I'm done tucking my dress between my thighs
  so they don't rub raw."* Swipe on inner thigh → glide. Names the shameful workaround, makes the barrier
  literal. Belief B1+B3. (Megababe "tucking my dress" adapted.)
- **A2 — "Under-bust, stays put till bedtime."** Hook: *"If your bra line is raw and stinging by noon —
  watch this."* Demo under-bust; contrast beat: powder clumps to paste vs the cream stays smooth. Big-busted
  specific. Belief B3. (ref V1 + S10/stays-put.)
- **A3 — "Stop the rub, not chase the rash."** Hook: *"Every cream I tried chased the rash. This stops the
  rubbing that causes it."* Mechanism-forward: two surfaces grind → barrier slides between → glide (our
  "second skin"). Belief B2+B3.

### FORMAT B — FIRST-PERSON CONFESSION MONOLOGUE  *(feel it — emotional; ref V5 Clairon)*
One woman, to camera, raw and honest. Shame → *not your fault, it's friction* → the barrier that helped.
Comfort-framed, dignified. **→ Destination: advertorial bridge → PDP.**
- **B1 — "12 summers, a folded tissue under each breast."** Hook (the proven opener, de-medicalized):
  *"For twelve summers I kept a folded tissue under each breast just to get through the day."* → "it was
  never hygiene, it's friction" → the barrier. Belief B1.
- **B2 — "It was never you."** Hook: *"Two doctors glanced for one second and told me to lose weight. It was
  never that — it's friction where your skin folds."* Anger at the wrong enemy → relief. Belief B1+B2
  (objection #8).
- **B3 — "I stopped hiding."** Hook: *"I started wearing cardigans in July so no one would get close to me."*
  Hiding → freedom/belonging (sundress, grandkids, pool). Identity payoff. Belief B5. (dignity angle,
  Veloma "granddaughter" adapted — comfort, not smell.)

### FORMAT C — 2-WOMAN CONFESSION SKIT  *(relate to it — social/dialogue; ref V4 Veloma 140d)*
Two older friends; one confides, the other reveals the barrier + handles her doubts on camera. Most natively
social; best feed-fit. **→ Destination: advertorial bridge → PDP.**
- **C1 — "The trip I almost didn't book."** One won't do a hot-climate holiday because of fold chafe; friend
  hands her the tube (situation-based, travel = Megababe "park day" pattern). Belief B3+B5.
- **C2 — "Another cream? I've tried them all."** The skeptic friend scoffs; the other handles the real
  objections out loud — scam / ships-from-US / no-sub-trap / "it's a barrier not a powder." Belief B4
  (objections #1,#2,#3,#5). Great for a solution-aware, burned audience.
- **C3 — "It's not a hygiene thing."** One whispers she thinks she's "just not clean enough"; friend reframes
  — *"it's not hygiene, it's friction, and nothing you tried was made for the fold."* De-shame core. Belief
  B1+B2.
