## 1 · STRATEGIC FRAME
- **The niche's #1 cold hook is OFF-LIMITS.** The longest/biggest cold winners run *"it's not chafing — it's a
  fungal infection/yeast"* + a **fake nurse/dermatologist**. ProudFold = comfort/barrier lane: **NO
  antifungal/cure/yeast claims, NO fake authority** (COPY §3, §8). Those two things are *why* their ads win —
  so we don't copy the angle, we copy the proven *structures* and fill them with our compliant story.
- **Our lane = the COMFORT sub-lane** (Megababe/Lume/Fresh/Body Glide): confession, reframe, demo. All **UGC**
  (trust map: peers > influencers > brands — COPY §2). Never brand/founder voice.
- **AUTHORITY — yes, but real, not faked (Nikita confirmed 2026-10-26).** Every ad should carry authority.
  Source = a **peer-expert**: a real-seeming woman who lived it ~20 years, tried everything, and *learned what's
  actually going on.* Authority comes from (a) lived experience + (b) the REAL mechanism stated as **learned
  fact** (*"the fold just never dries — it's friction, not hygiene"*), plus an honest *"I'm not a doctor, I just
  lived it"* beat (raises trust with our skeptical buyer). **Never** invent a nurse/derm who diagnosed her or
  endorsed the product (= the "wound-care nurse" line we already killed on the advertorial; same FTC/Meta risk,
  one step removed). A genuine credentialed voice = a **real partnered pro**, truthfully named — Wave 2 upgrade.
- **The one job (dominant): CHANGE STATE** — *"I've tried everything / maybe it's just me"* → *"it was never
  me, it's friction, and the honest barrier made for the fold finally worked."*
- **Belief chain:** B1 not-my-fault-it's-friction · B2 everything-failed-wrong-tool · B3 a-barrier-breaks-the-
  cycle · B4 I-can-trust-this-one · B5 worth-trying-once-more.
- **⚠ Mechanism differentiator (do NOT copy the competitor payoff):** Clairon/Veloma promise their product
  *"dries the fold to a powder."* **Ours is the opposite** — an occlusive **barrier that stays creamy so skin
  glides instead of grinds.** Never promise "dries you out." Our reframe: *"every cream either sat there wet or
  dried you out and cracked — this is a barrier that lets skin glide."* (This is also our honest con, COPY §5 #5.)

## 2 · COLD-FITNESS EVIDENCE (live creative scans, 2026-10-26)
Days-running = the market's verdict on whether a creative survives *cold*.
- **V1 Glide demo — Megababe `946169165228595`:** 19s, **retail brand** (9yr, 216k IG, 44k visits/mo), **30d**,
  → PDP. = warm/brand-aware demo, **not a proven cold DR unit.** Compliant + on-lens, but demote from cold.
- **V3 Expert VSL — Veloma `2275120106600267`:** ~3-min VSL, **18d**, → antifungal-soap PDP. Fake authority +
  "biofilm/destroys the fungus/tea tree." **Off-limits (both sins).**
- **V4 2-woman skit — Veloma `938409122575836`:** **141d**, → **advertorial** (`/pages/boob-itch-advertorial`).
  Proven cold workhorse. Structure: embarrassing moment → friend reframes "it's not hygiene" → powder-cakes-to-
  paste → "try X" → objection handling (scam/"how's it different"/severity) → CTA. **Fully adaptable** (strip
  candida/dermatologist/"gone in 2 weeks").
- **V5 Confession — Clairon `1072104489143020`:** 134s cinematic story, **22d**, de-shame spine (*"it was never
  me, it's the fold"*; cardigan-for-12-years → freedom). Emotional = our B1. ⚠ Payoff "dries to a powder" →
  **swap to barrier-glide.** Also trims odor-shame + the "40% off" tail (keep it cold).
- **V10 Failure-stack — Veloma `1477105807243842`:** **197d**, first-person, *"you've tried the creams, the
  sprays, the powders… so why is the itch still back?"* Compliant, proven cold.

**Takeaway:** the proven-cold units we can actually run are the **story formats (skit 141d, confession,
failure-stack 197d)** — not the glide demo (warm) and not the fungal/expert VSLs (banned).

## 3 · THE PLAN — 3 FORMATS × 3 VARIANTS = 9 VIDEO ADS
All UGC, vertical 9:16, re-cast for a 45–62 big-busted/plus-size woman, ~25–45s (skit/confession can run
longer like the 134–141s proven ones). Each → the **advertorial** → PDP. AI-UGC for Wave 1.

### FORMAT 1 — 2-WOMAN CONFESSION SKIT  *(dialogue/social — proven 141d, V4)*
Two older friends; one confides, the other reframes + handles doubts on camera.
- **1A — "The day/trip it ruined."** Humid wedding/church/holiday; she left early / nearly didn't go. Friend:
  *"it's not a hygiene thing — it's friction, and that powder just caked to paste."* → reveal → CTA. B1+B3.
- **1B — "Another cream? I've tried them all."** Skeptic friend; the other handles the real objections out
  loud — scam / ships-from-US / no-sub-trap / *"it's a barrier, it stays creamy, that's the point."* B4.
- **1C — "I thought I was just being disgusting."** Pure de-shame: *"it was never you — it's the fold, and
  nothing you tried was built for it."* B1+B2.

### FORMAT 2 — FIRST-PERSON CONFESSION STORY  *(emotional — proven pattern, V5)*
One woman, honest to camera (or lightly cinematic). Shame → not-your-fault → the barrier.
- **2A — "12 summers, a folded tissue."** The cardigan/hiding → freedom arc; *"it was never me, it's the
  fold."* Mechanism = barrier-glide (NOT "dries to powder"). B1+B5.
- **2B — "Two doctors said lose weight."** Anger at the wrong enemy → *"it was never that — it's friction."*
  B1+B2 (obj #8).
- **2C — "I stopped hiding."** Cardigans-in-July → sundress / grandkids' hug / pool. Belonging payoff. B5.

### FORMAT 3 — PEER-EXPERT REFRAME VSL  *(authority + rational — proven 197d V10, + the compliant half of V2/V3)*
The expert VSL Nikita wants — but authority from a **peer-expert** (lived it ~20 yrs, learned the mechanism),
NOT a fake nurse. First-person, talking-head or VO over B-roll. "Real reason" cold hook, no fungal/endorser.
Every variant opens with lived-experience authority + an honest "I'm not a doctor, I just lived it."
- **3A — "The creams, the powders, the sprays…"** Failure-stack → *"after 20 years I finally learned why they
  all failed: borrowed and chasing the rash"* → the barrier that stops the rub. B2+B3.
- **3B — "It's not a hygiene thing."** The compliant misdiagnosis reframe, as what she learned: *"that's not
  poor hygiene and it's not just chafing — it's friction + trapped moisture where skin folds."* B1.
- **3C — "Wet or dried-out — never a barrier."** Differentiation reframe: every cream either sat there wet or
  dried you out and cracked; this is a barrier that lets skin glide. B2+B3.

**Extra asset (not a primary cold format):** the **UGC glide/barrier demo** (V1-style) → use for
**retargeting + a cold-to-PDP test**, since it's on-lens but proven only warm.
