## §2B · Compliance — SAY / DON'T-SAY (US cosmetic + Meta)
Product sits on the cosmetic–drug fence. Zinc oxide = OTC **skin-protectant** (21 CFR 347.10(u)); undecylenic acid = OTC **antifungal** (21 CFR 333.210(f), only 10–25%, only tinea) — and ours is **0.01%** → any antifungal/"kills yeast"/"cures intertrigo" claim = **unapproved new drug + FTC exposure.** Market purely as a **skin-protectant barrier.**

| ✅ SAFE TO SAY | ❌ NEVER SAY |
|---|---|
| "Forms a protective barrier over skin." | "Cures / treats / heals intertrigo." |
| "Temporarily protects chafed, chapped or cracked skin." (monograph) | "Kills the yeast / fungus / bacteria causing your rash." |
| "Creates a shield between skin folds to reduce chafing + moisture buildup." | "Antifungal treatment for intertrigo / skin-fold infection." |
| "Soothes and conditions skin prone to rubbing and sweat." | "Do you have intertrigo? / Do you suffer from skin-fold rash?" (personal attribute) |
| "For anyone whose skin rubs, sweats and gets irritated — nurses, parents, anyone on their feet." (by activity) | "Are you embarrassed by the raw rash under YOUR belly?" (attribute + shame) |
| "Helps keep the area feeling dry and comfortable." (sensory) | "Clinically proven to cure Candida / heal HS / infected folds." |

**Meta Personal Attributes rule:** "you/your" is fine; **"you" + a condition is not.** Blessed pattern = *"New [X] now available"* (→ *"Skin-protectant barrier cream — now available"*). Reach by **context** (summer, sweat, long shifts, the nurse's locker); let them self-identify. **Landing pages are reviewed too.**
