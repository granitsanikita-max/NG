## What this CHANGES (read before writing any claim)

1. **"Antifungal / kills yeast / treats the infection" is DEAD — twice over.**
   Undecylenic acid is **0.01%**. The OTC antifungal monograph (21 CFR 333.210(f)) needs **10–25%**.
   At 0.01% the claim is both an **illegal unapproved-drug claim** AND **factually unsubstantiated**.
   We never say it. (This is the landmine flagged all through Step 1 — now confirmed.)

2. **"Zinc-oxide barrier cream" framing is WRONG.** Zinc oxide is **0.01%** (trace), not the barrier.
   The real occlusive barrier is **mineral oil 5% + glyceryl stearate 1% + glycerin 5%.**
   Correct the mechanism wording everywhere: **"occlusive barrier," not "zinc barrier."**

3. **The comfort/barrier mechanism STILL HOLDS — just on a different engine.**
   - Occlusive film (mineral oil + glyceryl stearate + glycerin) = real friction-reducing "second skin."
   - Allantoin 0.10% = soothes / conditions (legal cosmetic claim).
   - Menthol (mint) 0.01% = the cooling sensation people *feel* working.
   - All of these are **cosmetic claims**, all legal. The "second skin where your skin meets itself" metaphor is intact.

4. **Texture confirmed:** creamy, white, pH 6–7, 500–800 cps, water-based → **stays a cream.**
   Do NOT claim "dries to a powder" or "keeps the fold bone-dry." It is a leave-on comfort/barrier cream.

5. **Supplier = Hong Kong Cyravis Technology Ltd** → "ships from US" is a **dropship fulfilment** story,
   not a manufacturing one. Matters for the honesty/About-page angle: be truthful about where it's made.
