# Doc 1 — Market, Competition & Pricing (ProudFold)

Market = **USA / English** · Research date 2026-10-20 · Mirror of Notion "Doc 1 — Market, Competition & Pricing".

## Top 5 takeaways
1. **Real but seasonal demand, and we're in the trough.** Amazon shows **207 "intertrigo cream" SKUs, 39 with a "bought in past month" badge**, and commodity zinc-oxide barrier creams moving **10K–60K units/mo** — but the condition peaks **Mar–Aug** and incumbents are de-scaling now (Healora **1,227 → 536** active ads). → **Launch lean now, scale into spring.**
2. **It's a 4-brand fight on a commodity, anchored by a 268-day-proven Healora control at $29.95.** We can't out-spend or out-"mechanism" a $7–12.5M/mo leader. → **The only open lane is identity.**
3. **Market sophistication = Stage 4 tipping into 5.** Of 26 competitor ads, **13 lead with a mechanism, 10 with authority/identity, only 3 plain** — and **nobody owns a clean, non-fake identity.** → **Win on a Stage-5 plus-size identity carrying the proven mechanism, not a new claim.**
4. **Every incumbent is bleeding from the same self-inflicted wounds** (their own 1-star reviews): overseas slow shipping, hidden/hard-to-cancel subscriptions (Orgatics' #1 complaint = *unauthorized sub charges*), fake nurse/doctor personas. → **Our offer = honest easy-cancel subscription + real founder; our enemy = the scammy overclaiming funnel.**
5. **Economics are fat; the constraint is CAC + ad-ban risk + LTV, not margin.** At $29.95, contribution margin is **73.7% ($22.08), break-even ROAS 1.36.** → **Price at the proven $29.95 anchor + $49.95 3-pack + an honest Subscribe-&-Save; it's a consumable, so reorders are the business.**

## Audit first — the control we're modeling
Highest-traffic competitor = **Healora** (1.04M visits/mo). Funnel = **advertorial persona story** ("Dr. Rebecca Mills," "Barbara Collins," "Nancy Anderson") → PDP with a 1x/2x/3x bundle selector. **Weak (our openings):** fake persona (not a real founder); overseas/slow fulfilment + refund-process complaints; hidden auto-ship people can't skip; faceless funnel, not a brand. Every one is an attack surface for a real, US-ship, no-trap, plus-size identity brand.

## §0 · Product & core problem
A leave-on barrier cream that **reduces rubbing and protects raw skin where body folds press together** (under-breast, belly/apron, groin, inner thigh) — for **plus-size and large-breasted adults whose skin-on-skin folds chafe, burn and break down**, especially in heat and sweat.

**Mass desire (Schwartz):** Urgency 5/5 (*"searing pain," "armpits on FIRE,"* raw/weeping; buy mid-flare) · Staying power 5/5 (chronic, recurring; §2C failed_solution = 72, dominant) · Scope 4/5 (large, durable; plus-size/large-breasted/diabetic/elderly skew; US obesity ~35%+). → **A genuine painkiller market with high, persistent desire.** Not a nice-to-have.

## §0B · Demand & Seasonality (US)
**Stable-to-growing, strongly SEASONAL (summer-peaking), currently post-summer cooldown. Peak ≈ Mar–Aug, trough ≈ Oct–Jan.**
- **Search:** "intertrigo" is **not a tracked Exploding Topic** (problem-aware/paid, not viral). Nearest parent — **"Anti chafe stick": 33,100/mo, +60%**, clean annual summer peak (index 100 Mar-2026, troughs Oct–Dec).
- **Amazon (strongest hard signal):** **207 results**, **39 "bought in past month" badges** p1; dedicated intertrigo SKUs **100–1K+/mo** at $8.99–$26.99, commodity zinc-oxide barrier creams **10K–60K+/mo** at $5.48–$7.75.
- **TikTok Shop:** **zero** human intertrigo cream; adjacent anti-chafe powders/balms do **$18k–$129k/mo** at $19.95–$29.90 (Dr.Melaxin CACTOX $129k, HAPPY CURVES powder $51k). Cream SKU = white space on TTS. *(Note: ProudFold runs Facebook, not TTS — this is context, not a channel plan.)*
- **Meta:** a **4-advertiser fight** (Healora + advertorial fronts, Orgatics "Intertrigo Reset," Veloma, new tester Lupina). **Longest-running ad = Healora "Still dealing with painful skin fold rash? 50% off" — 268+ days, still live.** Healora active ads **peaked 1,227 (Sep) → ~536 (Oct)** = cooling off the summer peak.

**3 indirect (searched the PAIN):** anti-chafe **apparel** (Thigh Society, Step One, Shapermint — own *"prevent the rub with a garment"*); anti-chafe **powders** (HAPPY CURVES, Happy Nuts — own *"keep it dry,"* strong on TTS); **commodity Amazon barrier/antifungal creams** (Desitin/Lotrimin-class, $5–13 — the budget first-try).

**TAKEN map — owned vs open:**
- Healora = *"kill yeast + friction + heat → dry to powder, treat all three"* · Veloma = *"break the biofilm (soap)"* · Orgatics = *"treat the fold from the inside (oral)"* · apparel = *"prevent with a garment"* · powders = *"keep it dry."*
- **OPEN / UNCLAIMED:** a **plus-size IDENTITY** brand ("fold care made on purpose for bodies like mine"), a **real honest founder** (vs fake personas), an **easy-cancel subscription + US-ship promise**.

## §5 · Awareness level
Enter at **solution-aware**: she knows the problem, she's burned by failed fixes, she wants to know *what actually works and who to trust*. Funnel = **advertorial pre-sell** that validates her exhaustion, delivers the mechanism + proof, then the offer.

## §7 · Funnel level
Launch traffic = **cold, top-of-funnel** at a solution-aware stage. Ad's job = stop the scroll on the *emotional* truth (not a product shot); first page = a **pre-sell advertorial** (story + mechanism + proof), not straight-to-PDP — Stage-4/5 skepticism needs the bridge.
