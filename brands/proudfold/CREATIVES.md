## Generation settings (every scene)
- **Model:** Nano Banana Pro (`nano_banana_pro`), **2K**, **vertical 9:16** (1080×1920).
- **count = 1** per generation (hard rule — no variation spam / credits).
- **Character lock:** attach the **[LINDA]** 4-panel character sheet to EVERY scene so her face/hair/body stay
