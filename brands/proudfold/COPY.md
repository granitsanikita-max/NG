## §1 · THE POINT SENTENCE + THE ONE JOB  (Fundamental 1)

**Point sentence:**
> *After reading, a plus-size or big-busted woman who has tried everything for the raw, burning rash
> where her skin folds rub believes **it was never her fault and never a hygiene failure — it's friction
> + trapped moisture**, that **everything failed because it was borrowed from babies, feet or the
> pharmacy and built to chase the rash instead of stop the rub**, and — because a real person like her
> who tested it all is telling her so — that **the honest barrier cream made for the fold is worth trying**
> — and she clicks through to the offer page to get it.*

**The one job (ranked — dominant first):**
1. **CHANGE STATE** (dominant) — *"I've tried everything / maybe it's just me / I'm gross"* → *"it was
   never me, it's friction, and a woman who went through exactly this says the honest one finally worked."*
2. **INSTALL THE MECHANISM** — the occlusive "second skin" barrier that stops skin grinding on skin.
3. **PRE-HANDLE OBJECTIONS** — reframed as *the reviewer's own doubts, checked and resolved.*

If a block doesn't serve one of those three → cut it.

## §3 · FORMAT & NARRATOR  (the correction)

**Format: Story advertorial — a first-person honest REVIEW by an independent third party** (a real-seeming
fellow sufferer), with an embedded priced/effort "graveyard" of everything she tried and a plain-voice
mechanism. 13 beats (§7A). Listicle re-voiced as a reviewer list = test alt (§7B).

**The narrator = an independent reviewer, unaffiliated with ProudFold.** A plus-size / big-busted woman,
~45–60, who had the rash for years, tried the whole aisle, figured out what finally worked, and is now
writing to help women like her. She is **not** the founder, **not** "the brand," has **no** business
stake she's hiding. Her credibility = *"I've been exactly where you are, I tested all of it, and nobody
paid me to say this."* The brand appears **only** as her conclusion: *"the one that was actually built
for this, on purpose — and here's what I checked before I trusted it."*

**Why this converts harder than the brand/founder voice:**
- Her objections become **the reviewer's own doubts, investigated** — *"I figured it was another scam, so
  I checked where it ships from and the cancel terms myself"* — which kills the objection far better than
  a brand asserting "we're honest."
- The brand's honesty points (US-ship, no sub-trap, honest about what it is) land as **independent
  findings**, not self-praise. A third party reporting "it's honest" beats the brand claiming it.
- It fits her trust map (peers > influencers > brands) and the "tried everything" emotional category.

**⚠ HONESTY / COMPLIANCE on the persona (read this — it's a real line, not a formality):**
The brand's whole stance is *anti-fake-persona.* A third-party-review advertorial is only consistent with
that — and only FTC-safe — if **all four hold:**
1. **No fake authority.** The reviewer is a regular person, NOT a fake "nurse / dermatologist / Dr. ___."
   (Faking medical authority is exactly the enemy's sin in brain/01 + brain/04.)
2. **Every factual claim she "reports" is TRUE** (ships from US, ingredients, no forced sub, what it does).
3. **It's disclosed as an advertisement** in the footer, and if the reviewer is a composite/not a specific
   real person, a **fictional-customer note** sits in the footer (standard, e.g. Miracle; ④ Fundamental 8).
4. **No claim the product can't substantiate** goes in her mouth (no "cured my intertrigo," no "killed the
   yeast") — the reviewer stays in comfort/barrier language just like the brand must (§8).
**Best version = a REAL plus-size reviewer** (ties off the open "no verified creator" gap in the brain).
Interim = a disclosed, relatable composite reviewer. **Nikita decides which — §12.**

## §6 · INFORMATION INVENTORY  (everything she must be TOLD to buy → where)

1. **What the problem actually is** — skin-on-skin traps heat + moisture → friction + maceration → raw,
   re-irritated skin. De-shamed ("friction, not hygiene"). *(Describe it; never "do YOU have intertrigo.")* → Beats 2–4.
2. **Why everything failed** — borrowed + chases the rash; powder → paste; cornstarch *"feeds yeast"*;
   antifungals treat a symptom; diaper cream is for babies. → Beat 3.
3. **How it works** — occlusive barrier film (mineral oil 5% + glyceryl stearate + glycerin 5%) sits in
   the fold so **skin glides instead of grinds**; allantoin soothes; menthol cools. **"Second skin where
   your skin meets itself."** → Beat 9.
4. **What's in it (honest ingredient story)** — simple, fragrance-light barrier ingredients; name the
   soothing (allantoin) + cooling (menthol). Ingredient → benefit arrows. → Beat 9.
5. **What it IS and ISN'T** — comfort/barrier cream that stops the rub, soothes, cools. **NOT** antifungal,
   **NOT** a cure, **won't "dry to a powder."** Reviewer saying this = her honest con = trust. → Beats 9 + 11.
6. **Who it's for** — plus-size + big-busted, on purpose; also anyone whose skin rubs/sweats. By situation,
   not diagnosis. → Headline + Beats 2, 10.
7. **Texture / sensory truth** — creamy, a little goes a long way, stays put, brief cooling tingle. → Beats 9, 11.
8. **How to use it** — clean + dry the fold as best you can, thin layer in the fold, reapply after sweat/shower.
   Acknowledge *"I can't reach/dry it"* (S009) + a tip. → Beat 9 + post-purchase guide.
9. **Trust / who's behind it** — reviewer reports: real brand, US-ship, honest about where it's made, no
   fake nurses. → Beat 6.
10. **The offer + guarantee** — 60-day keep-it guarantee, no forced sub, one-click cancel, honest size +
    value. *(Full card on the PDP; advertorial teases it as "why I felt safe trying it.")* → Beat 12.
11. **Why now (honest)** — no fake countdown, no fake summer urgency (we're in the trough — don't lie). Honest
    "why now" = you've suffered long enough + a real launch offer. → Beat 12.

## §8 · COMPLIANCE RED-LINES — applied to a REVIEW advertorial  (brain/04 §2B + MSDS + FTC)

**Keeps a young Meta account alive; the landing page is reviewed too. Reviewer voice tempts bigger claims — hold the line.**

- ✅ **SAY (even in her mouth):** "forms a protective barrier," "temporarily protects chafed/chapped skin,"
  "a shield between the folds that cut the chafing + moisture," "soothed," "stayed comfortable," "the cooling
  felt good," "my skin finally got a break."
- ❌ **NEVER (not even as a 'review'):** "cured/healed my intertrigo," "killed the yeast," "antifungal,"
  "dries to a powder / kept me bone-dry," "Do YOU have [condition]?", graphic raw-skin before/after.
- **A testimonial can't carry a claim the product can't substantiate (FTC).** Her review stays in
  comfort/barrier language. No "it cured me."
- **Disclose:** advertisement disclaimer in the footer, every time. If the reviewer is composite/not a
  named real person → **fictional-customer note** in the footer. No fake medical credential, ever.
- **Mechanism wording (MSDS-corrected):** **"occlusive barrier"** (mineral oil + glyceryl stearate +
  glycerin), **never "zinc."** Zinc + undecylenic = 0.01% trace → antifungal claim is illegal AND false.
- **Honesty = legal armor:** no fake reviews, no fake countdowns/stock meters, no "Made in USA" if
  fulfilment is dropship from the HK supplier. Everything the reviewer "reports" must be TRUE.

4. **The simple-barrier pedigree** (soft/attributed; don't lean on zinc — ours is trace).
5. **Community voices framed as "what women say about this problem"** (VOC) — NOT as ProudFold reviews.

## ③ WHY IT KEEPS HAPPENING (AND WHY IT'S NEVER BEEN YOUR FAULT)

For twenty years I thought this was my fault.

I thought I wasn't clean enough. I thought if I just lost the weight like one doctor told me to — in about ten seconds, without really looking — it would finally go away. I believed that. For two decades.

I was wrong. And so was that doctor.

Here's what's actually going on down there, and once you see it, you can't un-see it:

- **It burns because skin is rubbing on skin.** Where a fold sits, two patches of your own skin press and drag against each other all day. That friction wears the skin raw. *(Customer problem 1)*
- **It traps heat and sweat with nowhere to go.** A fold is warm, damp and closed-in. The moisture can't dry out, so the skin stays soft, wet and fragile — perfect conditions for it to break down. *(Customer problem 2)*
