## §1 · THE POINT SENTENCE + THE ONE JOB  (Fundamental 1 — write this before any copy)

**Point sentence:**
> *After reading, a plus-size or big-busted woman who has tried everything for the raw, burning rash
> where her skin folds rub believes **it was never her fault and never a hygiene failure — it's
> friction + trapped moisture**, that **everything failed because it was borrowed from babies, feet
> or the pharmacy and built to chase the rash instead of stop the rub**, and that **ProudFold is the
> first honest barrier made for her fold on purpose, by a real person who won't scam or trap her** —
> and she is ready to click through to the offer page and try it.*

**The one job (ranked — this is a multi-job page, dominant job first):**
1. **CHANGE STATE** (dominant) — move her from *"I've tried everything / maybe it's just me / I'm
   gross"* → *"it was never me, it's friction, and someone finally built the honest thing for my body."*
   Her dominant state is **exhaustion + shame** (failed_solution 72/185, emotional_state 35). De-shame first.
2. **INSTALL THE MECHANISM** — the occlusive "second skin" barrier that stops skin grinding on skin.
3. **PRE-HANDLE OBJECTIONS** — scam / sub-trap / burns / too thick / price / "for someone like me."

If a block does not serve one of those three jobs → cut it.

## §6 · INFORMATION INVENTORY  (everything she must be TOLD to buy — nothing load-bearing missing)

Each item = a fact she needs before she can click. Mapped to where it's delivered.

1. **What the problem actually is** — skin-on-skin traps heat + moisture → friction + maceration →
   raw, broken, re-irritated skin. Framed general/de-shamed ("it's not hygiene, it's friction").
   *(Compliance: describe the problem; never "do YOU have intertrigo?")* → Beats 2–4.
2. **Why everything failed** — borrowed products that chase the rash; powder turns to paste; cornstarch
   *"feeds yeast"*; antifungals treat a symptom; diaper cream is made for babies. → Beat 3 (graveyard).
3. **How ProudFold works** — an **occlusive barrier film** (mineral oil 5% + glyceryl stearate +
   glycerin 5%) sits in the fold so **skin glides instead of grinds**; allantoin soothes; menthol
   cools. Metaphor: **"a second skin where your skin meets itself."** → Beat 9.
4. **What's in it (the honest ingredient story)** — simple, fragrance-light barrier ingredients; name
   the soothing (allantoin) + cooling (menthol) ones. Ingredient → benefit arrows. → Beat 9.
5. **What it IS and ISN'T** (the centerpiece) — it's a **comfort/barrier cream that stops the rub,
   soothes and cools.** It is **NOT** an antifungal, **NOT** a cure, and it **won't "dry to a powder."**
   Saying this out loud is both the trust weapon and the compliance-safe position. → Beats 9 + 11.
6. **Who it's for** — plus-size + big-busted bodies, on purpose; also anyone whose skin rubs/sweats
   (nurses, caregivers, on-their-feet, GLP-1 loose skin, new diabetics, summer). *By situation, not
   diagnosis.* → Headline + Beats 2, 10.
7. **Texture / sensory truth** — creamy (stays a cream), a little goes a long way, stays put, a brief
   cooling tingle. Set honestly so the tingle = "working," not "burning." → Beats 9, 11.
8. **How to use it** — clean + dry the fold as best you can, smooth a thin layer in the fold, reapply
   after a sweat or shower. Acknowledge the *"I can't reach/dry it"* pain (S009) + a tip. → Beat 9 +
   post-purchase guide (store).
9. **Trust / who's behind it** — real founder, real face, honest about where it's made + how it ships.
   No fake nurses. → Beats 5, 10.
10. **The offer + guarantee** — 60-day keep-it guarantee, no forced sub, one-click cancel, honest size
    + value, multi-tube option. *(Full stacked card lives on the offer/PDP; advertorial teases it.)* → Beat 12 / handoff.
11. **Why now (honest)** — NOT a fake countdown and NOT fake summer urgency (we're in the seasonal
    trough — don't lie). The honest "why now" = you've suffered long enough + a real launch offer. → Beat 12.

## §8 · COMPLIANCE RED-LINES — applied to the advertorial  (brain/04 §2B + msds-composition.md)

**This keeps a young Meta ad account alive. The landing page is reviewed too — same rules as the ad.**

- ✅ **SAY:** "forms a protective barrier," "temporarily protects chafed/chapped skin" (monograph
  language), "creates a shield between folds to reduce chafing + moisture buildup," "soothes and
  conditions," "helps the area feel comfortable + cool," reach "anyone whose skin rubs, sweats and
  gets irritated."
- ❌ **NEVER:** "cures/treats/heals intertrigo/candida/HS," "kills the yeast/fungus/bacteria,"
  "antifungal," "dries to a powder / keeps you bone-dry," "**Do you have** [condition]?" (personal
  attribute), "are you embarrassed by the rash under YOUR belly?", any graphic raw-skin before/after.
- **Mechanism wording is CORRECTED by the MSDS:** say **"occlusive barrier"** (mineral oil + glyceryl
  stearate + glycerin), **never "zinc-oxide barrier"** — zinc is 0.01% trace. Undecylenic acid is
  0.01% → the antifungal claim is illegal AND false. The docs' "zinc barrier" wording is stale.
- **Honesty = legal armor (FTC):** no fake reviews, no fake doctor/nurse, no fake countdown/stock
  meter, no "Made in USA" if fulfilment is dropship from the HK supplier. The honesty pledge is both
  the brand and the compliance position.
- **Editorial costume stays honest:** real byline, current date, advertisement disclaimer in footer;
  if a customer story is fictional/composite it is **disclosed in the footer** (but see §10 — prefer real).

## §9 · VOICE, CRAFT & TECHNIQUE  (Reference — Copywriting Fundamentals)

**Fundamentals active in every line:** argument > words (build the case, layer power words on the
edit pass) · slippery slide (3–6 word vacuum opener; each line earns the next) · 5th–8th grade,
active voice · speed to value · emotional resonance (recognition → anger at the enemy → relief) ·
human voice (her words; run **stop-slop**) · anticipate + resolve objections (at the price + before
the CTA) · **honesty (raise flaws first)** · consciousness method (reach the **identity/dignity** depth).

**Techniques to pull (per surface = cold advertorial, solution-aware, Stage 4→5):**
belief change · curiosity & contrast (open loops — pay every one off) · "that's-why" evidence ·
let-them-reach-the-conclusion · facts-over-opinions (state the ingredient %, don't adjective it) ·
desire to belong (the fold-care crew) · "I was just like you — but worse" (adapted: the honest
outsider who watched the scam) · the call-out · honest under-claim · sell-the-cure-not-prevention
(lead with the acute burning she feels NOW, not "prevent future rash").

**Her swipe words (use verbatim, confirm Reddit ones at source first):** "fold distress," "chub rub,"
"underboob," "BIG BOOB CREW," "fuller fluffy girl," "heavy set," "big busted," "boob sweat," "the
powder turns to paste, the creams stay wet," "tried everything, nothing works," "made for my body,"
"my absolute savior," "I'm constantly lifting my fat," "nobody made this on purpose."
**Avoid (clinical/shaming):** intertrigo, candidiasis, macerated, antifungal, obese, hygiene, "lose weight."
