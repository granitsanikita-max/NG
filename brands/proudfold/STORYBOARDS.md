## 0 · THE FORMAT (what Nikita showed — reaction-cam + arresting B-roll + one bold caption)
Reference he sent = a fungus VSL: a **small talking-head box in the corner**, the **rest of the 9:16 filled with
an arresting full-screen B-roll**, one **big 2-line caption** across the lower third. The gross macro is what
stops the scroll.

**⚠ THAT SCREENSHOT IS A CRAFT REFERENCE, NOT A CLAIM REFERENCE.** The two things that make it "work" — the
**fungus/pathogen imagery** and the **fake doctor in the lab coat** — are the exact two things that get our
account killed. We copy the *mechanics* (face-corner, visceral macro, bold caption, fast cuts) and fill them
with the **compliant** scroll-stopper: extreme-macro **friction / heat / skin-on-skin grind** + the **barrier
mechanism**. Visceral physics, never pathology. **No fungus. No open/raw wound. No lab coat. No "doctor."**

- **Face-cam = Linda** (locked brand model ~50, HF `c77e8935` / `00_brand_model`) — warm, real, same woman across
  the whole funnel. Talking-head recorded once, composited into the corner.
- **Caption:** burned-in, **word-by-word highlight** (CapCut/reels style), 5–7 words max on screen, key word
  terracotta. Hook caption is bigger (fills more of the band).
- **Audio:** one locked VO voice (same as advertorial). Light bed music, low. SFX accents on the hard cuts.

### Face-cam behavior (how each variant differs — keeps the A/B clean)
- **3A (peer montage):** face-cam **persistent** lower-left; it shrinks to a small bubble (or cuts away) on the
  full-screen mechanism animation + the macro demos, returns full-corner for bridge/payoff/CTA.
- **3B (animation-led):** the **full-screen animation is the hero** — face-cam appears **only** on the hook,
  payoff, and CTA (smaller, ~30%); the mechanism beats are animation full-frame, no face. (Authority = the
  animation, never a persona — this is 3B's whole identity.)
- **3C (storyteller):** face-cam **persistent** corner through the then-vs-now + baby-cream story; goes
  full-screen on the mechanism + payoff montage. Warm storyteller energy.

### Cut-pacing doctrine (attention is short — fast where it counts, breathe where it proves)
| Section | Cut cadence | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook 0–4s** | frame-1 holds ~0.8s, then 2 hard cuts | the punch — scroll-stop + promise |
| Bridge | 2–3s / cut | set up the loop, meet the peer |
| **Mechanism / animation** | 2.5–4s / cut | let the animation *read* (seeing = believing) |
| Twist / enemy | 1.5–2.5s / cut | fast, build the indignation |
| **Payoff** | 1–1.5s / cut | rapid dignity montage = momentum |
| Trust / CTA | hold 3–4s | let the offer + guarantee land |
Target lengths: **3A ~85s · 3B ~70s · 3C ~85s** (lean cold-test cuts; trim to <60s alts if hold-rate is strong).

## 5 · PRODUCTION METHOD (how Ad Creation makes these)
1. **B-roll stills** → Higgsfield **nano_banana** (2K) per the library prompts; keep the warm comfort palette
   (terracotta/cream), realistic "good AI microscope" look for the science-look shots (NOT plasticky — the style
   Nikita already approved on the mechanism GIF). **HARD RULE (memory #122): count=1 always, never multiple
   variations** (credits).
2. **Motion** → Kling i2v 3s per still for drag/glow/glide. **Mechanism montage already rendered**
   (`mech_scene2–5.mp4` in scratchpad) — reuse; only new science-look (SL-XSEC-TRAP, SL-GRIND drag) needs generating.
3. **Face-cam (Linda)** → AI-UGC talking-head (locked model) for v1; shoot a real plus-size creator on the Wave-2
   winner. Record the full VO once (one locked voice) — she carries all three.
4. **Compositing** → corner face-cam + full-screen B-roll + caption, in CapCut/editor. **Captions = word-by-word
   highlight**, terracotta key words, thick stroke. Cuts per the pacing table. Light music bed + cut SFX.
5. **End-card** → `OF-BUYBOX` screen-grab from the live #pfbb + wordmark + CTA.
6. **Disclosure** → each ad carries the "advertisement" + fictional-narrator (composite) note (COPY §8).

### Compliance guardrail (every shot, every variant)
NO fungus / mold / pathogen / "bacteria" imagery · NO open wound / pus / graphic rash · NO before/after
"healing" skin · NO lab coat / "doctor" / fake credential · friction-redness FLAT (never a raised welt) ·
dignified bodies, no body-shaming · mechanism = illustrative animation, not real pathology.
