# SCRIPT 3A — Peer-Expert Mechanism VSL · "You've tried everything — here's why it ALL failed"
Format 3, Variant A. The flagship. Failure-stack → wrong-tool belief-flip → barrier mechanism → payoff → CTA.
Built on the 🔥 Elite **Belief-Changework Body** spine + the Lupina VSL production system (caption-driven montage).

## ② GOAL + TARGET

### Avatar — "Dawn" (the one woman I'm writing to)
- **Demographic:** woman, **45–62**, **plus-size and/or big-busted**, US, working/lower-middle income, suburban/rural, on Facebook (not TikTok). Reads reviews, skeptical of slick FB ads.
- **Experiences:** 10–20+ years of raw, burning rash/chafe where skin folds — under the breasts, the belly fold, inner thighs. Has tried **the whole aisle**: powders, cornstarch, diaper cream, anti-chafe sticks, antifungals, steroid creams, prescriptions, blow-drying the fold, a folded tissue/paper towel tucked under each breast, two showers on hot days. Dismissed by doctors ("just lose weight"). Spent **hundreds to thousands**. Burned by scammy FB creams (sub traps, "US-based" that ships from China).
- **Behaviors:** covers up in summer (the cardigan that never comes off), skips the pool/the dress/the date, checks and dabs the fold through the day, packs powder "for noon," stands a little further back from people.
- **Emotions:** **shame** ("I thought I was just being disgusting"), frustration tipping into **desperation**, **anger** at dismissive doctors + scam funnels, quiet **grief** for the life she's edited down, and a **hope she's afraid to feel again.**

### Core desire — the "why?" chain (write to the bottom, not the top)
Stop the rash/chafe → *why?* get through a hot day without stinging + checking → *why?* stop hiding and planning life around her skin → *why?* feel normal, clean, un-ashamed → *why?* **be close to people and take up space without fear = DIGNITY + BELONGING.**
→ **Write to dignity.** The rash is the surface; "you can stop hiding, and it was never your fault" is the core.

### Angle + emotion (the ONE problem + the exact feeling)
- **Angle:** *Everything you tried failed because it was the **wrong tool** — borrowed from babies/feet/the pharmacy and built to **chase the rash** instead of **stop the rub.***
- **Emotion to transfer:** **relief-through-vindication** (the exhale of *"it was never me, and it was never going to work — here's why"*) with a flash of **anger at the real enemy** (the borrowed products + the "it's complicated" lie). Valence negative→positive; intensity medium-high (vindication is shareable). **NOT fear** — we don't use the fungal-scare the competitors lean on.

## DIALS + FUNNEL

- **Awareness → entry:** **Solution-aware** (she's tried solutions; wants what works + who to trust). We ENTER on the **failure-stack** (problem-aware recognition — "you've tried everything") to earn the stop, then flip immediately to the **mechanism** (solution-aware). That pivot is the whole hook.
- **Sophistication → positioning:** **Stage 4 (mechanism war).** The feed is saturated with *complex* mechanisms — "it's a fungal/bacterial 3-cause cycle, attack it with 6 actives" (Veloma/Lupina/Clairon). Stage-4 play per the playbook = **easier / more-proven mechanism.** Ours: reject the complexity. **One simple, century-old, proven fix — a barrier that stops skin grinding on skin.** Simpler than their biofilm story and more proven (barrier/occlusive protection is as old as zinc and diaper cream). A touch of Stage-5 category ownership rides on top: *"the first cream actually built for the fold."*
- **Funnel move:** cold FB stranger (scroll) **→** believes *it's a wrong-tool problem, and the simple barrier is the fix* **→** clicks to the **advertorial** (→ PDP). Ad → advertorial → PDP.

## THE 3 MODIFIERS
- **Belief-change depth = skepticism × price.** Skepticism HIGH (burned, "tried everything," scam-wary); price moderate ($29.95–94.95). → **deep flip required:** dismantle "another cream won't work" by explaining *why every other thing failed* (category reframe) + why this is categorically different (barrier, not a rash-chaser) + strip the scam risk (US-ship, no-sub, keep-it guarantee).
- **Iceberg / identity depth:** identity-linked buy (dignity / "made for me") → climb **belief → identity**: land the de-shame belief first, close on identity ("built for the fold, for a body like mine").
- **Length / story arc:** ~75–90s VSL → **use anticipation + an open loop** ("the real reason nothing worked isn't a better cream…") paid off at the mechanism beat. (Deliberately tighter than Lupina's 3.5 min for a lean cold test.)

## ④ EXECUTION FUNDAMENTALS (written into the ad)
- **Authority = FACTS stated as facts** (no fake credential). Peer-expert **lived experience** ("20 years, tried all of it") + **new specific true info** ("a fold traps heat + moisture, so skin rubs on skin and never dries"). Honest *"I'm not a doctor, I just lived it"* beat = trust with a skeptic.
- **Positioning:** Stage-4 easier/more-proven mechanism (barrier, not 6-active complexity).
- **Speed to value:** by ~5s she's had the failure-stack recognition + the promise *"here's the real reason."*
- **Clarity:** 5th–8th grade, active voice, one idea per line.
- **Value lens (Hormozi):** lead with **ease/simplicity** — "one simple thing," "a thin line in the fold," "it just stops the rub." Crush time/effort.
- **Delivery & performance:** short, varied beats; energy up; crystal enunciation; every line is **on-screen caption AND spoken**; **SHOW the contrast** (powder→paste vs barrier→glide); cuts every 2–4s.
