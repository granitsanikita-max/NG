=== RETURN AND REFUND POLICY ===

ProudFold 60-Day Keep-It Comfort Guarantee

We stand behind ProudFold. If you're not comfortable with your results, you're covered for a full 60 days from the day your order arrives.

How it works:
Try ProudFold for up to 60 days. If it's not right for you, email us at hello@proudfold.com within 60 days of delivery and we'll refund your order. You do not need to ship anything back — keep the tube.

To request a refund, email hello@proudfold.com with the name you ordered under and your order number. We'll process approved refunds to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Depending on your bank or card provider, it can take a few extra days to appear on your statement.

Damaged, defective, or wrong items: If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, email us at hello@proudfold.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or issue a refund.

Free digital guides: The free digital guides included with your order are yours to keep, whether or not you request a refund.

There is no restocking fee and no return shipping to pay.

Questions? Email hello@proudfold.com — a real person reads every message.

=== SHIPPING POLICY ===

Shipping Policy

Where we ship: We currently ship to addresses within the United States.

Shipping cost: Shipping is free on all US orders — no minimum, no surprises at checkout.

Order processing: Orders are processed within 1–3 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email when you place your order, and a second email with tracking once it ships.

Delivery time: After processing, standard delivery takes 7–12 business days. Total time from order to doorstep is typically 2–3 weeks. Delivery times can vary slightly during holidays or peak periods.

Tracking: Every order ships with tracking. If you haven't received tracking within 5 business days of ordering, email us at hello@proudfold.com and we'll chase it down.

Delayed or lost orders: If your tracking hasn't moved in 10 or more business days, or your order hasn't arrived within 30 days, email us at hello@proudfold.com — we'll resend it or refund you. No hoops.

Wrong address: Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We can update it if you email us before the order ships; once it has shipped we can't reroute it.
