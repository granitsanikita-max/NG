## 2. The patterns that repeat across EVERY cluster (steal these)

1. **"Starter kit on order #1 → cheap refills after"** (AG1, Seed, IM8, Hiya, Starface). First box feels like a one-time event; reorders are just product. The scoop/jar "only comes once" → churn friction by design.
2. **"90-Day Challenge" naming on the 3-unit hero** — used *independently by THREE iron brands* (Cavaé, Three Arrows, Active Iron). Reframes a 3-tin upsell as a **protocol with a finish line = the retest.** Kills "is one tin enough?"
3. **Subscription priced to the retest cadence** (Cavaé: *"3 tins every 90 days — gets you to the retest"*). The subscription IS the retest reminder.
4. **Digital gift that reinforces the trigger** (Active Iron gives a FREE period-tracking app worth $39.99 with any order; LOLA's pediatrician-co-authored guide). $0 COGS, on-mechanism, sets the retest date day 1.
5. **Wearable/carried identity object, never a kid sticker** (LOLA bracelet girls "wear every period," Cocokind collectible cap charms, Saalt carry pouch). Flat, cheap, collectible, makes it HERS.
6. **Streak / milestone gift tied to STAYING subscribed** (Cymbiotika streak rewards, Ritual "milestone gifts," e.l.f. birthday gift). Rewards the exact behavior we need — finishing the cycle + retesting — and costs nothing until they've already paid for months.
7. **Quiz → personalized plan + first-box discount + email capture** (Perelel, Pink Stork via Octane AI). The mum-facing acquisition bridge for a low-felt-demand product.
8. **HSA/FSA checkout (Truemed)** (Three Arrows, MaryRuth's, Thorne). ~30% cheaper to the buyer, **$0 cost to us** — kills the "$69 vs $16" objection.
9. **Store-CREDIT incentive, not %-off** (MaryRuth's CashBack; matches our playbook's winning popup test "$25 toward her first plan"). Keeps refund money in the brand.
10. **Cart free-gift / free-shipping progress bar + bundle anchoring with struck-through per-unit/per-day price** (Hero, Winky Lux, Bloom, MegaFood's $0.32/day, Nello's "unlock 3 free gifts").

**The open goal:** the 3 biggest sub brands (Hiya, First Day, Ritual) and Starface all have their WORST reviews on *forced subscriptions + impossible cancellation.* Our **buy-once default + one-click cancel (screenshot it on the page)** is a real, evidenced differentiator. Lead with it.

## 4. Recommended NEW IRYN gift + upsell stack

### A) GIFTS — lead with digital ($0 COGS), one cheap physical delighter
**Hero digital gift bundle — "The Know Her Number Kit" (free, all tiers, delivered via Her Page QR):**
1. **"Know Her Number" guide** — framed with a *real, named, published hematologist's* public words (Weyand/Powers/Ferri/Sholzberg). This is the LOLA pediatrician-guide move + our trust/ad-safety need. (never a fabricated doctor.)
2. **Doctor-Visit Script** — the printable "ask for a ferritin test" card.
3. **Period & Energy / Ferritin Tracker** — sets the day-85 retest date on day 1 (the Active Iron "free tracker" play, built in-house, $0).
   > Present these as **"3 free gifts worth ~$X" with struck-through values** (Nello value-stack).

**One physical delighter (replaces the eye-mask kit) — only if fulfillment allows:**
- **A flat enamel "milestone" charm/pin** (e.g. a minimalist IRYN mark) she clips to a backpack/water-bottle zip — LOLA bracelet + Cocokind charm. One with the first box, a **second "retest" charm unlocked at day 85** (= the streak reward). Flat, light, cents, collectible, *not* a kid sticker.
- ⚠️ **Dropship reality:** Supliful ships the tin; a physical charm needs either a Supliful insert (confirm they allow it) or a cheap separate shipment. If neither is clean for the $1,500 test, **run digital-only gifts for the test** and add the charm once we control fulfillment. Don't repeat the eye-mask mistake (separate ~$13 shipment).

### B) NAMING + SUBSCRIPTION (free, high-impact)
- Rename the hero: **"The Know-Her-Number 90-Day Challenge."**
- Say the cadence out loud: **"3 tins ship every 90 days — right when it's time to retest her number."** The subscription becomes the retest reminder.

### C) BUMPS & UPSELLS (ranked)
1. **Order bump — Tin for Mum (+$24):** keep. Seed it with a free line ("Mum's ferritin guide — free") per First Day. ("Heavy periods run in families.")
2. **Post-purchase 1-click — upgrade 30-Day → 90-Day Challenge** (+$50): the strongest on-mechanism upsell. *Demote the generic hydration bottle* — it's off-brand; only keep it as a cheap secondary if it clears margin.
3. **"Build her plan" / bundle builder:** her tin + Mum tin (+ sibling tin) at a stacked discount (Black Girl Vitamins / Lemme BYOB).
4. **Quiz bridge** ("Could it be her iron? heavy periods / sport / normal bloods") → personalized plan + email capture + the store-credit incentive.

### D) MARGIN-FREE LEVERS (add these)
- **Truemed HSA/FSA checkout** — pay with pre-tax dollars, ~30% cheaper to mum, $0 to us. Kills the price objection.
- **Store-credit first-order incentive** ("$25 toward her first plan"), not %-off.
- **Streak / milestone reward** — finish the first box + log the retest → unlock the retest charm or a bonus Her-Page module. This is the retention engine where our profit lives (past renewal #1).
- **Loyalty points + "give $10 / get $10" referral** at day 100 (peer-mum WOM = research's #1 trusted source).

### E) APP STACK (all cheap, all seen on the winners)
Recharge (subs) · Octane AI (quiz) · Rebuy or ReConvert (cart + post-purchase upsell) · **EG Auto-Add Free Gift + Easy Digital Products** (deliver the digital Kit as a $0-COGS cart gift) · Klaviyo (milestone/retest flows) · Rise.ai (streak rewards + store-credit refunds) · Truemed (HSA/FSA) · a loyalty app (Rivo/Smile) · Intelligems (A/B the offer).

## 5. The one-line verdict
Kill the eye-mask kit and the generic bottle. Make the **free gift a digital "Know Her Number Kit"** (hematologist guide + doctor script + retest tracker), rename the hero **"90-Day Challenge,"** price the sub **to the retest**, add **HSA/FSA + store-credit + a streak reward**, and keep the **Mum bump**. Put the money in the offer, not the gift. One cheap collectible charm is the only physical gift worth adding — and only if Supliful can insert it.
