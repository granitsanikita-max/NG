**Status:** Pre-launch. Capped $1,500 / 14-day US test in October 2026. Not a brand build yet.

## The one thing
Everyone sells iron a size up (women's, "not for under 18") or a size down (kids', "ages 2–12"). LEVEL makes *her* size — the 12–18 girl whose iron need nearly doubles at 14.

## Positioning
- **Enemy:** The Gap Years (system that treats her as a big kid or a small woman). Sub-enemy: The Fine Print (cancel mazes, planted reviews, hidden doses).
- **Lead angle:** "Know her number." Heavy periods + sport + 2026 AAP/ASH guidance. **Tiredness is support, never the lead.**
- **Mechanism:** The Ferritin Gap — blood count checks spending money, ferritin checks savings. UMS: Test → Top Up → Retest.

## Who
- **Buyer:** Jen — US mum 38–55 of a girl 12–18 (heavy periods, sport, vegetarian, or "normal bloods"). Often has her own low-ferritin history.
- **User/owner:** the daughter (e.g. Maddie, 15). Never advertised to; reached only through the box.

## Product
- **Test:** Supliful Iron Strips — 19 mg iron (ferric saccharate) + 400 mcg folate, dissolves on tongue, raspberry, no sugar. Dropship, no MOQ.
- **Upgrade after a win:** 25 mg bisglycinate capsule/gummy.

## Offer

- 100-day no-questions refund (no lab). Buy once or subscribe; one-click cancel.

## Hard rules for any LEVEL copy/creative
- **Master filter:** if it doesn't serve "her years, her number", the $69 default, or one of the 3 objection answers — cut it.
- Say the biggest flaw first: "Under ~15 or anemic = doctor's dose." Put it above the fold.
- Every stat in an ad carries a URL or PMID.
- Talk *about* her in ads; *to* her only inside the box.
- Never: "moody/drama/hormonal/lazy/attitude", "energy/boost/vitality" claims, "bypass the gut", fake urgency/reviews, Thorne as the price anchor, lead with "ask for ferritin by name".
- US English only (pediatrician, bloodwork, practice, season). No UK words.
- Claims we can't own → use the §13 substitute wording (no "absorbs better", "zero side effects", "gives her energy back", "fixes low ferritin").

## Numbers that matter
- 38.6% of US girls 12–21 iron-deficient; 83.6% of them not anemic (PMID 37367984).
- 2026 cutoff: ≤30 low (≤50 with heavy periods/sport). "Normal" floor is 8–11.
- Break-even CPA ≈$37 at AOV ≈$70. Win: CPA ≤$45, AOV ≥$70, 25+ orders by day 14. Kill: CPA >$70 or AOV <$55.
