# IRYN — CJ Dropshipping Gift Sourcing Guide

> The physical gifts for the offer, sourced via **CJ Dropshipping** (Supliful = tins only). What to search, specs, how it must look, target cost, and why *she* (teen girl 12–18) would actually want it. Aesthetic rule: **elevated, feminine, dainty — never kiddy/cartoon.** Palette: garnet `#A0203F`, cream `#F4ECDF`, warm gold `#D8A24A`. Girl has a hard veto on anything that looks like a "kids' vitamin" freebie.

## Buy rules (read first)
- **Prefer CJ US / local warehouse** listings → ship in days, not 2–3 weeks from China. Gifts ship as a **separate parcel** from the tins, so speed + a "your welcome gifts ship separately" note matters.
- **Order a sample of every item first** — judge it in hand (cheap tat kills the premium feel we're paying the brand tax for).
- **Cheap + flat + light** = low CJ shipping. Avoid bulky/heavy (bottles, boxes).
- **Plain on-palette for the test**; custom-printed/branded (IRYN logo) via CJ POD is a phase-2 upgrade once something converts.
- Pick solid garnet / cream / gold tones; avoid loud multicolor prints.

## CORE SET (what the 90-Day Challenge includes)

### 1. "Her Number" bracelet — the hero wearable (first box)
- **CJ search terms:** `dainty bracelet women stainless steel`, `minimalist adjustable bracelet gold`, `dainty beaded bracelet`, `charm bracelet stainless steel`.
- **Specs:** stainless steel or 18k-gold-plated (won't tarnish/green her skin), adjustable/slider clasp (one size fits all), dainty (2–3mm), <10g.
- **How it looks:** thin gold-tone chain OR a single garnet-colored bead/charm; clean and grown-up. Think "little treat she'd actually wear," not a plastic kids' charm.
- **Target cost:** ~$0.80–1.50 + ship. **Why she likes it:** wearable identity she keeps (LOLA's first-period bracelet got "wears it every time" reviews); feels the brand sees *her*. **Phase 2:** custom "Fe/26" or "iryn" charm via CJ POD.

### 2. Satin scrunchie — cheap everyday delight (first box)
- **CJ search terms:** `satin scrunchie solid color`, `silk scrunchie set`, `premium satin hair scrunchie`.
- **Specs:** satin/silk-feel (not cheap nylon), solid color, light.
- **How it looks:** solid **garnet** or **cream** satin to match the brand; premium sheen. One, not a rainbow pack.
- **Target cost:** ~$0.30–0.80 + ship. **Why she likes it:** teens wear scrunchies daily (hair up for sport/school — on-mechanism); feminine, on-palette, basically free to us.

### 3. Discreet carry pouch — the Day-85 Retest Reward (ships later, for staying)
- **CJ search terms:** `small cosmetic bag canvas`, `mini makeup pouch cotton`, `minimalist zipper pouch`, `small toiletry bag women`.
- **Specs:** cotton/canvas or matte PU, ~13–16cm, zip, light, holds the tin + a few period essentials.
- **How it looks:** matte **cream** or **garnet**, minimal (no loud logo); small enough for a backpack. Discreet = kills the embarrassment veto.
- **Target cost:** ~$1.50–3 + ship. **Why she likes it:** a real, useful thing she carries; discreet; feels like a grown-up kit, not a freebie. Deferring it to day 85 protects first-box margin AND rewards staying subscribed. **Phase 2:** screen-print "iryn" small + tonal.

## OPTIONAL ADD-ONS (test if you want a fuller box / more "stuff")
### 4. Dainty anklet or necklace (alt / add to the bracelet)
- **Search:** `dainty anklet stainless steel`, `minimalist necklace gold layered`. Cost ~$1–2. Same identity play as the bracelet; good for an A/B of which jewelry converts.

### 5. Aesthetic matte sticker (NOT cartoon)
- **Search:** `aesthetic vinyl stickers minimalist`, `matte waterproof stickers`. Cost cents. Minimal, grown-up designs only (a subtle mark / word) — a cartoon sheet trips the teen veto. For laptop/water-bottle, which teens do decorate.

### 6. Phone grip / card holder
- **Search:** `phone grip holder`, `silicone card holder phone`. Cost ~$0.50–1. Teen-relevant everyday object; on-palette solid color.

### Avoid for the test
Water bottles / shakers (bulky, heavy ship, you flagged generic), tote bags (bulky), anything plastic-cheap, anything cartoon/kid-coded, multicolor loud prints.
