# IRYN — The Grand-Slam Offer (IM8-modeled) — v4 proposal

> Modeled on IM8's welcome-kit offer, adapted for IRYN (teen-girl iron, buyer = mum Jen, Supliful dropship, thin margins). Goal: a MASSIVE-feeling offer that stays cheap to deliver and improves our break-even CPA. Run through `research.md` + `offer.md` + the gifts/upsells swipe.

## What makes IM8's offer win (the 6 levers we copy)
1. **Quarterly prepay hero** billed every 12 weeks (front-loads cash) with a per-day price ("$2.61/day").
2. **Free Welcome Kit = a value stack** — every item individually priced so the "free" stack dwarfs the price.
3. **Future gifts you only unlock by STAYING subscribed** (Month 2–6) — forfeit them if you cancel = loss-aversion retention.
4. **A big DIGITAL program bundled free** ($180 value, $0 COGS) — most of the perceived value costs nothing.
5. **HSA/FSA checkout + 90-day guarantee + free shipping** — risk and price killers.
6. **Value stack with struck-through prices** → "$X value, yours for $Y."

**The key adaptation for us:** IM8's physical kit (frother, bottle, hat, pouch) is a margin trap on dropship. We make our welcome kit **mostly digital** — which makes the offer look *bigger* AND keeps COGS near zero. That single move **raises our break-even CPA from ~$30 to ~$50** (see margins) — the biggest survivability lever we have for cold Meta.

## MARGINS — why this version is BETTER for us
| Tier | Customer pays | Our cost (landed + digital gifts + fees) | Gross |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐ 90-Day first box | **$99** | 3 tins $46 + charm $1.50 + fulfil $2.50 + $3.27 = **$53.27** | **~$45.73** |
| ⭐ 90-Day recurring | **$129 / 90d** | $46 + pouch $4 + fulfil $2.50 (day-85 box) + $4.17 = $56.67 | **~$72.33 / quarter** (profit engine) |
| 30-Day first month | **$29** | $18 + $1.17 = $19.17 | **~$9.83** |
| One-time 1 tin | **$45** | $19.64 | **~$25.36** |

**The headline number:** vs the old ~$19.60 eye-mask kit (first-box gross ~$30), this rebalanced kit — **1 cheap physical (charm) + 2 digital** in box 1, pouch moved to the day-85 reward — lands first-box gross at **~$46** (break-even CPA ~$46). Still a big improvement because the physical gifts are cheap/flat and one is deferred to the retest. Put BOTH physical in box 1 and gross drops to ~$34 (then raise the box to $109 to recover). The digital gifts carry the perceived value at $0 COGS; the physical gifts give the real unboxing.

## Why this beats the old offer
- **Bigger perceived value** (~$290 vs the old ~$205) — all the added value is digital/$0.
- **Higher margin + higher break-even CPA** (~$50 vs ~$30) — the thing that decides if we survive Meta.
- **On-mechanism** — the whole kit reinforces Test → Top Up → Retest (our UMS), not a random eye-mask.
- **Built-in retention** — the day-85 retest bonus + future unlocks attack churn exactly where our profit lives (past renewal #1).
- **No fulfillment drag** — digital gifts ship instantly; the only physical add (charm) is optional and only if Supliful inserts it.
