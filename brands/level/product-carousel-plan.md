# IRYN — Product Carousel Plan (the hero gallery / swipe set)

> Scope: ONLY the product image carousel — the swipeable gallery of product shots that sits by the buy box (and doubles as the hero product set). NOT the whole page. Each slide is a product image that also does one job (belief / info / trust). Built off `research.md` + `offer.md`. 8 slides.
> Brand: Garnet `#A0203F` · Cream `#F4ECDF` bg · Ink `#2B1418` text · Gold `#D8A24A` numbers · Space Grotesk. Clean, editorial, premium — never stock-supplement. Square 1:1 (works desktop + mobile).

| # | Slide | Job | What's in it | Why (ICP / objection) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero tin** | BRAND | The garnet tin, clean, centered on cream. Wordmark `iryn` + "gentle daily iron for teen girls." Fe-26 tile corner. | First frame = instant "this is made for her," premium not clinical. |
| 2 | **One strip, no pills** | INFO / objection | Strip dissolving on the tongue (or on fingertip), tin behind. Overlay: "One raspberry strip a day. Nothing to swallow." | Kills "she won't take pills" — the #1 adherence fear. |
| 3 | **The dose, done right** | BELIEF | Big gold **19 mg** + "gentle iron + folate." Small line: "not a 65 mg horse-pill that wrecks her stomach." | Kills "is it even enough?" and "iron wrecks her gut" at once. |
| 4 | **Where she fits** | BELIEF | Three-block age strip: *kids' iron 2–12* · **HER YEARS 12–18** (garnet, highlighted) · *women's 18+*. | The core positioning — everyone else sells a size up or down. |
| 5 | **Why her body burns through it** | INFO | 3 clean icons: heavy periods · growth spurt · sport. Overlay: "Her iron need nearly doubles at 14." | Makes it HER problem (periods/sport/growth), not just "tired." |
| 6 | **Tastes like raspberry, not pennies** | Objection | Strip + raspberry accent, cream bg. Overlay: "Raspberry. Dissolves. No metal taste. No sugar." | Kills "tastes like pennies" — the #1 reorder-killer complaint. |
| 7 | **What's in the box** | OFFER / value | Flat-lay: tin + planner + eye-mask kit, neatly arranged. Small labels. | Makes the welcome-kit value tangible right at the buy decision. |
| 8 | **Risk-free** | TRUST | Badges: "70-day money-back · cancel in one click · clean label (folic acid, no methylfolate) · made for girls 12+." | Kills subscription-trap + scam/safety fear before she buys. |

## Build notes
- **Make-method:** Slides 1, 2, 7 = real/AI product shots of the actual garnet tin + strips (shoot or render once we have the tin). Slides 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 = designed graphic cards (garnet/cream/gold, Space Grotesk) — fast to produce in Canva/AI, no photography needed.
- **Consistency:** same cream bg, same type system, gold only for numbers. The set should look like one system when you swipe.
- **Order logic:** brand → format → dose → who → why → taste → value → risk. Belief and objections handled in the swipe itself, so the gallery sells even if she doesn't read the page.
- **Must-have to launch:** all 8 (this is the gallery; it's small and high-leverage). If cutting for speed: 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8 are the core six.

> The lab-slip hook ("Hemoglobin: normal · Ferritin: 9"), discovery story, expert quotes, press, big comparison table, etc. are PAGE sections, not carousel slides — parked for the page build, not here.
