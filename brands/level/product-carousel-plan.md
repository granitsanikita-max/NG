# IRYN — Product Carousel Plan (conversion-engineered)

> The swipe gallery by the buy box. Built on the playbook carousel framework (Hero · Before/After · Main objection · All objections · Logical evidence · Take them to the future · Testimonials · Risk reversal) run through `research.md`.
> **Rule for every slide:** it is a *selling unit*, not a product photo. Each one moves her one step closer to buying — changes a belief, kills an objection, or makes her feel the outcome. If a slide only shows the product, it's dead weight.
> We advertise **about the daughter, to the mum (Jen).** Brand: Garnet `#A0203F` · Cream `#F4ECDF` · Ink `#2B1418` · Gold `#D8A24A` numbers · Space Grotesk. Square 1:1.

## Who's swiping + what each slide must do to her
**Jen, 44.** Daughter 12–18, heavy periods / sport / "normal bloods." She's **problem-unaware or skeptical**: doesn't yet believe her kid has an iron problem, has been burned by subscriptions and momfluencers. She's not buying iron — she's buying **4 feelings: absolution · vindication · clarity · control**, and the dream of **getting her girl back**.

**The belief ladder the carousel has to walk her up:**
1. "She's just a tired teenager." → *this is a real, missable thing* (Hero + Before/After)
2. "But the doctor said her bloodwork's normal." → *a normal blood count misses empty stores* (Main objection)
3. "Yeah but [10 other doubts]." → *they've thought of all of it* (All objections)
4. "Is this real / backed?" → *2026 experts changed the rules* (Logical evidence)
5. "What actually changes?" → *see her in 10 weeks* (Future + Testimonials)
6. "What if it doesn't work / I'm trapped?" → *nothing to lose* (Risk reversal)

## THE SLIDES

### 2 — BEFORE & AFTER · the number moves
- **Layout:** split card. LEFT "Before" / RIGHT "After — 10 weeks."
- **On-image copy:**
  - Before: lab-slip style → "Hemoglobin: normal ✓  ·  Ferritin: **9** ✗" + "always tired · heavy periods · 'her bloods are fine.'"

- **Job:** belief (normal ≠ full) + the transformation made concrete by a NUMBER (the uncopyable before/after — not a face). Moves: "maybe it got missed" → "there's a real, fixable thing here."
- **Make:** designed graphic (two lab-slip cards). Seeded-mom real numbers where available.

## Sequence logic (why this order converts)
Dream (1) → it's real + missable (2) → kill the #1 blocker (3) → kill the rest (4) → prove it's legit (5) → make her *want* the after (6) → moms like her did it (7) → remove all risk (8). Emotion first, logic to justify, risk removed last — she lands on the buy box already sold.
