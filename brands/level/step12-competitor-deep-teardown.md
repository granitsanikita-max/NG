# IRYN - Step 12: Master competitor deep teardown (2026-10-06)

## THE 5 THINGS THAT MATTER MOST
1. **Statics are the backbone of this entire category.** EllaOla scales 1,000+ ads on ~100% static image. Hiya ~60% image. NovaFerrum 53% image. First Day's longest runners (200-320 days) are static review/symptom ads. Cavaé runs the *same story* as both a static and a video. Your "native statics are cheap and effective" call is correct and now proven five times over. **Lead the next batch with statics.**
2. **Nobody owns teen-girl + iron + PERIODS.** NovaFerrum = babies/toddlers (zero teen, never says "periods"). Hiya just launched "Hiya Teen" but it's a general multi, not iron. Cavaé = adult women. First Day = teens but gut/brain/skin, not iron. EllaOla = picky kids. **The word "periods" is unclaimed in the entire competitive set.** That is our wedge.
3. **The proof bar is high and we must clear it honestly.** NovaFerrum: named real pediatricians + "only clinically-proven, multi-year trial." Cavaé: ferritin-number movement + WebMD screenshot. Hiya: "4000+ reviews, Clean Label award." First Day: "2,500+ reviews" review-as-creative. Our counter (per policy): real published expert quotes + seeded real-mum/teen ferritin-number UGC + the "know her number / 90-day retest tracker" as our version of Cavaé's lab-overlay. No fabricated doctors or lab numbers.
4. **Cavaé is the #1 template to clone** because it sells the same product form as us (sublingual iron strips). Steal its ferritin-number story, its hepcidin villain, its one-story-two-placements production, re-pointed from "I, the woman" to "I, the mum watching my daughter."
5. **Two live threats:** Hiya Teen is moving into our demo, and Cavaé already runs our exact mechanism to adult women. Speed matters.

- **Man-on-the-street interview** - First Day (mic-stops mums on a street, "do you have a teen 13-19?", stat cards spliced in; highest production).
- **Scripted retro-sitcom skit** - First Day ("Bob & Karen, Episode 1," 90s-sitcom set).
- **Pixar-style 3D animation** - First Day (animated gummy "mood" character in a stomach, no actor). This is the "animated video" style.
- **Physical-prop expert demo** - First Day (Jenga tower labelled READ/MEANING/FUEL; sock-monkey height measure; handwritten sticky-note script). Cheap, no on-camera actor.

**NOT found anywhere:** claymation, and "talking body-part" videos. Nobody in our set runs those. (Doesn't mean they can't work, just that they're unproven in this niche right now.)

- Format: long-form static story ads + 45-90s UGC/VSL video (same story, both placements).
