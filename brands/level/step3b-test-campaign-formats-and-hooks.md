## Two rules every format below passes

**1) Top of funnel only.** We have nobody to retarget, so all spend is cold. A TOF format's only job is to stop a cold mum, name a pain or a hidden cause, and create problem awareness. It does not compare products, show an offer, or sell on specs. Deliberately NOT in this test (these are MOF/BOF and are the wrong tool for a cold test): us-vs-them comparison, before/after ferritin numbers, spec/benefit stacks, discount, scarcity, back-in-stock, guarantee-led, cart reminders.

**2) Message match.** I read the live page. Its hero is a wide umbrella: *"She's exhausted and not herself, but every blood test says she's perfectly fine"* to *"her blood test checked the wrong number"* (the ferritin gap). Angles C, B and S all ladder into that one hero, so a cold click from any of them lands on a page that continues the ad. Angle A (periods) is not on the page, so its hooks are held until we add a periods thread + hero.

Facts a hook may cite (all sourced in research.md): about 40% of teen girls are low in iron; 83.6% of them are NOT anemic; a standard blood count misses more than half; AAP 2026 says check her ferritin by 14; ASH 2026 says 30 or under is low. Hook tags: `[Lib]` = your Proven Hook Library, `[Swipe]` = a proven long-running winner, `[Research]` = Jen's real words / the facts above.

## STATIC 1 - Native Advertorial  (Image board, format 09)  ★ flagship static
**The visual:** a candid, un-branded photo (looks like a real mum's phone snap, not a product shot) with a long first-person confession as the copy. It reads as an organic story someone posted, not an ad. The photo and the first line do the stopping; the long copy does the selling.
**Why this format wins (proof):** the single most durable static template in this exact lane. On your board the longest runner is **340 days**, and the refs are dead-on our buyer: **Mums Health Hacks (340d), Modern Health Insider, Emma's Mom Hacks** are all mum-health advertorials running 240 to 340 days. This is also the Cavaé 150-day "runner diary" static, rewritten as the mum about her daughter. Full ref list on the Miro card. Board: https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVHjfyDy4= (format 09).
**Angle it carries: C (normal bloodwork + the ferritin gap).**
**Headline hook (the first line, this is the whole stop):** **"Her blood test said normal. Twice. Here's the one number it never checked."**
**Exact hooks:**
- C: "I did everything right. Doctor visits. Bloodwork. 'She's fine.' She was not fine, and I wish I'd known what to ask for." `[Swipe: Cavaé advertorial]` `[Lib: Storytelling]`

**Body spine:** candid photo + confession hook -> the symptom year (pale, foggy, asleep by dinner) -> "her bloodwork came back normal, twice" -> the 2am googling -> the ferritin gap (a blood count checks her iron today, never her iron savings) -> ask for ferritin by name -> the gentle strip she'll actually take -> Test, Top Up, Retest -> "under 15 or anemic is a doctor's dose first" -> 70-day money-back.

# PART 3 - HOW TO RUN THE 6

**First flight:** run all 6, each on its page-matching angle. They all ladder into the same ferritin-gap hero, so she lands and thinks "this is us" every time. Spread: C is carried by the Native Advertorial (static) + Educational UGC (video); S by the X Signs checklist (static) + Epiphany (video); B by the avatar callout (static) + Mentor Story (video). That is every angle tested in both a static and a video, which tells you fast whether it's the angle or the format that's working.

**Modular heroes (build these so message match holds):**
- **C** -> "Her blood test said normal. It never checked her ferritin." (already the page hero)
- **B** -> "Her coach thought she stopped trying. Her bloodwork said fine."
- **S** -> "She's exhausted and not herself, but every blood test says she's fine." (already the page hero)

**Hold:** every **Angle A (heavy periods)** hook until we add a periods thread + hero to the page. A periods ad would jolt her on arrival.

**Day-3 gate (research):** an education/story hook should clear CTR >= 1.2% at CPC <= $1.50, or the belief gap is too wide for this budget.

**Benched for scaling later (real formats, not for this cold test):** Animated Mechanism Explainer / Clinical VSL (video 10, the ferritin savings-account explainer, strong Angle C) and the before/after ferritin-number static (MOF proof) once we have warm audiences to retarget.

**Guardrails baked in:** cold/TOF only (no comparison, specs, offers, discounts, scarcity, or before/after numbers in these ads); lead with the number/sport/the symptom, never generic "tired/energy"; absolution not fear; say the ceiling ("under 15 = doctor's dose"); no fabricated doctors; no invented ferritin numbers; no em-dashes; US English. Run every hook through Copywriting Fundamentals before it ships.
