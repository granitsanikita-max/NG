# IRYN - Test Campaign: 6 TOP-OF-FUNNEL Formats (3 static + 3 video)

## The funnel rule I'm holding every format to
We have no customers to retarget, so **every ad is cold prospecting = top of funnel.** A top-of-funnel ad's only job is to **stop a cold mum, name a pain or a hidden cause she hadn't connected, and create the problem awareness.** It does NOT compare products, show offers, or sell on specs, because all of those assume she is already shopping.

**A format is TOP OF FUNNEL only if it targets Unaware or Problem-aware.** Here is the test I applied:

| Stage | She is... | Format job | TOF? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | doesn't know low iron could be it | pattern-interrupt, tell a story, educate | ✅ TOF |
| Problem-aware | knows she's tired/struggling, not why | name the hidden cause (the ferritin gap) | ✅ TOF |
| Solution-aware | knows iron is the fix, comparing options | why THIS one (comparison, mechanism-deep, proof) | ❌ MOF |
| Product-aware | knows IRYN, deciding | specs, before/after numbers, reviews wall | ❌ MOF |
| Most-aware | ready to buy | offer, discount, scarcity, guarantee, cart | ❌ BOF |

**Excluded on purpose (and why), so this never slips again:**
- **Us-vs-them comparison** (kids vs women vs IRYN) = MOF. She has to already be shopping for iron to care. CUT from the test.
- **Before/after ferritin number** ("14 to 31") = MOF proof. It convinces someone already aware; it doesn't create awareness. Save for retargeting.
- **Spec / benefit stack** ("19mg + folate, no sugar") = MOF, product-aware.
- **Discount / scarcity / "back in stock" / guarantee-led / cart reminder** = BOF.
These are good ads later. They are the wrong tool for a $1,500 cold test.

## The angles that MATCH the live page (so she never lands confused)
I read the live product page. Its hero is a **wide umbrella**: *"She's exhausted and not herself, but every blood test says she's perfectly fine"* -> *"her blood test checked the wrong number"* (the ferritin gap). Multiple angles can all ladder into that one hero, which is exactly what we want: different cold entry points, same landing story, zero confusion. The page backs these:

- **C = "Normal" bloodwork + the ferritin gap + 2026 news.** This IS the hero. Perfect match. Primary angle.
- **B = Sport / fading at practice.** On the page ("finish the season strong," "energy at practice is back"). Strong match.
- **S = Everyday "not herself": tired by 4pm, foggy in class, nodding off, pale.** On the page (body + reviews: "no more nodding off in sixth period," "so pale I kept asking if she was okay," "her energy at practice is back"). Strong match. These are just softer symptom doorways into the same ferritin-gap story.

**All three (C, B, S) land cleanly on the current hero, so we can and should test multiple angles.** The one to HOLD is:
- **A = Heavy periods.** Not on the page at all. A periods ad would jolt her on arrival. Do not run it until we add a periods thread + hero to the page (easy to do later if we want it).

Every hook below is tagged with its angle (C / B / S, and A marked HOLD) so the ad and the page never drift apart.

## How to read the hooks
Each hook is tagged: `[Lib: category]` = your Proven Hook Library, `[Swipe: brand + run-days]` = lifted from a proven long-running winner, `[Research]` = Jen's real words / belief chain. All written on-voice, no em-dashes. Run each through Copywriting Fundamentals before it ships.
Facts a hook may cite (sourced in research.md): ~40% of teen girls are low in iron; 83.6% of them are NOT anemic; a standard blood count misses more than half; AAP 2026 says check her ferritin by 14; ASH 2026 says 30 or under is low.

# PART 1 - THE 3 STATIC FORMATS (all top of funnel)

## STATIC 1 - The lab-slip problem-reveal  ★ lead format
**Awareness it targets:** Unaware to Problem-aware. **Why it's TOF:** one image reveals a hidden problem (a "normal" result that missed the real number). It creates the awareness; it sells nothing. **Proof:** Cavaé's "a number on a lab slip, not a feeling" framing and Sophia Byrd's "Ferritin: Normal..." reveal are long-runners (150 to 319 days). The lab slip as the hero image is an open lane nobody runs for teens.
**Build it:** the image IS a lab slip, two lines highlighted ("Hemoglobin: NORMAL" / "Ferritin: 9"), one short headline on top. Minimal copy. Scroll-stopper.
**Primary hook (C):** **"Her blood test said normal. It never checked the one number that was empty."**
**Exact hooks:**
- C: "This is what 'her bloodwork is normal' actually looks like." (over the slip) `[Lib: Curiosity & Secrets]` `[Swipe: Cavaé]`
- C: "Hemoglobin: normal. Ferritin: 9. Only one of these was ever checked." `[Research]` `[Swipe: Sophia Byrd 319d]`
- C (news): "New in 2026: doctors say check her ferritin by 14. Here's why her last 'normal' result missed it." `[Research]`
- A (periods, only if page adds periods): "Her periods are heavy. Her blood count is 'normal.' Her iron stores were never tested." `[Research]`
- B (sport): "She's fading at practice. Her blood test says she's fine. One number nobody ran says otherwise." `[Swipe: Eden Superfoods 219d]`
**Text overlay:** `Her ferritin was 9 ("normal")` `[Lib: Text Hooks]`

## STATIC 2 - The symptom-callout / "please stop" pattern-interrupt  ★ cheapest to test
**Awareness it targets:** Unaware to Problem-aware. **Why it's TOF:** it names the daily pattern she blames on "being a teenager" and reframes the cause. Pure problem-agitation + absolution, no product sell. **Proof:** First Day's "it wasn't laziness, it wasn't attitude" symptom static (40d), Perimenopause Insiders' "Please STOP..." and "Mom, why are you sleeping on the couch again?" (352d), Flora's "You're dragging through your afternoons" (376d).
**Build it:** bold text-forward static (plain or a tired-mum/daughter photo), one contrarian/callout line as the headline, 2 to 3 lines of reframe. Short.
**Primary hook (C):** **"Please stop telling her she's just tired. Ask for the one iron number her blood test skips."**
**Exact hooks:**
- C: "Please stop going back for the same bloodwork. It will say 'normal' again. Here's the test to ask for instead." `[Swipe: Perimenopause Insiders 352d]` `[Lib: Negative]`
- S (absolution): "It wasn't laziness. It wasn't attitude. Her iron ran low and her blood test never caught it." `[Swipe: First Day 40d]` `[Lib: Problem / Relatable Pain]`
- S: "Asleep by 4pm. Foggy in class. Pale in every photo. You keep hearing 'she's a teenager.' Here's what no one checked." `[Research]` `[Lib: Problem / Relatable Pain]`
- S: "Please stop telling her to go to bed earlier. If she's still wiped out, there's one iron number her blood test skipped." `[Lib: Negative]` `[Research]`
- B: "Please stop assuming she's out of shape. A low iron tank can wreck a whole season, and her blood count won't show it." `[Lib: Negative]` `[Research]`
- A (HOLD, page has no periods yet): "Please stop blaming her moods on hormones. Heavy periods pull iron out every month, and the standard test misses it." `[Lib: Negative]` `[Research]`
**Text overlay:** `Please stop ("she's just tired")` `[Lib: Text Hooks]`

## STATIC 3 - The educational carousel ("the number nobody checks" / doctor-script)  ★ replaces the old comparison
**Awareness it targets:** Unaware to Problem-aware. **Why it's TOF:** it teaches a hidden mechanism and gives her a free action (what to ask for), with zero product comparison. This is the real swap for the us-vs-them slide. **Proof:** Organic Burst's stat/education carousels ran 233 days; Cavaé's "iron and ferritin are two different tests, ask for ferritin by name" carousel; NovaFerrum's "looks healthy, still deficient" education. Education carousels are a proven cold vehicle.
**Build it:** 3 to 5 swipe cards. Card 1 = the hook. Card 2 = the ferritin gap (spending vs savings). Card 3 = the 2026 guidance. Card 4 = "ask for ferritin by name" (the free doctor-script value). Card 5 = soft intro to IRYN. No competitor, no price.
**Primary hook (C):** **"A normal blood count checks her iron today. It never checks her iron savings. Swipe for the number to ask for."**
**Exact hooks:**
- C: "Iron and ferritin are two different tests, and they almost always run only the first one. Here's how to get the second." `[Swipe: Cavaé carousel]` `[Lib: Educational]`
- C: "She can look completely healthy and still be running on empty. Here's the one number that shows it." `[Swipe: NovaFerrum 81d]` `[Lib: Educational]`
- C (stat): "Nearly 40% of teen girls are low in iron, and most have no idea. Here's the test that catches it." `[Research]` `[Swipe: Organic Burst 233d]` `[Lib: Educational]`
- C (news): "The rules for 'low iron' in girls changed in 2026. Here's her new number." `[Research]` `[Lib: Curiosity]`
- C (doctor-script): "What to say at her next appointment to actually get her ferritin checked." `[Research]` `[Lib: Educational "everyone tells you X, nobody shows you how"]`
**Text overlay:** `The one number no one checks (ferritin)` `[Lib: Text Hooks]`

# PART 2 - THE 3 VIDEO FORMATS (all top of funnel)

## VIDEO 1 - The insider story VSL (coach / school-nurse POV)  ★ flagship
**Awareness it targets:** Unaware to Problem-aware. **Why it's TOF:** a first-person story from someone who has watched hundreds of girls. Story + borrowed authority, no product comparison, no offer. **Proof:** Chewy's "front office lady, 18 years" teen-to-mum story VSL is the single best-proven structure for selling a teen fix to a mom. Nobody runs it for iron. Primary angle **B** (coach), works A (nurse).
**Spoken hook (0 to 3s):** **"I've coached a few hundred teenage girls. I can tell which ones have run out of iron before they can."**
**Exact opening hooks:**
- B: "I've worked the sideline of girls' cross-country for 18 years. Every season the same thing happens to the same kind of girl, and it has nothing to do with training." `[Swipe: Chewy]` `[Lib: Authority]`
- A (nurse): "I've been a school nurse for 15 years. The same girls land in my office two days a month, and I finally figured out why." `[Swipe: Chewy]` `[Lib: Authority]`
- B: "A parent asked me why her daughter went from 7th at districts to 57th. It wasn't her training. It was a blood number no one had ordered." `[Research]`
**Beat spine (from Chewy):** insider pattern callout -> the system's useless answer ("rest, water, maybe see your GP") -> the one girl who disappeared -> it's my own athlete/daughter -> everything tried and failed -> reject the easy answer ("the doctor said she's fine") -> the ferritin gap reveal -> the gentle strip she'll actually take -> Test, Top Up, Retest -> "under 15 or anemic is a doctor's dose first" -> 70-day money-back.
**Text overlay:** `What a coach sees before the parents do` `[Lib: Text Hooks]`

# PART 3 - HOW THE 6 RUN (all TOF)

| Format | Type | Awareness | Primary angle | Page match |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Lab-slip problem-reveal | static | Unaware->Problem | C | perfect |
| 2. Symptom-callout / "please stop" | static | Unaware->Problem | S or C | match (S + C on page) |
| 3. Educational carousel | static | Unaware->Problem | C | perfect |
| 4. Coach/nurse insider VSL | video | Unaware->Problem | B (coach) | strong |
| 5. Expert reveal talking-head | video | Problem | C | perfect |
| 6. Mum story / regret VSL | video | Unaware->Problem | S, B or C | match (S/B/C on page) |

Angle key: **C** = normal bloodwork / ferritin gap / 2026 news · **B** = sport / fading at practice · **S** = everyday "not herself" (tired, foggy in class, pale) · **A** = heavy periods (HOLD, not on page).

**First flight:** run all 6, each on a page-matching angle (C, B or S). They all ladder into the same hero, so she lands and says "this is us" every time. **Hold the Angle A (heavy-periods) hooks** until we add a periods thread + hero to the page. Message-match heroes already live or easy to add: C -> "Her blood test said normal. It never checked her ferritin." B -> "Her coach thought she stopped trying. Her bloodwork said fine." S -> the current hero ("She's exhausted and not herself, but every blood test says she's fine") already covers it.
**Day-3 gate (research):** an education/story hook should clear CTR >= 1.2% at CPC <= $1.50, or the belief gap is too wide for this budget.

**Guardrails baked in:** cold/TOF only (no comparison, specs, offers, discounts, scarcity, or before/after numbers in these ads); lead with periods/sport/the number, never "tired/energy"; absolution not fear; say the ceiling ("under 15 = doctor's dose"); no fabricated doctors; no invented ferritin numbers; no em-dashes; US English.
