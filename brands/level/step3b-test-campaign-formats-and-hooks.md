# IRYN - Test Campaign: 3 Static Formats + 3 Video Formats (with angles + exact hooks)

## How this is built
- **3 angles** we test across every format: **A = Heavy periods**, **B = Sport / athlete**, **C = "Normal" bloodwork + 2026 news**.
- Each format below is a **proven top performer** from the swipe (run-days = the proof it's a winner). For each, you get the angle it carries and a stack of **exact, ready hooks**.
- Every hook is tagged: `[Lib: <category>]` = from your Proven Hook Library, `[Swipe: <brand> <days>]` = lifted from a competitor winner, `[Research]` = straight from Jen's real words / the belief chain.
- **Rule for all of them:** talk ABOUT the daughter TO the mum. Lead with periods/sport/the number, never "tired." Say the ceiling ("under 15 or anemic is a doctor's dose"). No fake doctors. No invented ferritin numbers in a cold ad. No em-dashes.
- Facts a hook may cite (all sourced in research.md): ~40% of teen girls are low in iron; 83.6% of them are NOT anemic; a standard blood count misses more than half; AAP 2026 says check her ferritin by 14; ASH 2026 says 30 or under is low (50 with heavy periods or sport).

## STATIC 1 - The lab-slip advertorial  ★ lead format
**Why it wins:** the single longest-running format in the whole category. Sophia Byrd's "Ferritin: Normal... why did I feel like I was dying?" advertorial ran **319 days**; Flora's gentle-iron advertorial **421 days**; Cavaé's runner-diary **150 days**. The lab-slip as the hero image is an open lane nobody runs for teens.
**Build it:** a real-looking lab slip as the image (two lines highlighted: "Hemoglobin: NORMAL" / "Ferritin: 9"), headline across the top, then long first-person copy underneath (mum's story) that runs the belief chain and ends on "know her number." Primary angle **C**, but the same format carries A and B by swapping the slip + first line.
**Primary hook (C):** **"Her blood test said normal. It never checked the one number that was empty."**

## STATIC 2 - The "Please STOP" pattern-interrupt  ★ cheapest to test
**Why it wins:** Perimenopause Insiders runs a whole "Please STOP..." series to moms (the exact emotional beat: dismissal + absolution), and it scales. It is the purest scroll-stopper: a negative command that names the enemy. Cheap, fast, lives for months.
**Build it:** bold text-forward static (plain background or a tired-mum/daughter photo), one contrarian command as the headline, 2 to 3 lines of reframe under it. Short. Works for all 3 angles.
**Primary hook (C):** **"Please stop telling her she's just tired. Ask for the one iron number her blood test skips."**

**Exact hooks to test:**
- C: "Please stop going back for more bloodwork. It will come back 'normal' again. Here's the test to ask for instead." `[Swipe: Perimenopause Insiders]` `[Lib: Negative]`

**Why it wins:** Chewy's "front office lady, 18 years" teen-to-mum story VSL is the single best-proven structure for selling a teen fix to a mom. The "insider who has seen hundreds of girls" POV gives instant authority with no fake doctor, and opens a curiosity loop. Nobody runs it for iron. Primary angle **B** (coach), also works A (nurse).
