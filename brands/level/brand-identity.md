# IRYN — Visual Identity (v2 — palette aligned to live assets)

> **Palette update (2026-10-01):** v1 listed Iron Oxide Red `#A63D2D` / Slate / Bone / Ferritin Gold. The locked, live palette used on the tin, back panel, and the store is **Garnet `#A0203F` · Cream `#F4ECDF` · Ink `#2B1418` · Gold `#D8A24A`**. This doc now reflects that. The rationale below is unchanged — garnet is a deeper, richer iron-oxide red.

> Brand name locked: **IRYN** (see brand.md). Wordmark is lowercase `iryn`.

> Built from `research.md` §4B. Locked here so every asset (tin, PDP, ads, Her Page) stays one system.

## Why this palette
The research demands "plain-spoken, on her side… chemistry class, not medicine." No candy, no kids, no clinical blue, no red cross. The palette has to read *mature, honest, a little fierce* — and literally signal iron.

## Core palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Garnet** | `#A0203F` | Hero brand color (buttons, headings, highlights, primary tin). Deep iron-oxide red — the product's own chemistry. Warm, grown-up, not candy. Gradient `#B62A4C` → deep `#7C1730`. |
| **Ink** | `#2B1418` | Near-black warm charcoal. Body text, back-panel type, alt dark surfaces. |
| **Cream** | `#F4ECDF` | Warm matte off-white. Type on garnet, paper feel, page/background. |
| **Gold** *(restrained accent)* | `#D8A24A` | ONLY for "her number" numerals (the 19, the dose, the streak) + fine lines. One warm hit, never decoration. |

Everything else stays monochrome. Discipline is the brand.

## Type direction
- **Wordmark:** `iryn` — always lowercase, Space Grotesk (technical/lab letterforms), tight tracking. Cool on her desk, clear in Mum's ad.
- **Tagline:** `iron for her years` — same family, lighter weight, sentence case.
- **Numbers:** slightly heavier weight, Gold, because the whole brand is about a number.
- No script, no rounded "friendly" fonts, no all-caps shouting.

## Signature device
**"Fe 26" periodic-table tile** — iron's element symbol + atomic number in a bordered square, like a chemistry chart. This is the ownable mark. Reads science-class, not pharmacy.

## Hard nos (from research)
Cartoons · stickers · candy colours · the word "kids" · red cross · clinical blue · "ANEMIA" · giant "TEEN" · glossy/plastic finishes. Matte only.

## Tin finish
Small matte tin, garnet body. Debossed or flat-matte wordmark. Feels like an object she'd leave out, not hide.
