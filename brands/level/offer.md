# IRYN — The Offer (Front-Loaded Subscription)

> Built from: Playbook Step 2 + "Reference — Offer Build (Grand Slam Offer)" (Type 1) + Funnel Selection Board, filtered through `research.md` and our real economics.
> Offer type chosen: **Front-Loaded Subscription** (Type 1) — correct for a monthly consumable where the first order can't be profitable.

## The core logic (why front-loaded subscription)
- First order **can't** turn a profit: break-even CPA ≈ $37, even a $45 "win" CPA loses ~$8 on order #1 (research). The win **depends on reorders**.
- Only ~10% of one-time buyers ever come back to subscribe (playbook). So we must get the subscribe on **order #1**.
- Subscribers are worth far more (playbook: month-6 LTV ≈ $226 sub vs $138 one-time).
- The biology backs a 90-day cadence: the plan is Test → Top Up → **Retest at 8–12 weeks (~day 85)**. A 90-day plan = "her plan runs exactly to her retest." That's the honest reason for the structure.

## ⚠️ The brand guardrail (non-negotiable)
This ICP hates subscription traps (Hiya's #1 Trustpilot complaint = cancel maze; her words: "I wanted to just try it once"). So:
- **Keep a one-time option** (don't default to subscribe-or-nothing — that's Variant C, test-only).
- **One-click cancel is the headline feature**, repeated next to every price: *"Cancel in one click — no email, no phone call, no 'are you sure.' We'll remind you 3 days before every renewal."* This directly attacks Hiya/Cavaé's cancel maze and is what makes a trust-sensitive mum willing to subscribe.
- Mantra (playbook): **jack the price, stack the value, make the subscription the obvious deal** — but *earn* the subscribe with honesty, don't trap it.

## Economics (confirm before launch)
- Product cost: **$11.99/tin** (30 strips = 30 days). Add Supliful fulfillment + shipping (~$5 est.) → **landed ≈ $17/tin** *(confirm Supliful's exact ship/fulfillment fee)*.
- Target first-order AOV: **~$89** (hero 90-day first box), ~$110 with the Mum bump.
- Track BOTH: first-order conversion **and** 90-day retention/LTV (an offer can win order #1 and crater repeats).

Everything she needs to run the full Test → Top Up → Retest cycle.
- First box: **3 tins (90 days)** + the full Welcome Kit + all digital tools.
- Value stack (show with prices struck through):
  - 3 tins ~~$147~~

  - **Total value ~$263**

- Then **$109 every 90 days** (small recurring discount — protects margin). One-click cancel.

**THE 30-DAY START — subscription**
For the mum who wants to try it before committing to the full plan.
- First tin + Starter Kit (Guide + Doctor-Visit Script + Tracker + Her Page + carry case).
- Value: 1 tin ~~$49~~ + starter tools ~~$48~~ + case ~~$12~~ = ~~$109~~

**ONE TIME — 1 tin (decoy; pays more, no kit, no gifts)**
- **$39**, shipping charged, no bonuses.

### Order bump (cart)
- **"A Tin for Mum" +$24** — *"Heavy periods run in families. So does low iron."* (validated by First Day/EllaOla selling the mum's product as a bump.)

- Opening line (kills regret): *"You just did what most parents never get to — a plan with a number."*

## Always-on components (layered on)

**Price anchor** (never anchor to Thorne $16):
- vs **Cavaé $49.99/tin** (adults only) → our tin effectively ~$30.
- vs a **$59 lab test just to find out**.
- vs **iron infusions billed $400–4,800** + a lost season.

**Reason for the discount** (honest, on-brand — this replaces a fake-scarcity reason):
> "We take a loss getting her started on purpose. This only works if she finishes the 90 days and you retest — so we make starting a no-brainer, then earn our keep by actually working."

**Guarantee — The 100-Day No-Questions Promise** (no fabricated stat):
> "Give her plan 100 days. If you're not happy for any reason — she won't take it, it didn't suit her, or you changed your mind — email us within 100 days for a full refund. No lab results, no returning bottles, no questions. Keep the guides."
> *(No refund-% claim until we have real data — research bans fake proof. "Zero reviews at launch, and we say so.")*

**Protect margin:** front-load the FIRST box hard, keep the recurring discount small (~$36/tin recurring vs $49 anchor). Jack the price, discount down to what we'd always charge.

**Specificity:** use the odd, specific numbers ($89 / $29 / $34), not round 40%-off framing.

## First-order pop-up (Type 3, layered on)
Playbook: never default to "10% off"; frame as one big number; AOV <$100 → **%-off / store-credit** wins. Research: the skeptic converts via the **free Doctor-Visit Script** lead magnet.

**These two sources conflict — so test both:**
- **A (playbook):** question-first pop-up ("What made you check her iron — heavy periods, sport, or 'normal' bloodwork?") → capture → **"$25 toward her first plan"** (store-credit framing; won the 8-way test in the playbook).
- **B (research):** same question → capture → **free Doctor-Visit Script** delivered in the welcome email (the skeptic's proven entry point).
- Likely best: **do both** — lead with the store-credit number for the ready buyer, deliver the Doctor-Visit Script in the welcome flow for the skeptic.

## Funnel (from the Funnel Selection Board)
**Discovery Stack: Advertorial → Offer Page** (the board's default for a single-hero product, and it matches the research funnel map: cold → advertorial pre-sell → offer page → retarget). **Never send cold traffic straight to the PDP** (it speaks to only ~17% of the market).

## The 2–3 variants to TEST (test offers, not discount depths)
1. **Variant A — "Welcome Kit"** (recommended lead): the hero above — one-time available, full kit + gifts. Tests the playbook's "banger" (option 3).
2. **Variant B — "Aggressive front-load, no kit"** (Armra-style): first box same low price but **digital tools only, no physical kit**, recurring 5% off. Tests whether the kit earns its cost vs raw discount.
3. **Variant C — "Subscribe-only"** (option 4): remove the one-time entirely. Highest LTV, but ⚠️ **directly fights the "just try it once" ICP** — test small, watch refunds/chargebacks.

**Kill/win read:** first-order CVR + day-90 retention. A variant that wins order #1 but churns at renewal loses.
