## THE OFFER — "Know Her Number Plan"

### ⭐ 90-DAY PLAN — subscription (HERO, auto-selected)
Runs the full Test → Top Up → Retest cycle (90 days = her retest date).
- First box: **3 tins + planner + eye-mask kit**, FREE shipping.
- Value stack (show struck-through): 3 tins ~~$147~~ · eye-mask 4-pc set ~~$39~~ · planner ~~$15~~ · Her Page + guide (digital) — free → **~$201 value**
- **First box $99** → *"Save $102."* (~$1.10/day) · then **$129 every 90 days**, free shipping, no kit.
- One-click cancel, anytime.
- **Margin:** first box gross ≈ **$36** before ad spend; **recurring gross ≈ $79/quarter** ← the profit engine.

### 30-DAY START — subscription
Smaller commitment for the cautious mum.
- First tin + planner, FREE shipping.
- **First month $29** → then **$36/month**, free shipping.
- **Margin:** first month gross ≈ $9 (thin — this tier barely covers CPA, so we push the 90-day); recurring gross ≈ **$17/mo**.

### ONE-TIME — 1 tin (decoy)
- **$39 + shipping charged (~$6)**, no gifts.
- Microcopy: *"Most moms start the 30-Day plan — free shipping and the kit included."*
- **Margin:** ~$25 gross (healthy — the decoy pays for itself).

### Cart order bump
- **"A Tin for Mum" +$24** — *"Heavy periods run in families. So does low iron."*
- Rides the same box (only ~$14 incremental cost) → **~$10 gross.** Pure AOV lift.

### Post-purchase 1-click upsell (one only)
- **30-Day buyers →** *"Upgrade to the full 90-day plan"* (+$50) — pure product margin.
- **90-Day buyers →** *"Add the hydration bottle"* **$19** (covers its ~$13 landed → ~$6 gross + reinforces "take with water").
- Opening line: *"You just did what most parents never get to — a plan with a number."*

| Where | Free shipping? | Why |

**Rule:** free shipping only where it's already priced in (subs). Never on the bare single.

## Money-back guarantee — "The 100-Day Know-Her-Number Promise"
> "Give her plan a full 100 days. If her number doesn't move, if she won't take it, or you just change your mind — email us for a full refund. Keep the gifts, keep the guides, don't ship a thing back. One email, done."

- No lab result required (research: a lab condition reads as unclaimable).
- Longer than Cavaé's 60 days → a competitive weapon.
- **No fabricated refund stat** (we have zero sales; research bans fake proof). The reason it's safe: the plan works over 90 days, so a 100-day window rarely gets used.
- Plan a refund reserve of **8–12%** + ~1% chargebacks.

## Reason for the front-load (honest, on-brand — replaces fake scarcity)
> "We front-load your first box at a loss on purpose. A plan only works if she finishes it and you retest at day 90 — so we make starting a no-brainer, then earn our keep when it actually works."

## Always-on components
- **Price anchors** (never Thorne $16): Cavaé $49.99/tin (adults only) · a $59 lab test just to find out · infusions billed $400–4,800.
- **Protect margin:** front-load the FIRST box, keep recurring near the anchor ($129 = ~$43/tin vs $49 anchor).
- **Specificity:** odd, exact numbers ($99 / $29 / $36 / $129), never round "40% off."
- **Target AOV:** 90-day first box **$99** (~$123 with the Mum bump).

## First-order pop-up (layered on)
Never "10% off." Frame as ONE number. AOV <$100 → %-off / store-credit wins. Test:
- **A:** question-first ("What made you check her iron — heavy periods, sport, or 'normal' bloodwork?") → capture → **"$25 toward her first plan"** (store-credit framing won the playbook's 8-way test).
- **B:** same → capture → **free Doctor-Visit Script** in the welcome email (research's skeptic entry point).
- **Best:** do both.

## Funnel (Funnel Selection Board) — DECISION
**Primary: a single long-form, click-through "advertorial-style Offer Page"** (ad → one long page → checkout), not advertorial → separate PDP.
- Why: education-heavy + low-AOV product can't afford the 20–40% drop on a page-to-page click. One page = one unbroken narrative.
- This is the board's **"Offer Pre-sell Page"** (content-rich LP that builds the case before revealing the price).
- **Hard rule:** the TOP of the page must read like an advertorial (editorial, story-led) — story → mechanism (Ferritin Gap) → who-it's-NOT-for → proof → THEN the offer + buy box + FAQ below. Never a "BUY NOW" hero. A short/normal PDP fails (speaks to only ~17%).
- Page spine (one narrative): Discovery Story → spending-vs-savings mechanism → 2026 guidance → why pills fail → who it's NOT for → the plan/offer → guarantee → FAQ.
- **Fallback A/B (later):** split the advertorial back out (advertorial → offer page) if cold CVR is soft.
- Never send cold traffic to a short PDP or a Home Page.

## Test 2–3 variants (test offers, not discount depths)
1. **A — "Welcome Kit"** (recommended lead): the hero above, one-time available, full kit on the 90-day.
2. **B — "Aggressive front-load, no kit"**: 90-day first box lower (e.g. $89) but **planner only, no eye-mask kit**, recurring smaller discount. Tests whether the kit earns its ~$13 cost.
3. **C — "Subscribe-only"**: remove the one-time. Highest LTV, but ⚠️ fights the "just try it once" ICP — test small, watch refunds.

## KPIs — measure BOTH (research rule)
- **Win (day 14):** CPA ≤ **$50**, AOV ≥ **$90**, 25+ orders, ≥50% choosing the 90-day plan.
- **Kill (day 14):** CPA > **$75** or AOV < **$70**.
- **Break-even CPA on the hero first box ≈ $36** — but real profit is the **90-day retention/LTV** ($79 gross per recurring quarter). Watch churn after the first box; a variant that wins order #1 but churns at renewal loses.

## Where the profit actually is
First box is **~breakeven by design** (front-loaded). Profit comes from: (1) the **$129/quarter recurring** (~$79 gross), (2) the **Mum bump** (~$10) and **bottle upsell** (~$6), (3) **charging shipping on the one-time**. Keep customers past ONE renewal and the whole thing prints.
