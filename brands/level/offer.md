## HOW PRICING IS DISPLAYED (the per-tin psychology)
Every tier shows **$/tin** big and **$/day** small — never the lump sum first. "$99" becomes **"$33 a tin · $1.10 a day."** The 90-Day is engineered to be the **cheapest per tin**, so per-tin framing makes it the obvious pick.

| | per tin | per day | what they pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-time (decoy) | $39 | $1.30 | $39 + shipping |
| 30-Day (recurring) | $36 | $1.20 | $36 / month |
| ⭐ 90-Day (recurring) | **$35** | **$1.17** | $105 / 90 days |
| ⭐ 90-Day (first box) | **$33** | **$1.10** | $99 |

→ 90-Day is the cheapest tin you can buy. That's the whole point.
