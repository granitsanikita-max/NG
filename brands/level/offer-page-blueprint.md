## 0 · WHO LANDS HERE, AND WHAT'S IN HER HEAD

**Jen, 44.** She clicked a Meta ad about her daughter being tired / heavy periods / "normal bloodwork." She is **~82% likely Unaware or Problem-aware** (research §5). She does NOT yet believe her daughter has an iron problem. She is skeptical of supplement brands, hates subscription traps, and has been burned by "momfluencers" and fake reviews. She knows ferritin *for herself* but has never linked it to her daughter.

**She buys 4 feelings, not iron:** absolution (it's not my fault), vindication (I wasn't imagining it), clarity (I finally know), control (I have a plan). The page must deliver all four.

**The belief chain she must climb, IN ORDER, before the buy box (research §9B):**
1. Her periods/growth/sport could be quietly draining her iron — it's not "just a teenager."
2. A normal blood count can miss it. **Ferritin** is the number (≤30 low; ≤50 with heavy periods).
3. The 2026 guidance is real: I should know her number, and I can get it this month.
4. If it's "normal" but low, a **gentle daily dose she'll actually take** beats a strong pill she quits. Under 15 = a doctor's job.
5. This brand is straight with me (says who it's NOT for, plans a retest).
6. Trying it costs me nothing (buy once, one-click cancel, refund).

**The 6 objections to cancel (research §11):** is it enough / needs an infusion · she won't take pills · tastes like pennies · why pay $89 ($33/tin) when drugstore iron is $16 · "the doctor said she's fine" · iron is dangerous/overdose · will it work & how fast · subscription trap · scam/heavy metals · "is it for someone like her."

**The page's ONE job:** take a mum who thinks her kid is just a tired teenager and walk her, belief by belief, to "I need to know my daughter's number, and this is the honest, easy way to do it." Sell the **plan AND the transformation** — not the pill.

## 0B · HER DREAM OUTCOME & CORE DESIRES (the emotional engine)

Belief-change gets her to "this is real." **Desire gets her to buy.** We must sell the *after*, not just fix the *before*. The research dream outcome is one line: **"She's herself again."** Under it sit her real core desires — tap ALL of them, not just one:

1. **Get her daughter back.** The #1 dream. Her spark, her energy, her focus, her season — the kid she used to be, back. Anchors (research §10, verbatim): *"she's like a different kid" · "she had a very successful running season" · "above 50 and stayed there."* This is the hero emotion: not "more iron," but **"I want my girl back."**
2. **Be the mum who caught it.** Identity + pride + vindication. She becomes the mother who didn't let her fall through the gap — not the one who missed it. Anchor: *"your post makes me feel like I'm not going mad!"* / "Be the mom who checked."
3. **Calm control.** End the midnight-Googling, the guessing, the helplessness. A number, a plan, a date. *"I know her number, I have a plan, I'm not guessing."* (We sell calm control, not fear relief — research §6.)
4. **Break the cycle.** The generational wound. Jen often has her OWN low-ferritin history: *"I lost my teens to 'just tired.' She won't lose hers."* Giving her daughter what no one gave her.
5. **No fight at breakfast.** The small daily win — a kid who'll actually take it, no nagging, no gagging. Relief she feels every morning.

**The 4 feelings she pays for (research §16), mapped to where the page delivers them:**

**The future-pace rule:** at least twice, the page must make her *see the after* — her daughter at the start line / back in class / her spark back, and herself calm with a number that moved. Vindication + control land on day 1; the physical change lands at day 85. The page sells the whole arc: **know her number → top it up → retest → get your girl back.**

**Where desire gets sold (not just where pain gets agitated):**

## THE 3 THINGS THAT MUST CHANGE VS THE TEMPLATE (read first)
1. **Flip product-first → problem-first.** The template hero sells the product. Ours must open on the *hidden problem + the dismissal story*. The product barely appears until she believes she has a problem.
2. **Build the proof out fully.** Every review / expert / stat / testimonial / transformation slot gets used — a cold page with thin proof doesn't convert. Fill each slot with the strongest proof we can put there: real cited numbers + published expert quotes where they hit hardest, AI-created creative (visuals, spokespeople, video), and seeded lab-slip mom UGC as it comes in. Lead with specific, verifiable stories (the "14 → 31 at 10 weeks" lab-number format) — specificity out-converts volume and keeps the ad account and payment processor out of trouble.
3. **Add what the template is missing:** the Discovery Story, the "who this is NOT for" block, the 2026 news peg, and the **front-loaded subscription offer ladder** (the template has a single $47 buy box — ours needs the 90-day hero / 30-day / one-time tiers + Mum bump + 70-day guarantee).
