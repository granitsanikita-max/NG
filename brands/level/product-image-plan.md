## GLOBAL ART DIRECTION (applies to every image unless overridden)

**Palette (from brief + `back-panel-copy.md` — current, supersedes brand-identity.md v1):**
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Garnet | `#A0203F` | hero brand color, section fills, the product tin body |
| Garnet gradient | `#B62A4C` → `#7C1730` | depth on blocks / tin / buttons |
| Cream | `#F4ECDF` | page + card backgrounds, type on garnet |
| Ink | `#2B1418` | body text on cream |
| Gold | `#D8A24A` | **numerals only** — "her number," the dose, streaks. One warm hit, never decoration |

> ⚑ **Discrepancy flagged (per CLAUDE.md hard rule):** `brand-identity.md` v1 lists Iron Oxide Red `#A63D2D` / Slate / Bone / Ferritin Gold `#C98A3A`. The brief, `back-panel-copy.md`, and the newest tin files (`tin-front-v3/v4-garnet`) all use the **Garnet `#A0203F` / Cream `#F4ECDF`** system. This plan follows Garnet as the live palette. Real tin photos must carry the tin's actual on-pack color; new graphics harmonize to Garnet. Someone should update `brand-identity.md` to match.

- **Type:** Space Grotesk throughout. Wordmark `iryn` always lowercase, tight tracking. Numbers slightly heavier, in Gold.
- **Signature device:** the **"Fe 26" periodic-table tile** (bordered square, element symbol + atomic number) — reads chemistry class, not pharmacy. Use as a small corner mark on diagram/info graphics.
- **Style:** editorial, color-block, premium, matte. NOT generic supplement stock — no glossy pills-on-white, no sparkle, no cartoon, no clinical blue, no red cross, no candy. Discipline/monochrome + one gold hit = the brand.
- **Honesty = design:** every stat carries its PMID/source **on the image** (research §20 rule). Lab numbers are specific and real-looking ("Ferritin: 9", "19 → 54"), never rounded hype.
- **Orientation:** page is **mobile-first**. Default each asset to a **square or 4:5 portrait** master that crops to a desktop 16:9/wide band; note per-asset where it differs. Keep scroll-animations light (load speed = cold CVR).
- **Existing assets to reuse:** `product/assets/tin-front-IRYN(-hires).png`, `tin-back-IRYN-final(-hires).png`, `tin-front-v4-garnet.png`, `logo-system.(png/svg)`.
- **On-pack facts (for any legible tin shot):** front = `iryn` + "gentle daily iron for teen girls" + "one a day" + 19 mg; Supplement Facts = 1 strip (200 mg) · 30 servings · Iron (ferric saccharate) 19 mg 106% · Folate 400 mcg DFE (240 mcg folic acid) 100%; raspberry, no sugar; QR → tryiryn.com.
