# PART 2 - THE BEST VISUAL HOOKS PER VIDEO FORMAT

A visual hook is what's on screen in the first 3 seconds, before a word lands. These are pulled from your Visual Hooks Library (https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVHhmCPn0=). I only picked hooks that work over a talking person or a story opener (not product-demo gimmicks like squeeze or unboxing), because all 3 of our videos are spoken-word.

## VIDEO 1 - Educational UGC: pick the hook that makes the claim feel like evidence
1. **Big Stat Callout** (Graphic & Text Overlays, 19). What you see: a giant number fills the frame. For us: open on **"Ferritin: 9"** or **"40%"** before she speaks. Makes the reveal feel like proof.
2. **Breaking News Lower Third** (Graphic & Text Overlays, 10). What you see: a "BREAKING:" TV-news ticker over footage. Borrows authority and urgency. For us: the 2026-news angle, "New 2026 guidance: check her ferritin by 14." Proven on a supplement (Hard Ketones, 115d).
3. **Bold Headline Text Box** (Graphic & Text Overlays, 09). What you see: a large high-contrast text block owns the top third from frame 1. For us: "The 1 iron number her blood test skips." Clean, cheap, reads instantly.
- **Bonus for the mechanism version:** **Whiteboard Explainer** (25) - a hand draws the diagram in real time. Perfect for the "iron savings account vs spending" explainer.

## VIDEO 2 - Mentor Story: pick the hook that signals a real insider, not an ad
1. **Yap Cold Open** (Subject Motion, 60). What you see: the person just starts talking, nothing happening visually. This is exactly how the Chewy insider opens, and it's the most native, trust-first start.
2. **Fly on Wall** (Subject Motion, 50). What you see: subject looking off-screen, podcast-interview style. Makes the coach/nurse feel interviewed, which reads as authority not ad.
3. **Slam/Drop Start** (Subject Motion, 88). What you see: a hand slams a book/clipboard on a table, then she speaks. For us: a coach drops a team roster or race sheet, "I've coached 400 girls. I can tell which ones ran out of iron."

## VIDEO 3 - Mum Epiphany: pick the hook that feels like a private confession
1. **Setting Down Phone** (Subject Motion, 68). What you see: first frames are shaky as she sets the phone down and settles in to talk. Signals a real, unscripted confession. Perfect for "I spent a year telling my daughter to try harder."
2. **Problem Reenactment** (Subject Motion, 62). What you see: the problem is acted out first (daughter face-down asleep at 4pm, pale, foggy) before the mum speaks. Shows the Angle S symptom instantly.
3. **Face Lean-In to Lens** (Subject Motion, 70). What you see: her face leans into the lens, eyes filling the frame. Intimacy and "listen to me" urgency for the regret line.
