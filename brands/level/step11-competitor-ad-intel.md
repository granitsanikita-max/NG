# IRYN - Step 11: Competitor ad intelligence (live pull, 2026-10-06)

## The one-line takeaway
The big scalers in our adjacent space (Hiya, EllaOla, First Day) are all **kid / picky-eater / behavior coded**, and they scale on **IMAGE and DCO (dynamic) statics far more than video.** None of them own teen-girl + iron + ferritin + mum. Our gap from the research holds. And it confirms your instinct: **cheap native statics are what actually scales in this category.**

## Per-competitor findings

### First Day  (BEST model for us)
- **Scale: ~1,460 active ads on the page.** This is a serious operator.
- **Format mix: image-heavy, with a video minority.** Their longest runners (150-205 days) are statics.
- **Winning concepts (proven, long-running):**
  1. **Testimonial static** - "I didn't think there would be anything noticeably different about these vitamins, but there is! I truly feel like the supplementing of vitamins and minerals..." (ran 204 days, scaled to 10 duplicates)
  2. **Problem-callout static** - "Constantly feeling exhausted? Struggling just to get through the day? 👇 You might be dealing with something COMPLETELY invisible to the human eye." (169 days)
  3. **Founder-story video** - "Trouble sleeping... picky eating... fighting to get out the door for school and crying over breakfast... sound familiar? I can relate, that was my life..." (167 days)
- **Why it matters:** same mum-of-kid/teen avatar, ~$80 AOV, and the exact formats we've built (testimonial, problem-callout static, founder/epiphany video). They prove our format choices scale. Steal the testimonial-static structure hardest.

### EllaOla  (scaling hardest right now)
- **Scale: 1,060 to 1,174 active ads.** Aggressively scaling.
- **Format mix: mostly DCO (dynamic creative) + image, almost no video.**
- **Winning concepts:**
  1. **Behavior/mood angle** - "Children today are struggling with focus, behavior, and appetite due to underlying nutrient deficiencies, often undetected by pediatricians."
  2. **"Behavior Board" static** - "Meltdown at breakfast? Attitude before lunch? Cranky by dinner? Bingo. You've just filled out the Behavior Board." (a checklist/self-diagnose static, same family as our X-Signs checklist)
  3. **Picky-eater product static** - "now available unflavored for even the pickiest eaters!"
  4. **Relatable mum-of-teen** - "They didn't tell you that raising teens meant making magic out of cheese sticks and breakfast refusals."
- **Why it matters:** they run the symptom-checklist / self-diagnose static at massive scale, which is exactly our Format 2. Validated. Their angle is behavior/mood (we don't touch that, per research), but the FORMAT is proven.

### Hiya  (category leader, kid-locked)
- **Scale: ~711 active ads.**
- **Format mix: image-dominant, some video.**
- **Winning concepts:**
  1. **Benefit-list static** - "15 essential vitamins, 12 fruits and veggies blend, 1 great taste, 0 added sugar."
  2. **Testimonial static** - "Our pediatrician said no more gummies so we switched to Hiya... 4000+ 5-star reviews."
  3. **Their iron ad** - "Iron is the #1 most common nutrient deficiency among kids... carbonyl iron, gentle on kids' stomachs." (image)
- **Why it matters:** proves the benefit-list and testimonial statics work, but they're "ages 2 to 12" locked. Their iron ad is kid-framed, which is the door we walk through for teens.

### NovaFerrum  (our "highest threat," but smaller + kid-coded)
- **Scale: ~27 active ads.** Much smaller than the above.
- **Format mix: balanced image / video / dco.**
- **Winning concepts:**
  1. **Dad-founder story** - "NovaFerrum was created by a dad who refused to give up."
  2. **Picky-eater drops** - "Does your little one turn their head every time you bring out the iron drops? NovaFerrum YUMMY is the raspberry-grape drop they'll actually open their mouths for."
  3. **Battle framing** - "Vitamin time shouldn't be a battle."
- **Why it matters:** they own "iron + picky kid + founder," but it's all **little-ones (drops, toddlers)**, zero teen framing. Still the closest on iron. Watch if they move to teens.

### The ferritin / "get her tested" landscape (US)
- Searching **"ferritin" in US ad copy** surfaces **blood-TEST companies, not iron supplements.** Dominated by **SiPhox Health** (~109 active ads) running **carousel + image** ads on at-home biomarker kits ("17 biomarker kit, $165 vs $694," witty Star-Wars/Yoda copy).
- **Why it matters:** the "know her number / test first" message in the US is currently owned by testing companies, carousel-listicle format. That's both a cue (carousel listicle works for the testing angle) and a reminder our edge is pairing the test message with the gentle daily fix, which they don't sell.

## Cross-competitor patterns (the money insights)
1. **Statics scale harder than video in this category.** Every mega-scaler (First Day 1,460 / EllaOla 1,060 / Hiya 711) is image + DCO dominant. Your "native statics are cheap and effective" instinct is correct and data-backed. Lead the next batch with statics.
2. **The three static structures that repeat across all of them:** (a) **testimonial** static, (b) **problem-callout / self-diagnose checklist** static, (c) **benefit-list** static. We've built the checklist and callout. We have NOT built a **testimonial static** or a **benefit-list static** yet. Gap to fill.
3. **Founder / origin story is the proven video** (First Day, NovaFerrum both run it). We built Epiphany (mum regret) which is close but not a founder story. A "why I made this" founder video is an untested proven format for us.
4. **DCO (dynamic creative) is how they scale a winning static** - once a static wins, they run many variations as DCO. That's a day-20 move for us, not now.
5. **Nobody owns teen-girl iron.** Confirmed again. The whole field is kids (2-12) or picky-eater/behavior. Our lane is still open.
