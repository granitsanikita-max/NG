# IRYN - Step 13: Batch 2 scripts (5 formats, framework-executed + chiefed)

## SHARED FOUNDATION (built once, every script marches to it)

**North Star (the ONE belief that unlocks the buy):** *"Her blood test was normal, but it never checked the number that was actually low (her ferritin), and there's a gentle daily strip that fixes it."*

**The 6 Necessary Beliefs (Copywriting ⑦ - list before writing; every line moves her down this chain, nothing else survives):**
1. My daughter's exhaustion might not just be "being a teenager."
2. A normal blood test can miss it, because ferritin is a separate number most panels never run.
3. Low iron stores (the ferritin gap) is a common, specific, fixable cause, and periods, growth and sport drain it.
4. A gentle daily strip will actually get into her, unlike the pills she quits.
5. IRYN specifically is the right one (right dose, made for teen girls, guaranteed).
6. Trying it now is low-risk (70-day money-back, cancel anytime).

**The mechanism, split UMP/UMS (Ad Fundamentals ⑤):**
- **UMP (mechanism of the PROBLEM) = the Ferritin Gap.** A standard blood count checks haemoglobin, the iron moving in her blood right now (her "spending money"). It does not check ferritin, the iron she keeps in storage (her "savings"). In teen girls the savings empty first, because periods, growth and sport all pull from them. So she tests "normal" while her reserve is gone. ~40% of teen girls are low; most are NOT anaemic, which is exactly why the usual test clears them.
- **UMS (mechanism of the SOLUTION) = a gentle daily strip she'll actually take.** IRYN is a dissolvable strip made for her years (a sensible daily dose, not a toddler's gummy and not a grown woman's horse-pill), raspberry, melts on her tongue, one a day. Adherence IS the fix, because the best iron on earth does nothing sitting in a drawer.
- **Honesty caveat beat (every ad, non-negotiable):** if her iron is very low or she's already anaemic, that's a doctor's dose first, not us.

**The enemy (research §4B): The Gap Years** - the shelf gives her a toddler's gummy or a grown woman's pill, the lab range calls empty "normal," and everyone calls it "just a teenager."

**Angles (research): C = normal bloodwork · B = sport · S = not herself.** Each script below has a primary angle + modular hook-swaps for the other two.

**Proof rule on every line:** real published guidance only (AAP 2026, ASH 2026, ~40%, 83.6% not anaemic). Persona mum stories are allowed; a specific fabricated customer ferritin number presented as a real lab result is NOT. No fake review counts, no named "doctors."
