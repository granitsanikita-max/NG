Read this once

Straight upYou're not lazy, and you're not a little kid.

If you've been dragging, falling asleep after practice, cold all the time, foggy in class, that's not a character flaw and it's not you being dramatic. Sometimes it's just low iron, and low iron is a number you can fix.

Here's the deal, no lecture. Your body stores iron like a savings account. Periods, growing, and training spend it. A normal blood test checks the cash in your pocket, not the savings. So you can look "fine" on paper and still be running low. That's probably what's been going on.

The whole job

One strip. Once a day. That's it. It melts on your tongue, tastes like raspberry, no pill to choke down, nothing to carry. You're not going to feel a lightning bolt tomorrow. It builds up quietly over a couple of months, and then one day you notice you're just... not wiped out anymore.

This page is so you can prove it to yourself. Write your starting number, tick off the days, and check the number again in a couple of months. Watch it climb. That's the point.

Why one a day actually matters

  • Every day beats a big day. Iron builds up slowly, so a strip most days does way more than doubling up now and then.
  • Take it with water or juice. A splash of orange juice helps it absorb. Skip milk, tea or coffee right after, they block it. Wait about an hour.
  • Pick a time you won't forget. Morning, before practice, whenever. Attach it to something you already do every day.

One honest thing

This isn't about being perfect. You'll miss a day. Fine. Just get back to it. The streak below is a tool, not a test you can fail.

What the new number tells you

  • Climbing and you feel better → keep going, recheck down the line.
  • Climbing but slowly → normal, iron takes time. Stay with it.
  • Not moving, or you still feel rough → tell your mum, and loop in your doctor. Something else might be going on, and that's worth checking.

You didn't need a personality transplant or more sleep you don't have time for. You needed a number brought back up. Now you know how to do it.
