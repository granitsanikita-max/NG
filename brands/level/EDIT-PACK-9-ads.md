# IRYN — Edit Pack · all 9 video ads

Everything to cut the 9 ads: the exact opening line + which mum, the clip order (with the 3 reorders baked in), the text hook (on-screen caption, first 3s), the visual hook (what's on screen first 3s + how to do it + a reference to watch), and the b-roll clips per ad with links.

B-roll links are free for commercial use (Pexels / Pixabay, no credit needed). Every clip was checked frame by frame. "use Xs" = best seconds to cut. (crop) = landscape, crop to 9:16. Full backup library with extra options: `broll-clips-PURCHASE-READY-VIDEOS.md` in the repo.

The repeats are by MUM/SET, not duplicates: 3 kitchen (Normal Bloodwork), 3 living room (Not Herself / Mum's History), 3 car (Sport). Same mum, 3 different scripts each.

Visual-hook device library (watch how each looks): https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVHhmCPn0=

# HOW TO USE A VISUAL HOOK (first 3 seconds)

All 9 are talking-head/selfie. The strongest opener is a **Problem Reenactment**: before the mum says a word, cut ~1.5s of b-roll of the tired teen (asleep at 4pm / head down in class / trailing at the track), then land on the mum. The symptom hits first, the text hook sits on top, then she talks. Each ad below names the exact b-roll clip to open on, plus backup devices you can swap in.
