## S4 — "See the Benefits and Compare" → THE FERRITIN GAP (mechanism reveal)
Blueprint job: the UMP. "Normal blood ≠ full stores." Belief #2.
- **Heading:** Her blood test checked the wrong number.
- **Col 1 (the gap the test misses):** A standard blood test measures the iron in her blood today — like the cash in her pocket. It doesn't check ferritin: the iron she keeps in storage — her savings. She can look "normal" on paper while her savings quietly run dry. That's the ferritin gap.
- **Col 2 (why it gets missed):** 83.6% of girls who are low in iron still have a normal hemoglobin — the one number most doctors check.¹ So "her bloodwork was fine" and "she's low on iron" can both be true at once. The only way to know is to check ferritin by name.
  - ¹ Powers et al., 2019 — PMID 37367984
- **Note:** metaphor (cash vs savings) is the memorable hook; heading stays plain-clear. Every stat carries its PMID.
