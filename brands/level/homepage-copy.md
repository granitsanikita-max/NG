## S4 — "See the Benefits and Compare" → THE FERRITIN GAP (mechanism reveal)
Blueprint job: the UMP. A normal blood count does not mean full stores. Belief #2.
- **Heading:** Her blood test checked the wrong number.
- **Col 1 (the gap the test misses):** A standard blood test only measures the iron in her blood right now. Think of it as the cash in her pocket. It doesn't check ferritin, which is the iron she keeps in storage, like a savings account. She can look fine on paper while that savings account runs empty. That's the ferritin gap.
- **Col 2 (why it gets missed):** Most doctors only check hemoglobin. But 83.6% of girls who are low in iron still show a normal hemoglobin.¹ So "her bloodwork was fine" and "she's low on iron" can both be true at the same time. The only way to know is to ask for ferritin by name.
  - ¹ Powers et al., 2019, PMID 37367984
- **Note:** cash vs savings is the memorable hook; heading stays plain-clear. Every stat carries its PMID.
