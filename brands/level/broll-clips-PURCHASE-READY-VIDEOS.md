# IRYN — Real B-roll Clips for the 9 Videos (vetted)

All clips are free for commercial use (Pexels / Pixabay, no attribution needed). Every clip was checked frame by frame (4 frames across each clip), not just the thumbnail. "use Xs" = the best seconds to cut. (crop) = landscape, crop to 9:16.
Rejected: wrong age (small kids / older women in daughter roles), corporate stock, anything that looks sick or medical, logos/watermarks.

Biggest gap: free stock has almost no real teen girls (12 to 16) in sport, and none of the exact story beats (the mum-to-teen pill refusal, the door slam, the cold dinner plate). Those are on the SHOOT list at the bottom.
