**How to read the Source column:**
- **REAL B-ROLL · clip #N** = use the vetted stock clip #N from the b-roll doc (clips 10 to 35 are already downloaded). No prompt, no credits.
- **EDITOR** = build it in CapCut from your product photos (tin photo, back label photo, end card). No credits.
- **AI · P#/S#** = make it with the prompts in Part A.

## Settings for every AI shot
- **Start image:** Nano Banana Pro · 2K · 9:16 · 1 image per generation. Attach the listed refs in order.
- **Video:** Kling 3.0 · Image to Video · 9:16 · Standard 720p (std) · **Sound OFF** (these ads use one mum voiceover made in ElevenLabs, so the clips are silent).
- **TIGHT TIMING RULE:** no dead air. The action starts at 0s and fills the whole clip. Length = the beat it covers, with a 3s minimum (Kling's floor). No holds at the end.
- **Product truth:** matte black square tin, 7.5 cm × 7.5 cm, 2 cm deep, hinged lid, garnet-and-cream label. Black foil sachets marked ORAL STRIP / RASPBERRY / FLAVOR, each holding one thin square **brown** strip (about 2.5 cm). Never red. Labels are copied from the photos, never invented. Any words you want read go on as overlays in the editor.
- **Faces:** the teen shots never show her full face (hands, lower face, shoulders down). That keeps it consistent across ads with no new avatar.

**Refs to attach (already uploaded to Higgsfield from your photos):**
- **TIN-FRONT:** tin front
- **TIN-HERO:** tin with raspberries and sachets
- **TIN-OPEN:** open tin with a sachet inside
- **TIN-SACHET:** sachet leaning on the tin
- **STRIP:** sachet with the brown strip
- **SCALE-09:** approved frame 09, hands holding the open tin, used for true size
