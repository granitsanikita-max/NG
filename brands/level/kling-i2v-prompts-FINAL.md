## How to run every clip (same settings each time)

- **Tool:** Kling 3.0 · **Mode:** Image to Video · **Aspect:** 9:16 · **Quality:** Standard 720p (std mode).
- **Start frame:** upload the exact file named on the clip. Don't crop or re-export it.
- **Duration:** set it to the seconds shown on the clip (5 to 10s). Each one is worked out from the line at normal talking pace, plus a short beat before and after. You trim to the "Use in edit" window.
- **Prompt:** paste the prompt text exactly.
- **Negative prompt:** paste the master negative below, every time.
- **Element (optional):** if your Kling has Elements, register @MUM_BLUE (images 01 + 22), @MUM_KITCHEN (02) and @MUM_SPORT (03), and add the right one. The start image already locks her face, so this only adds extra hold.
- **Sound: ON.** She actually speaks her line in every talking clip, so her mouth moves to real words. Every line is timed at a natural ~2.3 words per second (long words like "healthcare professional" and "19mg" get extra time). If her voice changes between clips, keep the video and lay one consistent ElevenLabs VO over it, then run lip-sync on the clips marked **LIP-SYNC**.
- **Text on screen** (captions, "within normal range", "19mg · one a day") goes on as **overlays in the edit**, never in the prompt.

**MASTER NEGATIVE PROMPT (paste in every clip):**
> text, captions, subtitles, watermark, logo, extra people, phone visible in frame, morphing face, face changing, plastic skin, airbrushed skin, warped hands, extra fingers, fused fingers, changing product label, product changing size, blurry face, jump cut, camera shake after settling, cartoon, CGI
