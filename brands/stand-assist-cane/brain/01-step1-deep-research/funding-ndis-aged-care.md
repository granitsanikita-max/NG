# S1 — FUNDING (AU) — can a buyer use government funding to buy from our DTC Shopify store? — 2026-11-25

## VERDICT
- **NDIS (under-65 disability scheme): YES for self-managed and plan-managed participants** — "You can buy from any supplier." NDIA-managed: NO (registered providers only). Small slice of our market (NDIS is for people who entered under 65 with a disability — older seniors mostly aren't on it; INFERENCE from scheme design, not verified here). [F1]
- **Support at Home AT-HM (aged-care, from 1 Nov 2025): effectively NO for a consumer-initiated DTC purchase.** The participant's Support at Home PROVIDER "sources and supplies" non-prescribed products; funding needs an aged-care assessment that approves AT-HM; items must be on the AT-HM List and match "assessed clinical need/s". A provider *could* buy from us as "an assistive technology supplier", but that is B2B, not a Meta DTC sale. Walking sticks ARE named as eligible AT. Low tier = under/up to $500. [F2][F3]
- **DVA RAP: NO.** Needs clinical assessment + prescription; items supplied via DVA contracted suppliers; "If you decide to buy one without a clinical assessment and prescription, we may not reimburse you afterwards." [F4]
- **So What → do this:** Don't build the offer around funding. At most an FAQ line: "NDIS self- or plan-managed? You can usually buy from any supplier — we provide a tax invoice." (Check with Nikita's accountant that invoices carry ABN/GST details plan managers need.) Do NOT claim "NDIS-approved" or "aged-care funded". The AT-HM wording ("walking sticks" listed as funded AT) is useful only as proof that government sees walking sticks as safety equipment.
