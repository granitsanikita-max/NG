She's sent to the spare room (73 of 479), it started with menopause (59), she fears it's apnoea (52), and she dreads shared rooms on trips (51). She's been burned by cheap mouthguards (95), strips and tape.
- **Region — the brief's UK-first is overturned:**

The device holds the jaw gently forward in small steps. Honest limits: boil-and-bite is the weakest MAD class; women report more side effects; say "can", never "stops / cures / first night".
