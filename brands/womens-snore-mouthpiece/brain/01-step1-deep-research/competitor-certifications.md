# Competitor certification check: anti-snoring mouthpieces (UK / AU / US)
Compiled 2026-11-23. This is a compliance research note, not legal advice.

**Method.** I read each product page live (curl and Firecrawl, 23 Nov 2026), cross-checked it against the saved `offers_raw/<name>/` captures and grepped the page text and HTML for CE, UKCA, MHRA, UKRP, ARTG, TGA, 510(k), K-numbers, FDA, Class and manufacturer names. **Limits:** I did not OCR images, so a CE mark that appears only in a product photo would be missed. I also did not check packaging or IFUs.

**How each database was checked.**
- **UK, MHRA PARD.** The pard.mhra.gov.uk front-end is a JavaScript single-page app. It has no captcha and no login. I queried the same public JSON endpoints the page itself calls:
  - `POST /searchManufacturers`, `/searchManufacturersAdvanced` (by productName and by GMDN 47526 "Mandible-repositioning sleep-disordered breathing orthosis", 324 manufacturers) and `/searchDevicesByManufacturers`.
  - Raw JSON is in `data/certs/pard/`.
- **AU, TGA ARTG.** I scraped the record and search pages (tga.gov.au/resources/artg/...). curl is blocked there, so I used Firecrawl, which does a normal fetch.
- **US.** I used openFDA `device/510k` (applicant and device_name) and `device/registrationlisting` (proprietary_name and k_number).

**What a "not found" result means.** If a brand is not found, no registration exists under its brand or manufacturer name. A white-label unit could still be registered under an OEM's name that the page doesn't disclose. Even so, a business that makes medical claims in UK ads must be able to show the conformity marking and registration (CAP 12.1).

## Takeaways for our listing
1. **Only 5 of the 15 can show a verifiable registration:** Snoreeze, SomnoGuard, Happy Sleep, Hushd Avera and Somnofit-S. Every Meta-style dropship store checked (AirVex, Honex, Feliora, Zcomfort, Hushly, Velarosa, Soothie, SnoreMedic) has none.
2. **Fabricated FDA claims.** Zcomfort says "FDA-cleared" and Hushly says "FDA APPROVED". Neither has a clearance on file, and the FDA doesn't use the word "approved" for these devices.
3. **Compliant patterns to copy:**
   - SomnoGuard 3 (UK): foreign manufacturer, UKRP, PARD Class I.
   - Somnofit-S (US): "FDA-cleared Class II … not intended to … treat OSA".
   - Happy Sleep (AU): ARTG number shown on the PDP.
