# BRAND BRIEF: Anti-snoring mouthpiece for women (Nikita's draft, Nov 2026, verbatim as given)

One-liner
The anti-snoring mouthpiece for women who are tired of being "the snorer".
Who we sell to

* Women 40–60 who snore, peaking at 50–59.
* She's embarrassed: it's "a man's problem", her partner jokes about it, she dreads sharing a room.
* She's tried nasal strips, mouth tape or sprays and they didn't work.

The play no one else is running

* Mouthpiece brands: every one sells to men or to "your partner", in the same medical language ("FDA-cleared, dentist-designed, jaw forward").
* Brands already targeting women:
   * Women's mouth tape (Beauty Sleep): 700+ ads, and its ads say "no mouthguards".
   * A tongue device (SnoreSolvers): aimed at women 50+, US and Canada only.
* The gap: nobody runs a women-first mouthpiece brand on Meta.
* Our position: we're the women's mouthpiece. We speak to her embarrassment, not to her husband's sleep, and we're the step up when tape didn't fix it.

Positioning levers

* Person: women who snore (not "your partner").
* Signal: a private, grown-up fix, not a medical device and not a joke.
* Frame: "It might be your jaw, not your lips" (the upgrade from tape and strips).

Enemy
"Snoring is a man's thing." Plus the jokes at breakfast, and the cheap tapes and strips that don't fix it.
Product

* Adjustable boil-and-bite mouthpiece (blue/white, VitalSleep style).
* Moulds to your teeth, adjusts in small steps, slim, with a case.

Offer

* 1 mouthpiece + case: £49 / A89/US59.
* Best value 2-pack (one for travel or a spare): £69 / A119/US79.
* 60-night "fits and works, or your money back" guarantee.
* Free shipping. Gentle-start guide included ("start at the lowest setting, one step every 3 nights").

Countries (in order)

1. UK first: no women's-mouthpiece ads at all, and the tape brand barely runs there. The only big mouthpiece seller (Feliora, £26.95 two-for-one) is weak.
2. Australia and New Zealand next: open for a mouthpiece, but the tape brand is strong in AU and pharmacies stock mouthpieces.
3. US later: biggest market (about 75% of women snorers), but crowded and needs the medical-device paperwork sorted first.

Test cells (1 ad set + 1 landing page each, same product)

1. "Women snore too" (embarrassment, 45–60)
   * "1 in 4 women snore. Nobody talks about it."
   * "He jokes about it at breakfast. You don't laugh."
   * "After 45, a lot of us start snoring. Here's the quiet fix."
2. Switch from tape (aimed at the tape brand's audience)
   * "Mouth tape didn't stop it? It might be your jaw, not your lips."
   * "Tape keeps your mouth shut. This keeps your airway open."
3. Girls' trip / sharing a room
   * "Girls' trip in 3 weeks and you're the one who snores?"
   * "Don't be the one they talk about at breakfast."
4. Back in his bed (relationship, her point of view)
   * "He moved to the spare room. Bring him back."
   * "Sleep in the same bed again, without the jokes."

Watch

* If "too big / doesn't fit" is more than about 10% of refunds or support messages in the first 50 orders, prioritise the Small size.
