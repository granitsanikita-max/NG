# §2B · removed from the page on 2026-11-03 (operator request)

No SAY / DON'T SAY table. The regulatory facts that limit claims are applied in §13 (Doc 3), §17 and below. These are the ones the offer and funnel must not break:

- **It is a medical device in every launch market.** UK: Class I (a device that repositions the jaw modifies "the anatomy or … a physiological process") [S2][S3]. AU: mandible-repositioning orthoses sit on the ARTG as **Class 1** [S5]. US: Class II, 510(k) [S7].
- **AU: no ARTG listing, no sale.** "medical devices must be included on the ARTG before they can be supplied in Australia"; penalties run to "up to five years imprisonment" [S5]. An overseas maker needs an Australian sponsor [S5].
- **AU ads: only the ARTG purpose.** "it is an offence to advertise therapeutic goods for an indication or purpose that is not included in the ARTG entry" [S6].
- **UK ads: no medical claim without CE/UKCA + MHRA registration** (via a UK Responsible Person). CAP 12.1 allows medical claims only "for a medical device with the applicable conformity marking" [S3][S4]. The ASA has a snoring note saying the same [S2].
- **Safest claim wording, all markets:** "an aid in the reduction of snoring" (the FDA OTC indication) [S7]. **Never** "stops / cures snoring", "silent nights guaranteed", "treats sleep apnoea", or "MHRA / TGA / FDA approved" [S2][S3][S10].
- **Apnoea = referral, not a sale.** CAP 12.2: never "discourage essential treatment" [S3]. A GP red-flag box goes on the pre-sell and the product page.
- **Meta, personal attributes:** no hook may "assert or imply" she has a health condition. "Do you snore?", "you're the one who snores?" and "Mouth tape didn't stop it?" are out. Stats, PSA-style lines and first-person stories are in [S8].
- **Meta, health & wellness:** 18+ targeting, and no "specific outcomes within a set timeframe" without a qualifier ("silent from night 1" is out) [S9].

Under all three sits the belief she's absorbed: **"snoring is a man's thing"**. That belief is why the trays are a man's size and why she's embarrassed to ask.
