**Formulas (as the page defines them)**
- CM $ = Price − (landed per unit × units in the order) − (3% × Price) − other per-order costs (0 here).
- CM % = CM $ ÷ Price.
- Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ CM % (= Price ÷ CM $). Break-even CPA = CM $.
- Target CPA at n% net = CM $ − (n × Price). Target ROAS = Price ÷ Target CPA. "Price" = the full order value (2-pack = the whole order).
- **Scale line** = the 30% net CPA (at or under it → scale). **Kill line** = the 20% net CPA (consistently above it → fix or kill). Break-even sits next to both in the table.
- Every variant has CM % above 30%, so every target is possible.

**AliExpress-cost scenario (one line):** at AUD 14.37/unit (£7.28), CM % rises to **80.9% / 72.8% / 82.1% / 75.9%** and break-even ROAS drops to **1.24 / 1.37 / 1.22 / 1.32**. Scale-line CPAs become A$45.26 / A$50.99 / £25.55 / £31.66 (INFERENCE arithmetic).

**Read-out:** the 2-pack earns more CM $ per order in both regions (+A$8.53 / +£8.98) but needs a higher ROAS. Treat the single as the volume entry and the 2-pack as the AOV lever. The table can't tell you which converts better; only a test can (INFERENCE).

# §0 · Product & core problem

**One line:** an adjustable boil-and-bite mouthpiece that holds the lower jaw forward. It solves ONE problem: **her own snoring**, which has turned her into "the snorer". She's sent to (or sends him to) the spare room, and she dreads every shared room. It's for **women 40–60 (peak 50–59)** [S1].

**Need, not want.** This is a nightly problem with a real social and health cost, not a "nicer gadget":
- Bank pains: embarrassed by her snoring **54 of 479** · relationship strain **42** · snoring got worse with menopause **42** · dread sharing a room **38** · fear it's sleep apnoea **35** [S20].
- The #1 trigger is a **partner complaint (49 of 479)** [S20].
