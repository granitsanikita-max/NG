## 0 — The Marlboro playbook (the model we're copying)

This is the operator's reference brand, and it's the clearest proof in marketing history that a *feeling* can be manufactured and delivered on purpose.

**The 3-line lesson:**
1. Marlboro **started as a failing women's cigarette** — "Mild as May," red lipstick-matching filter tips, down to **0.25% market share by 1954**. Then Leo Burnett won by selling an **IDENTITY, not a product**: he made the filter irrelevant and cast *"the most generally accepted symbol of masculinity … a cowboy,"* on the insight that *"the emotional response triggered by a package transfers directly to the product inside."*
2. The identity was delivered **almost entirely through imagery + one consistent world** ("Marlboro Country") — show-don't-tell, a character smokers projected onto, repeated so relentlessly that by the 1971 broadcast-ad ban a silent billboard still worked. It was so magnetic that by 1980 **more than a third of Marlboros were smoked by women** — a strong identity pulls in people outside its literal target; you never water it down.
3. Our play is the **exact inverse journey** (a women's-first object brought back and built for her) using the same machine: cast the heroine, build her world, show it forever, never explain it.

> **Guardrail:** Marlboro is a MODEL OF CRAFT only. We sell an honest leather good. We borrow the *machinery* (cast a character → build a world → show it relentlessly) and **none** of tobacco's deception, health-dodge, or brand marks.

## 1 — The feeling we sell + the identity we hand her

**The projectable self (this is our "cowboy" — the self she steps into when she pulls it out):**

> ## "When you carry this, you are the woman who has her life handled — the one who edited everything down to what matters and still walks in looking like the most put-together person in the room."

**The feeling, in her own register (all four fire at once):** *in-control & light* (edited down) · *elevated-without-trying* ("I felt very fancy") · *seen & complimented* (the compliment-at-the-register is the dominant identity hit) · *smart-insider, not duped* ("worth 10x" — she got there without the tax).

**Short forms (tiered by surface):**
- Register / insert moment: **"I'm the woman who carries less — on purpose."**
- Bio / hashtag form: **"She edited her life down. It shows."**
- One-word badge: **the edited woman** (vs. the logo-tax woman and the junk-drawer woman).

> **Why she'll actually adopt it:** it's **projection, not self-improvement** (the Nike / Glossier rule — "if you have a body, you're an athlete" / "you look good"). It affirms she *already is* her; the object just matches her. She is never broken-then-fixed by a purchase — she's recognized. This is the required dodge of the "that girl = buy-your-worth" critique.

## 3 — Voice & tone: how we talk

**The register (locked):** warm-but-sharp — a knowing friend with great taste. She feels *in on it*, not sold to. Never shouty, never precious.

### The 6 voice principles (each is a rule)
1. **Calm confidence, never shouty.** State the true thing once, plainly — no exclamation stacks, no ALL-CAPS, no fake urgency.
2. **Dry wit, not jokes.** The smile lives in a true observation ("you won't be going back to the brick"), never in puns or "lol."
3. **Say the true thing the category won't.** Name the logo tax and the peel-in-a-year fake leather out loud — honesty is the flex and the trust wedge no no-name offers.
4. **She's in on it, not sold to.** Write to a peer who already has taste ("if you know, you know"); never explain the obvious, never pitch.
5. **Plain words, 5th–8th grade.** Short sentences, concrete nouns, zero marketing abstraction.
6. **Show the feeling, don't claim it.** Give her the scene (three people asked at the register; the bag suddenly lighter) — affirm she already IS the woman; the object matches her, it doesn't fix her.

### DO vs DON'T (warm-but-sharp vs. the four tones we avoid)

| Dimension | ✅ DO — warm-but-sharp (us) | ❌ luxury-cold (Celine/Coach) | ❌ dropship-hype (Amazon/TikTok) | ❌ men's-EDC-techy (Ridge/Ekster) | ❌ girlboss-cringe |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Warmth | A knowing friend; invites her in | Aloof, gatekeeping | Over-familiar, "bestie!!" | Flat, gear-forum | Saccharine, "hey queen" |
| Register | Quietly confident, a little dry | Austere, "maison/atelier" | LOUD, "OBSESSED" | Spec-bravado, "tactical" | Pep-talk, "you've GOT this girl" |
| Proof | Honest material + a scene she pictures | "Heritage," implied exclusivity | "VIRAL," fake scarcity | "space-grade aluminium," torture tests | "I manifested this," vibes |
| Who she is | Already put-together; this matches her | Richer/better than you | Missing out until she buys | A user optimizing a loadout | Broken until she "treats herself" |
| Urgency | "When you're ready." / "Back in [color]." | None (too cool to sell) | "BUY NOW, 3 LEFT!!!" | "Restock drop, cop it" | "Don't sleep on this, bestie" |

### Lexicon — USE vs BAN
**USE (lift straight from her swipe):** `the edit` · `edited my life down` · `put-together` · `slim but holds everything` · `if you know, you know` · `taste is the receipt` · `less, done beautifully` · `grab it and go` · `ages, doesn't peel` · `the finishing piece` · `snug, never stuck` · `made for her` · `the brick` (old fat wallet = our villain) · `looks like 10x` · color names as proper nouns (Bone, Espresso, Sage, Butter, Oxblood).

### Copy-ready example lines
- **Hero:** *"You edited everything else down. Here's the piece that proves it."* · *"Taste is the receipt."* · *"Slim enough to forget. Holds everything you won't."*
- **Ad hook:** *"I stopped carrying the brick — and three people asked where I got it."* · *"You don't need a luxury budget to look like you have one."*

- **Restock email subject:** *"Oxblood is back (you asked)."*

> **Voice governance test:** Does it sound like a woman with great taste telling a friend the true thing — calm, a little dry, never shouty, never precious, zero spec-dump, zero hype? If yes, ship. If it argues a feature, begs, or hypes — rewrite.

## 4 — The visual & sensory world: how we present ("The Edit" = our Marlboro Country)

Voice says the true thing; the **world** lets her *see* it in half a second — as "that's me / that's mine," never as "an Amazon listing."

**The world in 3 lines:**
1. A woman who **carries less on purpose**, in warm late-morning light, in real small spaces — a clean nightstand, a café table, the mouth of a small bag — where the cardholder is the one considered object in frame.
2. The hero moment is **the compliment at the register** — the half-second she's *seen* as the most put-together person in the room; everything else is the calm life that earns it.
3. Always **golden / warm, never studio-cold** — one nameable color, lots of air; it belongs on *her* nightstand, not a tech-EDC flat-lay or a luxury glass vitrine.

> **Casting rule (the Marlboro-cowboy principle, non-negotiable):** she is **who the buyer wants to BE**, not a model selling to her. Primary: a quietly-confident woman ~30–45, neutral unbranded styling, **zero visible logos**, caught mid-life and unbothered — the object *matches* her, doesn't *fix* her. Cast micro / real women (she trusts peers over mega-influencers). Never: male hands, men's-EDC flat-lays, girlboss power-poses, aloof luxury models, or anyone holding it to camera saying "obsessed."

### Art direction — the rules that make a frame "ours" on sight
- **Palette:** warm neutrals only as the base (bone, oat, warm greige, espresso, ink — warm white, never clinical blue-white) + **exactly ONE nameable signature color per drop** (Bone · Espresso · Sage · Butter · Oxblood), treated as a proper noun. This is **our red-chevron equivalent** and our compliment-bait. One color owns a drop; never a rainbow grid.
- **Type:** a warm **humanist sans** for body + **one restrained high-taste serif** for headlines; generous white space. Never geometric-tech (reads Ridge/Ekster), never script-girly (reads coquette-cheap), never bold-condensed sale type (reads dropship).
- **Logo:** **no face logo, ever.** The signature is a tiny tone-on-tone **blind deboss** felt by the thumb, nearly invisible in photos. *The absence of a logo IS the logo* (Philo-era Céline / Aesop whisper-not-shout).
- **Photography:** warm natural light, real surfaces, the object *in use / in a bag / in a hand / on a nightstand*, negative space, a little lived-in imperfection. **The 7 nevers:** no white-seamless e-com tile · no floating-product cutout or 6-angle spec montage · no visible logos/competitor bags/clutter · no flash or cold blue-white · no stock "diverse hands holding product to camera" · no SALE/VIRAL/star-rating bursts · no RFID/tech iconography or feature-arrow callouts.

### The recurring image kit (our "cowboy + range + light")

| Element | What's in frame | Identity it signals |
|---|---|---|
| **The light** | Warm, low, late-morning daylight; soft shadow; never flash | Effortless, expensive, calm, well-rested |
| **The small real bag** | Cardholder in the mouth of a small/crossbody bag with 2–3 edited things | "I edited my life down"; slim-but-enough |
| **The hand at the café/register** | A woman's hand pulling ONE card with a thumb | "snug, never stuck" shown not claimed; the micro-status moment |
| **The clean nightstand** | Cardholder beside a book, a ceramic, a gold hoop | Quietly put-together; it's *hers* |
| **The color as hero** | Signature-color SKU shot large, close, color named on screen | Compliment-bait → brand recognition |
| **The ageing leather, close** | Macro of grain patina over time (genuine SKUs only) | "ages, doesn't peel"; honest material; BIFL |
| **The brick vs. the slim** | Old fat wallet beside ours; or a de-cluttered bag | The enemy made visible; the relief of lighter carry |

> **Hero scene we return to most (our "cowboy on the ridge"):** the **compliment at the register** — her hand sets / hands over the cardholder at a café counter; a second woman's glance; warm light; no logos, no faces-to-camera. It's the dominant identity hit in every customer thread, and our single most-repeated frame.

### Sensory — the unboxing world (where premium FEEL is delivered at an accessible price)
Budget sellers already win on unboxing ("wrapped in cloth," "its own special box," "that leather smell") — so we must **beat** it. This is where a $39–49 object earns its premium read without a premium price.
- **Box:** a slim rigid box (matte, warm-neutral, tiny blind-deboss wordmark only) — substantial, reusable, giftable as-is.
- **Wrap:** the cardholder in a cloth/muslin wrap (reusable dust pouch), tied simply — the ritual of unwrapping one considered thing.
- **Belief card:** one thick letterpress/deboss card, one line of voice: *"Less, done beautifully. Taste is the receipt — carry it like you've got your life handled."*
- **Material honesty tag:** names *exactly* what this SKU is (full-grain that patinas, OR premium microfiber that won't flake — never PU-as-leather).
- **Material feel:** supple grain that breaks in snug-not-stuck; matte or no hardware; hand-finished edges; **clean smell, never chemical** (a foul off-gas is what she expects from cheap PU — a clean scent is a quiet trust signal).

> **Our one-line sensory signature (our amber-bottle equivalent):** *"Opens like a small gift, breaks in like a good pair of shoes — cloth, then warm grain, then the quiet click of a card sliding home."*

**The 10-shot reusable library (every PDP / email / ad / UGC brief is built from these)**

1. **HERO: compliment at the register** — hand sets/hands over cardholder at a café counter; a second woman's glance.
2. **In the small bag** — cardholder in the mouth of a small/crossbody bag with 2–3 edited items.
3. **One-thumb pull** — macro of a thumb sliding ONE card out, snug-not-stuck.
4. **The color, large** — signature-color SKU close, its own warm light, color named on screen.
5. **Clean nightstand / vanity** — resting beside a book, ceramic, gold hoop.
6. **Ageing-leather macro** — grain softening/patina over time (genuine SKUs only).
7. **The brick vs. the slim (before → after)** — old fat wallet beside ours; or a de-cluttered bag.
8. **Unboxing ritual** — cloth wrap untied → rigid box → belief card.
9. **The gift gesture** — a hand tying the wrap / sliding in a handwritten card.
10. **The woman, caught (not posing)** — at a café/street, unbothered, cardholder incidental in hand.

*(+ optional: flat "the edit" still-life; real-woman UGC in-world.)* Shoot each in every signature color; hold the light + framing identical across years — the repetition is the moat.

## 5 — Belonging & identity mechanics: how we make her JOIN

The growth thesis: **she trusts peers over sponsors**, so belonging is the engine, not an afterthought. Glossier proved the payoff (roughly 80% of customers referred by a friend; customers became the salespeople). Harley proved belonging is a durable moat (H.O.G., 1M+ members). We engineer the same, sized for a $39–49 accessory.

### The enemy & the rally (the glue of the tribe)
A two-headed villain she already rages at — **never a named competitor**:

| Head | What it is | Her verbatim rage |
|---|---|---|
| **The logo tax** | Paying $300–700 for someone else's initials to borrow status | "No LV, Chanel, Prada" · "loud logos kill quiet luxury" · "fake rich buys hype, real rich buys timeless" |
| **The disposable junk drawer** | The $12 no-name that lies about leather and dies in a year | "it showed to not be genuine leather" · "less than a year and it's falling apart" · "a wallet that won't self-destruct after a year?" |

**The rally line:** *"You've been handed a false choice: pay for someone else's logo, or buy the cheap thing that peels in a year. We don't play either game. Taste is the receipt — not the price tag."*

**How we make it SHARED (mechanics, not a slogan):** give the villain nicknames she can use (**"the brick," "the junk-drawer wallet," "the logo tax"**) · make the receipt a brag format (**"Guess what I paid"** — she reveals the price as the punchline) · position US as the way out, never a rival (we punch at the *pattern*, never at Celine/Ridge by name) · make "the edit" the virtue the enemy lacks.

### The manifesto (About page + insert — first 4 lines stand alone on the card)

> **We think taste is quiet.**<br>**It doesn't shout a logo. It doesn't peel in a year.**<br>**We made one small thing, and we made it honestly — a leather that ages instead of flaking, a size built for your hand and your bag, and not one initial to pay for.**<br>**Because what you carry should prove you've got your life handled — not that you can afford someone else's name.**<br><br>**You already did the hard part. You edited your life down to what matters.**<br>**This is just the piece that matches you.**<br>**Snug, never stuck. Light in the bag, heavy on compliments.**<br>**No logo tax. No junk drawer. No apology.**<br>**Less, done beautifully — carry it like you mean it.**

### Community rituals (ranked)
1. **The Compliment Loop (flagship).** Design for the compliment-at-the-register and close the loop: nameable signature color = compliment-bait; a tiny insert line scripts her reply (*"When someone asks — they will — it's the Oxblood. Tell them."*); a day-10 "been complimented yet?" email + the "Guess what I paid" prompt captures UGC; her post → we repost → referral credit.
2. **Name-Your-Color Drops.** Curated color drops on a cadence (scarcity reads as *taste*, not fake-urgency); let the list **name + vote** the next color (co-creation); owners get drops 48h early (ownership = access).
3. **"The Edit" insider feel (tribe without a fee).** A named worldview she's let into, by email + packaging voice — drops early, the naming vote, a no-drama durability guarantee, the occasional "what to carry, what to cut" note. An identity badge, not a points program.
4. **Gifting-as-Recruitment.** Every gift is a new recruit: gift-native unboxing carries the manifesto's first 4 lines (the recipient is inducted, not just handed an object); tiny blind-deboss personalization is the moat vs. Amazon; lean into Q4 / Mother's Day / Valentine's / bridesmaid season.
5. **Community seed & hashtag.** Primary flag **#TheEditedWoman** (identity badge) or **#CarryLessOnPurpose** (her verbatim behavior); ride existing hubs (#whatsinmybag, #cleangirl, #quietluxury). Seed via honest, **over-disclosed** micro-creator UGC (50–300K) where she already trusts peers; earn organic love in r/handbags, r/HerOneBag, r/BuyItForLife; never astroturf.
