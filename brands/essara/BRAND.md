# BRAND — Essara

> Identity file. Filled when the brand is created; read by every station.

- **Slug:** essara
- **Product (one line):** a slim, female-first cardholder — "snug, never stuck" easy-but-secure access, one honestly-named material per SKU (full-grain OR premium microfiber), slim for the small/crossbody bag, at an accessible $39–49.
- **Niche / category:** women's slim cardholder · accessible "un-logo / quiet-luxury" leather accessory.
- **Target customer:** women ~28–55 (core avatar "Mara," 38, ~$130k HH), fashion-literate but logo-shy, clean-girl / quiet-luxury taste; mid "life-edit" (downsized to a small/crossbody bag, going cashless, old wallet dying, RTO, travel). Core pain: **no feminine slim option that works AND lasts** — 37 of 201 settle for a men's wallet. Buys Cuyana/Quince-tier; names Celine/Chanel as the aspiration she won't pay for.
- **Positioning (one line):** *"The warm-but-sharp brand that stands FOR 'taste is the receipt, not the price tag — less, done beautifully' and AGAINST the logo tax and the disposable junk drawer — the cardholder for the woman who edited her life down to what matters and still wants to feel like the most put-together person in the room."*
- **Brand name candidates:** **Essara (LOCKED)**. Rejected: PARED (research lead pick — pared.com + pared.co both taken), Curae, Parely, Less., The Edit, Lune/Odette/Mira.
- **Domain:** **essara.co** (purchased 2026-10-18). `.com` was taken.
- **Created:** 2026-10-18

## Notes (every station must know)
- **NO PRODUCT SOURCED YET.** Every spec is PENDING REAL SPEC — card count, dimensions, thickness, hardware, RFID, AirTag fit, warranty terms, the "snug, never stuck" claim. Do NOT publish a number until a real unit is measured.
- **Economics DEFERRED:** blocked on landed COGS + final price. Hero price to test $39–49; AOV target $65–90; est. landed COGS ~$5–12 (inference only). No ad budget / break-even until real numbers.
- **Material honesty is LAW:** never call vegan/PU "leather"; patina/ageing claims only on genuine-leather SKUs; name the exact grade in the ad itself (FTC 16 CFR 24). Never claim a luxury-house "dupe"; never invent a founder/lineage.
- **Market:** US-primary + UK/CA/AU/NZ. Seasonality Q4-gifting-dominant (Nov–Dec peak, Jan echo); NOT Valentine's/Mother's Day.
- **Strategy model:** Liquid Death (sell an identity a giant can't copy) + Marlboro (cast a character, build one world, show it relentlessly, never explain).
- **Hero mechanism:** "snug, never stuck" (kills the #1 slots-too-tight + #2 fiddly-metal objections). Growth engine: the compliment-at-the-register loop + nameable color drops.
- **Wedge:** positioning + product-execution gap inside an OCCUPIED category (Katie Loxton = cute/gift; The Daily Edited = monogram/premium). Not virgin territory — out-position on stance + mechanism + economics.
- **Tagline:** "Taste is the receipt." (+ "Less, done beautifully.")
