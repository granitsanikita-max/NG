# BRAND — skin-fold cream (working title)

> Identity file. Filled when the brand is created; read by every station.
> Name/identity are PROVISIONAL — the offer/identity station finalizes them.

- **Slug:** skinfold-cream
- **Product (one line):** A daily skin-fold barrier cream (intertrigo relief) — consumable, built for subscribe-and-save.
- **Niche / category:** Health / OTC-adjacent skincare — skin-fold (intertrigo) rash care.
- **Target customer:** Lead avatar = the chronic sufferers nobody sells to: **diabetics, seniors/limited-mobility + the caregivers who wash and dress them, and big / active men** (groin, inner thigh, under-belly, between toes). The pain: a recurring raw, burning, weeping, itchy rash in skin folds — relentless and humiliating. Every brand on the market aims the same "embarrassed plus-size woman, under-breast" story at it; these people are unserved. Secondary test avatar: active/larger men.
- **Positioning (one line):** **"Fold care, not rash cure."** The daily barrier ritual that keeps the fold dry so it never flares — normal body maintenance like brushing your teeth, for people who live with this year-round. CONTRAST vs the field's reactive, clinical, shame-driven "kill the rash" creams aimed at plus-size women. (Explicitly NOT an "honest / trustworthy brand" angle — per Nikita, that's not a real differentiator and wouldn't be true with AI creative.)
- **Brand name candidates (provisional — identity station decides):** FoldWell · DryFold · Stilla · Kove. (Avoid the clinical word "intertrigo" in the consumer name.)

## Notes
- Chosen from a live 6-product WinningHunter sweep (2026-10-19) as the best first brand on a small (~$1–3k) budget. Full reasoning + the other 5 verdicts are in PRODUCT.md.
- **Compliance guardrail (non-negotiable downstream):** lead with cosmetic "skin-protectant / barrier / comfort" claims (zinc-oxide is an OTC skin-protectant ingredient). NEVER "kills yeast/bacteria," "antifungal," "cures candida/intertrigo," no raw-skin before/after, no "do YOU have…" personal-attribute targeting. Those get a young ad account banned.
- **One open gate before money is spent:** a private-label supplier quote confirming landed COGS (target ≤$4–5/unit). Everything else is validated.
