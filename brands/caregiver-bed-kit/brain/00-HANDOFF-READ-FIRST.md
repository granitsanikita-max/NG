# HANDOFF — Caregiver Bed Kit (READ FIRST)

From: product-research (Hunts 3–6, 2026-11-04 → 11-06) · To: deep-research (Step 1 — Deep Market Research)

## 1. The decision (Nikita, 2026-11-05/06)
**Community = the adult daughter (mostly 45–65) caring for an ageing parent at home.** The brand is built FOR HER (the caregiver),
not for the patient. Every product in the line must serve the same customer + positioning (Nikita's rule: one positioning, no
off-brand upsells).

**Hero product = washable, reusable waterproof bed pad (underpad) WITH REINFORCED HANDLES** ("positioning pad"): protects the bed
from night accidents AND lets her slide/turn her parent up the bed without lifting. Ideal spec adds tuck-in wings (no sliding).
Working offer name: **"2am Bed Kit"**.

Why this community (from Hunt 6 discovery, WinningHunter Meta search 2026-11-05):
- ~53–63M US unpaid family caregivers; core buyer = women 45–65 = Facebook's heaviest-using, cheapest-to-reach demo.
- Meta search "caregiver" (US) top advertisers = Temu, an affiliate shower chair, POD ornaments — no caregiving brand.
  "caring for mom" = scattered small sellers only. Initial read: UNOWNED as a brand community (must be re-verified in §1/§2).
- Recurring consumable + lifetime extension potential (bed, bath, skin, mobility, dementia comfort, her self-care).

## 5. Nikita's standing rules
Meta ads only · US first (English) · AU possible · low capital (dropship first; Alibaba OEM after demand proven) · front-end profit ·
never use trust/reviews/founder as a lever · Amazon = info only · AI-creative difficulty = note only · 4+ synonym saturation checks ·
one brand positioning across every product · no copying a competitor's brand 1:1 · compliance: no personal-attribute hooks
("your mom's incontinence"), no graphic content, no medical claims (pressure sores/bedsores prevention = medical claim risk).
