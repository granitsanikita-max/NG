## #1 PICK — Potty training underwear (GO)
- **Proof:** UpAiry (upairy.com, NZ-owned, selling since Mar 2024) — est. **$1.8M–$3.4M/month** revenue, **356k visits/month, 95% US**. ~776 active ads on the brand page + two long-copy "mom persona" advertorial pages (Emma's Mom Hacks 295 ads, Raising Toddlers with Bec 259 ads) running since Nov–Dec 2025.
- **Problem (painkiller):** 3–4-year-olds still in diapers; daycare/preschool requires potty training; constant accidents; parental shame + exhaustion. Ad hook verbatim: "My 4-year-old is still in diapers. And I genuinely don't understand why."
- **Gap:** UpAiry is the only scaled DTC player in reusable training underwear on paid social (others = puppy pads, a toilet seat, Pull-Ups disposables at Target). And UpAiry has trust rot — Trustpilot 3.5 / 435 reviews / 124 negative: hidden subscription for "free gift" gummies (19), leaks & poor absorbency (22), refunds not processed (5), ignored support (9+), shrinks after washing, sizing off, "made in China, no branding" (4).
- **Brand positioning:** the honest, expert potty-training brand — a METHOD + the gear (not a funnel). Enemy = subscription-trap funnels + "diapers pretending to be underwear" that keep kids in diapers longer. Culture = no-shame late trainers, "big kid" identity for the child, a parent community + free potty-training plan.
- **Margin:** AliExpress retail ≈ $5.73/pair (wholesale lower); UpAiry ≈ $21/pair. Bundles (4–6 packs) + night inserts + waterproof bed mat + bedwetting alarm + potty seat = AOV levers.
- **Risks:** product quality is THE battleground (leaks = #1 complaint → order samples, pick a thicker multi-layer absorbent core, test before launch); sizing guide; US children's product rules (CPSIA: tracking label + Children's Product Certificate from a CPSC-accepted lab).
- **Scale lane later:** night-time bedwetting (older kids 5–10) — UpAiry already sells a bed mat + alarm.

## Runner-up — Kids' bath filter (Tubo)
- trytubo.com (CA) — est. $71–120k/month, 19k visits, since May 2024; "don't bathe your kids in chlorine water" (skin irritation/eczema parents). Only player in kids' BATH filters; adult shower filters (Kitsch, Water2skin) own the beauty angle.
- Recurring revenue built in — Tubo's #1 bestseller is 4× replacement filters ($142).
- Risks: smaller proven market; eczema = health-claim landmine.

## Third — Vacuum pet grooming kit (The Groomi)
- thegroomi.com (AU) — est. $147–245k/month, 38k visits, 325 active ads. Shedding pain is real but closer to a "nice-to-have"; Dyson + Amazon kits crowd it.
