## 2. What nobody owns (evidence)
- **Daycare.** 59% of US kids ≤5 are in weekly non-parental care (NCES 2019). Daycare deadline = most common trigger in parent voice (38/252). Daycares default to disposables because they're refastenable ("daycare requires these since they can be re-fastened" — Pull-Ups review). Parents: "3 day potty method…. Didn't really work. Now back to daycare" (r/workingmoms); "pull-ups led to a huge months long regression" (r/toddlers). Ads: daycare only appears as a FEAR deadline, or one watch gadget. No reusable brand sells side-open/refastenable trainers + a daycare plan. No multi-caregiver kit exists.
- Side-snap / velcro reusable trainers exist on AliExpress → sourceable.

## 3. RECOMMENDED POSITIONING — the potty training brand for working parents
**One line:** "Potty training built for parents with jobs — it works at home, at daycare, and at grandma's, because everyone uses the same system."

- **The market (not an angle):** dual-working / daycare families — the majority of US toddlers. Bound by one painful problem: every method assumes a parent is home all day, and daycare undoes the progress.
- **UMP (the villain):** "The Monday Reset." Kid trains over a weekend → goes back to daycare → gets put in pull-ups (refastenable, easy for staff) → mixed signals → regression. It's not your kid, and it's not you — it's the hand-off.
- **UMS (our fix):** one system across every caregiver: side-open trainers daycare can change like a pull-up (no shoes off) + the same pants at home and grandma's + a caregiver hand-off card / daycare plan + labelled packs (home bag / daycare bag / car).
- **Enemy:** the "3-day weekend method" built for a stay-at-home parent, and daycare pull-ups that quietly undo training.
- **Culture / identity:** "We don't have 3 days off." Working-parent realism, a team sport (parents + daycare teachers + grandparents). Real moms AND dads, real daycare teachers on the PDP.
- **Uncopyable test:** UpAiry's whole machine is stay-at-home-mom confessions + "3 days" speed. Adopting "the 3-day method doesn't work for working parents" contradicts its own ads. ✅
- **LTV / AOV:** daycare needs 6–8 spares a day → bigger packs naturally; transparent subscription = refill/replacement packs + size-ups; add-ons: wet bags, travel potty, night pants, bed mat.
- **Meta-safe:** no health terms, broad mainstream audience, no personal-attributes problem.

## 4. Risks (honest)
- Some daycares require disposables by policy — the brand must win the teacher (daycare plan + side-open design); not guaranteed everywhere.
- Product quality is still the battleground (leaks = #1 complaint across the category) — sample and pour-test before launch.
- US children's product rules: CPSIA tracking label + Children's Product Certificate.
- Needs Step 1 deep research to validate with full data before spend.

## 5. Alternative / later line
"Long-road kids" (sensory-gentle, sizes 4–12) — real gap in sizes 6–12, but BrightKidCo holds the ND sensory slot (to size 6), Meta health restrictions apply, and the sensory spec needs private label. Keep as a product line + creator/SEO content, not the lead brand identity.
