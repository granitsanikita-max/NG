## 🔴 A. Things on the page that are WRONG (fix these first — they actively hurt output)

**A1 · §8 Sophistication stages are wrong.** Page: "Stage 4–5 = you NEED a new unique mechanism." Schwartz actually says: Stage 3 = new mechanism · Stage 4 = elaborate/improve the mechanism (faster, easier, bigger) · Stage 5 = market is exhausted on claims AND mechanisms → sell **identification** (who she is). Right now a Stage-5 market (like bunions) gets told to build *another* mechanism instead of using your §4B brand identity — which is exactly the right Stage-5 move.
→ **Proposed fix:** rewrite the stage key in §8 to the correct 1–5 ladder + "Stage 5 → §4B identity, not another mechanism."

**A2 · §5 mixes up awareness and sophistication.** "Pages of threads = high awareness… they've been burned" — "burned" is sophistication, not awareness. Lots of threads only proves she knows the problem.
→ **Fix:** thread volume measures *problem* awareness; "burned/skeptical" gets measured in §8.

**A3 · §5 "AI % split across 5 stages" breaks your own One Rule.** An AI asked for percentages invents them — that's guessing with decimals.
→ **Fix:** replace with evidence-based split: count real sources by stage (e.g. "what is this bump" posts = problem-aware · "do splints work?" = solution-aware · "Hyggear review" = product-aware) and report the counts.

**A4 · §6 links to David Hawkins' "Map of Consciousness."** The "calibrated" numbers (20–1000) are pseudoscience (muscle-testing). BUT the emotional ladder itself (Shame → Guilt → Apathy → Grief → Fear → … → Courage) is usable.
→ **Fix (not delete):** keep §6, reframe as "**Emotional state** — where she sits on the ladder now (e.g. Shame/Fear) → where the copy moves her (Courage/Willingness). Ignore the numbers."

**A5 · §13B tells the AI to "craft a believable" discovery story.** An invented founder story presented as fact = FTC deception (2023 Endorsement Guides cover fictitious endorsers). This is exactly why the Bunion doc invented a founder on an *honesty* brand.
→ **Fix:** "Only TRUE facts. No true story yet? Write it as a template + list what has to be true before you use it."

**A6 · §4B "which reviews you show"** reads as "hide the bad ones." Suppressing negative reviews is illegal under the FTC fake-reviews rule (16 CFR 465, in force since Oct 2024).
→ **Fix:** add "show real reviews incl. honest negatives — never suppress."

**A7 · §19 AI-bot interview breaks the One Rule** (an AI can't be a source).
→ **Fix (not delete):** "None yet? Mine 5+ long first-person stories (Reddit posts, review essays, video comments) as stand-in interviews. AI persona only to generate *questions* — label anything it says as inference."
