# Leapproof · 12 native statics: Nano Banana Pro image prompts

These go with the ready-to-paste natives doc (https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ztpU_gxqrfpfzYBOAXA5oVI33Xjqg8l0F6PcCOpGUjs/edit). There is one prompt per ad, N1–N12, matching that doc's IMAGE line.

**Settings for every image**
- Model: Nano Banana Pro.
- Aspect ratio: **4:5**. Resolution: 2K.
- Generate 3–4 variations and pick the one that looks least like an ad.

**Reference images to attach**
- **PRODUCT ref** (attach on N3, N6, N8, N9, N10 and N11): `https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1026/8593/3939/files/leapproof-brief.png`. It's the store's own pair photo, so the ads match the store. Swap in your phone photo of the real CJ sample once it lands.
- **QUIZ ref** (N12 only): `refs/quiz-q1-live.png`. This is the live quiz Q1 screen, rendered from the Funnelish block.

**Why the prompts are written this way.** A native has to look like *her* photo, not an ad. Every prompt asks for an unedited iPhone shot with:
- slightly crooked, off-centre framing;
- phone HDR and a little shadow noise;
- no cinematic blur and no studio light;
- real clutter;
- no text or logos.

All panty liners are clean, unused, unbranded, in pastel wrappers. There are never stains.

---

## N1 · The parking-lot sneeze
Refs: none.
```
Unedited iPhone photo taken from the driver's seat of a parked hatchback, looking down and across at the passenger seat, overcast midday light coming flat through the windscreen. On the grey fabric passenger seat sits an open tan faux-leather handbag, unzipped and slumped open. Resting on top of a jumble of car keys, a crumpled supermarket receipt, sunglasses and a black hair tie is ONE individually wrapped white panty liner in a pale lilac plastic wrapper — completely unbranded, no printing, clean and unused. The liner is the clearest, sharpest thing in the frame. Through the side window, a blurry supermarket car park and the edge of a shopping trolley. Real, slightly messy, lived-in car: a coffee cup in the console, a little dust on the dash. Shot quickly by a regular woman, not a photographer: frame tilted a few degrees, off-centre, phone HDR look, faint sensor noise in the shadows, mild JPEG softness, everything mostly in focus. No people, no faces, no text, no logos, no brand names, no watermark.
```
Reject the take if the liner has any print, colour stain or brand, the bag looks like a catalogue shot, or there are cinematic bokeh balls.
