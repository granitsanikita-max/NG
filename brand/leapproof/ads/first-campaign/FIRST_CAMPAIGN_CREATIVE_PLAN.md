## 2. Awareness
**Problem-aware, led moment-first: the first line shows or names the sneeze, laugh or jump with the word pee, leak or sneeze. It is never a 'do you leak?' question.**: She knows she leaks and can name the moment. She doesn't know there is underwear made for it, so she improvises with liners, peeing first and crossing her legs. Many have normalised it (#16 'We have all done it'), and Meta rejects second-person condition lines. So the ad names the moment in brand voice or third person, she recognises herself, and the quiz asks the condition questions. The moment line must still say pee, leak or sneeze, or a stranger reads a liner or black-briefs ad as period or panty-line content. Solution-aware angles (period undies failed, burned skeptic) are Lynmiss-only. Product-aware and offer ads skip the quiz and belong in retargeting.

**Rule:** 1) The first line or first 3s names the moment AND the word pee, leak or sneeze (sneeze, big laugh, jumping jacks), in brand voice, third person or a bare moment line. Never 'you leak / your bladder / after your baby'. 'I / we / us' only from a real, disclosed tester it's true for. An actor re-enacting a moment gets 'Dramatization' on screen. On statics, the first ~125 caption characters do this. 2) The ad ends on a line the FROZEN hero repeats: 'Stop! I'll pee myself!', 'Common? Yes. Normal? No.' (only once the L1 source is live), 'Not a pad, not a diaper', or 'For the sneeze, the laugh and the jump' (never 'built / designed / made for', and only after the logged wear test). 3) CTA = the quiz ('Find my match'), never price, gifts or sale. 4) Show only the real CJ plain black brief, on a real woman 30-45: full figure, mid-movement, under leggings where possible, no butt- or crotch-centred crops, no children in any round-1 ad. 5) The only enemy line is the CJ one, in US wording: 'Pads and liners bunch, feel like a diaper, and go in the trash every day.' Contrasts use only those attributes (bunch, shift, diaper feel, shows, trash), never 'fails / doesn't hold / wrong tool'. 6) 'Hold' only inside the guarantee. No absolute or comparative leak claims, mechanism, reviews, doctors, stats, eco claims or urgency.
