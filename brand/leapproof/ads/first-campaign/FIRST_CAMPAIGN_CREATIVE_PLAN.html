2. Awareness level

Write for: Problem-aware, led moment-first: the first line shows or names the sneeze, laugh or jump with the word pee, leak or sneeze. It is never a 'do you leak?' question.

Why: She knows she leaks and can name the moment. She doesn't know there is underwear made for it, so she improvises with liners, peeing first and crossing her legs. Many have normalised it (#16 'We have all done it'), and Meta rejects second-person condition lines. So the ad names the moment in brand voice or third person, she recognises herself, and the quiz asks the condition questions. The moment line must still say pee, leak or sneeze, or a stranger reads a liner or black-briefs ad as period or panty-line content. Solution-aware angles (period undies failed, burned skeptic) are Lynmiss-only. Product-aware and offer ads skip the quiz and belong in retargeting.

Market split: Bank-wide, 71-72 tagged rows (sources differ by one): about 40% problem-aware, 27-28% solution-aware, 26-27% product-aware, 6% most-aware. Reddit, where the 30-45 ICP talks: 52 tagged, 46/29/25%. Core mom pool (about 16 rows): mostly problem-aware, with only 2 solution-aware (#33, #122). Of the 46 burned rows, 40 are on retail review sites (Trustpilot 22, Amazon 12, PissedConsumer 3, Walmart 3), and 42 are non-Reddit. Only 4 of 52 Reddit rows are burned.

Sophistication: Stage 4-5. The loud players sell age identity (Everdries 'over 60') and bigger mechanism claims. On CJ we can't run a mechanism or out-claim anyone. So we enter on experience: the moment she knows, plain black briefs she recognises as underwear, and the CJ enemy line limited to comfort and waste. Proof comes from the 30-Night Leak-Test and the Fit Promise, not from claims.

Job of every ad: Earn the click, not the sale. In the first line or first 3 seconds, name the moment with the word pee, leak or sneeze (the sneeze, the big laugh, jumping jacks), in brand voice or third person. Name the workaround she'll recognise (liner 'just in case', crossing her legs). Reveal the category, not a mechanism: plain black briefs, on a real body, under leggings, moving. De-risk in one line (30 nights to see if they hold). Hand off to 'Find my match' (the quiz). No price, gifts, 'new', reviews, stats or mechanism.

Beliefs to shift:

  • B2 (CJ wording): 'My options are liners and pads' becomes 'Liners bunch, feel like a diaper and go in the trash every day, and there are plain black briefs for the sneeze, the laugh and the jump.' No holding or performance comparison.
  • B5: 'It'll feel like a diaper or show under leggings' becomes 'It looks like plain black briefs.' Claim only what real footage of the sample shows.
  • B4: 'That stuff is for old women' becomes 'This is for women who laugh and move.' Show her standing or moving, never hidden.
  • B1 (beat only, once the L1 source is live): 'It's just normal' becomes 'Common? Yes. Normal? No.'
  • B3 (closing line only): 'It probably won't hold' becomes '30 nights to see if they hold, money back if they don't.'

7. How the visual hooks were picked

Board: Nikita's Miro 'Visual Hooks Library', https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVHhmCPn0=/ (131 hooks, 5 categories). Every pick and every miro_example_links URL was re-checked against the board export. Those arrays hold board links only; in-category twins (Ad Library ads) are named in why_for_this_ad and the references.

1) A hook earns its place only if its stopping mechanism IS the moment or the enemy. In frame 1 it must do three things:
- (a) show the moment (laugh, sneeze, the jumping-jacks cue) or the enemy (a clean liner);
- (b) name it ON SCREEN with sneeze/pee/laugh by about 1s, burned in and never VO-only, because most viewers are muted and a stranger mustn't read it as period care;
- (c) set up a line the live hero repeats ('Stop! I'll pee myself!', 'Not a pad, not a diaper', 'Feels like underwear').
Hooks that stop the scroll with something unrelated were dropped. A bait stop sends the wrong click into a 13-question quiz.

2) Hook type follows the angle:
- A1 (the moment) gets body-first hooks: 72+33 Stop-Hand wipe, 62 Problem Reenactment, 52+70 Move In Frame stare, 42 Freeze Frame, 09 Bold Headline, 12 Meme Caption.
- A2 (the enemy) gets object-first hooks: 75 handbag-cam POV, 33 Camera Wipe, 135 Out-of-Place, 06 Red Circle, 55 Holding Prop.
- A3 (just underwear) gets comparison hooks: 04 A vs B Comparison Graphics, 03 Split Screen, 131 First-Person POV Hands.

3) Diversity inside a format: the 3 variations use 3 different Miro hooks, so the test also tells us which visual hook works.
- V1 = 75 / 42 / 33
- V2 = 117+70 / 117+38 / 04+117
- V3 = 72+33 / 62 / 52+70
- S1 = 135 / 06 / 55
