2. Awareness level

Write for: Problem-aware, led moment-first: the first line shows or names the sneeze, laugh or jump with the word pee, leak or sneeze. It is never a 'do you leak?' question.

Why: She knows she leaks and can name the moment. She doesn't know there is underwear made for it, so she improvises with liners, peeing first and crossing her legs. Many have normalised it (#16 'We have all done it'), and Meta rejects second-person condition lines. So the ad names the moment in brand voice or third person, she recognises herself, and the quiz asks the condition questions. The moment line must still say pee, leak or sneeze, or a stranger reads a liner or black-briefs ad as period or panty-line content. Solution-aware angles (period undies failed, burned skeptic) are Lynmiss-only. Product-aware and offer ads skip the quiz and belong in retargeting.

Market split: Bank-wide, 71-72 tagged rows (sources differ by one): about 40% problem-aware, 27-28% solution-aware, 26-27% product-aware, 6% most-aware. Reddit, where the 30-45 ICP talks: 52 tagged, 46/29/25%. Core mom pool (about 16 rows): mostly problem-aware, with only 2 solution-aware (#33, #122). Of the 46 burned rows, 40 are on retail review sites (Trustpilot 22, Amazon 12, PissedConsumer 3, Walmart 3), and 42 are non-Reddit. Only 4 of 52 Reddit rows are burned.

Sophistication: Stage 4-5. The loud players sell age identity (Everdries 'over 60') and bigger mechanism claims. On CJ we can't run a mechanism or out-claim anyone. So we enter on experience: the moment she knows, plain black briefs she recognises as underwear, and the CJ enemy line limited to comfort and waste. Proof comes from the 30-Night Leak-Test and the Fit Promise, not from claims.

Job of every ad: Earn the click, not the sale. In the first line or first 3 seconds, name the moment with the word pee, leak or sneeze (the sneeze, the big laugh, jumping jacks), in brand voice or third person. Name the workaround she'll recognise (liner 'just in case', crossing her legs). Reveal the category, not a mechanism: plain black briefs, on a real body, under leggings, moving. De-risk in one line (30 nights to see if they hold). Hand off to 'Find my match' (the quiz). No price, gifts, 'new', reviews, stats or mechanism.
