# Leapproof first campaign — Format 1 (b-roll) VOICEOVER scripts

These are the narrator voiceover scripts for the three V1 b-roll ads, to be
**recorded in ElevenLabs** (not voiced by Kling). The Kling clips for these ads
are generated **silent / no audio** (save credits); the ElevenLabs VO is laid
over the b-roll + AI start-scene clips in CapCut.

- **Voice:** warm, wry, natural woman in her 30s — dry and reassuring, not a
  glossy announcer. Same voice across all three.
- `…` = deliberate short pause (aligns with the cuts).
- Format 2 (V2 Pixar duets) is NOT here — those keep their dialogue embedded in
  the Kling prompts so Kling voices the characters (see VIDEO_PROMPTS.html).
- Compliance held: opens on the moment (sneeze/laugh), no "incontinence/
  discreet/protection", no absolute leak claim, real 30-Night Leak-Test offer.

---

## V1-a — "The spare panty liner in the handbag"
**SFX only (not recorded):** a muffled off-screen `ACHOO!` to open.

**Narrator VO:**
Sneeze. Little pee. Still packing a spare panty liner? Here's that liner's day.
It bunches. It shifts. It feels like a diaper. Another one in the trash.
Tomorrow, a new one. Or… it could just be underwear. Plain black underwear.
Pull them on, and go about the day. Not a pad. Not a diaper. Take the quiz — it
finds your size and how many pairs. Wear them through your moment for thirty
nights. If they don't hold, you get your money back.

---

## V1-b — "The funny-friend dinner"
**Diegetic hook** (record as a 2nd, younger/laughing voice, or keep as on-screen pill):
> Stop — I'll pee myself!

**Narrator VO:**
Three hours earlier. The plan? A panty liner. But a panty liner… bunches. It
shifts. It feels like a… diaper. And by tonight, it's in the trash. Or… just
underwear. Plain black underwear. Not a pad, not a diaper. Just underwear —
wash, hang, wear again. For the woman who'd rather laugh than cross her legs.
Not a pad, not a diaper. Thirty nights to see if they hold. Find my match.

---

## V1-c — "A panty liner lasts a day"
**SFX/text hook only (not recorded):** sneeze SFX + on-screen pill "The sneeze-pee plan: a sticker."

**Narrator VO:**
A panty liner lasts a day… then it's gone. It bunches. It shifts. It feels like
a… diaper. By tonight — the bin. Tomorrow, a new one. Or… there's underwear.
Plain black underwear. Wash. Hang. Wear again. One pair — washed, worn again,
not binned. Not a pad. Not a… diaper. Just get dressed and go. The thirty-night
Leak-Test — thirty nights to see if they hold. Find your match.
