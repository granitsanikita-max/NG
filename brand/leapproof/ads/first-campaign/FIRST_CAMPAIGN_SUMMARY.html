Top of funnel · 9 videos + 6 statics · all made with AI · only formats competitors have run 90+ days. Visual hook numbers are from your Miro board.

1. Who we're selling to

The Pad-and-Liner Mom, 30–45. She pees a little when she sneezes, laughs hard or jumps. She gets by with liners “just in case”, peeing before she leaves and crossing her legs.

  • Awareness: problem-aware. She knows she leaks. She doesn't know leak-proof underwear exists.
  • Every ad must: open on the moment (sneeze / laugh / jump) in the first 3 seconds, show plain black briefs, and send her to the quiz. No price, no hard sell.
  • Her doubts: “It's just normal after kids” · “Will it actually hold?” · “I'm not wearing a diaper” · “Will it show under leggings?”

  • Measure real sizes. Take 5 photos of the real sample, and make every AI product shot match them.
