Leapproof · First Campaign: Summary

Top of funnel · 9 videos + 6 statics · all made with AI · only formats competitors have run 90+ days. Visual hook numbers are from your Miro board.

1. Who we're selling to

The Pad-and-Liner Mom, 30–45. She pees a little when she sneezes, laughs hard or jumps. She gets by with liners “just in case”, peeing before she leaves and crossing her legs.

  • Awareness: problem-aware. She knows she leaks. She doesn't know leak-proof underwear exists.
  • Every ad must: open on the moment (sneeze / laugh / jump) in the first 3 seconds, show plain black briefs, and send her to the quiz. No price, no hard sell.
  • Her doubts: “It's just normal after kids” · “Will it actually hold?” · “I'm not wearing a diaper” · “Will it show under leggings?”

3. Why these formats (top of funnel)

The best model for cold traffic is UltraDries, the newer brand still acquiring customers (not Everdries, whose top spend goes to people who already know them). With their fake-nurse/doctor ads removed (we can't run those), here's what actually works cold vs what's really bottom-of-funnel:

Statics: #1 long-form story photo ✅ S1 · #2 on-body photo → retargeting · #3 bold text / listicle card ✅ S4.

What changed: I'd ranked formats by Everdries' spend, but most of that targets their existing customers. A-vs-B and silent on-body are proven, but as warm/retargeting ads — so they move there, and the two top cold formats (VO + AI animation) carry the first campaign.
