PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information Leapproof collects, why, and what we do with it. Leapproof is based in Melbourne, Australia. We handle your information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

WHAT WE COLLECT
- Your details: name, email, phone number, and billing and delivery addresses.
- Your order: what you buy, your size, and your order history.
- Payment details: these are handled by our payment providers. We never see or store your full card number.
- Your quiz answers: for example when it happens, your size and how often you wash. We use them to show your match and to pre-select your set.
- How you use the site: your device, browser, IP address, the pages you visit and the buttons you tap. This is collected with cookies and similar tools.
- Messages you send us.

WHY WE USE IT
- To take, pack, ship and track your order.
- To process refunds, size swaps and Leak-Test claims.
- To reply when you contact us.
- To show you your quiz match and remember your answers while you shop.
- To improve the site and our products.
- To send you emails about Leapproof, if you agree. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.
- To show you our ads on other sites and apps, and to measure how well they work.
- To prevent fraud and meet our legal obligations.

WHO WE SHARE IT WITH
We share only what each one needs to do its job:
- Shopify, which runs our store and checkout.
- Funnelish, which runs our quiz and order pages.
- Payment providers, which process your payment.
- Our fulfilment partner and delivery carriers, which pack and deliver your order.
- Advertising partners such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram), which help us show and measure our ads.
- Government authorities, if the law requires it.
We never sell your personal information.

INFORMATION SENT OVERSEAS
Some of these businesses are outside Australia. Our fulfilment partner packs orders from a warehouse overseas, including in China, so it receives your name, delivery address, phone number and order details. Shopify, Funnelish and our advertising partners may store information in countries including the United States, Canada and Ireland.

TARGETED ADVERTISING
You can opt out of us sharing your information for targeted advertising here: https://leapproof.com/pages/data-sharing-opt-out
You can also change your ad settings in your Facebook or Instagram account.

COOKIES
We use cookies to run the store, remember your cart and quiz answers, understand how the site is used, and measure our ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser, but parts of the site may not work properly without them.

KEEPING IT SAFE
We use trusted, secure platforms and limit who can see your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't email us card details or passwords.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT
We keep order records for as long as the law requires (usually 5 to 7 years for tax). We keep other information only as long as we need it for the reasons above.

SEEING OR CORRECTING YOUR INFORMATION
You can ask to see the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@leapproof.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

COMPLAINTS
If you have a privacy concern, email hello@leapproof.com and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

CHILDREN
Our store is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
