REFUND POLICY

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The short version: wear them for 30 nights. If they don't hold, you get your money back and you keep the pairs. Wrong size? We'll send your new size free. Nothing to send back.

THE 30-NIGHT LEAK-TEST
- Your 30 nights start the day your order is delivered (the delivered date on your tracking).
- Wear them day or night, through your real moments.
- If they don't hold, email hello@leapproof.com any time during your 30 nights with your order number and a line about what happened.
- We refund the full amount you paid for your set, plus any extra pairs you added to the same order, to your original payment method.
- You keep the pairs and any free gifts. Nothing to send back.
- One Leak-Test claim per customer.

THE FIT PROMISE (SIZE SWAPS)
- Wrong size? Email hello@leapproof.com within your 30 nights with your order number and the size you need.
- We'll send your new size free. Nothing to send back.
- One free size swap per order.

DAMAGED, FAULTY OR WRONG ITEMS
If anything arrives damaged, faulty or isn't what you ordered, email hello@leapproof.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or refund you, your choice. You don't need to send the item back.

LOST OR LATE PARCELS
If your tracking hasn't moved for 10 business days, or your order hasn't arrived 20 business days after you placed it, email us. We'll resend it or refund you.

CANCELLING AN ORDER
Email hello@leapproof.com as soon as you can. If your order hasn't been packed yet, we cancel it and refund you in full. Once it has been packed, it's on its way, and you're still covered by the 30-Night Leak-Test when it arrives.

CHANGE OF MIND
Because these are underwear, we can't take back worn pairs to sell again. That's why the 30-Night Leak-Test and the Fit Promise let you keep the pairs instead.

HOW REFUNDS ARE PAID
We process approved refunds within 3 business days, back to your original payment method. Your bank may take a further 3 to 10 business days to show it.

YOUR RIGHTS IN THE UNITED STATES
The 30-Night Leak-Test is a limited warranty. It gives you specific legal rights, and you may also have other rights which vary from state to state. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under federal or state law.

YOUR RIGHTS UNDER AUSTRALIAN CONSUMER LAW (FOR ORDERS SHIPPED TO AUSTRALIA)
Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

The 30-Night Leak-Test and the Fit Promise are in addition to these rights, not instead of them.
