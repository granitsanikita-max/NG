## ADD — objection-handlers to place in the funnel
- **A compact "your doubts, answered" strip** on the **reveal/offer page** (the moment of the add-to-cart) covering the top 4: will it hold (guarantee) · will it fit (free swap) · how it feels (like underwear) · is it legit (honest + real person + guarantee). Short, scannable, right by the CTA.
- **The guarantee as the hero** near every buy button (reveal + checkout), not buried below.
- **A short founder note** (real, first-person) — the honest "I built this because I got burned too" — directly fights the scam objection.
- **Cost-vs-disposables** framing on the reveal page (fights price).
- **Real social proof** (see below) — the biggest missing piece.

## DELETE — doubt-creators / friction to remove before purchase
1. **The fake Limura / AI skincare reviews** hidden in the checkout code (amplify the exact "scam" feeling we're fighting + FTC). Highest priority delete.
2. **The 3-bundle re-selector at checkout** (re-opens the buying decision) — per the fix plan.
3. **Redundant size re-pick** at checkout (friction + the "check your details" error path) — pre-fill instead.
4. **Any absolute/claim language** anywhere ("leak-proof," "never leaks") that trips her scam radar — keep everything honest/hedged.
5. **Shipping-tier confusion** — make free shipping the obvious default so paid tiers don't read as "shipping costs extra."

## REVIEWS / social proof — the real gap (and the honest fix)
We have **zero real reviews.** For a new $59 brand this is the #1 thing suppressing conversion, and it's exactly why trust is thin at the decision.
- **Cannot fake it** — fake reviews are an FTC violation and the precise thing the brand stands against. (Delete the Limura ones.)
- **The real unlock:** seed the **Founding cohort** now — send free sets to real target women, disclosed ("received a free set to test"), collect real quotes + real photos + a star rating. This is the single highest-ROI move for conversion. Micro-creator UGC (real, disclosed) stacks on top.
- **Until real reviews exist, substitute trust with:** the guarantee (the strongest substitute for reviews), the founder note, the France/"common but not normal" fact, "a real person answers," transparent refund/shipping/privacy links.
- **Placement:** a review block on the **reveal page** (by the CTA) and a compact version at **checkout** near the pay button — swap the fake ones out for real ones the moment they land.
