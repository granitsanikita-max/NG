## 3. Who is arriving and what the funnel must do
- Cold Meta/TikTok traffic. Awareness: 40% problem-aware, 28% solution-aware, 26% product-aware. Sophistication Stage 4–5. About a third have already been burned (46–49 of 155 snippets).
- So (Copywriting ③): agitate the **moment**, reveal a way to manage it, prove ours. Don't out-claim the market; win on **identity** (active woman, not patient) and **proof you can check**.
- Emotion: anger at being lied to (20) beats shame (11). Move anger/shame → relief → pride. **Never lead with fear or shame.**
- Trust school (Universal 1.13): **premium restraint.** No volume proof, no fake urgency. Honesty is the edge: "we can't outspend them, so we out-honest them."
- Language for the condition: **"manage,"** never "fix" or "cure" (Meta cure ban, brand bible §13). For the product: **"find what holds."**

## 6. One body, swappable heroes (multi-angle)

Each ad links to the landing page with `?a=` (angle code). The hero **image and headline** swap; the body, quiz, product page and checkout are shared (Universal 1.5, 3.5; Quiz Zone 1).

**Meta "you" test (rule for every hero and ad):** no second-person statement about her condition, age, menopause or postpartum status ("your bladder," "you leak," "your perimenopause"). Speak in the third person, about the moment, or about the product. Because heroes lift the ad's words (F2), this rule applies to the ads too.

| Code | Angle | Hero headline direction (passes the "you" test) | Quiz intro headline | Review slot L3 | Allowed on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `m` (default) | MOMENT | "Sneeze. Laugh. Jump. No bracing required." | "Let's find what holds through the moment." | general | CJ + Lynmiss |
| `p` | POSTPARTUM | "Made for mums getting back to training." | "Made for the comeback. Let's find the match." | postpartum mum | CJ + Lynmiss |
| `t` | ATHLETE | "Double-unders. Box jumps. PRs. Underwear that keeps up." | "Let's match it to training days." | lifter / runner | CJ + Lynmiss |
| `r` | PERIMENOPAUSE | "Made for women who run and lift through perimenopause." | "Let's find what works now." | 40s active woman | CJ + Lynmiss |
| `n` | COMMON-NOT-NORMAL | "Common isn't the same as normal." (+ France fact in body; no "here they hand you a pad" — AU has pelvic-floor physio pathways) | "Let's find what holds." | general | CJ + Lynmiss |
| `f` | PERIOD-UNDIES-FAILED | "Period underwear was made for periods. This was made for the sneeze." | "Let's find underwear made for this kind of leak." | period-undies switcher | **Lynmiss only** |
| `b` | BURNED-SKEPTIC | "Made for the sneeze, the laugh and the jump. Not for a period." | "Let's see why the last pair didn't hold." | skeptic won over | **Lynmiss only** |

- **Precedence:** her quiz answer beats the ad angle. An ATHLETE click who answers "after my baby" is The Comeback.
- **Carry the angle:** `a` passes through to the product page and checkout (for the hero image and attribution).
- **Testing:** test hero variants **within** one angle, never angle vs angle (different traffic).
- Hero lines are directions; final lines are written from the actual ads.

## 7. Privacy and data (do this before launch)
- **Never put health answers in URLs or send them to Meta.** Quiz answers travel as one opaque code (e.g. `?r=K7Q2`) that Funnelish/our script decodes on the page. Meta receives only standard events + `a` (angle). No audiences built from quiz answers.
- Ask Meta's pixel not to capture query strings if Funnelish allows it; otherwise the opaque code is the safeguard.
- Gate consent (Spam Act): sending her match is the service she asked for; **marketing emails/SMS are a separate, unticked opt-in.**
- Privacy line at the gate: "Your answers stay with us. We don't share them with advertisers." (only true once the above is done.)

## 10. PAGE 2 — Quiz: "Find Your Leak Match"

- **Gate:** late, soft, with Skip. **Reward:** per D5 (recommended: "your free Wash Bag and Wash Sheets are unlocked"), framed as already given.
- **Length:** CJ 12 questions, Lynmiss 13, in 3 named chapters, about 2 minutes. Progress shows the chapter name and the real screen count. One question per screen; single-select auto-advances; multi-select Next disabled until picked.
- Every question either changes the product page or plays back on it. If it does neither, it's cut.
- Answers stored as one opaque code (§7).

### 10.1 Intro screen
- Headline by angle (§6).
- "You'll get: **1** what you need it to handle · **2** your size · **3** how many pairs you actually need · **4** your price and your free gifts."
- "About 2 minutes. No email needed to see your match."
- Trust row (3 only): "[30/60]-Night Leak-Test" · "Free shipping" · "Fit Promise"
- Button: "Start my match" · small line: "This isn't a medical assessment. It matches you to underwear."

### 10.2 Chapter 1 — "Your moment"
**Q1 moment** (done on the landing page if she tapped a tile)
"What catches you out most?" — Sneezing or coughing · Laughing · Jumping or running · Lifting · A bit of everything
→ playback + reveal headline ("…for every sneeze, laugh and jump" when "a bit of everything").

**Name** (optional, Skip) — "What should we call you?" *Why we ask: so your match is easy to find again. Skip if you like.* → used from here on.

**Q2 onset → persona**
"When did this start, [Name]?" — After having my baby (or babies) · In the last few years, kind of out of nowhere · When I started training harder · It's been around as long as I can remember · I'd rather not say
*Why we ask: it changes how we explain your match and the tips we send you. It doesn't change your price.*
→ The Comeback · The Reset · The Unbothered Athlete · The Unbothered

**Card 1 — Normalise** (cited, footnoted)
- Comeback: "You're not alone. About 1 in 3 women leak after having a baby.*"
- Reset: "Leaking gets more common as hormones change.* It still isn't something you just have to put up with."
- Athlete: "Studies put it at about 1 in 5 up to nearly half of women who do CrossFit.*"
- Unbothered: Leaking is "NEVER considered normal." — Ridgeview Medical Center
- All end: "Common. Not normal. And very manageable."

**Q3 what she's tried** (multi-select; confession)
"What have you tried so far? Pick all that apply." — Pads or liners · Period underwear · Leak-proof underwear that still leaked · Pelvic-floor physio or exercises · Peeing before I go, skipping the trampoline · Crossing my legs and hoping · Nothing yet
*Why we ask: so we don't recommend something you've already tried and hated.*
→ routes the PDP education block and emails.

**Reaction screen** (one line, by priority: leak-proof leaked > period underwear > pads > physio > avoiding > nothing)
- Pads/liners: "Pads bunch, feel like a nappy, and go in the bin every day. You deserve better than that."
- Physio: "Good. Keep going. Physio is how you build strength; this is what you wear while you do."
- Avoiding / crossing legs: "You shouldn't have to sit out the trampoline. Let's sort the bit you can sort today."
- Period underwear / leak-proof leaked: **Lynmiss:** "Period underwear is made for a slow flow. A sneeze sends a fast burst. That's usually why it goes out the sides." **CJ:** "Plenty of women try that first. Let's see what suits your leak."
- Nothing yet: "Then let's start you on the right foot."

### 10.3 Chapter 2 — "What's really happening"
**Q4 amount**
"On a normal day, how much is it?" — A few drops · A small splash on a big sneeze or jump · More than a splash, a liner can't keep up · It can be a lot, like I can't stop it
*Why we ask:* **CJ** (one absorbency): "this tells us honestly whether these will suit you." · **Lynmiss** (tiers): "this sets how much your pair needs to hold."
*Between two? Pick the bigger one.*
→ Lynmiss: absorbency tier. Both: honest "best for" line. **"It can be a lot" → PDP shows: "This is more than these are made for. A heavier brief, plus a GP or pelvic-floor physio, is the better start." She can still buy; no upsizing.**

**Q5 when it happens** (multi-select)
"When does it happen? Pick all that apply." — Sneezing or coughing · Laughing · Jumping, running, HIIT · Lifting · Standing up fast or getting out of bed · Not making it to the bathroom in time
*Why we ask: so your results show the moments you'll actually wear them for.*

"Got it. Your moments: [list]. They all happen fast, with no warning. So what you wear has to work before you can react." · buttons "That's me" / "Change my answers"

## 13. Compliance (Australia first)
- **Never anywhere:** "Do you suffer from…" · second-person condition statements (§6 "you" test) · "incontinence," "discreet," "dignity," "protection," "accident," "bladder weakness" in ads and on the landing page · "100% leak-proof," "zero leaks," "never leaks" · "cure," "fix," "treats," "prevents UTIs," "medical-grade," "clinically proven," bare "non-toxic" · fake or unnamed experts · forum quotes in review slots · gift "values" that aren't real sold prices · timers, fake stock, rolling deadlines · shame or smell hooks.
- **Australia:** ACL s18/s29 (no misleading price or "value" claims; two-price comparisons only against real prices) · consumer guarantees (no paid shipping protection) · GST shown correctly · Spam Act (consent, sender ID, unsubscribe) · Privacy Act APP 3 (health answers are sensitive information; collect with consent, keep first-party).
