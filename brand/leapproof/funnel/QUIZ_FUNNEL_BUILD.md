# LEAPPROOF — Quiz Funnel Build Spec (Funnelish, CJ launch)

**This is the build view of `FUNNEL_PLAN.md`, locked to the CJ period-panty claims column (FUNNEL_PLAN §2).** It maps our funnel onto Funnelish's Skincare Quiz Template skeleton and gives every screen build-ready, in the order you build it. Numbers come only from `NUMBER_SHEET.md`. Rationale and the full objection/belief logic live in `FUNNEL_PLAN.md`; this file is what you fill the pages with.

**CJ claims lock (never break):** no "built for pee, not blood", no attacking period underwear, no pour demo, no Leak-Lock mechanism, no absolute leak claims, no "incontinence/discreet/dignity/protection", no fake doctors/reviews/urgency. Open on the moment (sneeze/jump/run). Never shame.

The Funnelish template ships with **3 quiz questions**. We replace it with **13** (see §3 on why not 15). The template's page order is the skeleton:
`Landing → Quiz → Analysing loader → Results (reveal + offer) → Checkout → 1-click upsell → Thank-you.`

## 1. How each quiz question earns its place (the selling spine)

A quiz sells by making her **confess the problem in her own words, then feel understood, then see a product built around exactly what she said.** Every question below does one of two jobs, usually both (Kill #14: a question that does neither is cut):
- **Sell** — surfaces an objection, a pain, or a belief so a card/review can answer it *inside the quiz, before the price.*
- **Route** — changes the results page (set size, size variant, which objection block leads, which review shows).

| Q | Question does | Sells / handles | Routes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 moment | mirrors the ad, first micro-commitment | O11 (not old-lady), O14 (not shameful) | reveal headline |
| Q2 onset | makes her feel seen (persona) | O12 (it's normal) | persona label + Card 1 |
| Q3 tried | confession; stops us pitching what she hated | O1/O8 (leaked before), O6 (felt like diaper) | PDP education block |
| Q4 amount | honesty filter; sets expectation | O2 (only tiny drips), O4 (still need a pad) | honest "best for" line; "a lot" → redirect |
| Q5 when | shows the real moments back to her | O20 (nights), O18 (movement) | playback list |
| Q6 confidence | gauges skepticism | O8 (scam / fell for marketing) | how hard the pre-reveal proof leans |
| Q7 blocker | THE routing question | whichever she picks (O1,O6,O9,O10,O18,O12,O5) | objection block under buy box |
| Q8 cost | emotional stake | — (motivation) | reveal sub-line |
| Q9 wear | frequency | — | set size (with Q12) |
| Q10 size | fit, sized to THIS chart | O3 (sizing), O17 (won't fit) | variant pre-selected |
| Q11 priority | what she values most | O6/O10/O18/O7 | which benefit leads on PDP |
| Q12 wash | wash frequency | O7 (stops working after washes) | set size (with Q9) |
| Q13 want | the pain named last | — (desire) | reveal sub-headline |

## 2. PAGE 1 — Landing page (build in Funnelish as the funnel's first page)

One job: get the first tap. No nav, no popup, mobile first, AU spelling. Full block logic in FUNNEL_PLAN §9 — build these blocks top to bottom:

2. **Hero:** trust line (guarantee in ~6 words) → H1 lifted from the ad → subhead: *her moment → the enemy sentence → "find what holds in about 2 minutes."* Image: a real woman mid-laugh/jump, not a patient. 
   - CJ enemy sentence (NUMBER_SHEET §11): *"Pads and liners bunch, feel like a nappy, and go in the bin every day."*
3. **First tap (the quiz starts here):** *"What catches you out most?"* → 4 tiles: **Sneezing or coughing · Laughing · Jumping or running · Lifting** + button **"Find my match"**. Each tile = Q1 answered, so the quiz opens on Q2.
4. **Under the CTA:** `About 2 minutes · No email needed to see your match · 30-Night Leak-Test`
5. **"You're in the right place if…"** — actions, not conditions: *"you've crossed your legs before a sneeze · you've sat out the trampoline · pads feel like a nappy · you refuse to dress like you're 80."*
6. **Why what you've tried lets you down (3–4 lines):** *Common isn't normal* (exact Ridgeview quote, footnoted) → CJ enemy sentence → one honest admission.
7. **Proof strip** (see §6 for exactly which reviews go here and what each kills).
8. **Risk:** the 30-Night Leak-Test in one line + Fit Promise.
9. **Repeat CTA + sticky button:** "Find my match".
10. **Footer:** policies · *"Not a treatment. It's underwear."* · quiz disclaimer.

## 3. Quiz length — why 13, not 15

You said 10–15, sweet spot 15. I'm building **13** and not padding to 15 on purpose: every dead question drops completion roughly 5–10%, and completion is the whole funnel. 13 is already long for a quiz — it survives only because each one either sells or routes. If you want 15, the honest add is **not two more questions** — it's splitting Q7 (blocker) into "biggest worry" + "second worry" to drive two objection blocks. I'd rather keep it tight. Pushback logged; your call.

## 4. PAGE 2 — The 13 questions (build each as one Funnelish quiz step)

Rules for all: one question per screen; single-select auto-advances; multi-select needs a Next; progress shows chapter name + screen count; **store every answer as one opaque code** passed to the results page (FUNNEL_PLAN §7 — Meta only ever gets a non-health code). *Why we ask* lines are shown — they cut suspicion and lift completion.

### Intro screen (before Q1)
- Headline by ad angle.
- *"You'll get: 1 what it needs to handle · 2 your size · 3 how many pairs you actually need · 4 your price and free gifts."*
- `About 2 minutes. No email needed to see your match.`
- Trust row: `30-Night Leak-Test · Free shipping · Fit Promise`
- Button: **Start my match** · small: *"This isn't a medical assessment. It matches you to underwear."*

### Chapter 1 — "Your moment"

**Q1 — Moment** (pre-answered if she tapped a landing tile)
*What catches you out most?*
`Sneezing or coughing · Laughing · Jumping or running · Lifting · A bit of everything`

**Name** (optional, Skip) — *What should we call you?* *Why: so your match is easy to find again. Skip if you like.*

**Q2 — Onset → persona**
*When did this start[, Name]?*
`After having my baby (or babies) · In the last few years, out of nowhere · When I started training harder · It's been around as long as I can remember · I'd rather not say`
*Why: it changes how we explain your match and the tips we send. It doesn't change your price.*
→ Persona: **The Comeback · The Reset · The Unbothered Athlete · The Unbothered.**

**CARD 1 — Normalise** (cited, footnoted; this is a selling beat, not filler — kills O12):
- Comeback: *"You're not alone. About 1 in 3 women leak after having a baby.*"*
- Reset: *"Leaking gets more common as hormones change.* It still isn't something you just put up with."*
- Athlete: *"Studies put it at about 1 in 5, up to nearly half, of women who do CrossFit.*"*
- Unbothered: *Leaking is "NEVER considered normal." — Ridgeview Medical Center.*
- All end: **"Common. Not normal. And very manageable."**

**Q3 — What you've tried** (multi-select; confession)
*What have you tried so far? Pick all that apply.*
`Pads or liners · Period underwear · Leak-proof underwear that still leaked · Pelvic-floor physio or exercises · Peeing first, skipping the trampoline · Crossing my legs and hoping · Nothing yet`
*Why: so we don't recommend something you've already tried and hated.*

**REACTION screen** (one line, by priority — CJ wording):
- Pads/liners: *"Pads bunch, feel like a nappy, and go in the bin every day. You deserve better."*
- Physio: *"Good. Keep going — physio builds the strength; this is what you wear while you do."*
- Avoiding/crossing legs: *"You shouldn't have to sit out the trampoline. Let's sort the bit you can sort today."*
- Period / leak-proof leaked (CJ, neutral — do NOT attack): *"Plenty of women try that first. Let's see what suits your leak."*
- Nothing yet: *"Then let's start you on the right foot."*

### Chapter 2 — "What's really happening"

**Q4 — Amount**
*On a normal day, how much is it?*
`A few drops · A small splash on a big sneeze or jump · More than a splash — a liner can't keep up · It can be a lot, like I can't stop it`
*Why (CJ, one absorbency): this tells us honestly whether these will suit you.* *Between two? Pick the bigger.*
→ honest "best for" line on PDP. **"It can be a lot" → PDP shows:** *"This is more than these are made for. A heavier brief, plus a GP or pelvic-floor physio, is the better start."* (She can still buy; never upsize her as a fix.)

**Q5 — When it happens** (multi-select)
*When does it happen? Pick all that apply.*
`Sneezing or coughing · Laughing · Jumping, running, HIIT · Lifting · Standing up fast / getting out of bed · Not making it to the bathroom in time`
*Why: so your results show the moments you'll actually wear them for.*

**PLAYBACK screen (not a diagnosis):** *"Got it. Your moments: [list]. They happen fast, with no warning — so what you wear has to work before you can react."* → "That's me" / "Change my answers."
If "Not making it in time": *"That one can be more than a sneeze. These may not be enough on their own for it — a GP or pelvic-floor physio can help."*

**CARD 2 — the cost of the thing she's using** (CJ version — cost/waste, NOT a mechanism claim):
*"A pad a day is about $[real AUD]/year — and every one goes in the bin."* [source the real AU pad price before launch]

**Q6 — Confidence** (skepticism gauge — sells against O8)
*Honestly, how much do you believe anything can help?*
`I've given up a bit · I'm hopeful but I've been burned · I just haven't found the right thing · I'm sure the right thing exists`
*Why: so we show you proof, not promises.*
→ The more skeptical, the harder the pre-reveal card leans on the guarantee + a burned-skeptic review.

**Q7 — What's held you back** (THE routing question)
*What's made you hold off trying something new?*
`It'll feel like a nappy · It'll be bulky or show under leggings · I've bought stuff that leaked anyway · Things never fit right — they ride up or roll down · I'm not sure what's in the fabric · I figured it's just normal now · It's a lot of money for underwear`
*Why: whatever it is, we'll answer it straight on your results page.*
→ Picks the objection block that sits directly under the buy box.

**Q8 — What it's cost you** (emotional stake)
*Has it ever made you sit something out?*
`Skipped a class or a run · Stayed off the trampoline with the kids · Worn dark pants just in case · Held back a proper laugh · Not yet — and I'd like to keep it that way`
*Why: so we know what you're doing this for.* → reveal sub-line (e.g. "Back on the trampoline.").

### Chapter 3 — "Your fit & your week"

**Q9 — When you'll wear them**
`Every day · On training days · At night · A mix`
*Why: this and your washing tell us how many pairs keep you covered between washes.*

**Q10 — Size** (no pre-selected answer)
*What size are you in this pair?* — hip measurement in cm against **this exact garment's chart** + her usual size as a cross-check.
*Why: leg openings too loose are where leaks get out; too tight rides up. So we size you to this pair's chart, not a generic one.*
**Honesty lock:** this garment runs **S–2XL** only. Don't imply sizes you don't stock.

**CARD A — Fit Promise:** *"Wrong size? We'll send the right one first. No need to post the other back."*

**Q11 — Priority** (what she values most → which benefit leads on PDP)
*What matters most to you in a pair?*
`That it holds, full stop · That it feels like normal underwear · That it stays put when I move · That it's easy to wash and lasts`
*Why: so your results lead with what you care about.*

**Q12 — Washing**
*How often do you do a wash?*
`Once a week · Every few days · As rarely as I can, no judgement please`
*Why: see above.* → set size via routing table (NUMBER_SHEET §8).

**Q13 — The pain, named last**
*If one thing changed, what would it be?*
`Sneeze, laugh and jump without the second-guess · Stop buying pads every month · Stop changing my clothes halfway through the day · Feel like myself again`
→ reveal sub-headline.

### Confirm → gate → strongest card → loader
1. **Confirm:** *"[Name], here's what you told us: [moment] · [amount] · size [size] · [wear], washed [wash]. Look right?"* → "Yes, that's me" / "Change something".
2. **Gate (late, soft, Skip):** *"Where should we send your match[, Name]?"* — `[email]` · unticked `Send me tips and offers too` · `[mobile, optional, its own unticked consent]` · "Send my match" / "Skip, just show me" · privacy line.
3. **Strongest card (right before reveal):** the review matched to her Q7 answer (once real). Until then: *"Wear them through your moment for 30 nights. If they don't hold, you get your money back."* + Fit Promise.
4. **Loader (7s, 4 lines):** *Matching what it needs to handle…* → *Sizing you to this pair's chart…* → *Working out how many pairs fit your week…* → *Your free gifts are unlocked. Your 30-Night Leak-Test is on.*

## 7. PAGE 4–6 — Checkout, 1-click upsell, thank-you

- **Checkout:** order recap · savings above subtotal (pair savings only — gifts have no dollar value at launch) · guarantee badge by the pay button · express pay on top · **one unticked bump** (NUMBER_SHEET §6; needs a real overnight SKU, else "+2 spare pairs $29") · **no shipping protection** (ACL — we carry the risk) · no fake timers · "two parcels" said once (sheets ship separately).
- **1-click upsell (after payment, tiered — NUMBER_SHEET §6):** 5-pair buyer → *"+5 pairs for $70 ($14/pair) = 10 pairs"* · 3-pair → *"+2 pairs for $20"* · 10-pair → skip to thank-you. Visible decline link.
- **Thank-you (calm only, no third offer):** what happens next · delivery window · two-parcel note · how to wash (so they keep working) · how to swap a size. Refresh subscription and referral come later by email (Day 45 / Day 21), never here.
