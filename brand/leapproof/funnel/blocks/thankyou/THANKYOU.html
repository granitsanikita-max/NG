Before your first wear

Wash them once first.

  1. Machine wash cold, gentle cycle86°F / 30°C max. Zip them into the Wash Bag if you have one.
  2. Mild detergent, no fabric softenerOr the Wash Sheets. Many 2-in-1 detergents have softener mixed in, so check the label.
  3. Hang them to dryNo dryer, no bleach, no ironing.

After that: rinse in cold water after wearing, then wash the same way. Softener and heat are what wear down the lining, so skipping both keeps your pairs working.
