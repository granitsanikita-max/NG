## 3. Urgency & scarcity — what we use (all true)

Banned (fundamentals + ACL): countdown timers, "only X left", rolling "order today" deadlines, fake "selling fast", crossed-out fake prices.

Allowed and recommended:
1. **Real delivery line under the button** ("Arrives in about X–Y business days"). Once CJ gives a real cutoff, upgrade to a dated line: "Order by 2pm, dispatched [weekday]." Real dates create urgency without lying.
2. **Cost-of-waiting copy (emotional urgency):** block 11 ties the decision to her own dream ("30 nights from now…"). Optional line above the final button: `Every week you wait is another week of crossing your legs.`
3. **Gifts tied to the set, not the clock:** "Your free gifts are unlocked" carries over from the quiz. True, because they come with the set.
4. **Optional, only if Nikita commits to it:** a real launch-price end date ("Launch prices until 30 Nov"), and prices actually go up after. If not committed, leave it out.
