# Leapproof — Objection gap map + review briefs (v1)

Rule: a guarantee removes the RISK. Only a real customer can change the BELIEF ("will these actually work for someone like me?").
So every objection below the line needs a real review. Real testers only, their own words, labelled "Received a free set to test" where true.

## 3. Review briefs (who to recruit, what to ask, where it goes)

Recruit 10–15 women. Mix: 30–45, postpartum, perimenopause, gym/runners, sizes S–2XL (at least 3 in XL/2XL).
Each gets a free set + this brief. Ask the questions, never give example answers. Publish every review, including 2–3★.

| Slot | Kills | Recruit | Ask her (open questions only) | Goes on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 Held | won't hold | Anyone with a regular moment | "What moment did you wear them through? What happened?" | Offer page, right under the tiers |
| R3 Size | quiz gimmick / wrong size | XL or 2XL tester | "Did the quiz size fit? How did they sit after a full day?" | Next to the size chips |
| R2 Feel | nappy feel | Ex-pad user | "How did they feel compared to what you used before?" | Under the pads vs Leapproof table |
| R4 Washes | stop working | Tester who's washed them 10+ times | "After how many washes? Anything change?" | Next to "What's in the box" (Wash Bag) |
| R6 Leggings | shows / cheap | Gym or runner | "What did you wear them under? Could anyone tell?" | Near the tier cards |
| R9 Night | not for nights | Night-wearer | "Did you wear them to bed? How was the morning?" | FAQ 7 |
| R7 Price | not worth it | Tester who used to buy pads | "What were you using before, and what did it cost you?" | Above the final button |
| R8 Trust | scam fear | Any tester after delivery | "How was ordering and delivery? Anything surprise you?" | Leak-Test section |
| R10 Age | for older women | 30–35, postpartum | "What did you think this kind of underwear was for, before you tried it?" | Top of the review wall |
| R5 Smell | smell / wet | Gym tester | "Any smell or wet feeling after a session?" | FAQ 5 |

Tag every review card: first name + last initial · age · `Wearing: size X` · `Received a free set to test`.
Card 1 rule: a "they held" review only goes first if MOST testers said they held. Show the real tally: "[X] of [Y] testers said they held on their moment."
Until real reviews exist: the review block stays deleted (an empty widget is worse than none).
