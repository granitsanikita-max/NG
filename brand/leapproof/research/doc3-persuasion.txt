13 · Unique mechanism (UMP vs UMS)

Core Metaphor: "A seatbelt for your bladder leak" — there, you forget it, it catches you when the sudden force hits (sneeze, jump, lift).

UMP — mechanism of the PROBLEM: under sudden downward pressure a weakened/overloaded pelvic floor lets the urethra open for a split second → a fast, thin, high-volume burst of urine escapes — physically different from menstrual flow. "Menstrual pads can't absorb thin, fast-flowing urine effectively." [C115]; Mayo: stress incontinence = "movement or activity puts pressure on the bladder, causing urine to leak" [C140].

UMS — mechanism of the SOLUTION: a sealed, urine-tuned gusset = fast-wicking top layer (pulls the burst off skin) + a high-rate absorbent core sized in ml to a real leak + a heat-bonded TPU/PUL film with flatlock-sealed edges so it can't escape at the side-seams (the exact Everdries/Modibodi failure). Engineered for urine's speed/volume; reusable wash after wash. ⚠️ Spec numbers (micron thickness, exact wash-count) are vendor self-tests and UNVERIFIED (§20) — set them with your own supplier spec + third-party test; do NOT put a micron or wash-count number in copy until lab-confirmed.

Honesty check: claim ONLY what the garment owns — "holds X ml of fast liquid, lab-tested; sealed edges; PFAS-tested". NOT "treats/cures incontinence", "prevents UTIs", or "medical-grade" (§2B).
