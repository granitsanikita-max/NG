2.  Confirmed white space: NOBODY in the incontinence DR lane pairs real trust (named founder + published absorbency test + real reviews) with a money-back wear-test guarantee + a replenishment subscription → that combination is the offer and the moat.

4B · Brand Positioning System (Liquid Death model) — LOCKED

-   The Enemy: the disposable-industrial complex and the lie it sells — an industry (and medical culture) that tells women leaking is "just normal after kids / at your age" and hands them a pad to buy forever instead of a real fix. Found in the 1★ reviews ("fell for the marketing", 2.5★ Everdries, "still need a pad") and the one VERIFIED on-record line — Fortune: the category's "growth isn't due so much to population aging as it is marketing" (the spicier Bloomberg/K-C-exec quotes were cut in §20 as unverified; the enemy needs no fabricated quote).

-   What we stand FOR: the truth that leaking is common but NOT normal, not shameful, not a life sentence — and that you deserve gear that actually holds, proven, not whispered. We publish the test. We show the 1★ reviews. We'd rather lose a sale than lie.

-   The Attitude: blunt, dry-funny, athlete-to-athlete, matter-of-fact about pee — the opposite of the hushed/clinical/pastel register.

-   The Uncopyable Test: if Everdries/clones adopted "we publish third-party proof and show our bad reviews" tomorrow, it would INDICT them (their 2.5★ + fake testimonials are the whole problem); if Jude/Thinx chased the active-athlete frame they'd CANNIBALIZE their clinical-50+/menstruation equity. ✅ Real moat.

-   Whole-store thread: Name (athletic/engineering, not "-dri/-sure/continence"); About-page manifesto (common-but-not-normal + France story + founder's active origin); product page (published ml test, PFAS cert, real reviews incl. misses); offer/guarantee (wear-test refund); which reviews we show (the misses too); voice.

-   Weakness-as-weapon: small/new/broke = the underdog vs. the Everdries ad-flood + diaper giants. "We can't outspend them — so we out-honest them."

-   Guardrails: (1) we must ACTUALLY be it — real tests, real reviews, real guarantee; (2) attack the category PATTERN, never name-and-shame a specific competitor.

🔒 LOCKED one-liner: "We are the blunt, athlete-to-athlete brand that stands FOR proven, no-shame protection and AGAINST an industry that sells women pads instead of the truth — leak-proof underwear for active women who refuse to pee themselves quietly and refuse to be called old."

The Funnel Map

Chosen funnel: the Discovery Stack — Advertorial (pre-sell) → Offer Page. Why: she's problem/solution-aware (§5), the market is Stage 4–5 + ~⅓ burned (§8), single-hero defaults to the Discovery Stack. She needs mechanism + proof BEFORE the offer; cold traffic must not hit a bare PDP (§7).

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Stage                               What it does for THIS product
  ----------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Ad                                  Stops the scroll on a relatable MOMENT (double-under / trampoline / treadmill / sneeze) + a credible mechanism tease ("built for urine, not blood — lab-tested"). Earns the click, NOT the sale. Compliant framing.

  Pre-sell (Advertorial)              Gives the mechanism (UMP→UMS) + proof (published ml test, PFAS cert, pour-demo, real reviews) + the "common but not normal / France" story. Walks the §9B belief chain.

  Product page                        Sells the product + offer: ml-rated tiers, fit quiz (true-US sizing), real reviews incl. misses, the wear-test money-back guarantee as a headline, bundle ladder (3-pack hero). Offer built in Step 2 from §15 inputs.

  Cart                                Raises order value: bundle up-tier (add a pair / add overnight), free-shipping threshold, subscribe-&-save (replenish every 2–3 months).

  After purchase                      Post-purchase fit follow-up (cuts returns, seeds exchanges), replenishment reminder at ~60–90 days, new-color cross-sell, review/UGC request. Where the LTV (and the economics) are made.
