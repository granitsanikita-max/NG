How do I wash them?

1. Rinse them in cold water after you take them off.
2. Zip them into a mesh wash bag (the Wash Bag comes free with 5 or 10 pairs) and machine wash cold (86°F / 30°C max) on a gentle cycle.
3. Use a mild detergent with no fabric softener, or the Wash Sheets.
4. Hang them to dry. No dryer, no bleach, no ironing.

Heat and softener are hard on the lining. Skip both and your pairs are made to be worn and washed again and again.

Will they smell?

Wash them after every wear, like any underwear, and follow the steps above. Skipping softener keeps the lining soaking up properly. We don’t claim they’re “antibacterial,” because we haven’t tested that.
