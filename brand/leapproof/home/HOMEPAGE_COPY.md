## 2. Who she is (the reader in one paragraph)

Mara, 36. Two kids, the youngest is two. Back at CrossFit or Orangetheory three or four mornings a week, runs a 10k, gets dragged onto the trampoline at birthday parties. A few drops escape on double-unders, a hard sneeze, a belly laugh, the last kilometre. She has a ritual: cross the legs, clench, freeze, check the leggings. She has tried "just in case" period underwear and pads that bunch and rustle. She's bought a "leak-proof" pair off an ad before and it soaked straight through, so tonight, on the couch after bedtime, she typed our name into Google to see if we're another one. She is not old, not a patient, and she'll close the tab the second we make her feel like either.

Her own words we write in (data bank): "pee when I cough, sneeze, laugh, jump, run, or get startled" · "it's just a little leak that might look like a crotch sweat" · "You think twice before jumping rope or hitting the trampoline park" · "Trying for a squat PR, pee your pants" · "walking around in a wet diaper" · "I fell for the marketing because I really wanted them to work" · "I'm so tired of false ads" · "I forget I am wearing special underwear" · "We. Do. Not. Have. To. Pee. Ourselves." · "crossing their legs every time they cough or laugh."

Iceberg (Fundamental #10), deepest depth this page reaches: **identity** — "I'm still me. Strong, capable, not old, not broken. This is plumbing, not a verdict." We sell that back, not a gusset.
