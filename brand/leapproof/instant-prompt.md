## The brand
Leapproof makes leak-proof underwear for active women in their 30s–40s who leak a little when they sneeze, laugh, jump, run or lift — new moms, perimenopause, gym-goers. The whole brand is the opposite of the old "incontinence" category: not medical, not a diaper, not beige and hushed. It's confident, warm, a little blunt, athlete-to-athlete. Think premium women's intimates/activewear (Knix, Skims, Thinx), not a pharmacy. The mechanism is "built for urine, not blood — a seatbelt for your bladder." Positioning line: "the active woman's leak — not a diaper, not a period."

Tone of all placeholder copy: warm, direct, dry-funny, never ashamed, never clinical. Open on the moment (a sneeze, a jump, the trampoline with the kids), never on the condition. Never use the words "incontinence", "discreet", "dignity" or "protection", and never make absolute claims like "100% leak-proof".

## The funnel — build these screens in order
1. **Quiz landing page** — one job: get the first tap. A warm hero (space for a motion photo/video of a real woman mid-jump or laughing), a headline opening on the moment, 3 trust badges, and one big claret CTA: "Find your match + price in 60 seconds." No navigation distractions.
2. **The quiz — "The 60-Second Leak-Test"** — ~15 questions across 3 named chapters ("Your Moment", "What's Really Happening", "Your Fit & Life"). One question per screen, big tappable answer tiles (Blush fill, Claret selected state), single-select auto-advances, an honest progress bar, a "why we ask" line on sensitive questions, and a trust/proof card every few screens. This is where the selling happens — make it feel like a conversation, not a form.
3. **Soft email/SMS gate** — appears late, framed as "where should we send your match?", with a visible Skip. Ask name first.
4. **Labor-illusion loader** (4–8s animated) — "Matching your absorbency… checking your size… applying your discount + free gifts…" with a progress animation, then reveals the result.
5. **Reveal / Offer page** — the hero of the funnel. Shows a personalised result ("The Comeback" / "The Reset" / "The Unbothered Athlete"), a "built for you because…" checklist of her quiz answers, then ONE recommended bundle pre-selected and badged, with the other bundles selectable below it. Price per pair, the saving, the free gifts, and the guarantee all visible. Big claret "Get my set" CTA.
6. **Cart drawer** (slide-out, not a page) — a free-shipping/free-gift progress meter at the top, an order-bump ("add an overnight pair"), and one clean savings total.
7. **Checkout recap** — reassurance block near the pay button: what she's getting, the gifts, the guarantee.
8. **Post-purchase one-click upsell** page — "add 3 more pairs to your box, one tap."

A buy box with the bundle selector, subscription option, and per-pair price.

Keep it smooth and premium, never flashy or slow. Respect reduced-motion settings.
