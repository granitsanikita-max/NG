# 🎬 V1 — Persona Advertorial Listicle × Angle B (fluid, not fat)

## ② Goal + target

**Goal of this ad / what it must do:** take a cold woman who believes her puffiness is fat/aging/willpower and doesn't know us, stop her scroll with a native "here's exactly what I changed" post (reads like a real woman, not an ad), **install the belief-flip (fluid, not fat), and earn the click** to the advertorial. It is NOT a hard sell — its job is belief + click.

### The target — Avatar (write to ONE: *"Dana, 46, nurse in Newcastle, rings won't come off after a shift"*)
- **Demographic:** woman 42–54, peri/menopausal, AU suburban, mid income, partnered, desk-or-on-feet job, phone-first, scrolls FB/IG in the evening.
- **Experiences:** clothes that fit last month are tight by afternoon; rings won't come off at night; catches her puffy reflection at 5pm; "slightly pregnant" belly after meals; a 5-minute doctor who said "try to lose a bit of weight"; burned by teas, gummies, magnesium, water pills.
- **Behaviours:** reads ingredient labels; googles "why am I so bloated"; lurks menopause/Mumsnet threads; adds debloat products to cart then abandons over "is it a scam"; untags herself from photos.
- **Emotions:** frustrated, betrayed by her own body, self-conscious, quietly resigned ("maybe this is just my age"), a flicker of hope she keeps getting burned on.

### Core desire — the "why?" method
- **Surface:** "I want to get rid of the puffiness / not feel bloated by evening."
- Why? → so my clothes fit and I don't look swollen in photos.
- Why? → because when I'm puffy I feel like I've let myself go.
- Why? → because I don't look or feel like *myself* anymore.
- Why? → because I'm scared this is just aging and there's nothing I can do — my body's doing this now and I've lost control of it.
- Why? → because I want to feel like ME again, in control of my own body, not a tired swollen stranger disappearing into middle age.
- **DEEP DRIVER → regain control of her own body + feel like herself again (agency + identity).**
- *This format leans on the **agency** rung — "here's exactly what I did" = you CAN do something about it.*

### Angle + emotion
- **Angle (the ONE problem attacked):** the belief that puffiness = fat/willpower. Flip it: it's **fluid, and it's not your fault.**
- **Emotion to transfer:** relief + validated indignation ("it was never you — you were sold the wrong fix") → hope. **Valence positive, intensity moderate.** Warm educational entry (not fear-led) because she enters closer to Unaware.

### Dials + funnel
- **Awareness (Dial 1):** Unaware → Problem-aware → **enter with a relatable moment/story + curiosity gap** ("here's exactly what I changed, and it wasn't more water").
- **Sophistication (Dial 2):** Stage 4→5 → **new mechanism (fluid/tide-not-drain) + identity/honesty.**
- **Funnel move:** cold scroll (believes it's fat / has given up) → **clicks to the advertorial**, now curious it might be fluid. (TOF → advertorial.)

### The 3 modifiers
- **Belief-change depth (skepticism × price):** skepticism medium-high × price mid → **medium-deep** belief change. The native listicle *lowers* skepticism by not looking like an ad and giving free value first.
- **Iceberg / identity depth:** identity-linked buy → go to **belief/identity** ("feel like yourself"), not surface "less bloat."
- **Length / story arc:** Mid (1–3 min) → **use anticipation + a light arc** (struggle → the shift → the steps → payoff).

## ③ Decide — pull from the swipe file
1. **Format:** Persona-authored advertorial listicle (native/story-led, TOF). Container = *"a real woman's 'here's what I changed' post."*
2. **Hook format:** Listicle/Steps + Storytelling/Personal, delivered spoken + text overlay. **Chosen hook:** *"I'm 44 and puffy by 5pm every day — here's the 3 things I changed, and none of it was another debloat pill."* **Bridge = Proof** ("by week two my rings came off without the soap trick").
3. **Structure:** **Native Mini Sales Letter (MSL) 🔥 + Belief-changework Body 🔥.** Beats: hook → symptom story → belief-flip (UMP) → 3 steps (move / less salt / the drops as the missing support = UMS) → honesty (what it can't do) → 60-day keep-the-bottle → CTA.
4. **Sanity check (stage playbook):** Unaware/Problem = viral / educational / before-after / founder-story / question → a **native educational listicle-story fits ✓.** Sophistication 4-5 = new mechanism + identity ✓.

# 🎬 V2 — UGC Testimonial (skeptic-converted) × Angle A (honest skeptic)

## ② Goal + target

**Goal of this ad / what it must do:** take a problem/solution-aware woman who's TRIED debloat stuff and been burned (skeptical — "plant extracts won't change your anatomy"), stop her with a real woman voicing that exact skepticism, then **collapse it with an honest, specific conversion story** and earn the click. This is the **volume format — mass-produce 5+ variants.**

### The target — Avatar (write to ONE: *"Joanne, 49, still got charged $115/mo after she cancelled once, swears she'll never fall for it again"*)
- **Demographic:** same 40–55 AU woman, but specifically the **skeptic / burned-before** segment — has bought 2–3 products, possibly hit by an unwanted subscription, reads reviews obsessively.
- **Experiences:** left a 1-star review once; got charged after "cancelling"; tried magnesium/teas/water pills; her specific symptom (rings / face / waistband / ankles); told herself to stop wasting money.
- **Behaviours:** reads every review before buying; screenshots receipts; warns friends about scams; cynical about before/afters — but still quietly searching for the one that's real.
- **Emotions:** cynical, burned, defensive, protective of her money, tired of being fooled; underneath, a guarded hope + a wish to be proven wrong.

### Core desire — the "why?" method
- **Surface:** "I want something that actually works for the bloat — and won't rip me off."
- Why? → because I've wasted money and felt stupid too many times.
- Why? → because I hate feeling like a fool / being taken advantage of.
- Why? → because I want to trust my own judgment again and stop being the one who gets scammed.
- Why? → because I want to feel smart and in control, not naïve — and still get the relief I came for.
- Why? → because underneath I still want to feel like myself (lighter, less puffy) **without surrendering my dignity to another scam.**
- **DEEP DRIVER → get the relief AND keep her dignity/self-trust — be proven right to hope again without being made a fool (control over being deceived + restored hope).**
- *This format leans on the **dignity / self-trust (vindication)** rung.*

### Angle + emotion
- **Angle:** attack the belief "these are all scams / plant drops can't work" — by **voicing it and flipping it honestly.**
- **Emotion:** vindicated skepticism / the **shock of "I was wrong"** → cautious hope → relief. (Doc: fear & shock share hardest — V2 uses the shock of a skeptic admitting she was wrong.) **Valence negative→positive; intensity moderate-high.**

### Dials + funnel
- **Awareness (Dial 1):** Problem-aware → Solution-aware → **enter with the problem agitated + the skeptic frame**, then the mechanism ("there's a new way — and here's why this one's honest").
- **Sophistication:** Stage 4→5 → **identity/honesty (the un-scammy one) + proven/easier mechanism.** In an exhausted market the honest-skeptic testimonial IS the identity play.
- **Funnel move:** burned skeptic scrolling → **clicks believing this might be the honest one.** (TOF/MOF → product/advertorial.)

### The 3 modifiers
- **Belief-change depth:** skepticism **HIGH** × price mid → **deep** belief change. The peer-testimonial + honesty (what it can't do, no subscription) is the depth vehicle.
- **Iceberg / identity depth:** identity-linked (dignity, self-trust) → go **deep to identity**, not "it reduces bloat."
- **Length / story arc:** Short (<60s) → **punch + mini-arc** (skeptic line → the turn → concrete proof → honest reassurance → CTA). Skip long anticipation.

## ③ Decide — pull from the swipe file
1. **Format:** UGC testimonial, single spokesperson (mass-produce 5+). Container = *"a real woman talking to her phone."*
2. **Hook format:** Storytelling/Personal + Myth-Busting. **Chosen hook:** *"A month ago I typed 'plant extracts won't change your body' into a review. Then my rings started fitting again."* **Bridge = Proof** (line 2 is the result) **+ qualifier hook** ("if you've been burned by debloat stuff before, watch this").
3. **Structure:** **Mountain Ice — Testimonial/Review-Quote-Led 🔥** (objection + turnaround inside the opening) + **Stamny Product-Teaser Problem-Story** + **Hero's Journey 1st-person.** Beats: skeptic quote → problem → honest discovery → concrete proof → what it can't do + no subscription → CTA.
4. **Sanity check (stage playbook):** Problem/Solution = before/after, founder story, us-vs-them, benefit callouts, **testimonial ✓.** Honest-skeptic testimonial squarely fits. Mass-produce across: rings · face-in-photos · waistband · ankles/socks · wedding-in-3-weeks.

# 🎬 V3 — Trigger Events AI-Animation VSL × Angle B + honesty

## ② Goal + target

**Goal of this ad / what it must do:** take a problem-aware woman, drop her into an emotional cold-open of the moment her puffiness became unbearable, walk her through rock-bottom + failed fixes so she feels **SEEN**, reveal (via AI animation) the hidden mechanism (lymph/fluid) she's never understood, let her **discover** the product without a pitch, and pay it off with her **return-to-self**. Long-form; carries the deepest belief change + emotional transfer.

### The target — Avatar (write to ONE: *"Dana, 46, caught her reflection at her best friend's wedding and didn't recognise the puffy, tired face"*)
- **Demographic:** same 40–55 AU woman, problem-aware, but the one who has **RESIGNED** — "this is just my age now," stopped actively searching, worn down, scrolls in the evening.
- **Experiences:** a specific trigger moment (5pm wedding / change-room / rings at dinner); the full failed-fix graveyard (salt, water pills → dizzy, magnesium, teas, the dismissive doctor); private low moments (untagging photos, "what is wrong with me").
- **Behaviours:** has stopped trying new things; half-watches health content; susceptible to a story that finally explains WHY (nobody's ever explained the mechanism); responds to being understood.
- **Emotions:** resignation, quiet grief for her old self, self-blame, invisibility, exhaustion; beneath it, a buried wish to feel like herself again.

### Core desire — the "why?" method (deepest of the three)
- **Surface:** "I want the puffiness gone."
- Why? → so I look and feel like myself in the mirror and in photos.
- Why? → because right now I don't recognise myself — I look tired and swollen and older than I feel.
- Why? → because it makes me feel like I'm disappearing / losing myself to middle age.
- Why? → because I'm grieving the version of me that felt light, confident, seen.
- Why? → because I want to feel like ME again — visible, in control of my body, not resigned to a stranger's face.
- **DEEP DRIVER → reclaim her identity and stop disappearing — feel seen and like herself again, and believe her body isn't beyond her control (the identity/consciousness rung).**
- *This format leans on the deepest **identity / self-recognition** rung — which is why it earns a long emotional VSL.*

### Angle + emotion
- **Angle:** it's fluid, not fat, not your fault — and **here's the hidden reason (your lymphatic system), shown**, so the resignation lifts.
- **Emotion:** full arc — recognition of the low moment (resignation / quiet shame — negative, high intensity) → relief/understanding (the mechanism explains it, it's not your fault) → hope → the joy of return-to-self. (Doc: fear & shock share hardest — the cold-open trigger + "this is happening inside your body and no one told you" carries the intensity; **paired with relief/hope** so it converts, not just scares.) **Valence negative→positive; intensity high.**

### Dials + funnel
- **Awareness (Dial 1):** Problem-aware → **enter with the problem, agitated** — the trigger scene ("if you deal with this every evening…").
- **Sophistication:** Stage 4→5 → **new mechanism shown via animation** (strongest possible mechanism proof at stage 4) **+ identity/honesty** (stage 5). The animation IS the "easier/more proven mechanism," made visible.
- **Funnel move:** resigned problem-aware woman scrolling → **clicks believing it's fluid + that there's finally hope + trust.** (TOF → advertorial/VSL funnel.)

### The 3 modifiers
- **Belief-change depth:** skepticism high × price mid + a mechanism she's *never understood* → **deepest** belief change. The animated UMP→UMS + honesty is the vehicle; long form gives room.
- **Iceberg / identity depth:** fully **identity/consciousness-linked** → go all the way ("feel like yourself again," "stop disappearing"). Never stay surface.
- **Length / story arc:** Long-form VSL (3 min+) → **full anticipation + 5-act arc**; open & close loops; return to the Act 1 scene.

## ③ Decide — pull from the swipe file
1. **Format:** Long-form **AI-animation VSL**, Trigger Events container (Video Ad Formats: VSL/long-form, TOF, problem-aware).
2. **Hook format:** Trigger-scene cold open (Problem/Pain + Storytelling). **Chosen hook:** *"It was 5pm at my best friend's wedding when I caught my reflection — and didn't recognise the puffy, tired face looking back."* **Bridge = Warning/qualifier** ("if you've ever felt this by evening, you need to see what's actually causing it").
3. **Structure:** **Trigger Events Ad — 5-Act (product enters late) + Restural Clinical-Animation Belief-Flip Mechanism VSL** (for Act 4's animated mechanism). Beats = the 5 acts already mapped (Trigger → Rock Bottom → Discovery → Mechanism (animation) → Payoff/return-to-scene + guarantee + CTA).
4. **Sanity check (stage playbook):** Problem-aware = before/after, question, founder story → a problem-aware story-VSL fits ✓. Sophistication 4-5 = new mechanism (animation) + identity ✓. Length >20s narrative = use anticipation & arc ✓.
