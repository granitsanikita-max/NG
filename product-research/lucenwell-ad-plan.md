## 0 · Locks that apply to EVERY ad

- **ICP:** women 35–55. Priority segments (by VoC volume): perimenopause/menopause → cyclical/PMDD "puff up every luteal" → "puffy face by 5pm" 30+ → post-op/event-driven. Market: AU.
- **Awareness:** mostly **Problem Aware** (they know they're puffy/bloated, tried things) + **Solution Aware** (know supplements/lymphatic exist, skeptical). A slice is **Unaware** of the "it's fluid, not fat" reframe → that's what TOF education ads convert.
- **Sophistication: HIGH** (crowded category, scam-fatigued). Schwartz rule: at high sophistication you win with a **NEW mechanism** or an **identity/honesty** angle — NOT "it works great." We use both.
- **Funnel: TOF-heavy on purpose.** Store has no sales history yet, so per your own rule we do NOT run bottom-of-funnel winners. Every proven model below is TOF cold-traffic.

### The mechanism (this is our "new mechanism" — use in every ad)
- **UMP — Unique Mechanism of the Problem:** the puffiness isn't fat and isn't willpower. Your lymphatic drainage system has no pump — it relies on movement — and it slows down from **salt, hormones (perimenopause/luteal), and sitting/standing all day.** Fluid then pools in your face, fingers, ankles, belly → "puffy, heavy, bloated," worse by evening.
- **UMS — Unique Mechanism of the Solution:** 21 traditional botanicals (burdock, cleavers, dandelion…) with a long history of supporting the body's **natural fluid balance** — *"a tide, not a drain."* Gentle support, **NOT a diuretic** (no dehydration, no running to the loo), **NOT a detox.**
- **Honesty layer (our moat):** we say what it CAN'T do. No "flush your toxins," no "lose 10kg."

### The 3 angles (all pulled from the VoC, all honesty-native)
- **A — The Honest Skeptic:** agree with the hater first. *("Plant drops won't change your body" — I said that too.)*
- **B — Fluid, not fat, not your fault:** the mechanism reframe.
- **C — No subscription, no scam:** rides the category's #1 complaint (every drops competitor is torched on Trustpilot for subscription traps).

### The offer (same everywhere)
60-day keep-the-bottle guarantee · **no subscription, buy once** · free shipping · AU.
